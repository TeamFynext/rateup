# Google Review Setup

RateUp allows you to seamlessly integrate Google Reviews into your customer feedback system. By setting up your Google Review page within RateUp, you can harness the power of customer reviews from one of the most widely used platforms on the internet.

##### Questions which support Google Review

1. Rating
2. Smiley
3. Opinion scale

##### How to setup google review 

1. Add link to the google review page in the team setting under Google review section
2. Click on `Google Review` in question you want to setup and enable Google Reviews
3. Select the measure for enabling google review
4. save the changes
