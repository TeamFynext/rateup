## How to export responses from Survey

RateUp provides facility to export the responses from each survey for your further analytics. Mainly there are types of export available.

#### 1. Google Sheet Sync

You can sync the responses into your Google sheet account. This features adds responses to google sheet whenever a user submit a response if the sheet is synced to the rateup.

To sync the response,
 - Go to the `Result` tab of survey whose responses you want to export.
 - Switch to `Response` tab
 - If the responses are available, then export button will be enabled in the right end. Click on that.
 - Click on `Sync to Google Sheet`.
 - If google sheet account is not synced to the RateUp, a modal appear to redirect to the settings. You can also directly go to advanced settings tab in the setting.
 - Click on `Sync Account`.
 - Give the consent to access the google drive from the selected account and then continue.
 - Once it is synced, You can go to Survey page and Click on `Sync to Google Sheet`.
 - Then, the current response will be exported to Google sheet. Also any incoming response will be added.
 - You can also resync the response, which will rewrite all the available response to sheet.
 - The sheet will be available in your drive in **RateUp** folder having name of survey.
 - If you couldn't find the sheet,go to the survey setting page. Move to Google Sheet section where you will find the link to the sheet.

#### 2. Excel Download

You can also download responses in xlsx file. To do that,  
 - Go to the `Result` tab of survey whose responses you want to export.
 - Switch to `Response` tab
 - If the responses are available, then export button will be enabled in the right end. Click on that.
 - Click on `Excel Download`.

Note that the incoming responses will not automatically added to the sheet in excel download.


#### 3. Import Contacts

If your survey has [Contact Question](../websurvey/editsurvey/questiontypes/contact), then the import contacts option will be available in responses.
It saves contacts recieved from the responses to `Contacts` in WhatsApp section. To import contacts,

 - Go to the `Result` tab of survey whose responses you want to export.
 - Switch to `Response` tab
 - If the responses are available, then export button will be enabled in the right end. Click on that.
 - Click on `Import Contacts`.
