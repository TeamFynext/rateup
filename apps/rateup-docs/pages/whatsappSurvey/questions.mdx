# Edit WhatsApp Survey

## Question Types

You can add questions similar like web survey ( [Add Question](/webSurvey/questiontypes#steps-to-add-questions) ).

Please note that following question types are not available for WhatsApp Survey.

1. Multiple Select
2. Dropdown Single Select,
3. Dropdown Multiple Select,
4. Large Text.

### Preview Question

You can preview the question you have created to ensure how it will be appear on your user device.

- Click on the eye icon at the right end of the question view for preview.

### Share Survey

Once your questions are ready, you can publish your survey and share to the customer through generated link or QR code.
