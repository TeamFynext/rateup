# WhatsApp Survey
RateUp's WhatsApp Survey feature is designed to enhance user engagement and convenience by enabling the creation of surveys accessible directly through WhatsApp. This integration leverages the popularity and widespread usage of WhatsApp, providing a seamless experience for participants to respond to surveys without the need for additional apps or complicated access methods.

#### Create Your WhatsApp Survey
 - Navigate to the `Survey` menu under WhatsApp Survey Section. 
 - Then Provide details for the survey. You can refer [Web Survey Creation](/webSurvey) page.
