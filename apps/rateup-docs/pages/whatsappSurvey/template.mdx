# WhatsApp Templates

Templates provide a structured and predefined format for your WhatsApp flows, making it convenient for users to understand the survey content and for you to maintain consistency in survey design. These templates can be particularly useful when conducting similar types of WhatsApp flows or when you want to standardize the WhatsApp flow format for your audience.

#### Create Template

- To create template go to `Templates` under WhatsApp. Click on `Create Template`.
- Give the necessary information and click on `Submit`.

#### Edit Template

You can also edit template you created by clicking on template card.
