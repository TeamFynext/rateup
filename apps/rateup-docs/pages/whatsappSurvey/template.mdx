# WhatsApp Templates

Templates provide a structured and predefined format for your surveys, making it convenient for users to understand the survey content and for you to maintain consistency in survey design. These templates can be particularly useful when conducting similar types of surveys or when you want to standardize the survey format for your audience.

#### Create Template

- To create template go to `Templates` under WhatsApp Survey. Click on `Add Template`.
- Give the necessary information and click on `Create`.

#### Edit Template

You can also edit template you created either by clicking on template or `...` icon.
