# Survey Welcome page

Customizing the welcome page allows you to create a branded and engaging introduction to your survey. It helps in making respondents feel more comfortable and sets a positive tone for their survey-taking experience. Additionally, the customization options ensure that the welcome page aligns with your brand aesthetics and survey objectives.

#### Enabling the Welcome Page
   - The welcome page is the first impression respondents have when they access your survey. To customize it, you first need to enable the welcome page in the `Welcome Page` tab of the survey settings. By default, it is usually disabled.

#### Background Customization
   - You have the option to choose the background for the welcome page. This can be either a color or an image. Choosing a background helps set the tone and visual appeal of the welcome page.

#### Logo Upload
   - You can upload a logo for the survey on the welcome page. This logo could be your brand logo or any relevant image. Additionally, you can adjust the size of the logo to ensure it fits well with the overall design.

#### Title Customization
   - The title of the welcome page is a crucial element. You can give your survey a catchy or informative title and style it according to your preferences. This helps in conveying the purpose or theme of the survey.

#### Description Customization
   - A brief description provides additional context or instructions to respondents. You can give a description on the welcome page and style it to enhance readability and visual appeal.

#### Button Customization
   - The button on the welcome page is typically what respondents click to start the survey. You can customize the content of the button and style it to match the overall theme. This includes changing the text on the button and adjusting its appearance.

#### Preview Options
   - Once you have completed the customization of the welcome page, you can preview how it will look on both web and mobile platforms. This is important to ensure that the design is visually appealing and user-friendly on different devices.
