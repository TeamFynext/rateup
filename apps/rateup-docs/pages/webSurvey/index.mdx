# Web Survey

## Create Your Web Survey

With RateUp, you have the ability to craft engaging web surveys that provide users with a rich experience and yield valuable feedback.

This platform enables you to design surveys in multiple languages and across various teams, catering to a diverse audience.

## Survey Creation Steps:

### 1. Access Web Survey Section

Navigate to the `Web Survey` section in the sidebar and initiate the survey creation process by clicking the **Create Survey** button.

### 2. Choose a Template

- **Option 1: Use Existing Template**
  - Select a template from an existing survey.
  - Click the **Start** button to proceed.

- **Option 2: Start Blank**
  - If you prefer to start from scratch, click the **Start Blank** button.

### 3. Survey Details (If Starting Blank)

- If creating a new survey from scratch, provide:
  - **Name:** Assign a name to your survey.
  - **Languages:** Specify the languages for your survey.
  - **Teams:** Choose the teams to which the survey will be assigned.

### Final Steps

Once these steps are complete, your survey is ready to be edited and customized according to your preferences.

Now, you can tailor the survey to meet your specific requirements and gather valuable insights. This streamlined process ensures flexibility and ease in creating surveys tailored to your needs.
