# Edit Web Survey

## Adding Questions to Your Survey

Once your survey is created, you can seamlessly add questions by navigating to the`Edit` tab of the selected survey. Here, you'll find the `Add Question` button, which opens up a list of available question types. Choose any question type from the categories, and it will generate a blank question that you can customize according to your preferences.

### Steps to Add Questions

1. Click on `Add Question` in the `Edit` tab of your selected survey.
2. Choose a question type from the available categories.
3. A blank question will be created, and you can modify it to suit your needs.

### Question Details

For all questions, provide the following details:

1. **Question Title:** Clearly state your question in the text edior.
2. **Multiple Languages:** Input the question and description in all selected languages.
3. Save the changes.

### Preview and Categories

Preview your question to ensure it appears as intended. There are five categories of question types available for your survey:

##### Contact Questions
   - **Contact** : Accepts only name, phone and email responses.

##### NPS Questions
   - **Rating** : Rate out of 5 stars.
   - **Smiley** : Choose from 5 smileys (very sad, sad, average, happy, very happy).
   - **Yes or No** : Provide a yes or no response.
   - **Like or Dislike** : Indicate whether the response is like or dislike.
   - **Opinion Scale** : Rate on a scale of 10.
   - **Custom NPS** : Choose from multiple options with assigned weightage (promoter, passive, detractor).

##### Choice Questions
   - **Single Select** : Allows selecting one choice from multiple options.
   - **Multiple Select** : Permits choosing more than one option.
   - **Dropdown Single Select** : Requires selecting one choice from a dropdown list.
