# Survey Theme

Theme provides a facility to customize user interface of the survey page, welcome page, thank you page and language selection page.

Customizing the pages allows you to create a branded and engaging introduction to your survey. It helps in making respondents feel more comfortable and sets a positive tone for their survey-taking experience. Additionally, the customization options ensure that the welcome page aligns with your brand aesthetics and survey objectives.


In order to customize these, Move to `Theme` tab of selected survey and then you can customize each page by switching the tab.
You can also use once created theme in another survey.

You can customize following things for each page.

#### 1. Background Customization
   - You have the option to choose the background for the all pages. This can be either a color or an image. Choosing a background helps set the tone and visual appeal of the page.

#### 2. Logo Upload
   - You can upload a logo for the survey page. This logo could be your brand logo or any relevant image. Additionally, you can adjust the size of the logo to ensure it fits well with the overall design.

#### 3. Title Customization
   - The title of page is a crucial element. You can give your survey a catchy or informative title and style it according to your preferences. This helps in conveying the purpose or theme of the survey.

#### 4. SubTitle Customization
   - A brief SubTitle provides additional context or instructions to respondents. You can give a description on the pages and style it to enhance readability and visual appeal.

#### 5. Button Customization
   - The button is typically what respondents click to start the survey or to interact with pages. You can customize the content of the button and style it to match the overall theme. This includes changing the text on the button and adjusting its appearance.

#### 6. Input Customization
   - You can customize input of the questions response for survey page. It ensures your survey page is aligned with theme perfectly.

#### Preview Options
   - Once you have completed the customization, you can preview how it will look on both web and mobile platforms. This is important to ensure that the design is visually appealing and user-friendly on different devices.
