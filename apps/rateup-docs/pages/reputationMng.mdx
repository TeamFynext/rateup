# Reputation Management

RateUp's Reputation Management provides the facility to manage customer reviews from google and facebook reviews.

It provides various features such as :
 - Overview of ratings and sentiments from various platforms
 - Visualization through graph
 - Detailed reviews and ratings
 - Provide a feature to take appropriate action
 - You can add topics to view its sentiments
