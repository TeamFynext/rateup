## Contact Questions

Contact question allow you get the contact details of the respondent. By using contact question, you can get following details:

**1. Name:** Name of the respondent.  
**2. Phone Number:** Phone Number of respondent including the country code.  
**3. Email ID:** Email ID of respondent.  

The greater flexibility of contact question is that you can disable any of the above field as needed. Additionally, you can 
provide a custom heading for each of these fields.

### Add Contact Question

To add a contact question,

 1. Click on `Add Content` in edit tab of selected Survey and then click on **`Contact`** under Contact Questions.
 2. A blank contact question will be created. By default, all fields will be enabled and there will be no custom heading provided.
 3. Then, provide a proper question in the text editor.
 4. To provide a custom heading for name field, click on `enter name heading here` below the enable button and then provide heading.
    Similarly, you can do for other fields.
 5. If multiple langauges are selected, Switch the language in the right of text editor and repeat the steps 3 & 4 in the selected language.
