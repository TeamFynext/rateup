# Campaign

A Campaign is a valuable tool for gathering customer feedback during special occasions, employing WhatsApp surveys. By creating a campaign and adding participants to it, you can efficiently collect opinions. Once the campaign begins, surveys are sent individually to each participant, streamlining the feedback process.

## Create Campaign

You can create a campaign by navigate to `Campaign` in side bar and click on `create campaign`.

 - Provide a name for the campaign.
 - Select a whatsapp survey for the campaign
 - Select a survey template for the campaign.

#### Add Participants

once the campaign is created, you can add participants by clicking on `Add Participants`.

You can only add participants from created contacts and Group. To create contact and contact group [click here](/campaign/contact).

The added participants will list in campaign page with their status. If the participants are added from group, then group members will be listed.

#### Remove participants from campaign

To remove participants from campaign click on `⋯` and select Remove participant

#### Start campaign

To start the campaign enable `start campaign`. Once it is started, the survey will be sent to the all participants.
