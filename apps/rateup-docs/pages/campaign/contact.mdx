# Customer Contact 

In the RateUp Customer Engagement Platform, we understand the critical role that customer contacts play in your business success. Our Customer Contact Management feature allows you to efficiently save and organize customer contacts, empowering you to create targeted groups for campaigns and personalized engagement.


#### Create Contact

To create contact navigate `contact` on the sidebar and click on `Create`. Then choose `Customer`.

There are 2 choices:

1. **Manual :** Manually provide information such as name, phone and email.
2. **Import csv :** You can save contacts by uploading an csv file having the information of customer contacts with headers `name, phone and email`.

#### Create Group

To create a cutomer group click on `Create` and choose `Group`.

Provide information such as group name, select a team to the which group belongs to and upload an profile picture for the group.

##### Add customer to Group

Once you created group, you can add customers to the group by clicking on `Add Customer`.

There are 2 choices:

1. **Add new contact :** You can create similar like [creating contact](#create-contact). It will create new contact and add the customer to group.
2. **Add existing contact :** You can select the already created customers and can add to group.


#### Edit Contact and Group
 - To update or delete contact info click on `⋯` in the customer card and select `Edit` or `delete contact` respectively.
 - To update and delete Group info click on `Edit Profile` and `Delete this Group` respectively.

#### Remove customer from Group
- To remove customers from group there are 2 methods:

Navigate to group. Then,
  1. To remove individual customer click on `⋯` in the customer and select `Remove customer`.
  2. To remove more customers at once, click on `Edit` near to the `Add customer` and select the customers you want to remove and click on `Remove customers`
