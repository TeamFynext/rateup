# Group Survey
RateUp's Group Survey feature enhances the survey-taking experience by allowing users to access multiple surveys seamlessly through a single link. Whether you're conducting related research, business assessments, or event feedback, this functionality streamlines the process, providing a unified and organized approach to data collection.

### How to Use Group Survey Feature

#### Create a Group Survey
   - Log in to your RateUp account and navigate to the `Group Survey` under `Web Survey` Section .
   - Click on `Creatr Group` and Give a proper name and header for th group Survey. The header will be displayed on the landing page of the survey.
   - Select the surveys you want to group together. Click on `Group`.
#### Group Survey Welcom page
     Once your group created, you can customize welcome page similar like in web survey. Please refer [here](/webSurvey/welcompage).

#### Group Survey Link 
   - Once your surveys are grouped, a unique link for the entire group will be provided in the `Share` tab. This link will be the single access point for participants.
   - Share the generated link with your participants through emails, social media, or any other communication channels. Participants can now access all surveys in the group with just one click.
   - It also generate QR code for the created group survey and you can customize the qr code according to your preference.

By following these steps, you can harness the power of Rateup's Group Survey feature to simplify data collection and analysis, providing a more efficient and organized survey-taking experience.
