A **link page** is a single page of buttons behind one short link: your surveys, a WhatsApp chat,
your menu, your website. Share one address and let people choose where to go.

It is the answer to "we only get one link in the bio", and the way to let customers pick which
survey to fill in from one printed code.

## Create a Link Page

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **QR & Links → Link pages** and click **Create link page**.
</Step>
<Step>
Give it a **Name**, such as *Instagram bio*. Only you see it. Up to 200 characters.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create and add links**. The editor opens on the **Links** tab.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Add link** and choose what the button opens (see below). Repeat for each button.
</Step>
<Step>
Switch on **Published** at the top of the editor. Until you do, the page is a draft and its link
shows *"This page isn't available"*.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## The List

Each tile shows the page's name, **Live** or **Draft**, how many links it holds, how many visits it
has had, and a copy button for its link (greyed out on a draft). Click the name to open the editor.

The search box, sort menu and **⋯** menu (**Rename**, **Delete**) work the same as on
[QR codes](/qr-studio/qr-creator#the-list).

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a link page breaks its link and every QR code printed for it: *"Anyone who opens its
  link, or scans a QR code for it, will find nothing."* To take a page down for a while, switch off
  **Published** instead. Its address and its counts survive.
</Callout>

## Add Links

**Add link** asks *"What should the button open?"*:

| Choice | What you pick |
|--------|---------------|
| **Web survey** | One of your [web surveys](/web-forms), by name |
| **WhatsApp survey** | One of your WhatsApp surveys, which starts in a chat |
| **Any web address** | **Button text** and a **Web address** starting with `https://`: your menu, website, booking page or social profile |

For a survey, pick it from **Survey** and RateUp fills in the link. If the survey is shared with
several teams, pick whose copy under **Team**. **Button text** starts as the survey's title; change
it to whatever visitors should read.

<Callout type="info">
  A survey that is not shared with any team has no link yet, so it cannot be added: *"This survey
  isn't shared with any team yet, so it has no link."*
</Callout>

Every change on the **Links** tab saves straight away, and goes live straight away on a published
page.

| On a link row | Does |
|---------------|------|
| Grip handle | Drag to change the order the buttons appear in |
| Pencil | **Edit link**: change its button text or web address |
| Bin | Removes the link after a confirmation. Its click count goes with it |

<Callout type="warn">
  Removing the last link unpublishes the page, since a published page cannot be empty. A page with
  no links cannot be published at all: *"Add at least one link before you publish."*
</Callout>

## Change the Look

The **Appearance** tab styles the page. The phone preview beside the editor updates as you go, but
**nothing on this tab is live until you click Save** at the bottom. The bar there reads *"Unsaved
changes"* until you do, and **Discard** puts back the saved look.

| Card | Options |
|------|---------|
| **Theme** | Sets the colours in one go: **Light**, **Dark**, **Navy**, **Warm**, **Mint** or **Blush** |
| **Header** | A **Logo** (PNG or JPG, cropped to 4:3) with a **Logo size** slider; the **Title** with **Small**, **Medium** or **Large** and a **Bold** switch; an optional **Subtitle** with its size; and a **Text colour** for both |
| **Buttons** | Shape (**Square**, **Rounded** or **Pill**), **Button colour** and **Button text** colour |
| **Background** | **Page colour**, plus optional photos: **Photo on phones** and **Photo on computers** |

For a background photo, **Choose** offers **Upload from Device** or **Select from Unsplash**. A
photo covers the page colour, and a page showing an Unsplash photo credits the photographer
automatically.

The editor warns you when text will be hard to read: *"The text is hard to read on this page
colour"* and *"The button text is hard to read on this button colour."*

On a narrow screen the phone preview is hidden; **Preview** at the top opens it in a side panel.

## Share the Page

The **Share** tab has everything for handing the page out. On a draft it is all greyed out:
*"This page is a draft. Switch on Published at the top before you share it."*

| Control | Use |
|---------|-----|
| **Page link** | The page's address. Copy it for your bio or a message |
| **WhatsApp**, **Email**, **Facebook** | Opens a share with the link filled in |
| **Embed on a website** | Code to put the page inside your own site |
| **QR code** | A printable code for the page, with the same designer as [QR codes](/qr-studio/qr-creator#design-the-code): style, colours, logo, **Save design**, **PNG**, **Print** and **Copy link** |

Once published, **Open** at the top of the editor opens the live page in a new tab.

## See What Gets Clicked

The **Stats** tab shows two totals, **Visits** and **Link clicks**, and a table of every link with
its **Clicks**, most-clicked first. **Refresh** fetches the latest numbers.

*"A visitor is counted once an hour, and so is each link they click."* The same person opening the
page five times in an afternoon counts as several visits, not one, so read the numbers as reach
rather than unique people.

## Best Practices

- Put the most important link first. Most visitors tap one thing and leave
- Keep the list short; five good links beat fifteen
- Print the page's QR code, not a code per survey, so you can swap surveys without reprinting
- Check **Stats** monthly and remove the links nobody uses

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| The **Published** switch is greyed out | The page has no links | Add at least one link |
| Visitors see *"This page isn't available"* | The page is a draft, or was unpublished when its last link was removed | Add a link and switch on **Published** |
| A new colour or photo is not on the live page | **Appearance** changes need **Save** | Click **Save** at the bottom of the tab |
| *An image didn't upload, so nothing was saved* | A logo or photo upload failed | Try **Save** again |
| A survey is missing from the **Survey** list | It is the other kind of survey, or you have none of that kind | Go back and choose **Web survey** or **WhatsApp survey** |
