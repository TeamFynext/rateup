An **access link** is a single page that holds several links. Share one URL and let people choose
where to go — a survey, your menu, a booking form, your socials.

It is the answer to "we only get one link in the bio".

## Create an Access Link

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **QR Studio → Access Links** and click **Create Access Link**.
</Step>
<Step>
Give it a **name** — this identifies it in your list.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create Link**. It opens in the editor.
</Step>
<Step>
Add the links you want on the page, and arrange them in the order visitors should see them.
</Step>
<Step>
**Publish** when you are ready. Until then it is not live.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Status

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Active** | The link is live and reachable |
| **Inactive** | The link is not serving visitors |
| **Published** | The current version has been published |

Editing a published link does not go live until you publish again — so you can rework a page
without breaking what people are currently visiting.

---

## Tracking Clicks

Each access link shows a **Clicks** count.

Compare it with the scan count on any [QR code](/qr-studio/qr-creator) pointing at it: scans tell
you how many people picked up their phone, clicks tell you how many actually went somewhere.

## Sharing

| Method | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Share** | Copy the URL to send digitally |
| **Share QR Code** | Get a QR for the access link, for print |

---

## Managing

- **Rename** — change the name without affecting the URL or its content
- **Delete** — removes the link permanently; anyone visiting the old URL will find nothing
- **Search** — find a link by name once you have several

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting an access link breaks every QR code and printed material pointing at it. If it is in the
  wild, set it inactive rather than deleting it.
</Callout>

---

## Best Practices

- Put the most important link first — most visitors tap one thing and leave
- Keep the list short; five good links beat fifteen
- Point printed QR codes at an access link, so the destinations can change later
- Check the click counts monthly and prune the links nobody uses
