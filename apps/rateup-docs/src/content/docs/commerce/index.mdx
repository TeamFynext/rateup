## How an Order Happens

1. A customer browses your **catalog** in WhatsApp
2. They add items and send the order to you
3. RateUp creates an **order** and moves it through the stages
4. A **checkout link** is sent for payment, unless the order is cash on delivery
5. Once paid, the order moves to packing and delivery
6. The conversation stays in the [Inbox](/inbox) throughout

---

## Before You Start

Commerce is built on top of your WhatsApp Business account. You need:

- A connected **WhatsApp Business** account
- A **WhatsApp catalog** connected to that account
- A **payment method** configured, if you want in-chat payment

<Callout type="warn">
  Without a connected catalog, Catalogs and Payments show a "no catalog connected" notice and the
  rest of Commerce has nothing to work with. Connect one first.
</Callout>
