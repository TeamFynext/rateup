**Commerce** is RateUp's shop. You add your products once and they publish to your WhatsApp catalog,
your Instagram shop and your own storefront page. Customers check out in the chat or on the
storefront, and every order — whichever channel it came from — lands in one list.

## Open Commerce

Clicking **Commerce** in the sidebar opens **Store Setup** while your shop still has steps left, and
**Overview** once they are all done. While setup is unfinished, the **Store Setup** entry carries a badge
with the steps done out of the total, such as **3/7**.

The **Orders** entry carries a badge with the number of orders in its **Needs action** tab — cash
orders still to be confirmed, paid orders to pack, failed deliveries and requested returns.

## How an Order Happens

| Way in | What happens |
|--------|--------------|
| A customer sends a cart from your WhatsApp catalog | RateUp turns the cart into an order and walks them through checkout in the chat |
| You build the cart in a conversation | **Sell → Create order / payment link** in the [Inbox](/inbox), or **Create order** on the Orders page, sends them a payment or checkout link |
| A shopper buys on your storefront | They check out on the storefront's own checkout page — see [Storefront](/commerce/storefront) |
| An Instagram comment or click-to-WhatsApp ad | An automation sends the products with a way to buy — see [Send Products to Buy](/automation/actions#send-products-to-buy) |

Wherever it started, the order appears under [Orders](/commerce/orders) and the conversation stays
in the [Inbox](/inbox).

## Before You Start

| You need | Where |
|----------|-------|
| Commerce turned on for your organisation | [Customize Menu](/settings/features) |
| A connected WhatsApp number | [Channels](/settings/channels) |
| A Meta catalog connected to your shop, so WhatsApp and Instagram can show your products | **Commerce → Settings → Store** |
| At least one way to get paid | [Getting Paid](/commerce/payments) |

[Store Setup](/commerce/setup) walks you through all of these in order.

## Who Can Do What

Commerce has its own permissions, set per role under [Members & Roles](/settings/users):

| Permission | Lets someone |
|------------|--------------|
| *"See products, orders and shop analytics"* | Open Commerce at all |
| *"Send products and payment links in chat"* | Sell in conversations, create orders and test orders |
| *"Fulfil, cancel and update orders"* | Mark orders packed, shipped or paid, cancel them |
| *"Add and edit products and stock"* | Add, import, edit and archive products |
| *"Create and edit coupons"* | Manage [Coupons](/commerce/coupons) |
| *"Connect payments, set shipping and refund"* | Payment providers, delivery rates, checkout settings, refunds |
| *"Set up the storefront"* | Store details and the storefront |
