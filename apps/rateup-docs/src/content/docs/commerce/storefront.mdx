The **storefront** is *"A link-in-bio shop for Instagram and anywhere else. Buyers check out in
WhatsApp or on the page."* It lives at `rateup.app/s/<your-link>` and shows every active product with
**Storefront** switched on under **Where it sells**.

## Publish Your Storefront

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Commerce → Settings → Store** and scroll to **Storefront**.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the **Link** — the part after `/s/`. It is lower-cased and anything other than letters, numbers
and dashes becomes a dash.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally add a **Banner headline**, **Banner text**, **Banner image** and your **Instagram
profile**.
</Step>
<Step>
Turn on the **Publish storefront** switch and click **Save changes**. The badge turns **Live**.
</Step>
</Steps>

**Preview** opens the **Storefront preview** — *"How shoppers will see your storefront. Checkout is
off in the preview."* — before you publish.

Once live you can **Copy storefront link**, **Open** it, and **Download QR** — *"Print it on
packaging, receipts or your counter so shoppers can scan straight in."*

<Callout type="warn">
  Changing the **Link** of a live storefront breaks every link and QR code you already shared — the
  old address shows *"This shop isn't available"*. Pick it once.
</Callout>

Without a banner headline, the top of the page advertises your newest active coupon that anyone can
use — *"Use code … at checkout"* — unless customers pay on a Shopify or WooCommerce checkout, where
RateUp codes don't work.

## Share a Product's Own Link

Every product has its own address: `rateup.app/s/<your-link>/p/<product-name>-<code>`, built from its
name plus a short code. Shared in a chat or on social media, it shows the product's photo and price.
Chat messages, comment replies and catalog links that RateUp sends use the same addresses.

| You change | The old link |
|------------|--------------|
| The product's name | Moves permanently to the new address |
| The product is hidden, a draft or sold off | Goes to the storefront instead |
| Older `?product=` links | Move permanently to the product's address |

## Get Found in Search

A live storefront and its product pages can be indexed by search engines; a preview, a switched-off
storefront and checkout pages cannot. RateUp lists every live storefront and its products in a
sitemap at `rateup.app/s/sitemap.xml`, refreshed hourly. There is nothing to turn on beyond
publishing the storefront.

## How Shoppers Buy

| Shopper does | Happens |
|--------------|---------|
| Adds products to the bag and taps **Checkout** | The checkout page asks for **Contact**, **Delivery address**, a delivery option, a **Coupon code** and points where offered, then the payment choice |
| Pays online (**Pay**) | Card payment through your [Stripe or Razorpay](/commerce/payments) |
| Picks **Cash on delivery** or **Bank transfer** | Confirms their number on WhatsApp first — see below |
| Your store checkout is chosen | *"You'll pay on Shopify's secure checkout"* — see [Checkout](/commerce/checkout-settings#choose-where-customers-pay) |
| Taps **Order on WhatsApp** | Shown when *"… can't take orders online right now"*, for example when the plan's monthly orders are used |

After ordering, the shopper's order page shows progress, tracking (**Track your parcel**), bank
details for a transfer, **Download invoice** once paid, and **Request a return** once shipped.

## How Shoppers Confirm Their Number

A number typed into a web form proves nothing, so RateUp messages it only once its owner has written
to you from it — or once the order is paid, since a payment is proof enough.

| When | The shopper sees |
|------|------------------|
| A sold-out product's **Notify me on WhatsApp** | *"Send us a quick WhatsApp message and we'll tell you when it's back."* and **Open WhatsApp** |
| Choosing **Cash on delivery** or **Bank transfer** (or a free order) | *"Confirm on WhatsApp to place your order. Send the message that opens, from the number we'll contact you on."* and **Open WhatsApp** |

**Open WhatsApp** opens a chat with your WhatsApp number with a message such as *"Confirm my order
#1042. RU-K7P2QX"* ready to send. When it arrives, RateUp places the order (or records the restock
request) and replies in that chat — so the conversation is open and later updates can reach them.

| Rule | Value |
|------|-------|
| The code expires after | 15 minutes — then *"That confirmation has run out. Choose how to pay again to get a new one."* |
| Unpaid cash or transfer orders one number can hold at once | 3 — *"You already have 3 orders waiting for payment. Pay or cancel one first."* |
| Unpaid transfer orders are cancelled after | 72 hours by default — **Hours to pay before cancelling** in [Getting Paid](/commerce/payments#take-bank-transfers) |

<Callout type="info">
  The number that sends the message is the one RateUp keeps, even if the shopper typed another. Until
  a storefront order is confirmed or paid, it gets no order messages; its timeline notes *"the number
  isn't confirmed yet"*.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"This shop isn't available"* | The storefront is off, the link changed, or Commerce is turned off | Check **Publish storefront** and the **Link** |
| A product is missing | It is a draft, archived, or **Storefront** is off under **Where it sells** | Turn it on in the product |
| *"This shop can't confirm numbers on WhatsApp yet"* | No WhatsApp number is connected | Connect one under [Channels](/settings/channels) |
| *"Too many confirmation codes from here."* | More than 5 codes from one visitor in 15 minutes | Wait a few minutes |
| No offer strip | A banner headline is set, or no active coupon is open to anyone | Expected |
