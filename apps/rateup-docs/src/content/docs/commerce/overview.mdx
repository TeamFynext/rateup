## The Headline Numbers

| Card | What it counts |
|------|----------------|
| **Total Sales** | Revenue over the selected period |
| **Orders** | Number of orders placed |
| **Avg Order Value** | Total sales divided by order count |
| **Products Live** | Products currently available in the connected catalog |

---

## Sales Over Time

A chart of sales and order volume across the period. Hover any point to see the exact figures for
that day.

Use it to spot the pattern behind the totals — a good month driven by one big order looks very
different from a good month driven by steady volume.

---

## Top Products

Your best sellers for the period, ranked. Use this to decide what to feature in campaigns, and to
notice when a product stops moving.

---

## Recent Orders

The latest orders, with the customer, the stage each has reached, and the date.

Click through to [Orders](/commerce/orders) for the full list with filtering.

---

## Reading the Numbers Honestly

- **Average order value** moves sharply on low volume — treat it as noise until you have enough
  orders for it to mean something
- **Products live** counts what is *available*, not what is selling; a high number with flat sales
  usually means the catalog needs curating, not expanding
- Compare periods rather than reading a single number in isolation
