## Read the Key Numbers

| Tile | What it counts |
|------|----------------|
| **Revenue** | Money from paid orders (including partly refunded ones), by the day they were paid |
| **Orders** | Orders placed in the period |
| **Chat to paid order** | Paid orders as a share of every order started in the period, carts included |
| **Recovered from cart reminders** | Revenue from orders paid after at least one cart or payment reminder |

The first three compare with the period of the same length just before, shown as *"+12.4% vs the
previous period"* (percentage **pts** for the conversion tile). **Recovered from cart reminders**
reads *"In the selected dates"*.

If your plan caps orders, a banner shows *"Orders this month — 12 of 50"*. When the month's orders
are used up, *"new orders are refused until the month resets"* — **Upgrade plan** opens
[Billing](/settings/billing).

## Compare Messaging Cost With Revenue

**Messaging cost vs revenue** puts what your WhatsApp messages cost next to the paid orders they
brought in. *"Orders count towards the last campaign or automation message in the 7 days before
them."*

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/commerce/message-cost.png" alt="The 'Messaging cost vs revenue' card: Messaging cost, Revenue from messages, Return on spend and Billed messages, the per-category line, and the Source table with a campaign row, 'Automations and flows' and 'Inbox replies'. Message rates button top right" />

| Figure | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Messaging cost** | Billed messages × your rate for their category |
| **Revenue from messages** | Paid orders credited to a message |
| **Return on spend** | Revenue divided by cost, as `12×` |
| **Billed messages** | Messages Meta marked as billable; a line under it splits them into **Marketing**, **Utility**, **Authentication** and **Service**, billed and free |

The **Source** table breaks the same figures down per row: each **campaign** by name, **Automations
and flows** (all automations together), **One-off templates**, **Inbox replies**, each
**Click-to-WhatsApp ad**, and **Instagram comments and stories**.

### Enter Your Own Message Rates

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Message rates** (or **Enter rates** in the notice).
</Step>
<Step>
Enter *"what Meta charges you for one delivered message"* for **Marketing**, **Utility**,
**Authentication** and **Service**, in the shop's currency. Each rate is between 0 and 10. *"Find
your rates in WhatsApp Manager under Insights."*
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save rates**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Until you do, a shop selling in US dollars uses typical rates, and the card says *"Costs are
estimates from typical rates."* A shop in any other currency shows no cost at all until rates are
entered (*"Enter what Meta charges you per message in … to see what your messages cost."*). A US
dollar shop can go back with **Use typical rates**. Entering rates needs *"Connect payments, set
shipping and refund"*.

<Callout type="info">
  These are estimates. Meta prices by the customer's country and bills your WhatsApp Business account
  directly — RateUp does not charge you for them. The card also notes that *"Replies inside the
  24-hour service window are charged at the utility rate from 1 October 2026."*
</Callout>

## See What Your Click-to-WhatsApp Ads Sold

**Click-to-WhatsApp ads** appears only when someone reached you from such an ad in the period. *"A
sale counts towards the last ad the customer clicked in the 7 days before it. Cost is what the
messages to them in that week cost."*

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Ad** | The ad's headline (linked to the ad when Meta gave a link) and *"Ad 1234…"* |
| **Clicks** / **People** | Taps on the ad, and the distinct people who tapped |
| **Cost** | What the messages to those people cost, at your [message rates](#enter-your-own-message-rates) |
| **Orders** / **Revenue** / **Return** | Paid orders credited to the ad, their value, and revenue ÷ cost |

When an order credited to an ad is paid, RateUp also reports it to Meta as a **Purchase** from that
ad, so Ads Manager sees the sale. The order page shows *"Reported to Meta as a purchase from the ad
on …"* — see [Where This Sale Came From](/commerce/orders#see-where-a-sale-came-from).

To answer ad clicks with the products the ad promoted, use the **Came From a Click-to-WhatsApp Ad**
trigger with [Send products to buy](/automation/actions#send-products-to-buy).

## Work Through Needs Attention

**Needs attention** lists what needs a person, each linked to where you fix it. *"Nothing needs you
right now."* when it is empty.

| Tag | Item |
|-----|------|
| **Stock** | Up to three tracked variants at or below their low-stock alert, with *"… people waiting for a restock"* when back-in-stock requests are waiting |
| **COD** | Cash orders the customer has not confirmed on WhatsApp yet |
| **Carts** | Carts opened today and not yet paid, with their value |
| **Return** | Return requests waiting for you |
| **Delivery** | Deliveries marked failed — *"Arrange a new slot with the customer."* |
| **Meta** | Active products Meta rejected — *"Fix them so they show in WhatsApp and Instagram."* |

**Recent orders** shows the six newest orders with payment and fulfilment status; **View all** opens
[Orders](/commerce/orders).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **Messaging cost** shows **—** | No rates for a non-USD shop | **Message rates** → enter your rates |
| No **Click-to-WhatsApp ads** card | No ad clicks in the period | Widen the dates; the card hides itself when there is nothing to show |
| A sale is not credited to a campaign | The customer's last campaign or automation message was more than 7 days before the order | Expected — only the 7 days before count |
| Revenue lower than the order total | Revenue counts paid orders only, by payment date | Check **Awaiting payment** in Orders |
