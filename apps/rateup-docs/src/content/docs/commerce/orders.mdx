## Order Stages

An order moves through these stages:

| Stage | Shown as | Meaning |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **Pending payment** | Pending | The order exists but has not been paid |
| **Checkout sent** | Confirmed | A checkout link has gone to the customer |
| **Pending delivery** | Packed | Paid and being prepared |
| **Pending address** | Shipped | On its way, awaiting or using the delivery address |
| **Completed** | Delivered | Fulfilled |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled | Called off |

<Callout type="info">
  Stages are the operational truth of the order. The customer's conversation about it stays in the
  [Inbox](/inbox), so support and fulfilment never drift apart.
</Callout>

---

## Filtering

Tabs across the top narrow the list:

- **All orders**
- **Pending** — not yet paid
- **To ship** — paid, needs packing
- **Shipped** — on the way
- **Delivered** — done

Work from **Pending** and **To ship** day to day; the rest are for reference.

---

## Payment Methods

Each order shows how it was paid:

| Method | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **UPI** | Paid by UPI |
| **Card** | Paid by card |
| **WhatsApp Pay** | Paid inside WhatsApp |
| **COD** | Cash on delivery — collect on handover |
| **Pending** | No payment recorded yet |

---

## Working an Order

1. Open the order to see its items, amount and customer
2. Move it to the next stage as fulfilment progresses
3. If the customer asks about it, their conversation is in the Inbox under their ticket
4. Mark it **Completed** on delivery, or **Cancelled** if it falls through

<Callout type="warn">
  Cancelling does not automatically refund the customer. Handle the refund in your payment
  provider, then record the cancellation here.
</Callout>
