**Orders** lists *"Every order from WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and your storefront, in one
list."* Each order has a payment status and a fulfilment status, and the list tells you the **Next
step** for each.

## Work From the Tabs

| Tab | Shows |
|-----|-------|
| **All** | Every order except drafts |
| **Needs action** | Cash orders not yet confirmed, paid orders still to pack, failed deliveries and requested returns. Its count is also the badge on **Orders** in the sidebar |
| **Awaiting payment** | Orders at the payment step that are not paid |
| **To pack** | Paid (or confirmed cash) orders not yet shipped |
| **Shipped** | **Shipped** or **Out for delivery** |
| **Delivered** | Delivered |
| **Returns** | Orders with a return, or refunded in full or part |
| **Drafts** | Carts that have not been placed yet |
| **Cancelled** | Cancelled |

Orders paid on your Shopify or WooCommerce checkout are fulfilled in that store, so they never land
in **Needs action** or **To pack** — their next step reads *"Fulfil it in Shopify"*.

Search by *"order, name, phone or Instagram handle"*, and filter by channel or payment status. Tick
orders and click **Mark packed** to pack several at once.

### Payment and Fulfilment Badges

| Payment | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Unpaid** | Nothing sent or paid yet |
| **Link sent** | A payment link is waiting |
| **Paid** / **Partly refunded** / **Refunded** | Paid, then refunded in part or full |
| **Payment failed** / **Expired** | The attempt failed or the link ran out |
| **Cash on delivery** | To be collected on delivery |

| Fulfillment | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **Unfulfilled** / **To pack** | Not packed (**To pack** once paid) |
| **Packed**, **Shipped**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered** | On its way |
| **Delivery failed**, **Returned**, **Cancelled** | Ended without delivery |

An order with a **Needs review** badge was paid, but something could not be applied — for example a
coupon used up first or points already spent. The order page says what, and how much was *"given to
the customer but not covered by the payment"*.

## Create an Order for a Customer

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create order** — *"Build a cart for a customer and send them a link to pay in their chat."*
In a conversation, the same thing is **Sell → Create order / payment link** in the [Inbox](/inbox).
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the customer and channel (or **Manual (no chat)**), add products to the **Cart**, and optionally
a coupon code and a delivery rate (**Let them choose** leaves it to the customer).
</Step>
<Step>
Choose what to send and create the order.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Send | The customer |
|------|--------------|
| **Send payment link** | *"They pay straight away; delivery details are asked in chat."* Needs Stripe or Razorpay |
| **Send checkout link** | *"They add their address, pick delivery and pay on one page."* With a store checkout: *"They pay on your Shopify checkout page."* |
| **Just create** | *"Save the order without messaging them."* |

If the customer's 24-hour window is closed, nothing can be sent in the chat — create the order and
share its link another way, or send a template first.

**Create test order** makes a draft marked as a test with one of your active products, so you can
go through checkout yourself. **Save shoppers as an audience** builds an audience from who bought
what (products, collections, number of orders, amount spent, recency, channel) to use as a
[campaign](/campaign) audience.

## Work an Order

Click an order number to open its page: **Order #N**, its badges, *"date · via channel"*, and
sections for **Items**, **Ship this order**, **Timeline**, **Customer**, **Delivery**, **Payment**,
**Where this sale came from**, **Returns** and **Staff note** (*"Only your team sees this."*).

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/commerce/order-detail.png" alt="An order page for a paid WhatsApp order: Paid and To pack badges, the Open chat / More buttons, Items with subtotal, delivery and total, 'Ship this order' with Packed / Out for delivery / Delivered / Delivery failed buttons and the Courier / Tracking number / Tracking link form, and the Customer, Payment (with Invoice INV-0012) and 'Where this sale came from' cards on the right" />

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Open chat** | Opens the customer's conversation in the Inbox |
| **Confirm cash order** | Confirms a cash order on the customer's behalf |
| **Send payment link** / **Resend checkout link** | Sends a fresh link in the chat. If it cannot be delivered, it is copied so you can paste it |
| **More** → **Copy checkout link**, **Open in Shopify** | The order's link, or the order in the connected store |
| **More** → **Download invoice**, **Send invoice to customer** | See [Invoices](#send-an-invoice) |
| **More** → **Mark as paid** | **Paid in cash**, **Paid by bank transfer** or **Paid some other way** |
| **More** → **Refund** | Refund all or part of a paid order |
| **More** → **Cancel order** | Cancel, with a reason, **Put the items back in stock** and **Tell the customer** |

<Callout type="warn">
  Cancelling *"cannot be undone. A paid order is not refunded automatically; refund it separately."*
  Refund first with **Refund**, then cancel.
</Callout>

### Ship an Order

**Ship this order** shows the next step and quick buttons for **Packed**, **Out for delivery**,
**Delivered** and **Delivery failed**. *"Order updated. The customer gets a message if that update is
switched on."* — see [Customer messages](/commerce/settings#choose-what-customers-hear).

To ship, enter **Courier**, **Tracking number** and **Tracking link** and click **Mark as shipped**.
With EasyPost connected (**Commerce → Settings → Delivery**), **Buy a label** compares carriers by
**Parcel weight (g)** — **Get rates**, then **Buy label**; **Enter tracking myself** is the manual
form. *"… gets a message with the tracking link as soon as you mark it shipped."*

Orders paid on Shopify or WooCommerce show *"Fulfilled in Shopify"*: *"Pack, ship, cancel and refund
it in Shopify; this order follows along."*

### Handle a Return

Customers can **Request a return** from their order page once it has shipped. Requests show under
**Returns** with **Approve** or **Reject**, then **Mark received** (optionally **Put the items back in
stock**) and **Refund** — *"Leave the amount empty to refund the returned items in full."*

### See Where a Sale Came From

| Source | Shown as |
|--------|----------|
| A click-to-WhatsApp ad | *"Click-to-chat ad · “headline”"*, with when the free messaging window ends and *"Reported to Meta as a purchase from the ad on …"* once reported |
| An Instagram comment or story | **Instagram comment** / **Instagram story** |
| A campaign | **Broadcast campaign** |
| The storefront or a shared link | **Storefront** / **Shared link** |

## Send an Invoice

Every paid order has a numbered PDF invoice. It is issued the first time someone asks for it and
never redrawn after that, so its number and contents stay fixed.

| Action | Where |
|--------|-------|
| **Download invoice** | **More** on the order page. Issuing a new invoice needs *"Fulfil, cancel and update orders"* |
| **Send invoice to customer** / **Resend invoice** | **More** on the order page; the **Payment** card then shows *"Sent …"* |
| Send automatically on payment | **Send invoices automatically** in [Getting Paid](/commerce/payments#set-up-invoices) |
| The customer's own copy | **Download invoice** on their order page |

Once any order on the page has an invoice, the list gains an **Invoice** column.

<Callout type="info">
  WhatsApp only accepts files inside the customer's 24-hour window. Outside it the send is refused:
  *"Download the invoice and share it another way."* Orders paid on Shopify or WooCommerce get their
  invoice from that store, not RateUp.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"The link could not be sent in chat"* | The 24-hour window is closed | The link is copied — paste it to the customer another way |
| *"Connect Stripe or Razorpay to take payments online"* | No online provider | **Open Getting paid** |
| *"Monthly order limit reached"* | The plan's orders for the month are used | **Upgrade plan** |
| No **Refund** in **More** | Not paid, paid on a store checkout, or you lack *"Connect payments, set shipping and refund"* | Refund in the store, or ask an admin |
| A storefront cash order never appears as placed | The shopper did not confirm on WhatsApp | See [Confirm on WhatsApp](/commerce/storefront#how-shoppers-confirm-their-number) |
