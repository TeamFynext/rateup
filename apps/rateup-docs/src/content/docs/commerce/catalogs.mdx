## Products

Each product shows its image, title, brand or category, price, and a stock badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Active** | In stock and buyable |
| **Out of stock** | Visible but cannot be ordered |

Use the row menu on any product to manage or delete it.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a product removes it from the catalog customers browse. Existing orders that contain it
  are unaffected.
</Callout>

---

## Syncing

Products come from the WhatsApp catalog connected to your account. Click **Sync** to pull the
latest state.

<Steps>
<Step>
Make the change in your product source — your WhatsApp catalog or connected store.
</Step>
<Step>
Return to **Commerce → Catalogs** and click **Sync**.
</Step>
<Step>
You will see *"Products synced"* when it completes.
</Step>
</Steps>

Sync when a price changes, stock runs out, or a product is added — the catalog customers see is
whatever was last synced.

---

## Collections

Collections group products so customers can browse by theme rather than scrolling everything —
"New arrivals", "Under ₹500", seasonal ranges.

Manage them from the collections area of the Catalogs page.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause |
|---------|--------------|
| "No catalog connected" | No WhatsApp catalog is linked — connect one in [WhatsApp Catalog](/whatsapp/catalogs) |
| A product is missing | It has not been synced, or it is not published in the source catalog |
| Sync failed | The connection to the catalog has expired; reconnect the account |
| Price is wrong in chat | The customer is seeing a cached catalog; sync and ask them to reopen it |
