**Catalogs** is the product side of Commerce: *"Manage products and collections synced to WhatsApp &
Instagram."* It is also where a catalog is connected to a WhatsApp number — the old **WhatsApp →
Catalog** page now redirects here.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/commerce/catalogs.png" alt="Products in the connected catalog" />

---

## Pick the Store and Catalog

Two switchers sit at the top of the page:

| Switcher | Picks |
|----------|-------|
| **Select store** | The WhatsApp number whose commerce you are viewing |
| **Select catalog** | Which of your Meta catalogs to show. The one linked to the selected number is marked **Connected** |

The page opens on the number's connected catalog. Changing the store switches back to that store's
own catalog.

The page has three tabs: **Products · N**, **Collections · N** and **Settings**.

---

## Connect a Catalog to Your WhatsApp Account

A catalog has to be linked to the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) behind your number before
customers can browse it in chat or automations can use it.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/commerce/catalog-settings.png" alt="A catalog's Settings tab: WhatsApp Business Account, Shopify checkout automation and Danger zone" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Make sure the number is connected under [Channels](/settings/channels).
</Step>
<Step>
Open **Commerce → Catalogs**, choose the number in **Select store** and the catalog in **Select
catalog**.
</Step>
<Step>
Open the **Settings** tab. Leave **SMB (Coexistence Account)** off for a normal Cloud API number; turn
it on for a number shared with the WhatsApp Business app.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the account under **Connected WhatsApp Business Account ID**. *"Selecting an account connects
the catalog immediately"* — confirm **Connect** in the dialog.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Mode | What connecting does |
|------|----------------------|
| **SMB (Coexistence Account)** off | *"Normal Account — will also update in Meta"* |
| **SMB (Coexistence Account)** on | *"DB only, no Meta update"* — RateUp records the link without changing it at Meta |

**Disconnect WhatsApp Business Account** undoes the link. *"The catalog in automation on the connected
account won't work"* afterwards.

## Manage Products

Each product shows its image, title, brand, price and a stock badge:

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Active** | In stock and buyable |
| **Out of stock** | Visible but cannot be ordered |

**Add Product** opens the product form; click a product to edit it, or use its menu to delete it.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a product *"removes the product from your Meta catalog and cannot be undone."* Existing
  orders that contain it are unaffected.
</Callout>

Only product (commerce) catalogs can be managed here. A hotel or other catalog type shows
*"Unsupported catalog type"*.

## Sync With Meta

Products live in your Meta catalog and are pulled into RateUp on sync.

<Steps>
<Step>
Make the change in your product source — Meta Commerce Manager or your connected store.
</Step>
<Step>
Return to **Commerce → Catalogs** and click **Sync with Meta**.
</Step>
<Step>
You will see *"Syncing with Meta…"* and then *"Products synced"*.
</Step>
</Steps>

Sync when a price changes, stock runs out, or a product is added — the catalog customers see is
whatever was last synced. The **Collections** tab has its own **Sync collections** button.

---

## Collections

Collections group products so customers can browse by theme rather than scrolling everything —
"New arrivals", "Under ₹500", seasonal ranges. They are created in Meta and pulled in with **Sync
collections**.

---

## Send a Shopify Checkout Link Automatically

The Settings tab's **Shopify checkout automation** card offers **Auto-generate
Shopify checkout link** — *"Automatically send a Shopify checkout link when a customer sends a cart."*
Turn it on and write the **Checkout message**, with `{link}` where the URL should go.

---

## Delete a Catalog

The **Danger zone** card's **Delete Catalog** is permanent. It removes *"All products,
orders, product sets, and automation links tied to this catalog"*. Any automation using it stops
working.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| *"No catalog connected"* | The selected number has no linked catalog | Pick a catalog in **Select catalog** and connect it from **Settings** |
| No **Select catalog** switcher at all | RateUp has no catalogs from your Meta business | Create the catalog in Meta Commerce Manager first |
| *"No products yet"* | The catalog is empty or was never synced | **Sync with Meta**, or **Add Product** |
| A product is missing | It has not been synced, or it is not published in the source catalog | **Sync with Meta** |
| *"Sync failed"* | The connection to Meta has expired | Reconnect the number under [Channels](/settings/channels) |
| Price is wrong in chat | The customer is seeing a cached catalog | Sync, and ask them to reopen it |
