## Prerequisites

Payments needs two things in place:

| Requirement | If missing |
|-------------|-----------|
| A **WhatsApp account** set up | The page shows *"WhatsApp settings not found"* |
| A **catalog connected** to it | The page shows a *"No catalog connected"* notice |

Connect a catalog under [Catalogs](/commerce/catalogs) before configuring payments.

---

## What You Configure

The payment panel covers how money is collected for orders from this catalog — the provider, the
methods customers are offered, and whether cash on delivery is allowed.

Which options appear depends on your region, your WhatsApp Business account and whether a store
integration is connected.

<Callout type="info">
  If your store is connected through **Shopify**, payment handling follows the Shopify
  configuration rather than being set here.
</Callout>

---

## Cash on Delivery

COD orders skip the payment step: they move straight to fulfilment and the money is collected on
handover. In the [Orders](/commerce/orders) list they show a **COD** marker so your team knows to
collect.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---------|---------------|
| "WhatsApp settings not found" | Set up a WhatsApp Business account first |
| "No catalog connected" | Connect a catalog in Catalogs |
| Customers cannot complete checkout | Confirm the payment provider is live, not in test mode |
| Payment succeeded but the order still shows Pending | Refresh the Orders list; if it persists, check the provider's webhook |
