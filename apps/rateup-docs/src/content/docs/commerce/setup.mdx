**Store Setup** — *"Set up your shop"* — is a checklist of the few things a shop needs before a customer can
pay for an order in WhatsApp. *"Each one saves as you go, so you can stop and come back."*

## Find Store Setup

**Store Setup** appears in the Commerce sidebar group only while steps are left, with a badge such as
**3/7**. While it shows, clicking **Commerce** itself opens Store Setup. The [Overview](/commerce/overview)
also carries a **Finish setup · 3/7** banner with **Continue setup** until you are done.

Once every step is done the page reads *"Your shop is ready"* with **Open your overview**, and the
sidebar entry disappears. **Go to overview** at the top leaves setup at any time.

---

## Work Through the Steps

The first unfinished step opens on its own; finishing it moves on to the next. Click any step's title
to open or close it.

| Step | Done when | What you do there |
|------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Connect WhatsApp** | A WhatsApp number is connected | Connect a number under [Channels](/settings/channels). Members without that right see *"Ask an admin of your account to connect it."* |
| **Connect your Meta catalog** | The shop has a Meta catalog | **Find my catalogs**, then **Use this catalog** — see [Connect Your Meta Catalog](/commerce/settings#connect-your-meta-catalog) |
| **Add a product** | At least one active product exists | **Add a product**, or **Import from CSV** (**Import from Shopify** / **Import from WooCommerce** for a shop that follows a store) |
| **Choose how you get paid** | Stripe or Razorpay is connected, or cash on delivery or bank transfer is on | Connect a provider or flip a switch — see [Getting Paid](/commerce/payments) |
| **Set a delivery rate** | At least one active delivery rate exists | A *"standard rate"* is filled in for you; set its price and **Save delivery rate** |
| **Try a test order** | A test order exists, or any order has been placed | **Create a test order** |

The step list changes when customers pay on your Shopify or WooCommerce checkout (see
[Checkout](/commerce/checkout-settings#choose-where-customers-pay)):

| Shop | Steps |
|------|-------|
| Pays on RateUp checkout | All six above |
| Pays on the WooCommerce checkout | **Choose how you get paid** becomes **Choose where customers pay**, done while WooCommerce stays connected. **Set a delivery rate** stays, because WooCommerce's payment page has no delivery step |
| Pays on the Shopify checkout | As WooCommerce, and **Set a delivery rate** is dropped — Shopify handles delivery |

## Change the Shop's Currency

The card under the progress bar reads *"Your shop sells in USD"* (or your currency): *"New shops
start with your loyalty programme's currency, or US dollars. Prices, orders and payment links all use
it."* Click **Change**, pick a currency and **Save**. The same setting is under
**Commerce → Settings → Store → Currency and tax**.

## Try a Test Order

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Try a test order** and click **Create a test order**. It needs an active product (*"Add a
product first."*).
</Step>
<Step>
RateUp makes *"a real order for one of your products, marked as a test"*. **Test order #N is ready**
offers **Open checkout**, **Copy checkout link** and **See the order**.
</Step>
<Step>
Open the checkout and go through it as a customer would.
</Step>
</Steps>

To pay it without being charged, connect Stripe or Razorpay with **test-mode keys** — their test
cards then work at checkout. Cash on delivery and bank transfer orders can be placed straight away.

<Callout type="warn">
  If a provider is in live mode, the page warns *"… is in live mode, so paying this order charges the
  card for real."* Refund it from the order afterwards, or switch to test keys. When customers pay on
  Shopify or WooCommerce, opening the checkout creates a real order there, tagged `rateup` — delete it
  there afterwards, or cancel it in RateUp and RateUp closes it there.
</Callout>

The same test order is offered on the [Orders](/commerce/orders) page as **Create test order**.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"Connect a WhatsApp number first"* when finding catalogs | No number is connected | Do **Connect WhatsApp** first |
| *"No catalog on your Meta account yet"* | Meta has no catalog for your business | **Open Meta Commerce Manager**, create one, then **Find my catalogs again** |
| **Create a test order** is greyed out | No active product, or you lack *"Send products and payment links in chat"* | Add a product, or ask an admin |
| The **Store Setup** entry stays in the sidebar | A step is still open | Open Store Setup and finish the step without a **Done** badge |
