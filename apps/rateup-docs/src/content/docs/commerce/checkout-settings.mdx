## Taxes & Charges

The money added to, or waived from, the cart total:

| Setting | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Tax rate** | The tax applied to the order |
| **Shipping charge** | Flat delivery fee |
| **Free shipping over** | Cart value above which shipping is waived |
| **Minimum order value** | The smallest order you will accept |

<Callout type="warn">
  A **free shipping** threshold set close to your average order value is a strong nudge; set too
  far above it and it does nothing but annoy people. Check your average order value on the
  [Overview](/commerce/overview) page before choosing a number.
</Callout>

## Abandoned Cart Recovery

Customers who start an order and stop can be reminded automatically.

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Toggle** | Turns recovery reminders on or off |
| **Timing** | How long to wait before reminding — 30 minutes, 1 hour, 3 hours or 24 hours |

Shorter timings catch people who got distracted; longer ones catch people who were comparing
prices. One reminder that arrives while the intent is still fresh beats several that read as
nagging.

---

## Checkout Experience

Three toggles that change what the customer goes through:

| Setting | Effect when on |
|---------|----------------|
| **Send order confirmation** | The customer gets a confirmation message once the order is placed |
| **Guest checkout** | Customers can buy without registering |
| **Order notes** | Customers can add a note to their order — delivery instructions, gift messages |
