The **Manage Members** section is used to add, view, and remove contacts within a group. All actions described here are performed from the Group Details page.

## Adding Members to a Group

To add contacts to a group:

1. Open the required group from the **Groups List**
2. Click **Add Members**

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/add-members-modal.png`}
  alt="Add members to group modal"
  width={400}
/>

The Add Members modal opens.

---

### Ways to Add Members

Choose one of the following methods to add contacts:

- **Add a new contact**  
  Create a new contact and add it directly to the group.

- **Select existing contacts**  
  Choose one or more contacts from your existing contact list.

- **Import contacts**  
  Upload contacts using **CSV** or **Excel** files.

Each method adds contacts directly to the selected group.

---

## Setting Member Information During Add

While adding members, you can optionally configure additional information:

- Labels
- Lifecycle stage
- Assignee
- Custom fields (for example, coupon)
- Internal notes

This ensures contacts are enriched at the time of addition.

---

## Viewing Group Members

After members are added:

1. Scroll to the **Group Members List**
2. Review the listed contacts

Each member row displays:

- Name and avatar
- Phone / IGSID
- Assigned labels (if any)
- Source
- Date added to the group

This list confirms successful member addition.

---

## Customizing the Members List View

To customize visible columns:

1. Click **View Settings** in the Group Members section

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/group-view-settings.png`}
  alt="Toggle columns in group members list"
  width={200}
/>

2. Enable or disable required columns

This helps focus on relevant member information when managing large groups.  
Changes apply instantly and affect only your current view.

---

## Removing Members from a Group

To remove a contact from a group:

1. Locate the contact in the Group Members List
2. Click **Remove member**

The contact is removed from the group but remains available in the Contacts List.

---

## Best Practices for Managing Members

- Keep group membership updated regularly
- Use labels and lifecycle stages for better segmentation
- Avoid adding unnecessary contacts to large groups
- Review group members periodically

---

## Why Managing Members Matters

Managing group members correctly helps teams:

- Maintain clean and accurate groups
- Improve targeting and segmentation
- Avoid outdated or irrelevant members
- Work efficiently with large contact sets
