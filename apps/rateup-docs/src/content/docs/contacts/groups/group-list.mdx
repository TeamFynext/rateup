The **Groups List** displays all contact groups in a card-based layout. It allows teams to quickly browse, filter, create, and manage groups used for organizing contacts.

## Group Card Details

Each group card displays key information at a glance:

- **Group Name** – The name of the contact group
- **Team** – The team the group belongs to
- **Total Members** – Number of contacts in the group
- **Created Date** – When the group was created
- **Action Menu (⋮)** – Access group actions

This layout makes it easy to identify and manage groups quickly.

---

## Create Group

You can create a new contact group directly from the Groups List.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/create-group-modal.png`}
  alt="Create new group modal"
  width={500}
/>

To create a group:

1. Click **Create Group**
2. Enter a **Group Name**
3. Select the **Team**
4. (Optional) Upload a **Group Icon**
5. Click **Save** to create the group

Click **Cancel** to exit without creating a group.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/team-filter.png`}

Use the **Team filter** to view groups associated with a specific team.

Available options include:
- All Teams
- Individual teams such as Main, Marketing, Customers, Demo, and others

This helps teams focus only on the groups relevant to them.

---

## Group Actions

Each group card includes an **Action Menu (⋮)** for managing the group.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/group-card-actions.png`}
  alt="Group card actions menu"
  width={350}
/>

Available actions include:

- **Edit Group** – Update the group name, team, or icon
- **Delete Group** – Permanently remove the group

> Deleting a group does not delete the contacts inside it.

---

## Opening a Group

Clicking on a group card opens the **Group Details** view, where you can:

- View group members
- Add or remove contacts
- Manage group-specific settings

Learn more in [Group Details](./group-details).

---

## Why the Groups List Matters

The Groups List helps teams:

- Organize contacts efficiently  
- Manage groups across multiple teams  
- Quickly access group members  
- Maintain a clear overview of all contact groups
