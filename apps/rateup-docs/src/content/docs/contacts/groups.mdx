A **group** is a list you put contacts into deliberately. Groups are what [campaigns](/campaign) are
sent to. Every group belongs to a **team**.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/contacts/groups.png" alt="Contact groups" />

Each group is a card showing its icon, name, how many members it has, owning team and creation date. Click a
card to open the group. Use **Search contact groups...** to find one once you have several.

---

## Create a Group

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create Group**. The **Create New Group** dialog opens.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **Group Name**. Name the audience, not the campaign — *Newsletter subscribers* outlives
*March mailer*.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the team that owns it under **Select Team**. This is required.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally **Upload Icon** — an image shown on the card.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create Group**.
</Step>
</Steps>

To rename a group, change its team or icon, open the card's **⋮** menu and choose **Edit Group**.

## Inside a Group

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/contacts/group-page.png" alt="A contact group page with two members selected and the Remove from group bar" />

The top of the page shows the group's icon, its name, how many members it has and the owning team.

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Groups** | Return to the list |
| **Icon** | Click to open **Group icon** — **Upload new icon** (a square image, at least 200×200 pixels) or **Remove icon** |
| **Rename** | Edit the name in place — **Enter** or ✓ saves, **Esc** or ✕ cancels |
| **Delete Group** | Delete the group — see below |

Below sits the members table. Search it with **Search by name or phone...**.

## Add Members

There are three ways to put people in a group:

| From | How |
|------|-----|
| **The group page** | **Add existing**, **New contact** or **Import** |
| **Contacts list** | Tick contacts → **Add to Group** |
| **A contact's details** | **Groups → Add to group**, or the **Group** field when adding or editing a contact |

### From the group page

| Button | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Add existing** | Opens **Add existing contacts** — *"Tick the contacts to add to"* the group |
| **New contact** | Opens **Add Contact**. The contact you create lands in the group |
| **Import** | Imports a file — *"Each row becomes a contact in this group."* |

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add existing**.
</Step>
<Step>
Find people with **Search Contact**, or narrow the list with the group picker (to pull members of
other groups) and the label, stage, source and custom-field filters. Current members are shown
ticked and locked.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the contacts you want — or **Select all on this page** — and click **Add N contacts**.

While a search or filter is active, **Add all matching** adds every contact that matches, across
all pages. Anyone already in the group is skipped.
</Step>
</Steps>

Importing into a group works like the main
[contact import](/contacts/managing-contacts#import-contacts-from-a-file) — same columns, same
**Update contacts that already exist** and header-row switches, same background progress — but every
valid row in the file is also added to this group, whether the contact was created, updated or left
as it was. The summary adds an **Added to this group** count.

## Remove Members

<Steps>
<Step>
Tick members in the members table.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Remove from group**. **Clear selection** unticks them instead.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also remove one person from a group in their [contact details](/contacts/managing-contacts#view-a-contacts-details)
— in the **Groups** section.

Removing someone from a group does not delete the contact — it only takes them off that list.

---

## Delete a Group

Use **Delete Group** on the group page, or **⋮ → Delete Group** on its card, and confirm *"Are you
sure you want to delete this group?"*

Deleting a group removes the list only. Its members stay in your contacts, in their other groups,
with everything else about them intact.

## Groups vs Labels

This is the distinction people get wrong most often:

| | Groups | [Labels](/contacts/labels) |
|---|--------|--------|
| **Are** | Lists you build | Tags describing a trait |
| **Answer** | "Who should get this campaign?" | "What is true about this person?" |
| **Owned by** | A team | The whole organisation |
| **Visible in the Inbox** | No | Yes, on every conversation row |
| **Typical name** | *Newsletter subscribers* | *VIP*, *Refund pending* |

<Callout type="info">
  Rule of thumb: if you would ever want to **send something to it**, it is a group. If it describes
  **what someone is**, it is a label.
</Callout>

## Designing Your Groups

- Keep them purposeful — a group nobody sends to is dead weight
- Prefer a few durable groups over one per campaign
- Review membership before big sends; groups drift
