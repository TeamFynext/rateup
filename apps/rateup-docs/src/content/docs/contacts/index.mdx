**Contacts** is the record of everyone you talk to. Every conversation in the
[Inbox](/inbox), every [campaign](/campaigns) send and every [order](/commerce/orders) is attached
to a contact here.

## The Four Ways to Organise Contacts

RateUp gives you four separate tools. They do different jobs, and using the right one is what keeps
the database usable as it grows.

| Tool | What it is | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **[Audiences](/contacts/audiences)** | Team-owned lists you add people to and send campaigns to | *Newsletter subscribers* |
| **[Labels](/contacts/labels)** | Coloured tags for traits, shown across the Inbox | *VIP*, *Refund pending* |
| **[Lead stage](/pipelines)** | Where someone sits on your pipeline board | *Qualified → Won* |
| **[Custom fields](/contacts/custom-fields)** | Extra data you define | *Membership number* |

<Callout type="info">
  A contact sits on **one** pipeline stage at a time, but can carry **many** labels and belong to
  **many** audiences. If you find yourself creating an audience called "VIP", what you want is a label.
</Callout>

Organisations without a pipeline board track progress with [lifecycle stages](/contacts/lifecycle)
instead. That page is no longer in the sidebar, but still works.

## One Person, One Contact

A contact can hold several phone numbers and emails as well as their WhatsApp, Instagram and
Messenger accounts, so someone who writes on two channels is still one contact. When the same
person does end up twice, **Contacts → Possible duplicates** lists the pair and you can
[merge](/contacts/merging-contacts) them.

---

## Contacts and Consent

Every contact carries a **consent** status, which controls whether they may receive template
messages and campaigns. It shows as **Granted** or **Denied** in the contacts table's **Consent**
column, and contacts without it carry a **DND** badge in the Inbox and a **No consent** badge in
their details.

<Callout type="warn">
  Consent is not a formality. Messaging people who did not opt in damages your WhatsApp account's
  quality rating, and that is far harder to repair than a slow month of campaigns.
</Callout>
