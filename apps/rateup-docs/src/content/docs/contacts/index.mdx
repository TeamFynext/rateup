**Contacts** is the record of everyone you talk to. Every conversation in the
[Inbox](/inbox), every [campaign](/campaign) send and every [order](/commerce/orders) is attached
to a contact here.

## The Four Ways to Organise Contacts

RateUp gives you four separate tools. They do different jobs, and using the right one is what keeps
the database usable as it grows.

| Tool | What it is | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **[Groups](/contacts/groups)** | Explicit lists you add people to | *Newsletter subscribers* |
| **[Labels](/contacts/labels)** | Coloured tags for cross-cutting traits | *VIP*, *Refund pending* |
| **[Lifecycle stages](/contacts/lifecycle)** | Where someone is in your funnel | *Lead → Customer* |
| **[Custom fields](/contacts/custom-fields)** | Extra data you define | *Membership number* |

<Callout type="info">
  A contact sits in **one** lifecycle stage at a time, but can carry **many** labels and belong to
  **many** groups. If you find yourself creating a group called "VIP", what you want is a label.
</Callout>

## Contacts and Consent

Every contact carries a **consent** status, which controls whether they may receive template
messages and campaigns. Contacts without consent show a **DND** badge in the Inbox.

<Callout type="warn">
  Consent is not a formality. Messaging people who did not opt in damages your WhatsApp account's
  quality rating, and that is far harder to repair than a slow month of campaigns.
</Callout>
