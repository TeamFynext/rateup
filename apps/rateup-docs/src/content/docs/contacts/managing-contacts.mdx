people in, finding them again, working on them in bulk, and keeping the data clean.

## Where Contacts Come From

Every contact carries a **source**, shown as a badge on the list and in their details:

| Source badge | How they arrived |
|--------------|------------------|
| **WhatsApp** | They messaged one of your WhatsApp numbers |
| **Instagram** | They messaged or commented on your Instagram account |
| **Web Chat** | They wrote in through your website chat |
| **Manual** | Someone on your team added them by hand |
| **Imported** | They came in through a file [import](#import-contacts-from-a-file) |
| **Loyalty Customer** | They joined your [loyalty programme](/loyalty) |

Anyone who messages you becomes a contact automatically — you never have to add inbound customers
yourself.

## The Contacts Table

By default the table shows **Name**, **Phone/IGSID**, **Label**, **Source** and **Date Added**.
More columns are available from the **View** menu at the top right of the toolbar:

| Column | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **Name** | The contact's name — click it to open their [details](#view-a-contacts-details) |
| **Phone/IGSID** | Phone number, or Instagram ID for Instagram contacts |
| **Status** | **Active**, or **Blocked** if you have blocked them on WhatsApp |
| **Other** | Extra information, such as a location |
| **Label** | Their [labels](/contacts/labels) |
| **Lead stage** | Their column on your [pipeline](/pipeline) board. Organisations without a board see **Lifecycle Stage** instead, if lifecycle display is on |
| *Each custom field* | One column per [custom field](/contacts/custom-fields) |
| **Source** | Where they came from — see above |
| **Consent** | **Granted** or **Denied** — whether they may receive templates and campaigns |
| **Date Added** | When the contact was created |
| **Last Contact** | When they last messaged you |

Sort by **Name**, **Date Added** or **Last Contact** by clicking the column header. The list is
paged; pick how many rows to show at the foot of the table.

## Search and Filter

The toolbar holds one search box and three filters. They combine, so *Label: VIP* plus
*Source: Instagram* shows only VIPs who came in through Instagram.

| Control | Narrows by |
|---------|-----------|
| **Search by name, phone, or email...** | Text anywhere in the name, phone number or email. A leading `+` is ignored, so paste numbers as they are |
| **Stage** | A column of your pipeline board. Without a board, this is a **Lifecycle** filter instead |
| **Label** | **All Contacts**, one label, or a status: **Active** or **Blocked** |
| **Source** | **All Sources**, **WhatsApp**, **Instagram**, **Web**, **Manual** or **Imported** |

**Reset** appears once a filter is active and clears them.

<Callout type="info">
  There is no filter for groups or custom fields on this page. To see who is in a group, open the
  group itself under [Groups](/contacts/groups).
</Callout>

## Add a Contact

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add Contact**.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in **Full Name** and **Phone Number** — both are required, and the phone number must be valid
with its country code. *"Invalid phone number"* means it is not.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally add:

- **Email**
- **Labels** — pick any number
- **Lead stage** — puts them straight onto your [pipeline](/pipeline) board (shown only if your board
  has columns)
- **Assignee** — the team members responsible for them
- **Group** — any number of [groups](/contacts/groups)
- **notes**
- A value for each [custom field](/contacts/custom-fields)
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**. If something is wrong, a *"Fix the following before saving:"* list tells you what.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also add a contact from the Inbox, with **New Contact** in the
[Inbox toolbar](/inbox/toolbar#adding-a-contact).

<Callout type="info">
  Adding a contact does not open a messaging window. If they have never written to you, your first
  message must be an approved [template](/whatsapp/templates).
</Callout>

## Act on One Contact

Every row has a **⋮** menu at the end:

| Option | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **View Details** | Opens the contact's details — the same as clicking their name |
| **Edit** | Opens **Edit Contact** with every field from [Add a Contact](#add-a-contact) |
| **Send Message** | Sends a quick text message without leaving the page — see below |
| **Open in Inbox** | Jumps to this contact's conversation in the [Inbox](/inbox) |
| **Delete contact** | Deletes them — see [Deleting Contacts](#deleting-contacts) |

### View a contact's details

Click a contact's **name** (or **⋮ → View Details**) to open everything RateUp knows about them in
one place.

| Section | Contains |
|---------|----------|
| **Header** | Name and phone, with badges for source, inbox status, lifecycle, **No consent**, **Blocked** and **Spam** where they apply |
| **Contact** | Phone, Email, WhatsApp name, Location, Date of birth, Consent, Added, External ID |
| **Activity** | Last received, Last sent, Assigned to, Lead stage, Lead owner, and how many campaigns they have been in |
| **Labels** | Their labels, or *"No labels"* |
| **Custom fields** | Every custom field with a value — hidden fields are left out |
| **Loyalty** | Programme, Tier, Points (available and lifetime) and Referral code — only for loyalty members |
| **Groups** | Every group they are in. Click **✕** to remove them from one, or **Add to group** to add them to another |
| **Notes** | Free-text notes about the customer. Click the pencil to edit, then **Save** |

Rows with nothing to show are left out, so a sparse contact gets a short dialog. Hover a phone
number, email, ID or referral code to reveal a **copy** button.

**Send Message** at the bottom of the dialog opens the quick-message box.

### Send a quick message

<Steps>
<Step>
Choose **⋮ → Send Message**, or **Send Message** in the details dialog.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the channel under **Select Channel** — **WhatsApp** or **Instagram** — and the **Account** to send
from.
</Step>
<Step>
Type the **Message** and send it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  This sends free text, which WhatsApp and Instagram only accept within 24 hours of the customer's
  last message. For anyone who has not written recently, it will fail — send a
  [template](/inbox/send-templates) from the Inbox instead.
</Callout>

## Bulk Actions

Tick the checkbox on each row you want — or the checkbox in the header to take the whole page. A bar
appears showing *"N Selected"* with four actions:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Add to Group** | Adds everyone selected to one [group](/contacts/groups) |
| **Apply Label** | Opens **Apply Labels to Contacts** — pick one or more labels and click **Apply** |
| **Update lead stage** | Puts everyone on one column of your [pipeline](/pipeline) board, opening a lead for anyone not on it. Organisations without the Pipeline feature see **Update Lifecycle Stage** |
| **Delete** | Deletes everyone selected, after a confirmation |

The header checkbox selects the current **page** only. To act on more people, raise the rows per
page first.

## Import Contacts From a File

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/contacts/import.png" alt="Importing contacts" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Import**. The **Import Contacts** dialog opens.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Download Demo Csv File** and lay your data out the same way. The file needs a **name** and a
**phone** column.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Custom Fields**, tick the custom fields your file contains. Each one's column must be headed
with that field's **Field ID** — shown on the [Custom Fields](/contacts/custom-fields) page.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the two switches:

- **Update the existing data while importing** — see below
- **First row contains column headers (name, phone, ...)** — on by default. Turn it off for a file
  with no header row. The columns must then run **name**, **phone**, then the ticked custom fields in
  order
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the file — **CSV**, **XLSX** or **ODS** — and click **Import**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Imported contacts get the source **Imported**.

### Existing contacts

A phone number can only belong to one contact, so an import never creates duplicates:

| Update the existing data while importing | A phone number already in your contacts is… |
|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| **Off** (default) | Skipped — the existing contact is left exactly as it is |
| **On** | Updated with the file's **name** and any custom-field values it has |

A blank custom-field cell never overwrites a value that is already there.

### Rows that are rejected

A row is rejected if its **name** is missing or its **phone number** is missing or invalid. The rest
still import, and a **Failed contact import** list shows each rejected row with the reason — for
example *"phone is missing"* or *"…: Invalid phone number"*.

<Callout type="info">
  Phone numbers must include the **country code** — `447700900123`, not `07700900123`. Numbers
  spreadsheets have turned into scientific notation (`4.477E+11`) are converted back for you.
</Callout>

### What an import can set

An import sets **name**, **phone** and **custom fields** only. Email, labels, groups and stages are
not read from the file — apply them afterwards with [bulk actions](#bulk-actions). To import straight
into a group, import from the group's **Add Members** dialog instead — see
[Groups](/contacts/groups#add-members).

<Callout type="warn">
  Only import people who have agreed to hear from you. Messaging a list that never opted in lowers
  your WhatsApp quality rating, and that is far harder to repair than a slow month of campaigns.
</Callout>

## Export Contacts

<Steps>
<Step>
Use the **View** menu to show the columns you want in the file.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Export** and choose **Download as Excel** or **Download as OpenDocument (ODS)**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  The export contains **every contact in your organisation**, newest first — your search and filters
  do not narrow it. It includes these visible columns: name, phone, status, other, source, consent,
  date added and custom fields. The Label, Lead stage and Last Contact columns come out empty.
</Callout>

Exports contain personal data. Treat the file accordingly and delete it when you are done.

## Deleting Contacts

Delete one contact from **⋮ → Delete contact**, or several with the **Delete** bulk action.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a contact cannot be undone, and it reaches further than RateUp:

  - **Every conversation and message** with them is deleted, along with their group memberships,
    labels and campaign history
  - If you have [Zoho CRM or Bigin connected](/settings/integrations), the matching contact is
    **deleted there too**
  - Zapier receives a **contact deleted** event

  Their pipeline lead is kept but no longer linked to anyone. If someone just asks not to be
  contacted, withdraw their consent instead — deletion is for data-removal requests.
</Callout>

## Keeping the Database Clean

- Import carefully; most bad data arrives in bulk
- Keep country codes on every phone number
- Review contacts with repeated message failures — see
  [Message Analytics](/analytics/message-analytics)
- Agree naming conventions for [labels](/contacts/labels) before everyone invents their own
