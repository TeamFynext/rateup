## Opening the Loyalty Overview

To access the Loyalty dashboard:

1. Open the **Loyalty** module from the sidebar.
2. Click **Overview**.

The page loads with performance metrics and analytics for the loyalty program.

---

## Viewing Performance Metrics

The **Performance Metrics** section shows a quick summary of your loyalty program.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/loyalty-performance-metrics.png`}
  alt="Loyalty performance metrics cards"
/>

To review the key metrics:

1. Look at the cards displayed at the top of the page.
2. Monitor the following statistics:

- **Total Customers** – Number of customers enrolled in the loyalty program.
- **Points Issued** – Total loyalty points issued to customers.
- **Points Redeemed** – Total points redeemed for rewards.

These metrics help you understand the current usage of the loyalty program.

---

## Analyzing Points Activity

The **Points Activity** chart displays how points are earned and redeemed over time.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/loyalty-points-activity.png`}
  alt="Points activity chart"
/>

To analyze points activity:

1. Review the **Points Earned** bars to see how many points customers earned.
2. Review the **Points Redeemed** bars to see how many points were used for rewards.
3. Compare both trends over time to understand engagement.

You can also adjust the **time filter** (for example, *Last 7 days*) to change the reporting period.

---

## Tracking Customer Growth

The **Customer Growth** chart shows how the number of loyalty customers changes over time.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/loyalty-customer-growth.png`}
  alt="Customer growth chart"
/>

To monitor customer growth:

1. Review the growth line in the chart.
2. Identify increases in the total number of customers.
3. Track how quickly new customers are joining the loyalty program.

This helps evaluate the adoption of your loyalty program.

---

## Understanding Customer Tier Distribution

The **Customer Tier Distribution** section shows how customers are distributed across loyalty tiers.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/loyalty-tier-distribution.png`}
  alt="Customer tier distribution"
/>

To review tier distribution:

1. Look at each loyalty tier shown in the section.
2. Review the number of members in each tier.
3. Check the percentage distribution across tiers.

Common tiers include:

- **Silver**
- **Platinum**
- **Bronze**

This section helps you understand how customers progress through loyalty levels.

---

## Reviewing Most Popular Rewards

The **Most Popular Rewards** section highlights rewards that customers redeem frequently.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/loyalty-popular-rewards.png`}
  alt="Most popular rewards section"

To review reward performance:

1. Check which rewards appear in this section.
2. Identify rewards that customers redeem the most.
3. Use this information to optimize reward offerings.

---

## Why the Loyalty Overview Matters

The Loyalty Overview helps teams:

- Monitor loyalty program performance
- Track customer engagement
- Analyze points earning and redemption trends
- Understand customer tier distribution
- Identify high-performing rewards

This dashboard provides a quick snapshot of how well your loyalty program is performing.
