The **Cashier Portal** allows store staff to quickly find loyalty customers and process loyalty transactions during checkout. It is designed for fast customer lookup using a phone number and provides quick access to actions such as issuing points, redeeming rewards, and applying coupons.

## Opening the Cashier Portal

To open the Cashier Portal:

1. Navigate to the **Loyalty** module from the sidebar.
2. Click **Cashier Portal**.

The page opens with a search interface where staff can locate loyalty members during transactions.

---

## Searching for a Customer

The Cashier Portal allows staff to quickly locate customers using their phone number.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-customer-search.png`}
  alt="Customer search by phone"
/>

To search for a customer:

1. Select the **country code** from the dropdown.
2. Enter the **customer's phone number**.
3. Click **Search Customer**.

If the customer exists in the loyalty program, their profile and loyalty details will appear below the search bar.

This helps staff identify the correct loyalty member during checkout.

---

## Viewing Customer Loyalty Information

Once a customer is found, the system displays key loyalty details.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-customer-summary.png`}
  alt="Customer loyalty summary"
/>

The summary section displays:

- **Customer Name** – The loyalty member's name.
- **Phone Number** – The registered phone number.
- **Available Points** – The number of points currently available for redemption.
- **Lifetime Points** – Total points the customer has earned.
- **Current Tier** – The loyalty tier of the customer (e.g., Platinum).
- **Status** – Indicates whether the loyalty account is active.

These details help staff understand the customer’s loyalty status before applying rewards.

---

## Using Quick Actions

The **Quick Actions** section allows staff to perform common loyalty operations directly from the Cashier Portal after locating a customer.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-quick-actions.png`}
  alt="Cashier quick actions"
/>

These actions allow staff to quickly manage loyalty points, rewards, and coupons during a customer transaction.

### Issue Points

The **Issue Points** action allows staff to add loyalty points to a customer's account.

To issue points:

1. Click **Issue Points**.
2. Enter the number of points to add.
3. Confirm the transaction.

This is typically used when a customer completes a purchase.

---

### Issue Reward

The **Issue Reward** option allows staff to grant a reward directly to the customer.

To issue a reward:

1. Click **Issue Reward**.
2. Select the reward to grant.
3. Confirm the action.

This can be used for promotions or manual reward distribution.

---

### Redeem Coupon

The **Redeem Coupon** action allows staff to apply a coupon during a transaction.

To redeem a coupon:

1. Click **Redeem Coupon**.
2. Enter or select the coupon code.
3. Confirm redemption.

The coupon will then be applied to the purchase.

---

### Redeem Reward

The **Redeem Reward** action allows customers to redeem loyalty points for rewards.

To redeem a reward:

1. Click **Redeem Reward**.
2. Choose the reward the customer wants.
3. Confirm the redemption.

Points will be deducted from the customer's account.

---

## Viewing Available Rewards

The **Available Rewards for Customer** section displays rewards that the customer can redeem.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-available-rewards.png`}
  alt="Available rewards for customer"
/>

Each reward card includes:

- **Reward Name** – The name of the reward.
- **Reward Description** – Details about the reward.
- **Reward Code** – A code used for redemption.
- **Points Required** – Number of points required for the reward.
- **Availability Status** – Indicates whether the reward is currently available.

Staff can review these rewards and help customers redeem them during checkout.

---

## Why the Cashier Portal Matters

The Cashier Portal helps businesses:

- Quickly identify loyalty customers
- Issue or redeem loyalty points instantly
- Apply rewards during purchases
- Improve in-store customer experience
- Reduce checkout time for loyalty members

This ensures loyalty benefits are seamlessly integrated into the **point-of-sale workflow**.
