title: "Menu Categories"
---

### Before you begin:

- **Create a menu:** Head over to the "Menu" tab to set the stage.
- **Select your canvas:** Click on the created menu card to start adding categories.

### Creating your first category:

1. Click "Add Category" to button to embark on category creation.
2. **Essential details:**
   - **Name:** Enter a clear and descriptive name for easy identification.
   - **Visual flair (optional):** Add an image to make it shine (it's your choice!).
3. **Schedule the category (optional):**
   - **Schedule magic:** Toggle "Schedule" to show this category at specific times.
   - **Choose wise times:** Set the start and end times (remember, end after the start!).
4. Click "Save Category" to create the category.

### Editing existing categories:

1. Find the category needing a touch-up and click the pencil icon to edit.
2. Modify the details confidently to ensure they reflect your current needs.
3. Click "Create Category" to update the data.

### Deleting a category (proceed with caution):

1. Locate the category to delete and click the trash icon to delete.
2. **Remember, it's permanent:** Acknowledge that deleted categories can't return, then click the confirmation button to finalize the removal.

### Bonus features:

- **Swap order:** Rearrange your categories:
   - Click the swap icon on the blue banner showcasing category names and icons.
   - A modal awaits! Grab and hold the 6-dot icon next to each category name, drag and drop to your desired order, then click "Save" to make it official.
