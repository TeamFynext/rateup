### Before You Begin:

- **Create a Team (if needed):** If you need additional teams, head over to the team management section to create one.
- **Access the Menus:** Click "Menus" in the sidebar to enter your workspace.

### Creating Your First Menu:

1. Click "Create Menu" to button for menu creation.
2. Select the team you want to associate the menu with this menu (only they and admins will have access).
3. **Essence of the Menu:**
   - **Name it Wisely:** Enter a descriptive name for easy identification.
   - **Public Display Name:** Choose a name for the world to see (e.g., your restaurant name).
4. Click "Create Menu" to witness your creation debut at the top.

### Editing an Existing Menu:

1. Find the menu needing a touch-up and click the pencil icon to edit.
2. Update the menu content as needed, ensuring it aligns with your current needs..
3. Click "Save Menu" to update the menu.

### Deleting a category (proceed with caution):

1. **Initiate Deletion:**
   - Click the three-dot icon adjacent to the menu you wish to delete.
   - Select the **"Delete"** option from the dropdown menu.
2. Acknowledge the permanence of deletion and click the confirmation button to execute the removal.

### Additional Features

- **Viewing Team-Specific Menus:**
   - Narrow your focus to a particular team's menus by selecting the desired team from the **"All Teams"** dropdown.

- **Sorting Menus:**
   - Click the **"Sort"** button in the top right corner to arrange menus chronologically, toggling between ascending and descending order based on their creation time.
