##### General Settings
  - **Teams:** This setting could be relevant if your menu is associated with specific teams. You can select the teams that have access to this menu. Click `Save` to apply these changes on the site.

- **Menu Name:** To change the name of the menu.

- **Display Name:** The main name that will be displayed in the landing page. It could be your hotel/restaurant name or zone name.

- **Hide the category if the schedule isn't right:** This option hides the category if the current schedule is not within the specified timeframe.

- **Location Label:** Location name of your hotel/restaurant.

- **Location URL:** Location URL to your hotel/restaurant from (Google Maps).

- **Choose a survey to display in the menu page:** Allows selection of a specific survey to be featured and displayed on the menu page landing page, and its icon will be displayed.

- **Currency:** Selects and sets the preferred currency for use in menu pricing.

- **Website Label:** Name of the website, displayed on the landing page.

- **Website URL:** The URL of the website.




Make sure to review and configure these settings based on the specific requirements and goals of your menu.
