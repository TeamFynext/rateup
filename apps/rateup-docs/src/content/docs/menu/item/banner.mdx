---
title: "Menu Banner"
---

It a Feature where special menu food item card is showed as a banner in public page of digital menu list

### Creating Menu Banner

- Click on the "Mannage Banner" 
- The details should be same as Menu Item (*expect Image is required*)
- Click Save

Note: *You can Create Menu Banner upto 4*
Note: On the public page, the banner will be displayed as a carousel, resembling a slideshow.
