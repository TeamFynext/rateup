**Settings → Notifications** decides which events reach you and on which channels. *"These settings
are yours alone"* — changing them never affects anyone else in your organisation.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/notifications.png" alt="Notifications settings with the channel switches and the Inbox section" />

Every member can open this page from **Settings → Notifications**. Changes save as soon as you flip a
switch — there is no Save button.

---

## Choose Where You Get Notified

The first card, **Where you get notified**, holds one master switch per channel. *"Turning a channel
off silences it for everything below."*

| Channel | Delivers |
|---------|----------|
| **In RateUp** | *"The bell and pop-ups while RateUp is open"* |
| **Mobile app** | *"Push notifications on phones signed in to the RateUp app"* |
| **Email** | *"Sent to your sign-in email address"* |
| **Desktop** | *"System notifications from this browser while RateUp is in another tab"* |
| **Sound** | *"Play a sound when a notification arrives while RateUp is open"* |

All five start switched on.

### Allow Desktop Notifications in Your Browser

Desktop alerts also need your browser's permission, and that is granted per browser. With **Desktop**
on, the card shows one of:

| You see | Meaning | Do this |
|---------|---------|---------|
| **Allow in this browser** | The browser has not been asked yet | Click it and accept the browser prompt |
| *"This browser is blocking notifications from RateUp. Allow them in the site settings."* | You refused earlier | Re-allow notifications for RateUp in the browser's site settings |
| *"This browser does not support desktop notifications."* | The browser cannot show them | Use another browser, or rely on **In RateUp** |
| Nothing | Permission is granted | — |

## Turn Individual Notifications On or Off

Below the first card, events are grouped into sections: **Inbox**, **Pipelines**, **Campaigns**,
**Loyalty**, **Channels and integrations** and **Billing**. Each event has a switch per channel.

<Steps>
<Step>
Find the event, for example **Conversation assigned to me** under **Inbox**.
</Step>
<Step>
Flip the switch in the column for the channel you want — **In RateUp**, **Mobile app**, **Email** or
**Desktop**.
</Step>
</Steps>

- A **–** means the event is not offered on that channel.
- A greyed-out switch means its channel is off in **Where you get notified** (hover it: *"Turned off
  under Where you get notified"*). Turn the channel back on to use it.
- The switch beside a section's title turns every event in that section on or off at once. It shows
  on while anything in the section is on, so one click silences the whole section.

You only see events your role allows you to receive. An Agent, for example, has no **Billing**
section, and **Request waiting for my approval** appears only for people who can approve loyalty
requests. See [Members & Roles](/settings/users#the-six-roles).

### Who Sees Each Section

| Section | Shown to people who can… | Roles by default |
|---------|--------------------------|------------------|
| **Inbox** | See conversations | Viewer and above |
| **Pipelines** | See pipelines | Viewer, Staff and above |
| **Campaigns** | See campaigns | Viewer, Staff and above |
| **Loyalty** — approval requests | Approve or reject point requests | Manager and above |
| **Loyalty** — unhappy feedback | Configure tiers, rewards and rules | Manager and above |
| **Channels and integrations** — templates | Create and edit templates | Staff and above |
| **Channels and integrations** — account restricted, integration errors | Receive integration alerts | Manager and above |
| **Billing** | Receive billing alerts | Manager and above |

<Callout type="info">
  The two new-message events show live in the app but do not add a row to the bell — one row per
  incoming message would bury everything else.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| A switch is greyed out | Its channel is off under **Where you get notified** | Turn the channel back on |
| No desktop alerts although **Desktop** is on | The browser has not been allowed, or RateUp is the active tab | Click **Allow in this browser**; desktop alerts show while RateUp is in another tab |
| No phone notifications | You are not signed in to the RateUp mobile app, or **Mobile app** is off | Sign in to the app and check the switch |
| An event you expect is missing | Your role does not include the ability the event needs | Ask an Admin to adjust your role or [fine-tune your abilities](/settings/users#fine-tune-one-persons-abilities) |
| *"Could not load your notification settings"* | The page could not reach RateUp | Reload the page |
