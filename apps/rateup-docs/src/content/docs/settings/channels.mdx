**Channels** is where your messaging accounts are connected. Everything else in RateUp — the Inbox,
Commerce, Campaigns, Loyalty notifications — depends on at least one channel being live here.

Open it from **Settings → Channels**. You need a role that can **connect, remove and configure
channels**; without it the menu item is hidden.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/channels.png" alt="Channels page with WhatsApp Business, Facebook Messenger and Instagram Messaging rows" />

---

## Supported Channels

| Channel | What connects | Opens |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| **WhatsApp Business** | WhatsApp Business phone numbers | `/dashboard/settings/channels/whatsapp` |
| **Facebook Messenger** | Facebook Pages | `/dashboard/settings/channels/facebook` |
| **Instagram Messaging** | Instagram professional accounts | `/dashboard/settings/channels/instagram` |

The three are independent. You can run RateUp on Instagram or Messenger alone, with no WhatsApp
number at all — any one channel is enough to get going, and every conversation lands in the same
inbox whichever channel it came in on.

Each row shows **N connected** once something is live, and its button reads **Connect** or
**Configure**.

---

## Multiple Accounts

Every number, Page and account is its own channel, so you can connect as many as you like — several
WhatsApp numbers, one per brand or region, or a dozen Facebook Pages.

In the Inbox, each ticket shows which account the customer wrote to, so agents always know which
identity they are replying as.

## Connect Facebook Messenger or Instagram

Messenger and Instagram each have their own login, so connecting one asks Meta only for that
platform's permissions.

<Steps>
  <Step>Open **Facebook Messenger** or **Instagram Messaging** and click **Continue with Facebook**.</Step>
  <Step>In Meta's window, choose the Pages — for Instagram, the Page each Instagram account is linked to — and finish the login.</Step>
  <Step>In **Choose Facebook Pages** (or **Choose Instagram accounts**), tick what you want. Everything you just picked in Meta starts ticked.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Connect** (or **Connect N**).</Step>
</Steps>

In the picker:

| Shown | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Connected to another organization** | Greyed out — that Page or account is already a channel in a different RateUp organisation |
| **Already connected — reconnects it** | It is already one of your channels; connecting it again reconnects it |
| **Linked to …** | Instagram only — the Facebook Page the account belongs to |

Each connected Page or account then appears as its own row with **Connected** and a **Disconnect**
button. Disconnecting one stops its conversations arriving in the inbox; other channels are not
affected.

<Callout type="info">
  If the picker says *"No Instagram professional accounts were shared"*, log in again and choose the
  Facebook Page each Instagram account is linked to — Meta shares the account through its Page.
</Callout>

## Connect a WhatsApp Number

The WhatsApp page lists every connected number under **Connected accounts**, each with a badge
saying how it was added:

| Badge | How it was added |
|-------|------------------|
| **Automated** | Through **Continue with Facebook** (Meta's own sign-up flow, which supplies the credentials) |
| **Manual** | Through **Add manually**, with IDs and an access token from your own Meta app |

Each row shows the number's **Business ID**, **Phone ID** and **App ID**, an **Edit channel** button,
and **Disconnect**.

Use **Continue with Facebook** wherever you can. **Add manually** is for when you cannot connect
through Facebook — see [Add a Number Manually](#add-a-number-manually).

## Edit a WhatsApp Number

Click **Edit channel** on a number. The dialog keeps apart the things that are yours and the things
that belong to Meta:

| Tab | What it changes | Where it lives |
|-----|-----------------|----------------|
| **Channel** | The name you use for the number inside RateUp | RateUp only |
| **Display name** | The business name customers see in WhatsApp | Meta — needs review |
| **Profile** | Photo, about line, description, category and contact details | Meta — applies immediately |
| **Calling** | Whether customers can call the number, and when | Meta — applies immediately |
| **Credentials** | The WABA ID, Phone ID, App ID, webhook password and access token | RateUp — **Manual** numbers only |

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/channel-edit-tabs.png" alt="Edit channel dialog on the Channel tab, showing all five tabs" />

<Callout type="warn">
  Changing a channel needs the **Connect, remove and configure channels** permission on your role.
  Without it the save is refused.
</Callout>

## Rename a Channel

The **Channel name** is your own label — it names the number in channel pickers, template screens
and the Inbox's channel filter. Customers never see it.

<Steps>
  <Step>Open the number and stay on the **Channel** tab.</Step>
  <Step>Type the new name.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Save name**.</Step>
</Steps>

It saves on its own. You do not need to resubmit the number's credentials, and nothing is sent to
Meta — so there is no review and no waiting.

## Change the Display Name

The **display name** is the business name WhatsApp shows customers. Meta reviews every change
against its display name guidelines, so this is a request, not an edit.

<Steps>
  <Step>Open the **Display name** tab. The name Meta currently holds is shown at the top with its status.</Step>
  <Step>Type the name you want in **New display name**.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Submit for review**.</Step>
  <Step>Check back with **Sync from Meta** until the requested name is approved.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Re-register number** to put the approved name into use.</Step>
</Steps>

### What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Approved** | The name in use has passed review |
| **In review** | Meta is still deciding |
| **Approved — not in use yet** | Meta approved your requested name, but the number still shows the old one until you re-register |
| **Rejected** | The name breaks Meta's guidelines — edit it and submit again |
| **Expired** | The approval lapsed before the number was re-registered |

<Callout type="warn">
  An approved name does nothing until you click **Re-register number**, and the approval only lasts
  **14 days**. Miss that window and the name has to go through review again.
</Callout>

You can change a display name **10 times per 30 days**. Beyond that Meta refuses the request, and
its own message is shown back to you.

## Update the Business Profile

The **Profile** tab is what customers see when they open your business in WhatsApp: the photo, the
about line under your name, and your contact details. Unlike the display name, these apply as soon
as they are saved.

<Steps>
  <Step>Open the **Profile** tab.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Upload photo** (or **Change photo**) and pick a JPEG or PNG of up to 5 MB. It uploads on selection.</Step>
  <Step>Fill in the fields you want to change.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Save profile**.</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/channel-profile-tab.png" alt="Profile tab of the Edit channel dialog with the photo and business details" />

| Field | Shown as | Limit |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Photo | The business avatar | JPEG or PNG, 5 MB; square images work best |
| **About** | The one-line status under your name | 139 characters |
| **Description** | The longer blurb on the business page | 512 characters |
| **Email** | Contact email | 128 characters |
| **Category** | The business category, from WhatsApp's own list | — |
| **Address** | Freeform text — WhatsApp does not check it against a map | 256 characters |
| **Website** / **Second website** | Up to two links | 256 characters each |

<Callout type="warn">
  WhatsApp has no way to clear a profile field. Anything you leave blank is left exactly as it is
  rather than emptied, and a save with every field blank is refused.
</Callout>

## Set Up WhatsApp Calling

Customers can call your WhatsApp number, and agents answer in the Inbox, in their browser. Calling
is switched on per number, and the settings are held by Meta — the tab always shows what WhatsApp is
enforcing.

<Steps>
  <Step>Click **Edit channel** on the number and open the **Calling** tab.</Step>
  <Step>Turn on **Voice calls**.</Step>
  <Step>Choose the options below, then click **Save**. You will see *"Calling settings saved"*.</Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Voice calls** | Customers can call this number on WhatsApp, and agents answer from the inbox. The other settings only apply while this is on |
| **Call button in chat** | Shows the call icon at the top of the customer's chat with you |
| **Callback requests** | When a customer calls and nobody answers, asks them whether you may call them back |
| **Calling hours** | Outside these hours customers cannot call; they see when you are open |

With **Calling hours** on, set a **Time zone** (for example `Asia/Kolkata`) and, for each day,
switch it on and give the opening and closing times — or leave it **Closed**. A number with no
hours yet starts from Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00.

<Callout type="warn">
  WhatsApp calling needs a messaging limit of at least 2,000 customers a day, and works only on a
  number that runs purely on the Cloud API — **not on a number shared with the WhatsApp Business app
  (coexistence)**. If the number does not qualify, or Meta cannot be reached, the tab shows **Could
  not load calling settings** with Meta's reason instead of the switches. All of WhatsApp's calling
  rules are listed in [WhatsApp's Calling Limits](/inbox/whatsapp-calls#whatsapps-calling-limits).
</Callout>

Answered and missed calls are listed under **Analytics → Calls** — see [Analytics](/analytics).

## Add a Number Manually

**Add manually** is for a number whose credentials you hold yourself, from your own Meta app — use it
when you cannot connect through Facebook.

<Steps>
  <Step>On the WhatsApp page, click **Add manually**.</Step>
  <Step>In **Add a number manually**, give it a **Channel name** (optional — your own label).</Step>
  <Step>Fill in **WABA ID**, **Phone ID**, **App ID**, **Webhook Password** and **Access Token**. All five are required; use a permanent access token.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Save Changes**.</Step>
</Steps>

The number then appears in the list with a **Manual** badge, and **Edit channel** gives it the same
**Channel**, **Display name**, **Profile** and **Calling** tabs as any other number, plus
**Credentials**. Saving on **Credentials** replaces the IDs and token it was added with.

### Disconnect a Number

**Disconnect** on a number's row does different things depending on how it was added:

| Number | What **Disconnect** does |
|--------|--------------------------|
| **Automated** | De-registers the WhatsApp Business account, after a confirmation |
| **Manual** | Asks **Remove this number?** — its IDs and access token are deleted from RateUp, and nothing changes on Meta's side |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---------|---------------|
| Messages stopped arriving | The channel's connection may have expired — reconnect it |
| "WhatsApp settings not found" elsewhere in RateUp | No WhatsApp account is connected |
| An Instagram account will not connect | It must be a professional account linked to a Facebook Page — choose that Page in Meta's login |
| A Page or account is greyed out in the picker | It is connected to another RateUp organisation; disconnect it there first |
| "This channel is not set up yet. Contact support." | The Messenger or Instagram login is not configured for your workspace |
| The **Calling** tab shows **Could not load calling settings** | The number does not meet WhatsApp's calling requirements, or its access token has expired |
| Replies fail on one number only | Check that specific account's status, not the channel as a whole |
| The approved display name still is not showing | Re-register the number — approval alone does not apply it |
| "Nothing to update — WhatsApp does not accept an empty profile field" | Every profile field was blank; fill in at least one |
| "Profile photo must be a JPEG or PNG image" | Convert the file — WhatsApp takes no other format for a profile photo |
| A display name change is refused outright | You may have used all 10 changes for this 30-day period |
| The Display name or Profile tab cannot read from Meta | The number's access token has expired — reconnect the account |

## Who Can See Which Channel

Every member of the organisation sees every channel unless you say otherwise. To restrict a group to
one number or account, set **Channel Access** on their [team](/settings/teams).

Agents can then narrow their own view further with the Inbox's
[Channel filter](/inbox/inbox-filter) — which never widens what their team is allowed to see.
