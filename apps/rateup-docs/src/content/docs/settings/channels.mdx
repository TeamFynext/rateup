**Channels** is where your messaging accounts are connected. Everything else in RateUp — the Inbox,
Commerce, Campaigns, Loyalty notifications — depends on at least one channel being live here.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/channels.png" alt="Connected channels" />

## Supported Channels

| Channel | What connects |
|---------|---------------|
| **WhatsApp** | A WhatsApp Business account and its phone numbers |
| **Instagram** | An Instagram professional account |
| **Facebook** | A Facebook page |

Each channel lists its connected **accounts** and **pages**, so you can see at a glance what is
live.

---

## Multiple Accounts

You can connect more than one account per channel — several WhatsApp numbers, for example, one per
brand or region.

In the Inbox, each ticket shows which account the customer wrote to, so agents always know which
identity they are replying as.

## Edit a WhatsApp Number

A connected number has three separate things you can change, and they are deliberately kept apart —
one is yours, two belong to Meta.

| Tab | What it changes | Where it lives |
|-----|-----------------|----------------|
| **Channel** | The name you use for the number inside RateUp, plus its credentials | RateUp only |
| **Display name** | The business name customers see in WhatsApp | Meta — needs review |
| **Profile** | Photo, about line, description, category and contact details | Meta — applies immediately |

Open them from the pencil (**Edit channel**) on a number under **Automated**. A number configured
under **Manual** carries the same three tabs on its own card.

## Rename a Channel

The **Channel name** is your own label — it names the number in channel pickers, template screens
and the Inbox's channel filter. Customers never see it.

<Steps>
  <Step>Open the number and stay on the **Channel** tab.</Step>
  <Step>Type the new name.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Save name**.</Step>
</Steps>

It saves on its own. You do not need to resubmit the number's credentials, and nothing is sent to
Meta — so there is no review and no waiting.

## Change the Display Name

The **display name** is the business name WhatsApp shows customers. Meta reviews every change
against its display name guidelines, so this is a request, not an edit.

<Steps>
  <Step>Open the **Display name** tab. The name Meta currently holds is shown at the top with its status.</Step>
  <Step>Type the name you want in **New display name**.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Submit for review**.</Step>
  <Step>Check back with **Sync from Meta** until the requested name is approved.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Re-register number** to put the approved name into use.</Step>
</Steps>

### What the statuses mean

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Approved** | The name in use has passed review |
| **In review** | Meta is still deciding |
| **Approved — not in use yet** | Meta approved your requested name, but the number still shows the old one until you re-register |
| **Rejected** | The name breaks Meta's guidelines — edit it and submit again |
| **Expired** | The approval lapsed before the number was re-registered |

<Callout type="warn">
  An approved name does nothing until you click **Re-register number**, and the approval only lasts
  **14 days**. Miss that window and the name has to go through review again.
</Callout>

You can change a display name **10 times per 30 days**. Beyond that Meta refuses the request, and
its own message is shown back to you.

## Update the Business Profile

The **Profile** tab is what customers see when they open your business in WhatsApp: the photo, the
about line under your name, and your contact details. Unlike the display name, these apply as soon
as they are saved.

<Steps>
  <Step>Open the **Profile** tab.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Upload photo** (or **Change photo**) and pick a JPEG or PNG of up to 5 MB. It uploads on selection.</Step>
  <Step>Fill in the fields you want to change.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Save profile**.</Step>
</Steps>

| Field | Shown as | Limit |
|-------|----------|-------|
| Photo | The business avatar | JPEG or PNG, 5 MB; square images work best |
| **About** | The one-line status under your name | 139 characters |
| **Description** | The longer blurb on the business page | 512 characters |
| **Email** | Contact email | 128 characters |
| **Category** | The business category, from WhatsApp's own list | — |
| **Address** | Freeform text — WhatsApp does not check it against a map | 256 characters |
| **Website** / **Second website** | Up to two links | 256 characters each |

<Callout type="warn">
  WhatsApp has no way to clear a profile field. Anything you leave blank is left exactly as it is
  rather than emptied, and a save with every field blank is refused.
</Callout>

## Connect a Number Manually

Most accounts connect through the **Automated** tab, where Meta's own login flow supplies the
credentials. **Manual** is for numbers whose credentials you hold yourself — WABA ID, Phone ID,
App ID, webhook password and access token.

A manually configured number gets the same **Channel**, **Display name** and **Profile** tabs once
it exists. Its credentials sit under the channel name on the **Channel** tab, saved with **Save
Changes**; **Delete Configuration** removes the number.

The two modes are exclusive: a number onboarded automatically cannot be edited as a manual one until
it is de-registered, and vice versa.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---------|---------------|
| Messages stopped arriving | The channel's connection may have expired — reconnect it |
| "WhatsApp settings not found" elsewhere in RateUp | No WhatsApp account is connected |
| An Instagram account will not connect | It must be a professional account linked to a Facebook page |
| Replies fail on one number only | Check that specific account's status, not the channel as a whole |
| The approved display name still is not showing | Re-register the number — approval alone does not apply it |
| "Nothing to update — WhatsApp does not accept an empty profile field" | Every profile field was blank; fill in at least one |
| "Profile photo must be a JPEG or PNG image" | Convert the file — WhatsApp takes no other format for a profile photo |
| A display name change is refused outright | You may have used all 10 changes for this 30-day period |
| The Display name or Profile tab cannot read from Meta | The number's access token has expired — reconnect the account |

## Who Can See Which Channel

Every member of the organisation sees every channel unless you say otherwise. To restrict a group to
one number or account, set **Channel Access** on their [team](/settings/teams).

Agents can then narrow their own view further with the Inbox's
[Channel filter](/inbox/inbox-filter) — which never widens what their team is allowed to see.
