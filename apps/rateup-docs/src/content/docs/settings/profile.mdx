**Settings → Profile & Business** (`/dashboard/settings/profile`) holds your own profile and
language, plus the organisation's business details and business hours. It was previously called
**Account**.

## Your Profile

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Profile photo** | JPG, PNG or GIF, max 2 MB |
| **Title** | **Mr**, **Mrs** or **Mx** — required |
| **Full Name** | Shown on your messages and internal notes — cannot be blank |
| **Email Address** | Your login address — read-only |
| **Phone Number** | Must be a valid number for its country |
| **Role** | Your role in the organisation — read-only; an admin changes it under [Members](/settings/members) |
| **Member since** | When you joined, read-only |

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Edit** on your profile card. The **Edit Profile** dialog opens.
</Step>
<Step>
Update the fields you need.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save Changes**. Your name updates everywhere it appears, including past messages.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Your name is what colleagues see on internal notes and what customers may see on replies. Use the
  name people would recognise, not a nickname.
</Callout>

## Changing Your Photo

Click the camera icon on your avatar and choose an image. Large uploads take a moment — the page
shows *"Hold on, changing your profile photo"* while it processes.

The same applies to the organisation logo, which is uploaded separately.

---

## Business Information

The organisation's own details, shared by everyone. Only people whose role can change organisation
settings see **Edit** on this card; everyone else sees the details read-only.

| Field | Used for |
|-------|----------|
| **Logo** | Uploaded with **Upload Logo** — JPG, PNG or GIF, max 2 MB |
| **Business Name** | Displayed across RateUp and in customer-facing pages |
| **Industry** | Categorises your organisation |
| **Website** | The business's website |
| **Business Phone** | The organisation's contact number |
| **Business Email** | The organisation's contact address — optional |
| **Location** | Where the business operates |
| **Address** | Your full business address |

Click **Save Changes** in the dialog to apply them.

<Callout type="warn">
  These fields appear on customer-facing surfaces such as survey pages and loyalty registration.
  Keep them accurate — a wrong business name here shows up in front of customers.
</Callout>

## Language

Choose the language RateUp uses under **Language**. It is saved to your account and used wherever
you sign in. It changes the interface for you only; it does not change the language of messages
sent to customers.

---

## Set Your Business Hours

**Business hours** at the bottom of the page records when a person answers. Outside these hours your
auto-replies take over — automation rules can check a schedule and hold replies until someone is
around.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Set up business hours**. The first schedule is named **Main hours** and starts from a
standard week, Monday to Friday, 9:00–17:30.
</Step>
<Step>
Change the days and times that do not fit. Use **Add a break** for a gap in the day, and
**Apply to weekdays** or **Apply to all days** to copy one day's hours across.
</Step>
<Step>
Check the time zone, then click **Save changes**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Add more schedules with **Add schedule**. Each card shows whether you
are **Open now** or **Closed now** and which automation rules use it.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Who can edit | The same people who can edit Business Information |
| Deleting a schedule | Any automation rule using it stops checking hours. The last remaining schedule cannot be deleted |
