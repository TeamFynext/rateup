**Teams** groups people so work can be routed to a group rather than to individuals. A person's
[role](/settings/users#the-six-roles) decides *what* they may do; their teams decide *where* — which
conversations, campaigns and channels are theirs.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/team-page.png" alt="A team page on the Members tab with Add existing user and Invite new user" />

---

## Who Can Do What

| Task | Ability needed | Roles by default |
|------|---------------|------------------|
| Open **Teams** and see who is in a team | *See teams* | Everyone |
| Add and remove people, edit a team's **Details** | *Add and remove team members* | Manager, Admin, Owner |
| Create or delete a team, set **Channel Access** | *Create, edit and delete teams* | Admin, Owner |

**Team Management** at `/dashboard/settings/team` lists the teams you belong to, each with its
colour, code and member count. Click a card to open the team's page.

## Create a Team

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create Team**. You only see it if you can create teams.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **Team Name** of at least three characters.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **Team Code** — at least two characters, letters, numbers and hyphens only, no spaces. Type a
space and the form suggests a hyphenated version.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a **Team Color**, or type a hex value such as `#5A8CC6`. It marks the team across the dashboard.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create Team**.
</Step>
</Steps>

If your plan's team allowance is used up, creation is refused with *"You have reached the maximum
limit of N teams."*

## Add People to a Team

Open the team. The **Members** tab lists everyone in it with their **Role** and the date they
**Joined**, plus anyone invited but not yet joined.

<Steps>
<Step>
To add someone who already has an account, click **Add existing user** and click **Add** next to
them. *"All users are already in this team"* means there is nobody left to add.
</Step>
<Step>
To bring in someone new, click **Invite new user**, enter their **Name**, **Email** and **Role**, and
click **Send invite**. *"They will join this team as soon as they accept."*
</Step>
</Steps>

To take someone out, use **Remove Member** on their row and confirm. Removing someone from a team
does not remove them from the organisation — that happens in [Members](/settings/users#pause-or-remove-someone).

You can also set a person's teams from their own page in [Members](/settings/users#open-a-members-page).
A person can belong to several teams.

<Callout type="info">
  Owners and Admins reach every team whether or not they are in it. Team membership matters for
  Managers, Staff, Agents and Viewers — it is what limits them to their own teams' work.
</Callout>

## Choose Which Channels a Team Sees

The **Channels** tab holds **Channel Access**: *"Choose which channels this team can see. Changes
save on their own. Org owners always see every channel."* It decides which of your WhatsApp numbers,
Instagram accounts, Facebook pages and webchat channels the team's conversations come from.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the team and go to the **Channels** tab.
</Step>
<Step>
Leave **All channels** on to give the team everything — *"Includes any channel connected in the
future."*
</Step>
<Step>
Or switch **All channels** off and turn on the individual channels this team should see. Each is
labelled with its name and a platform badge.
</Step>
<Step>
Wait for **Saved** to appear. There is no Save button.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Switching **All channels** off and selecting nothing shows *"No channels selected — members of this
  team will not see any channel."* Their Inbox will be empty. Either pick a channel or turn **All
  channels** back on.
</Callout>

### How the Scope Is Worked Out

| Situation | What the person sees |
|-----------|---------------------|
| Owner or Admin | Every channel, always |
| In several teams | The **union** of every team's channels — being in a team can only widen access |
| In one team with **All channels** on | Every channel |
| In no team at all | Every channel — scoping is opt-in per team |

<Callout type="info">
  Channel access controls what the dashboard **shows**. It is not an API permission boundary: API
  keys are organisation-wide and are not limited by team or channel. A key can, however, be limited
  to selected features — see [Keys and Access](/external-api#keys-and-access).
</Callout>

A channel change takes effect straight away. Adding or removing a **member** can take up to a minute
to change what they see — if a new team member still has the old view, wait, then refresh.

## Edit or Delete a Team

The **Details** tab, under **Team Settings**, lets you change the **Team Name**, **Team Code**,
**Team Color** and **Google review link**, then **Save Changes**.

Below it, people who can delete teams see **Delete Team**. Confirm in the dialog — *"This action
cannot be undone."*

---

## Why Teams Matter for the Inbox

Teams are how work is routed without naming individuals:

- A ticket assigned to a **team** is available to every member of it
- The Inbox's [team filter](/inbox/inbox-filter) narrows any view to one team's work
- **Channel Access** decides which conversations reach the team in the first place
- Team-based routing survives people being on leave; individual routing does not

## Designing Your Teams

| Approach | Works when |
|----------|-----------|
| **By function** — Support, Sales, Billing | Different skills handle different questions |
| **By language or region** | You serve several markets |
| **By shift** | You run extended hours |

Avoid one team per person — routing becomes brittle. What a person may do is set by their
[role](/settings/users#the-six-roles), and [fine-tuning](/settings/users#fine-tune-one-persons-abilities)
covers the exceptions. Which features appear in the menu at all is an organisation-wide choice in
[Features](/settings/features) — there is no per-team feature setting.
