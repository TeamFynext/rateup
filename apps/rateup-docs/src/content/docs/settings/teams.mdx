**Teams** organises members into groups and controls what each group can reach. It is the main tool
for routing work and for limiting access without editing every user one by one.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/teams.png" alt="Team Management" />

---

## Creating a Team

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create Team**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **team name**. Spaces are not allowed — use a single word or hyphenate.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a **team colour**. It identifies the team at a glance across the dashboard.
</Step>
<Step>
Set **feature access** for the team — see below.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each team gets a **team code**, used when referring to the team elsewhere in RateUp.

---

## Feature Access

This is the important part. Each team is granted or denied access to RateUp's features:

| Feature | Controls |
|---------|----------|
| **Inbox** | The shared conversation inbox |
| **Analytics** | Reports and performance data |
| **Contacts** | The contact database |
| **Campaigns** | Bulk template sends |
| **Flows** | WhatsApp flows |
| **Chatbots** | Automated chat bots |
| **Templates** | WhatsApp message templates |
| **Automation** | Automation rules |
| **Loyalty** | The loyalty programme |
| **Web Forms** | Surveys and forms |
| **Pipeline** | The lead [pipeline](/pipeline) board |
| **QR Studio** | QR codes and access links |
| **Apps & Services** | Integrations |
| **Settings** | Organisation settings |

Use **Enable All** or **Disable All** to set the whole list quickly, then adjust.

<Callout type="info">
  Feature access decides what appears in the sidebar. A member of a team without **Loyalty** simply
  does not see Loyalty — it is hidden, not greyed out.
</Callout>

## Channel Access

Under **Feature Access**, on the same tab, sits **Channel Access** — *"Choose which channels this
team can see."* It decides which of your WhatsApp numbers, Instagram accounts and Facebook pages the
team's conversations come from.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the team and go to **Feature Access**.
</Step>
<Step>
Leave **All channels** on to give the team everything — including any channel you connect in future.
</Step>
<Step>
Or switch **All channels** off, then turn on the individual channels this team should see. Each is
labelled with its name and a platform badge.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save Changes**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Switching **All channels** off and selecting nothing shows *"No channels selected — members of this
  team will not see any channel."* Their Inbox will be empty. Either pick a channel or turn **All
  channels** back on.
</Callout>

### How the scope is worked out

| Situation | What the person sees |
|-----------|---------------------|
| In several teams | The **union** of every team's channels — being in a team can only widen access |
| In one team with **All channels** on | Every channel |
| In no team at all | Every channel — scoping is opt-in per team |
| Organisation owner | Every channel, always |

<Callout type="info">
  Channel access controls what the dashboard **shows**. It is not an API permission boundary: the
  [External API](/external-api) key is organisation-wide and is not scoped by team.
</Callout>

Saving a channel selection takes effect straight away. Adding or removing a **member** can take up
to a minute to change what they see — if a new team member still has the old view, wait, then
refresh.

## Members

Add members to a team from the team's detail page, or assign a team when
[creating a user](/settings/users).

A person can belong to more than one team, and sees the combined access of all of them.

---

## Why Teams Matter for the Inbox

Teams are how work is routed without naming individuals:

- A ticket assigned to a **team** is available to every member of it
- The Inbox's [team filter](/inbox/inbox-filter) narrows any view to one team's work
- **Channel Access** decides which conversations reach the team in the first place
- Team-based routing survives people being on leave; individual routing does not

---

## Designing Your Teams

| Approach | Works when |
|----------|-----------|
| **By function** — Support, Sales, Billing | Different skills handle different questions |
| **By language or region** | You serve several markets |
| **By shift** | You run extended hours |

Avoid one team per person — that is what user permissions are for, and it makes routing brittle.
