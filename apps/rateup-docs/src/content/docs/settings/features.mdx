**Features** lists everything RateUp offers and lets an admin choose which optional features show in
the menu for the whole organisation. A feature you turn off is only hidden: nothing in it is deleted,
and nothing you built on it stops running.

## Open Features

Click the **grid** icon (**Features**) at the bottom of the sidebar rail, between the notification
bell and your avatar. It opens `/dashboard/features`. Everyone in the organisation can open it.

The old address `/dashboard/settings/features` redirects there.

---

## The Optional Features

| Feature | What it is | Needs a plan that includes it |
|---------|------------|-------------------------------|
| [**Pipeline**](/pipeline) | *"Track leads and work on boards, from first message to closed deal."* | No |
| [**Loyalty**](/loyalty) | *"Points, tiers and rewards your customers collect over WhatsApp."* | Yes |
| [**Commerce**](/commerce) | *"Catalogs, orders and payments inside the chat."* | Yes |
| [**Web Forms**](/web-forms) | *"Surveys and feedback forms you share as a link."* | Yes |
| [**QR & Links**](/qr-studio) | *"QR codes and link pages that open a chat with you."* | No |
| [**Meta AI Agent**](/meta-agent/overview) | *"Meta's AI agent answering customers on your WhatsApp number."* | Yes |

Each card also explains the feature in a sentence and lists three things a team does with it. A
feature your plan does not include carries a **Not on your plan** badge. You can still tick it, but
opening it shows the plan-upgrade screen.

Under the cards, **Always included** lists what cannot be turned off: **Inbox**, **Contacts**,
**Campaigns**, **Automation**, **WhatsApp templates, chatbots and flows**, **Instagram and
Messenger** and **Analytics**.

## Turn a Feature On or Off

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Features**. If you are allowed to change it, the page is headed *"Which features will you
use?"* and every card has a tick box.
</Step>
<Step>
Click a card to tick or untick it. The footer counts the selection — *"4 features selected"*.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save changes**. RateUp confirms *"Your features are saved"*. **Discard** throws away
unsaved clicks.
</Step>
</Steps>

The change applies to **everyone in the organisation**, not just you.

### Who can change it

Changing features needs the ability *Edit organisation details and business hours*. By default that
is **Managers**, **Admins** and the **Owner** — see [Members & Roles](/settings/users).

Everyone else sees the same cards as a catalogue headed *"What RateUp offers"*, with an **In your
menu** or **Turned off** badge on each and the note *"Features marked In your menu are on for your
organization. Ask an admin to turn on any of the others."*

## What Turning a Feature Off Does

| Where | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Sidebar** | The feature's entry disappears for everyone |
| **Its pages** | Opening one by URL or bookmark shows, for example, *"Loyalty is turned off"*. Admins get a **Turn on Loyalty** button there; everyone else is told *"Ask an admin to turn on Loyalty from Features in the menu."* |
| **Inbox** | With **Pipeline** off, conversation rows lose their stage chip and the ticket panel loses its board section. With **Commerce** off, the ticket panel's orders section is hidden |
| **Members & Roles** | The roles table and **Fine-tune abilities** hide the abilities for that feature, with *"Abilities for features your organization has turned off are hidden."* and a **Manage features** link. Fine-tuned abilities you set earlier are kept |
| **Setup guide** | With **Loyalty** off, the guide's loyalty step is not offered |
| **Your data and automations** | Untouched. Programmes, boards, products, forms and codes stay, and automations, reminders and scheduled jobs that rely on the feature keep running |

<Callout type="warn">
  Turning a feature off does **not** stop it. A loyalty programme keeps awarding points and sending
  its messages, and published QR codes and link pages keep working. To stop a feature, switch it off
  inside the feature itself. Turning it off here only hides it.
</Callout>

## Which Features a New Organisation Starts With

The answers to **What should RateUp do first?** at [signup](/getting-started/register) pick the
starting set:

| Signup answer | Turns on |
|---------------|----------|
| *(always)* | **Pipeline** |
| **Loyalty inside chat** | **Loyalty** |
| **Surveys on the web** | **Web Forms** |
| **Sell products on WhatsApp & Instagram** | **Commerce** |

Everything else starts off, including **QR & Links** and **Meta AI Agent**. If you skipped every
answer, every feature starts on. The [setup guide](/getting-started/setup-guide) opens with a
**Choose features** step so you can change this straight away.

Connecting a Shopify or WooCommerce store in [Integrations](/settings/integrations) turns
**Commerce** on automatically.

## Features, Roles and Teams

Three different settings decide what someone sees. They stack: an entry shows only if all of them
allow it.

| Setting | Decides | Applies to |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| **Features** (this page) | Whether a feature is in the menu at all | The whole organisation |
| [**Members & Roles**](/settings/users) | What a person may do in each feature — read, manage, export | One person, through their role and fine-tuned abilities |
| [**Teams → Channel Access**](/settings/teams) | Which channels' conversations a team sees | The members of that team |

So if Loyalty is missing for one agent but not for others, it is their role. If it is missing for
everyone, it is turned off here.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| A feature has vanished from everyone's sidebar | Someone turned it off on this page | Tick it and **Save changes** |
| A page says *"… is turned off"* | The feature is hidden for the organisation | Click **Turn on …** if you can, or ask an admin |
| No tick boxes, only **In your menu** / **Turned off** badges | You lack *Edit organisation details and business hours* | Ask a Manager, Admin or the Owner |
| Ticked a feature, but it opens an upgrade screen | **Not on your plan** | Upgrade in [Billing](/settings/billing) |
| A feature shows for some people and not others | Their roles differ | Check abilities in [Members & Roles](/settings/users) |
| Commerce came back after you turned it off | A Shopify or WooCommerce store was connected | Turn it off again if you do not want it in the menu |
