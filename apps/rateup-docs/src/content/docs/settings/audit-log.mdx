**Audit log** lives under **Settings → Audit log**, in the **Organization** group: *"Who on your
team created, changed or deleted what, and when."* Use it to answer "who changed this, and when?"

The page is at `/dashboard/settings/audit-log`. It was previously the **Team activity** tab under
**Analytics → Logs**; old links to it open **Settings → Audit log**.

## The Columns

| Column | Contains |
|--------|----------|
| **Action** | What was done, e.g. **Channel added**, **Rule created**, **Journey updated** |
| **User** | Who did it |
| **Details** | What was sent with the change. Click it to read all of it |
| **Time** | When, to the second |

Sort by **User** or **Time** from their headings. Rows come 25 to a page.

Changes to automation use the current feature names: an automation rule reads **Rule created**,
**Rule updated** or **Rule deleted**, and a journey **Journey created**, **Journey updated** or
**Journey deleted**. A change to an audience starts with **Audience**, followed by the audience's ID when there is one.

## Find a Change

<Steps>
<Step>
Set the date range with the picker at the top right of the page (**Last 7 Days** by default). The
page has its own picker and no **Team** filter — it covers the whole organisation.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a person in the first select (**Everyone** by default).
</Step>
<Step>
Pick an area in the second select (**All features** by default) — for example **Contacts**,
**Audiences**, **Campaigns**, **Channels**, **Automation rules** or **Journeys**. The list only
offers areas that have activity.
</Step>
</Steps>

With nothing to show, it reads *"No activity found"* — *"Nothing matches these filters in this date
range."* when you have filtered, or *"Nothing your team did falls in this date range."* when you
have not.

The page needs the **See the audit log** ability — Admins and the Owner have it by default. Without
it, **Audit log** is missing from the Settings list, and opening the page by URL reads *"You can't see
the audit log"* — *"Ask an admin to give your role access to the audit log."* It has no PDF report.

## When to Use It

| Situation | What to look for |
|-----------|------------------|
| A channel stopped receiving messages | A **Channel updated** or **Channel removed** row, and who made it |
| A setting changed and nobody remembers why | Filter by the feature, then read **Details** |
| Reviewing a new admin's first week | Filter by that person |

<Callout type="info">
  The audit log records changes, not presence. To see who is online right now, use the **Right
  now** card on [Overview](/analytics/overview) or the **Status** column on
  [Team Performance → Agents](/analytics/agent-performance).
</Callout>
