## Change Your Password

<Steps>
<Step>
Under **Password**, click **Change password** to open the form.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter your **Current Password**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter your **New Password**. It must be at least **8 characters** and include **1 uppercase letter,
1 lowercase letter, 1 number and 1 special character** — the same rules as
[registration](/getting-started/register).
</Step>
<Step>
Enter it again in **Confirm New Password** — the two must match, or you see *"Passwords do not match"*.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Update Password**. You will see *"Password Changed Successfully"*.
</Step>
</Steps>

If you cannot remember your current password, sign out and use
[Reset Password](/getting-started/resetpassword) instead.

## Danger Zone

Two actions live here. Both are permanent.

### Delete Account

Permanently deletes **your own user account** — it removes your login, and your team keeps working.

To confirm, enter your current password in the **Delete Account?** dialog. If you sign in with
Google and have no RateUp password, type `DELETE ACCOUNT` instead.

- Your access ends immediately
- You are removed from every team
- Tickets you handled remain, with your name on them

<Callout type="warn">
  This action cannot be undone. If you are leaving the organisation, ask an admin to remove you
  instead — that way your open work gets reassigned first.
</Callout>

### Delete Organization

Only shown to people whose role can delete the organisation. To confirm, type
`DELETE ORGANIZATION` in the dialog.

Permanently deletes **the entire organisation** and everything in it:

- All chats and contacts
- All team members and their access
- All integrations
- All billing information

<Callout type="warn">
  This is permanent and irreversible. Every person in the organisation loses access, and no export
  is produced automatically. Export anything you need to keep **before** starting.
</Callout>

## Before Deleting an Organisation

<Steps>
<Step>
Export conversation history from the [Inbox toolbar](/inbox/toolbar).
</Step>
<Step>
Export contacts from [Contacts](/contacts).
</Step>
<Step>
Export survey and campaign results you need to retain.
</Step>
<Step>
Cancel or check your [billing](/settings/billing) status.
</Step>
<Step>
Tell your team — they lose access the moment it completes.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Good Password Practice

- Use a unique password for RateUp, not one shared with other services
- Longer beats complicated — a passphrase is easier to remember and harder to break
- Change it immediately if you suspect it has been shared
- Never share a login; add the person as a [member](/settings/members) instead
