**Users** is the list of everyone with access to your organisation, and the controls that decide
what each of them can do.

## Adding a User

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add New User**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter their **email address** — this is required, and it is where the invite goes.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter their name and phone number.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose their **role** and set any **additional permissions**.
</Step>
<Step>
Assign them to a **team**, so team-routed work reaches them.
</Step>
<Step>
Save. They receive an invite and appear in the list.
</Step>
</Steps>

An invite that has not been accepted can be removed with **Delete Invite**.

---

## Permissions

Beyond the role, each user has individually switchable permissions:

| Permission | Grants |
|------------|--------|
| **Access to Inbox** | Can open the Inbox and work on tickets |
| **Access reports and data insights** | Can open Analytics |
| **Create and edit workflow automation** | Can build and change automations |
| **Allow mobile app access** | Can sign in from the mobile app |
| **Email notifications enabled** | Receives email notifications |

<Callout type="info">
  Permissions are additive on top of the role. Removing **Access to Inbox** from an agent hides the
  Inbox for them entirely, whatever their role says.
</Callout>

---

## Account Status

Each member shows whether their account is **active**. Deactivating is the reversible option — use
it for someone on extended leave, rather than deleting and re-inviting them later.

---

## Editing a User

Click **Edit User** to change their name, photo, contact details, role, permissions or team
assignment. Changes take effect immediately for that person.

---

## Removing a User

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the user and choose **Delete User**.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm in the dialog — you will be asked to confirm the specific person.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a user account is permanent. Their tickets stay in the system with their name on them,
  but their access ends immediately. If they might come back, deactivate instead.
</Callout>

---

## Before Someone Leaves

<Steps>
<Step>
Reassign their open tickets — check the [Mine](/inbox/inboxview/mine) view filtered to them from
[the sidebar's Users filter](/inbox/inbox-filter).
</Step>
<Step>
Check whether any automation or routing points at them specifically.
</Step>
<Step>
Then deactivate or delete the account.
</Step>
</Steps>

Deleting first leaves their tickets ownerless and invisible in personal views.
