**Account Settings** holds two things: your own profile, and the business details of the
organisation.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/account.png" alt="Account Settings" />

---

## Your Profile

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Profile photo** | JPG, PNG or GIF, max 2 MB |
| **Full name** | Shown on your messages and internal notes — cannot be blank |
| **Email address** | Your login address |
| **Phone** | Must be a valid number for its country |
| **Member since** | When you joined, read-only |

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Edit Profile**.
</Step>
<Step>
Update the fields you need.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**. Your name updates everywhere it appears, including past messages.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Your name is what colleagues see on internal notes and what customers may see on replies. Use the
  name people would recognise, not a nickname.
</Callout>

---

## Changing Your Photo

Click the camera icon on your avatar and choose an image. Large uploads take a moment — the page
shows *"Hold on, changing your profile photo"* while it processes.

The same applies to the organisation logo, which is uploaded separately.

---

## Business Information

The organisation's own details, shared by everyone:

| Field | Used for |
|-------|----------|
| **Business name** | Displayed across RateUp and in customer-facing pages |
| **Business email** | The organisation's contact address |
| **Business phone** | The organisation's contact number |
| **Industry** | Categorises your organisation |
| **Address** | Your full business address |
| **Location** | Where the business operates |

<Callout type="warn">
  These fields appear on customer-facing surfaces such as survey pages and loyalty registration.
  Keep them accurate — a wrong business name here shows up in front of customers.
</Callout>

---

## Language Preferences

Set the language RateUp uses for your account. This changes the interface for you only; it does not
change the language of messages sent to customers.
