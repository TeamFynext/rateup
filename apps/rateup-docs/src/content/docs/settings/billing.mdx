## Current Plan

The plan card shows what you are subscribed to and what it includes:

| Shown | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Plan features** | What the plan unlocks |
| **Annual message limit** | Messages included per year |
| **Usage** | How much of the limit you have consumed |

Click **Refresh** if the figures look stale after a payment.

---

## Upgrading

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Buy RateUp Premium**.
</Step>
<Step>
Review the order on the **Confirm your Order** screen.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Continue to Payment**.
</Step>
<Step>
Complete payment. You will see *"Payment Successful"*, and a confirmation follows by email.
</Step>
</Steps>

Payments are handled by **Razorpay**. If the payment window fails to open, disable any script
blocker for the page and retry.

---

## Add-ons

**Buy Addons** tops up capacity without changing your plan — most often extra message volume.

Buy an add-on when you are approaching your limit mid-cycle; upgrade the plan when you hit it
every cycle.

---

## Running Out of Messages

When the conversation limit is reached, the Inbox composer shows an amber callout with your usage
and an **Upgrade Plan** button, and outbound messaging stops until you upgrade or the limit resets.

<Callout type="warn">
  The limit is on **conversations**, not on individual messages. A long back-and-forth with one
  customer counts once; a broadcast to a thousand contacts counts a thousand times. Campaigns are
  what usually exhaust a plan.
</Callout>

---

## After Paying

Processing is not always instant. You may see **Order Processing Started** before the plan updates.

If the plan has not changed after a few minutes:

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Refresh** on the billing page.
</Step>
<Step>
Check your email for the payment confirmation.
</Step>
<Step>
If the payment succeeded but the plan did not change, contact support with the payment reference.
</Step>
</Steps>
