**Settings → Members** lists everyone who can sign in to your organisation, with their role, status
and teams. A **role** decides what someone may do; their **teams** decide where they may do it.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/members-list.png" alt="Members page listing people with their role, status and teams" />

Members was previously called **Users**.

---

## Find Someone in the List

The list has one row per person, with the columns **Person**, **Role**, **Status**, **Teams** and
**Actions**. Pending invitations are listed after the people.

| Column | What it shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Person** | Name, email and photo. Your own row is marked *(you)* |
| **Role** | The person's role. **Customised** means their abilities have been fine-tuned away from the role — see [Fine-Tune One Person's Abilities](#fine-tune-one-persons-abilities) |
| **Status** | **Online**, **Busy** or **Offline**, or **Paused** for an account that cannot sign in. Invitations show **Invited** |
| **Teams** | The teams they belong to. Owners and Admins with no team show **Every team**; anyone else with no team shows **Not in any team** |
| **Actions** | The arrow opens the person's page. For an invitation: copy the link, resend or revoke it |

To narrow the list:

- Type in **Search by name or email**.
- Pick a role from **Every role** or a team from **Every team**.
- Click one of the counts under the heading — *people*, *in no team*, *invited*, *paused* — to show
  only those rows. Click it again to go back to everyone.

*"No one matches"* means the filters exclude everybody — clear them to see the whole list.

## Invite Someone

You need the **Invite people** ability, which comes with the Manager role and above.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Invite**. The **Invite someone** dialog opens.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter their **Name** (at least two characters) and **Email**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a **Role**. Only roles you could hold yourself are offered — a Manager cannot invite an
Admin. **Staff** is selected by default. Each role card shows whether it covers **Every team** or
**Their teams**, and the selected card lists a few things the role adds.
</Step>
<Step>
For Manager, Staff, Agent and Viewer, pick at least one team under **Teams** — the first team is
filled in for you. Owners and Admins reach every team, so there is nothing to pick.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Send invite**. They get an email with a link to set their own password.
</Step>
</Steps>

If the email already belongs to someone in your organisation, RateUp adds them to the chosen teams
instead of inviting them again, and shows *"Added to the team"*.

<Callout type="warn">
  An invitation sent as **Agent** or **Viewer** currently arrives as **Staff** when it is accepted —
  more access than you chose. After they join, open their page and set the role again.
</Callout>

You can also invite from a team's own page — see [Teams](/settings/teams#add-people-to-a-team).

### When an Invite Is Refused

| Message | Cause |
|---------|-------|
| *"Invitation with the provided email is already pending."* | An unaccepted invite to that address already exists. Resend it instead |
| *"User with the provided email is already a member of another organization."* | One email address can belong to only one organisation |
| *"Team member with the provided email already exists in the team."* | They are already in that team |
| *"You have reached the maximum limit of N users."* | Your plan's user allowance is used up. Pending invitations count towards it |

## Manage Pending Invitations

Invitations sit at the bottom of the list with an **Invited** badge until they are accepted. Use the
icons in the **Actions** column:

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Copy invite link** | Copies the invite link, so you can send it yourself — useful if the email went to spam |
| **Resend invite** | Sends the invite email again |
| **Revoke invite** | Deletes the invitation; the link stops working |

Revoking an invitation you no longer need also frees the user allowance it was holding.

## The Six Roles

Click **Roles** on the Members page to open the roles table at `/dashboard/settings/members/roles`.
It lists every ability down the side and the six roles across the top. From Agent upward, each role
includes everything the one before it can do; **Viewer** is the read-only slice of Staff.

| Role | Reaches | What it is for |
|------|---------|----------------|
| **Owner** | Every team | *"Everything, including billing, deleting the organisation and handing it over."* There is exactly one |
| **Admin** | Every team | *"Everything across every team, except deleting or transferring the organisation."* |
| **Manager** | Their teams | *"Runs their teams: invites people, manages the inbox, integrations and loyalty."* |
| **Staff** | Their teams | *"Works in their teams: inbox, campaigns, surveys, flows, contacts and the cashier."* |
| **Agent** | Their teams | *"Replies in the inbox and serves the loyalty counter for their teams."* |
| **Viewer** | Their teams | *"Read-only access to their teams' data and analytics."* |

In the table, a solid dot marks the role where an ability first appears, and a faint dot means the
role inherits it from the one below.

Abilities for a feature your organisation has turned off in [Customize Menu](/settings/customize-menu) are left
out of the table and of **Fine-tune abilities**, with the note *"Abilities for features your
organization has turned off are hidden."* Fine-tuned abilities already saved for them are kept.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/members-roles.png" alt="Roles matrix with each role's abilities for Inbox, Contacts and Campaigns" />

| | Reset, delete and sync responses | Manager |
| **WhatsApp Flows** | See flows † | Staff |
| | Create and publish flows | Staff |
| **Message Templates** | See templates † | Staff |
| | Create and edit templates | Staff |
| | Sync templates with Meta | Manager |
| **Automation Rules** | See automation rules † | Staff |
| | Create and edit automation rules | Staff |
| **Journeys** | Use journeys † | Staff |
| | Build and edit journeys | Manager |
| **Meta AI Agent** | See the Meta AI Agent † | Staff |
| | Run agent actions | Staff |
| | Connect systems and credentials | Manager |
| **Catalogue** | Manage the product catalogue | Staff |
| **Pipelines** | See pipelines † | Staff |
| | Edit stages and move leads | Staff |

| **Analytics** | See analytics † | Agent |

<Callout type="info">
  The sidebar follows these abilities. Someone without *See campaigns* does not see **Campaigns** at
  all — it is hidden, not greyed out. **Settings → Members** itself appears only for people who have
  *See who is in the organisation*, and **Settings → Audit log** only for people who have *See the
  audit log*. **Meta → Instagram** and **Meta → Messenger** follow *See automation rules*, not
  *Use journeys*.
</Callout>

*Merge duplicate contacts* is held back to Owners and Admins because a
[merge](/contacts/merging-contacts) deletes one of the two contacts and cannot be undone. To let a
Manager or Staff member clean up duplicates, add it to them with
[Fine-tune abilities](#fine-tune-one-persons-abilities). Without it, people still see the
**Possible duplicates** list, but not **Review**, **Not the same person** or **Scan for duplicates**,
nor **Merge with…** on a contact.

## Open a Member's Page

Click the arrow at the end of a row to open that person's page at
`/dashboard/settings/members/<id>`. The arrow appears on your own row, and on everyone else's if you
can pause or remove people, change roles, or add and remove team members.

The page has two tabs: **Profile** and **Activity**.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/settings/member-page.png" alt="A Staff member's Profile tab with Fine-tune abilities expanded" />

### Profile Tab

| Card | What you can change | Who can change it |
|------|--------------------|-------------------|
| **Role and access** | **Role**, **Fine-tune abilities**, **Teams** | Role and abilities: anyone who can *Change roles*. Teams: anyone who can *Add and remove team members* |
| **Profile** | **Title**, **Name**, **Phone Number**, then **Save profile** | The person themselves, or anyone who can *Pause or remove people* |
| **Account** | **Active** / **Paused**, **Transfer ownership**, **Remove from organisation** | See [Pause or Remove Someone](#pause-or-remove-someone) |

Team changes save as soon as you pick or drop a team (*"Team changes are saved immediately."*). The
**Teams** picker only appears for team-bound roles — Owners and Admins reach every team.

### Activity Tab

**Activity** lists the changes this person has made — sending a campaign, inviting someone, changing
a role — newest first, with a time on each. Reading and browsing are not recorded. You need *See the
audit log* (Admin and Owner) to view it.

## Change Someone's Role

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the person's page.
</Step>
<Step>
In **Role and access**, pick a new **Role**. It saves straight away, and a message confirms how many
abilities they gained or lost.
</Step>
</Steps>

| You cannot… | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| Change your own role | Someone else has to do it |
| Change the Owner's role | *"The owner's role cannot be changed, and the owner cannot be paused or removed. Hand the organisation over first."* |
| Give a role with abilities you lack | The picker only offers roles inside your own. The API refuses anything else |
| Make someone Owner from the role picker | Ownership is handed over, not granted — see [Transfer Ownership](#transfer-ownership) |

If the role picker is greyed out, the page says *"Changing roles needs the Manage roles permission."*
— in the roles table that ability is called *Change roles*.

## Fine-Tune One Person's Abilities

When a role is almost right, adjust that one person rather than inventing a new role.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the person's page and expand **Fine-tune abilities** under the role.
</Step>
<Step>
Every ability is listed by group. Ticked abilities marked **From role** come with their role.
</Step>
<Step>
Untick one to take it away (it is marked **Removed**), or tick one the role lacks to add it (marked
**Added**).
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save abilities**. **Reset to role** clears every change.
</Step>
</Steps>

The person's row in the list then shows **Customised**, and the panel header shows how many abilities
are customised.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| You can only add what you hold | An ability you do not have yourself is greyed out with **Not yours to give** |
| Anyone with *Change roles* can take an ability away | Removing never exceeds your own reach |
| Not on yourself | *"You cannot change your own permissions."* |
| Not on the Owner | The Owner always has every ability |
| Survives a role change | Fine-tuning stays with the person when their role changes — check the panel afterwards |

## Pause or Remove Someone

Both need *Pause or remove people*, which comes with Admin and Owner. Neither is available on your
own page or on the Owner's.

### Pause

Switch **Active** off in the **Account** card. *"A paused account cannot sign in until it is
re-enabled."* The person keeps their role, teams and fine-tuning, and shows as **Paused** in the
list. Switch it back on to restore access.

Pause is the reversible option — use it for someone on leave, or while you check what they own.

### Remove

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the person's page and, under **Remove from organisation**, click **Remove**.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm in the **Remove …?** dialog: *"They lose access straight away. Their conversations and work
stay with the organisation."*
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Removing someone cannot be undone. Their sign-in is deleted and they disappear from the Members
  list. To bring them back you have to invite them again, and they arrive as a new account. If they
  might return, pause them instead.
</Callout>

## Before Someone Leaves

Removing a person does not reassign anything they own. Their conversations stay assigned to them,
so do this first:

<Steps>
<Step>
Reassign their open conversations — filter the Inbox to them with the
[sidebar filters](/inbox/inbox-filter), then [reassign](/inbox/asigning-conversation) each one or
send it back to their team.
</Step>
<Step>
Check whether any automation, journey or pipeline assigns work to them by name.
</Step>
<Step>
If they are the Owner, [transfer ownership](#transfer-ownership) — the Owner cannot be paused or
removed.
</Step>
<Step>
Then pause or remove the account.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Transfer Ownership

Only the Owner can do this, and only to an active member.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the new owner's page.
</Step>
<Step>
In the **Account** card, under **Transfer ownership**, click **Transfer**.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm: *"You stay an Admin. Only the owner can delete the organisation or hand it over again."*
</Step>
</Steps>

There is always exactly one Owner. After the handover you keep every ability except deleting or
transferring the organisation.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No **Invite** button | You lack *Invite people* | Ask a Manager, Admin or the Owner |
| The Members list is empty for a Staff, Agent or Viewer | The list currently loads only for people who can invite (Manager and above) | Ask a Manager or Admin to look the person up |
| A role is missing from the invite or role picker | It has abilities you do not hold | Ask someone with a higher role |
| *"This person is not in your organisation"* | They were removed, or the link is from another organisation | Go back to **Members** |
| *"Seeing a person's activity needs the View audit log permission."* | You lack *See the audit log* | Ask an Admin or the Owner |
| A new member has more access than you chose | They were invited as Agent or Viewer and arrived as Staff | Set their role again on their page |
