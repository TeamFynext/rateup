The **Board** is the default view: your columns left to right, and a card for every lead sitting in
one of them.

## Status Bands

Columns are grouped under a band that says what landing there does to a lead:

| Band | Hint | What it means |
|------|------|---------------|
| **Open** | *still being worked* | An ordinary step. The lead stays open |
| **Won** | *closed as customer* | Dropping a lead here closes it as won |
| **Lost** | *closed, no deal* | Dropping a lead here closes it as lost |

A band breaks wherever the status changes, so a board that interleaves statuses gets a band per run
— your column order is never silently rearranged.

---

## A Column

The header carries everything you need to judge a column without opening anything in it:

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Name** and count | The column, and how many leads sit in it — its own total, not the cards loaded so far |
| **{`{n}`}d avg in stage** | Average days its leads have sat in this column. Hidden while a filter is on |
| Red triangle and a number | How many of them have gone quiet — see below |
| **+** | Adds a lead straight into this column. Only on an open column |

A trophy on a **Won** column and a cross on a **Lost** column mark the ones that close a lead.
An empty column reads *"Drop a lead here"*.

---

## How a Column Loads

Each column asks the server for its own cards, fifty at a time, and fetches the next page as you
scroll to the bottom of it. Nothing is counted from what is on screen: the header count, the red
triangle, the attention chip and every sidebar row come from the board's own totals, so a column
that has loaded one page still says how many leads it really holds.

While a filter or a search is on, the header count and the triangle describe what actually matched
rather than putting an unrelated total above the cards, and the average is hidden.

A page that fails to load says *"Could not load this column."* with a **Try again** underneath, so a
broken request never reads as an empty column.

## Moving a Lead

<Steps>
<Step>
Pick a card up by dragging it. The card it came from dims while it is in the air.
</Step>
<Step>
Drop it on the column you want. The target column highlights as you cross it.
</Step>
<Step>
On an ordinary column the move is committed immediately, and the timeline records
*"Moved to {`{stage}`}"*.
</Step>
</Steps>

Dropping onto a **Won** or **Lost** column is a close, not a move: it opens the confirmation
described in [Working a Lead](/pipeline/leads#closing-a-lead). Losing asks for a reason, and the
column you dropped on is the one the lead lands in — so a board with two Lost columns puts it in
the one you actually chose.

<Callout type="info">
  The whole board scrolls sideways with the wheel; each column scrolls its own cards vertically, so
  a long column never pushes the headers off screen.
</Callout>

## A Lead Card

| On the card | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| Avatar with a channel pip | The customer, and where they are reachable |
| Name and, under it, the company — or **Direct customer** | Who this is |
| A message icon on hover | Opens their conversation in the [Inbox](/inbox) |
| A broken-link icon | *Not linked to a contact* — there is no conversation to open |
| Label chips | The [labels](/pipeline/leads#labels) put on this lead |
| A strip with the next activity | The one thing due next, or **No next activity scheduled** in amber |
| Team chip and assignee avatars | Who is working it |
| **{`{n}`}d in stage** | How long it has sat where it is |

The card's left edge carries its stage colour. On a lead that has gone quiet it turns red instead,
and the day count changes to **Idle {`{n}`}d** — the most important thing about a card is the thing
you can read without focusing on it.

A card for a won lead replaces the activity strip with **Won · closed**.

## Leads That Have Gone Quiet

A lead is flagged as needing attention when **all** of these hold:

- it is still open
- its stage sets a rotting window of more than zero days
- **Flag stalled leads** is on for the board — see [Settings](/pipeline/settings)
- nothing has touched it for longer than that window

The count appears in three places at once: the red triangle on the column, the
**{`{n}`} need attention** chip in the header, and the **Needs attention** row in the sidebar. All
three are the same number, and clicking either of the last two filters the board down to exactly
those leads.

<Callout type="warn">
  A rotting window is measured from the last activity, not from when the lead entered the stage. A
  lead you keep noting but never move will never flag — which is the point, but it means the count
  is a measure of silence, not of progress.
</Callout>

## Filtering What You See

| Control | Where | Does |
|---------|-------|------|
| **Search leads** | Header | Matches the lead's name, company and phone number. Two characters minimum |
| Assignee avatars | Header | Click one to show only their leads; click again to clear |
| **Views** | Sidebar | The six saved filters |
| **Lead stages**, **Teams**, **Sources** | Sidebar | Click to apply, click the active one again to clear |

Filtering runs on the server, so a facet narrows what each column asks for rather than hiding cards
it already has. The search waits for your typing to settle, and a single character is ignored — it
would match nearly every lead on the board.

The header badge reads **{`{n}`} open · {`{n}`} leads**, and both numbers describe the whole board
whatever is filtered — it is the board's size, not the size of what you are looking at.

<Callout type="info">
  **All leads on board** means all of them, won and lost included. Hiding closed leads made the Won
  and Lost columns read as empty — use the **Won** and **Lost** views to narrow to one outcome.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *This board has no columns* | Every stage was deleted | **Open settings** and add one, with a status |
| A card will not drop on a column | It was released between columns | Drop it on the column body, not the gap |
| A column looks empty but the sidebar counts leads | A facet or search is on | Clear the active sidebar row, or empty the search |
| *Could not load this column.* | That column's request failed | **Try again** in the column; the rest of the board is unaffected |
| Typing one letter changes nothing | A search needs two characters | Type one more |
| No **need attention** chip despite old leads | **Flag stalled leads** is off, or the stage has no rotting window | [Settings](/pipeline/settings) and [Stages](/pipeline/stages) |
