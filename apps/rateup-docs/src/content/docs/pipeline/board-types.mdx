Every pipeline has a **type**, chosen when you create it. The type decides the columns a board
starts with, what its cards are called, and which sales features it carries. An organisation has
**one Leads board** and up to **ten** boards of the other types beside it.

## The Four Types

| Type | For | A card is | Starts with |
|------|-----|-----------|-------------|
| **Leads** | Your sales funnel. The inbox, automations and CRM integrations use this board | A lead: a customer | **New Lead**, **Contacted**, **Qualified**, **Demo / Proposal**, **Negotiation**, **Won**, **Lost**, and five lost reasons |
| **Support** | Service requests, repairs and complaints, from new to resolved | A ticket | **New**, **In progress**, **Waiting on customer**, **Resolved**, **Closed** |
| **Projects** | Client onboarding and project work, with tasks and due dates | A task | **To do**, **In progress**, **Review**, **Done**, **Cancelled** |
| **Custom** | Anything else. Name the columns yourself | A card | **To do**, **Doing**, **Done** |

Every column is editable afterwards, on any board. See [Stages](/pipeline/stages) for the Leads
columns' probabilities and rotting windows.

<Callout type="info">
  A Support ticket here is a card on a board, not an [inbox ticket](/inbox). Moving a Support card
  does nothing to the customer's conversation, and closing a conversation does nothing to the card.
</Callout>

## Create a Board

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **New pipeline** under **Pipelines** in the sidebar. With no boards yet, the Pipeline screen
offers **Create a pipeline** instead.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a **Type**. A type you cannot create any more is greyed out and says why: *"Your organisation
already has a Leads pipeline."*, or *"You already have 10 boards besides Leads."*
</Step>
<Step>
Give it a **Name**, and pick the **Teams** that work it. *"Everyone on these teams can see the board
and be assigned to its cards."* You can only pick teams you are on yourself.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally add a **Description**, then click **Create pipeline**. The new board opens.
</Step>
</Steps>

Once an organisation has a Leads board and ten others, **New pipeline** disappears from the
sidebar.

## What Only Leads Boards Have

Support, Projects and Custom boards are for work rather than sales, so the sales parts of the board
are left out:

| Feature | Leads | Other types |
|---------|-------|-------------|
| **Forecast** tab | Yes | Hidden |
| Win **Probability** on stages | Yes | Hidden |
| **Lost reasons** | Yes | Hidden; closing never asks for one |
| **Company** and **Source** | Yes | Not asked for; the sidebar has no **Sources** section |
| **Conversion tracking** (Meta CAPI) | Yes | Hidden |
| Used by the inbox, **Assign Lead Stage** automations, the contacts table and CRM syncs | Yes | No |

Everything else (columns, drag and drop, assignees, next activities, notes, stalled-card flagging,
[import and export](/pipeline/import-export), the [List](/pipeline/list) and
[Insights](/pipeline/insights)) works the same on every type.

<Callout type="warn">
  Only the Leads board is wired to the rest of RateUp. An automation's **Assign Lead Stage** action, the
  inbox's **Pipeline Stage** chips, **Update Pipeline Stage** on contacts, and the Zoho, HubSpot,
  Pipedrive and GetLead syncs all read and write the Leads board, never a Support or Projects
  board. Cards on those boards are only ever added by hand or by
  [import](/pipeline/import-export). See [Leads That Open Themselves](/pipeline/automatic-leads).
</Callout>

## The Wording Follows the Type

The same screen speaks the board's language, so a Support board never talks about leads:

| | Leads | Support | Projects | Custom |
|-|-------|---------|----------|--------|
| Header button | **Add lead** | **Add ticket** | **Add task** | **Add card** |
| Closing outcomes | **Won** / **Lost** | **Resolved** / **Closed** | **Done** / **Cancelled** | **Done** / **Cancelled** |
| Header count | **{`{n}`} open · {`{n}`} leads** | **… tickets** | **… tasks** | **… cards** |
| Insights headline | **Win rate** | **Completion rate** | **Completion rate** | **Completion rate** |

## Cards on Support, Projects and Custom Boards

A card on these boards is a piece of work first and a customer second. **Add ticket** (or
**Add task**, **Add card**) asks for:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Title** | Required, up to 160 characters, e.g. *Order arrived damaged* |
| **Description** | Optional, up to 5,000 characters |
| **Due date** | Optional; today or later. Shown on the card as **Due {`{date}`}**, in red once it is past |
| **Stage** | The column it starts in |
| **Assignees** | Anyone on the board's teams |
| **Labels** | Only shown when the board has labels |
| **Link a contact** | Optional. Open by default on Support boards, collapsed on the others. A linked contact gives the card an **Open conversation** button |

In the card window, **Description**, **Due date** and **Contact** can all be edited in place.

On these boards, dropping a card on a closing column closes it straight away, with no confirmation:
there is no lost reason to ask for. A **Custom** board starts with no **Cancelled** column, so a
card marked **Cancelled** there is closed where it stands until you add one in
[Settings](/pipeline/stages).

## Switching Between Boards

With more than one board, the board name in the header becomes a menu, and every board you can see
is listed under **Pipelines** in the sidebar with its open-card count. The Leads board is always
listed first, and opening **Pipelines** without picking a board lands on it.

You only see boards attached to one of your teams.
