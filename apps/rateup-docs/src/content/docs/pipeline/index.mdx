**Pipeline** is where a conversation becomes a deal you are working. Each customer sits on a
**lead** card, each card sits in a **stage** column, and moving it across the board is how your team
says where things stand.

## What a Lead Is

A lead is one customer's progress towards one outcome. It carries the details of the contact it was
raised against — name, company, phone, email — plus everything your team adds while working it:
assignees, labels, a next activity, notes and a full timeline.

| A lead has | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **Stage** | The column it sits in, and with it a win probability |
| **Status** | **Open**, **Won** or **Lost** — set by the stage it lands on |
| **Source** | How it reached you — a WhatsApp enquiry, a web survey, added manually |
| **Assignees** | Everyone working it. A handover adds a name rather than replacing one |
| **Owner** | Whoever opened it. Fixed at creation, and empty for a lead a rule opened |
| **Next activity** | The one thing due next. A lead without one is a lead that stalls |

A contact can hold **one lead per board**. Putting a contact who is already on the board onto
another stage moves their existing lead rather than opening a second one.

## Getting In

**Pipeline** appears in the sidebar for anyone on a team that has the **Pipeline** feature switched
on, and who is on a team attached to the board. See
[Teams](/settings/teams) for both.

<Callout type="info">
  An organisation has **one pipeline** while the feature beds in. Creating a second is refused with
  *"Your organisation already has a pipeline"* — the switcher and the **New pipeline** row exist for
  when that limit lifts.
</Callout>

If nobody has made one yet, the screen offers **Create a pipeline**: give it a name, pick the teams
that work it, and it opens with seven columns and five lost reasons already set up. Every one of
them is yours to change — see [Stages](/pipeline/stages).

## The Sidebar

Under **Pipeline**, the sidebar carries the board's own filters. Everything except the board name is
a click, not a page load, and each row counts across **every** lead so a count never shrinks just
because another filter is on.

| Section | Rows |
|---------|------|
| **Pipelines** | Each board, with its open-lead count, plus **New pipeline** |
| **Views** | **All leads on board** · **Mine** · **Needs attention** · **No next activity** · **Won** · **Lost** |
| **Stages** | One row per column, in board order |
| **Teams** | One row per team with leads on the board |
| **Sources** | One row per source in use |

Below them sits **Pipeline settings**.

<Callout type="info">
  Which tab and which filters you had open are remembered in your browser, so returning to Pipeline
  puts you back where you left it. Only the board itself is in the URL, so a link you share opens
  the board, not your filters.
</Callout>
