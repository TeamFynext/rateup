**Pipelines** is where a conversation becomes a deal you are working. Each customer sits on a
**lead** card, each card sits in a **stage** column, and moving it across the board is how your team
says where things stand.

Your **Leads** board is the sales funnel the rest of RateUp feeds. Beside it you can keep
**Support**, **Projects** and **Custom** boards for work that is not a sale. See
[Board Types](/pipeline/board-types).

## What a Lead Is

A lead is one customer's progress towards one outcome. It carries the details of the contact it was
raised against — name, company, phone, email — plus everything your team adds while working it:
assignees, labels, a next activity, notes and a full timeline.

| A lead has | Meaning |
|------------|---------|
| **Stage** | The column it sits in, and with it a win probability |
| **Status** | **Open**, **Won** or **Lost** — set by the stage it lands on |
| **Source** | How it reached you — a WhatsApp enquiry, a web survey, added manually |
| **Assignees** | Everyone working it. A handover adds a name rather than replacing one |
| **Owner** | Whoever opened it. Fixed at creation, and empty for a lead a rule opened |
| **Next activity** | The one thing due next. A lead without one is a lead that stalls |

A contact can hold **one lead per board**. Putting a contact who is already on the board onto
another stage moves their existing lead rather than opening a second one.

On Support, Projects and Custom boards a card is a ticket, a task or a plain card instead: a
**title** first, with the contact optional. Most of this section reads the same for them; where it
does not, [Board Types](/pipeline/board-types) says so.

## Getting In

**Pipelines** appears in the sidebar for anyone on a team that has the **Pipeline** feature switched
on, and who is on a team attached to the board. See
[Teams](/settings/teams) for both.

If nobody has made one yet, the screen reads *"No pipelines yet"* and offers **Create a pipeline**:
pick a type, give it a name, pick the teams that work it, and it opens with its columns already set
up. A Leads board starts with seven columns and five lost reasons. Every one of them is yours to
change — see [Stages](/pipeline/stages).

An organisation has **one Leads board** and up to **ten** others. **New pipeline** in the sidebar
creates the rest — see [Board Types](/pipeline/board-types).

## The Five Views

The same board, rendered five ways. **Forecast** appears on Leads boards only. **Board** and
**List** answer to the sidebar's filters;
**Forecast** and **Insights** report on the whole board and ignore them, so a facet you left on
never quietly rewrites a figure you are reading.

## The Sidebar

Under **Pipelines**, the sidebar carries the board's own filters. Everything except the board name is
a click, not a page load, and each row counts across **every** lead on the board — counts come from
the server rather than from the cards on screen, so a row never shrinks because another filter is on
or because a column has only loaded its first page.

| Section | Rows |
|---------|------|
| **Pipelines** | Each board you can see, the Leads board first, with its open-card count, plus **New pipeline** while you are under the limit |
| **Views** | **All leads on board** · **Mine** · **Needs attention** · **No next activity** · **Won** · **Lost**. Other board types read **Everything on board**, and their own closing words in place of **Won** and **Lost** |
| **Lead stages** | One row per column, in board order. **Stages** on other board types |
| **Sources** | One row per source in use, counting open leads. Leads boards only |

Below them sits **Pipeline settings**. On a plan with a monthly active-contact allowance, an
**Active contacts left** meter sits under the panel: what is left of this month's allowance, which
is what decides whether a new lead can still be messaged.

Every filter that is narrowing the board also shows as a chip under the header (**View**,
**Stage**, **Source**, **Assignee**), each with its own **×**, and **Clear filters** resets them
all. A filter you set days ago and forgot about can always be undone from the board itself.

<Callout type="info">
  Which tab and which filters you had open are remembered in your browser, so returning to Pipeline
  puts you back where you left it. Only the board itself is in the URL, so a link you share opens
  the board, not your filters.
</Callout>
