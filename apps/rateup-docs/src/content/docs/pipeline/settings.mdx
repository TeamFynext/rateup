The **Settings** tab holds everything about the board that is not a column. **Lead stages** and
**Lost reasons**, which open the tab, are covered separately in [Stages](/pipeline/stages). On
Support, Projects and Custom boards the first section is called **Stages**, and **Lost reasons** and
**Conversion tracking** are not shown — see [Board Types](/pipeline/board-types).

## Who Can Change What

| Section | Who |
|---------|-----|
| Lead stages, lost reasons, stalled-card flagging, conversion tracking | Anyone on a team attached to the board whose role can **Edit stages and move leads** — Staff and above by default |
| **Teams**, **Name**, **Delete this pipeline** | People whose role can **Delete boards or move them between teams** — Owners and Admins by default |

The split is deliberate: a member can retune their own funnel without being able to hand the board
to another team or destroy it. See [Members & Roles](/settings/users#the-six-roles) for the roles and
for fine-tuning one person's abilities.

---

## Teams

Membership of a team attached to the board is the board's **only** access rule. Everyone on those
teams can open it and be assigned its leads; nobody else sees it in the sidebar at all.

You may only add teams **you are on yourself**. A team already on the board that you are not a
member of stays listed and marked **Not your team**, and cannot be removed here — dropping one would
be a door you could not reopen.

<Callout type="warn">
  Removing a team hides the board from its members. Their leads stay exactly where they are, still
  assigned to them, still counting in the totals — they simply cannot see them any more.
</Callout>

The picker will not let you save an empty list, so you cannot lock yourself out of the board you are
editing.

## Automation

One switch, and it is the only rule on this screen that actually runs:

| Switch | Does |
|--------|------|
| **Flag stalled cards** | Highlights cards idle past their stage's rotting window |

With it off, no lead is ever flagged, the header's attention chip disappears, and
[Insights](/pipeline/insights) reports **Rotting checks are off** rather than a zero.

Opening leads by rule is not configured here — that lives in the
[automation builder](/pipeline/automatic-leads).

---

## Name

What the board is called in the sidebar and the switcher. Owners and Admins only. Saving the name it already has
does nothing, and an empty name is refused.

---

## Conversion Tracking

Leads boards only. **Meta Conversions API (CAPI)** sends a conversion event to Meta when a lead is
**won** on this board. It belongs to the pipeline rather than to a contact's lifecycle, because reaching a Won
column is what fires the event.

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Pixel ID** | Auto-filled when a Meta dataset is found for your account, and shown as *"Auto-selected dataset: {`{name}`}"* |
| **Event Name** | **Lead** or **Purchase** |
| **Currency** | **INR**, **USD**, **EUR**, **GBP** or **AED** |

The section needs a connected WhatsApp account: without one it says *"Please connect WhatsApp to
configure Meta Conversions API for tracking."* See [Channels](/settings/channels).

## Danger Zone

**Delete this pipeline** is for Owners and Admins, and asks you to type the board's name to
confirm. The dialog
lists exactly what goes:

- every stage and every lead sitting in them
- all notes, timeline entries and next activities on those leads
- the board's labels and lost reasons

<Callout type="warn">
  This cannot be undone. Deleting the Leads board deletes your entire sales history in RateUp, and
  until you create a new one the inbox, automations and CRM syncs have no board to file leads on.
  Contacts are left alone — but nothing that recorded what happened with them survives.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No **Teams**, **Name** or **Danger zone** section | Your role cannot delete boards or move them between teams | Ask an Owner or Admin |
| A teammate cannot see the board | They are on no team attached to it, or their team lacks the **Pipeline** feature | Add the team here, and check [Teams](/settings/teams) |
| Nothing flags as stalled | **Flag stalled cards** is off, or no stage has a rotting window | This page, then [Stages](/pipeline/stages) |
| Conversion tracking will not save | No WhatsApp channel is connected | [Channels](/settings/channels) |
