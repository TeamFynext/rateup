**Insights** is the board's health in four panels and four headline figures. Every one of them is
counted on the server over the **whole board**: the sidebar's facets and the search box narrow the
[Board](/pipeline/board) and the [List](/pipeline/list), and leave this report alone. It answers
*"how is this pipeline doing"*, not *"how is it doing under the filter I left on"*.

---

## The Four Figures

| Card | What it counts |
|------|----------------|
| **Leads in pipeline** | Open leads, with the number of lead stages under it |
| **Win rate** | Won as a share of everything closed — won *and* lost. An em dash until something closes |
| **Average time to win** | Days from creation to close, across won leads only |
| **Stalled leads** | Leads flagged as needing attention, with how many have no next activity |

**Average time to win** is deliberately end-to-end rather than time-in-stage: it says how long a sale
takes, not how slow the board is. With nothing won yet it reads **Nothing won yet**.

If **Flag stalled leads** is off for the board, the stalled card says **Rotting checks are off**
rather than reporting a zero it cannot stand behind.

---

## Conversion Funnel

One bar per **open** stage, in board order, showing how many leads sit there now. Closing columns
are left out — they are the funnel's outcome, not a step along it.

Each row is labelled with what **carried** from the stage above: *"72% carried"*. The first row
reads **entry**, since nothing feeds it. The label turns red below **70%**, which is the point where
a stage is losing more than it passes on.

<Callout type="info">
  This is a snapshot of where leads are standing, not a historical conversion rate. A stage that has
  just been cleared reads narrow even if everything that passed through it converted.
</Callout>

---

## Where Leads Stall

One row per open stage that has a rotting window, healthy ones included. Each bar is that stage's
own stalled share — *"3 of 11 idle over 5d"* — so a row answers *"how much of this column has gone
quiet"* rather than only ranking columns against each other. A stage with nothing stuck reads
**all healthy** in green.

Stages with no rotting window are left out entirely: nothing there can be late. If the panel says
*"No stage has a rotting window set"*, set one in [Stages](/pipeline/stages).

---

## Leads by Source

Every source in use, ranked, with a bar scaled against the largest. This counts every lead on the
board, closed ones included — it answers where your leads come from, not which ones are still live.

A loud source is not the same as a good one: read it against **Assignee performance** and the
funnel, and use the sidebar's **Sources** rows on the [Board](/pipeline/board) or the
[List](/pipeline/list) to go and look at the leads themselves.

---

## Assignee Performance

One row per person, plus an **Unassigned** bucket.

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Open** | Leads they are on that are still open |
| **Won** | Leads they were on that closed won |
| **Win rate** | Won as a share of everything they closed. Green at 50% or above, an em dash with nothing closed |
| **Stalled** | How many of theirs have gone quiet. An em dash rather than a zero |

<Callout type="warn">
  A lead worked by two people counts for **both** of them, so these columns sum to more than your
  lead count. That is the point — the table reads per person, not per lead — but it means you cannot
  add the rows up to check them against the board.
</Callout>
