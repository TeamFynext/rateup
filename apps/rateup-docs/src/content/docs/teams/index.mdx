### Elevate Your Experience on RateUp

At RateUp, we believe in empowering our users to harness the full potential of customer feedback. Whether you're aiming to boost sales or enrich your business, our platform provides a tailored experience based on your role.

#### 1. Super Admin 🚀
   - **Unlock Everything:**  Enjoy unparalleled access to the entire system and all teams.
   - **Take the Lead:** Lead with authority, overseeing every aspect of the platform.

#### 2. Admin 🌐
   - **Team-Centric Access:** Connect with the teams you're a part of for focused collaboration.
   - **Empower Teams:** Facilitate team efforts with access to specific functionalities.

#### 3. Member 🤝
   - **Team Insight:** Dive into team-based survey responses and insightful statistics.
   - **Contribute Valuable Input:** Play a crucial role by sharing your feedback within your team.

#### Ready to Get Started?

Choose a role aligned with your goals and embark on a journey of enriching your organization's experience with RateUp.
