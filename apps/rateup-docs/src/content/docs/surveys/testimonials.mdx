**Testimonials** gathers what customers write in a survey's **Testimonial** question into
collections you can style and embed anywhere — your website, a landing page, a proposal.

Testimonials now live under Surveys, at `/dashboard/surveys/testimonials` (previously
`/dashboard/testimonial`). The page has no sidebar entry or tab of its own: open that address
directly. It shows the **Surveys** and **Templates** tabs at the top, and its own **Testimonials**
and **Collections** tabs below them.

## Collections

A **collection** is a set of testimonials you present together. Keep separate collections for
separate purposes — one for the homepage, one per product, one per market.

<Steps>
<Step>
On `/dashboard/surveys/testimonials`, click **Create Collection**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a collection name — it cannot be blank. The new collection opens straight away.
</Step>
<Step>
Add testimonials to it.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Where Testimonials Come From

Testimonials are drawn from the answers to [Testimonial questions](/surveys/question-types) in
your surveys. The **Testimonials** tab lists every one received (*"No testimonials found"* until
the first arrives). Filter by:

| Filter | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Survey** | Responses from a specific survey, or **All Surveys** |
| **Team** | One team's responses, or **All Teams** |
| **Date** | When the response came in |
| **Length** | Short or **longest** responses |

<Callout type="info">
  Filter by **longest** first. A specific, detailed testimonial persuades far more than five short
  ones saying "great service".
</Callout>

## Styling

**Customize** and **Edit Testimonial Style** control how a collection looks when embedded:

| Setting | Controls |
|---------|----------|
| **Layout** | How cards are arranged |
| **Background Color** | The card background |
| **Color** | Text colour |
| **Change Font** | Typeface |
| **Font Size** | Text size |
| **Font Weight** | Text weight |

Match your own site rather than RateUp's defaults — an embed that looks foreign reads as an advert
and gets ignored.

## Embedding

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the collection and choose the share option.
</Step>
<Step>
**Copy code** — you will see *"Copied code"* when it is on your clipboard.
</Step>
<Step>
Paste it into your site where the testimonials should appear.
</Step>
</Steps>

Restyling the collection updates the embed — you do not need to paste the code again.

---

## Managing

| Action | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Edit** | Change a testimonial's presentation |
| **Delete testimonials** | Removes selected testimonials from the collection |
| **Delete Testimonial Collection** | Removes the whole collection |

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a collection breaks any embed pointing at it. Check where a collection is used before
  removing it.
</Callout>

---

## Getting Better Testimonials

- Ask right after a good outcome, not weeks later
- Ask a specific question — "what changed for you?" beats "any feedback?"
- Add a **Testimonial** question to a short [survey](/surveys/creating-a-survey) so answering takes seconds
- Always get permission before publishing someone's words with their name
