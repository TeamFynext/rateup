The toolbar sits above the conversation list. The left side names the view you are in; the right
side holds the actions.

<ScreenShot
  src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/search_options.png"
  alt="The Inbox toolbar"

/>

| Icon | Tooltip | What it does |
|------|---------|--------------|
| ↕️ | **Switch to compact list** / **Switch to detailed list** | Toggle between full rows and one-line rows |
| 🔍 | **Search** | Find tickets by contact name or phone number |
| ✅ | **Select Chats** | Turn on selection mode for bulk actions |
| ⬇️ | **Download Chats** | Export conversation history |
| 🔄 | **Refresh** | Reload the list |
| 👤➕ | **New Contact** | Add a WhatsApp contact and start a conversation |

Below them, a second row shows the **count for the current view** on the left and the **sort**
control on the right.

<Callout type="info">
  The count comes from the server, so it reflects the entire view — not just the rows loaded so
  far. While a search is running it shows the number of matching rows instead. In **Spam** it counts
  contacts, because that tab lists one row per contact.
</Callout>

Filtering is **not** in this toolbar. Channel, lifecycle, label, tag, user and team filters live in
the sidebar — see [Sidebar Filters](/inbox/inbox-filter).

## Switch to a Compact List

Click the **compact list** icon, the first in the toolbar, to fit more tickets on screen.

| | Detailed list | Compact list |
|---|---|---|
| **Name, time, unread badge, assignee** | ✅ | ✅ |
| **`#number` and ticket title** | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Last-message preview** | ✅ | — |
| **Chip row** — status, channel, mention, labels, tags, stage | ✅ | — |

The icon is highlighted while the compact list is on. Click it again to go back.

The choice is remembered in this browser and survives clearing your filters. It is a display
preference only and never changes which tickets are listed.

## Search

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the **search** icon. A **Search contact...** bar slides over the toolbar.
</Step>
<Step>
Type a contact name or phone number. Results narrow as you type.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **✕** to clear the search and restore the full view.
</Step>
</Steps>

Search runs **within the current view**. To search everything — open and closed — switch to
[**All**](/inbox/inboxview/all) first.

### Searching for a number you have never messaged

If you search for a **10-digit phone number** and nothing matches, the list offers to add it:
*"This PhoneNumber is not in your contact list. Message … on WhatsApp?"* Click **Add and Message**
to open the **Add New Contact** form with the number already filled in. The form is described under
[Adding a Contact](#adding-a-contact) below.

Any other search with no results reads *"No chats found — search for a valid phone number or contact
name"*.

## Bulk Actions

Selection mode lets you close, assign or delete many tickets in one go. This is the fastest way to
clear out a backlog, and it's useful at shift changes too.

<ScreenShot
  src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/bulk_action.png"
  alt="Selection mode with rows ticked and the selection bar"
/>

### Select tickets

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the **Select Chats** (✅) icon in the toolbar. It turns blue and a checkbox appears on every
row.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the tickets you want, or click anywhere on a row. While selection mode is on, clicking a row
ticks it instead of opening it.
</Step>
<Step>
Once at least one row is ticked, a **selection bar** appears above the list showing *"N
selected"*.
</Step>
</Steps>

The selection bar holds:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Select-all checkbox** | Ticks every ticket currently loaded in the list. Click it again to untick them all |
| **N selected** | How many tickets are ticked |
| **Clear** | Unticks everything but stays in selection mode |
| **Assign selected chats** (person ✓) | Assign every ticked ticket to one person — see below |
| **Close selected chats** (✓ circle) | Close every ticked ticket — see below |
| **Delete selected chats** (bin, red) | Permanently delete every ticked ticket — admins only |

<Callout type="info">
  **Select all only covers the rows that have loaded.** The list loads more tickets as you
  scroll, so to select a long view, scroll to the bottom before ticking the select-all checkbox.
  Compare the *"N selected"* figure with the count above the list before you act.
</Callout>

Click the **Select Chats** icon again to leave selection mode. It also turns off automatically when
you switch views.

### Which actions each view offers

| View | Assign | Close | Delete |
|------|:------:|:-----:|:------:|
| **Open**, **New**, **All**, **@Mentions**, **Favourites** | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Mine** | — | ✅ | ✅ |
| **Closed** | ✅ | — | ✅ |
| **Spam** | — | — | ✅ |

**Delete** is only shown to **Admins** and **Super Admins**.

## Close Many Tickets at Once

<Steps>
<Step>
Enter selection mode and tick the tickets to close. Use the select-all checkbox to take the whole
loaded list.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Close selected chats** (the ✓ circle) in the selection bar.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm: *"Close selected chats? Are you sure you want to close the selected chats."* Click
**Confirm**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Every selected ticket closes with the status **Closed**, its assignees are cleared, and it moves to
the [**Closed**](/inbox/inboxview/closed) view. The selection bar closes and the list refreshes.

Bulk close does not ask for an outcome. To record **Resolved** or **Unresolved** instead, close
those tickets one at a time from the chat header. See
[Closing & Reopening](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle).

<Callout type="warn">
  Bulk close works **per customer**. For each customer you selected, it closes their **most
  recent** ticket. If a customer has two open tickets and you ticked the older one, the newer one
  is the one that closes. Close a customer's older tickets individually from the chat header.
</Callout>

## Assign Many Tickets at Once

<Steps>
<Step>
Enter selection mode and tick the tickets.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Assign selected chats** (the person icon). A dialog opens: *"Assign N Chats"*.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a team under **Select Team**, then a person under **Select Member**. Each member shows
whether they are online.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Assign Chats**. Or skip the pickers and click **Assign To Me** to take them all yourself.
</Step>
</Steps>

Bulk assignment **replaces** whoever was assigned before. Each ticket ends up with exactly the one
person you picked. Like bulk close, it acts on each selected customer's most recent ticket.

[Assignment in detail →](/inbox/asigning-conversation)

## Delete Many Tickets at Once

Admins and Super Admins can permanently delete tickets and every message in them.

<Steps>
<Step>
Enter selection mode and tick the tickets.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Delete selected chats** (the red bin).
</Step>
<Step>
Read the confirmation — *"Delete N conversations"* — and click **Delete**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting cannot be undone. Every message in the selected tickets is removed for everyone, and it
  does not appear in exports or reports afterwards. The contacts themselves are **not** deleted, and
  their other tickets are untouched. To take a customer out of your working views without losing
  anything, [mark them as spam](/inbox/inboxview/spam) instead.
</Callout>

You can delete up to **200** tickets in one go. If a colleague deleted one of your selected tickets
in the meantime, the rest are still deleted.

To delete a single ticket, see
[Deleting a Conversation](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle#deleting-a-conversation).

## Exporting

Click the **download** icon to open **Export Whatsapp chats**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Choose how to bound the dates: **Between**, **Before**, **After** or **On**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the date — or, for **Between**, a **from** and a **to** date. **Export** stays disabled until
the date is filled in.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally tick:

- **Replies Only** — export only the messages your **customers** sent, leaving out everything
  your team and your automations sent
- **Inclusive** — include the chosen day itself (offered for **Before** and **After**; **Between**
  always includes both end dates)
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Export**. A *"Exporting chats..."* notice shows while the file is prepared.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each row of the file is one message, with its **date**, the customer's **phone** and **name**, and
the text in a **received** or **sent** column. A template you sent is listed by its **template
name**.

<Callout type="warn">
  The export covers **every conversation in your organisation** within the dates you pick. The view
  you are in, your search and the sidebar filters do not narrow it. Keep the date range tight, and
  check the file before sharing it outside the company.
</Callout>

Messages that were [deleted on WhatsApp](/inbox/responding-to-conversation) keep their row in the
export but their text is withheld — the cell reads *"This message was deleted"*. Tickets you
[deleted in RateUp](#delete-many-tickets-at-once) are not exported at all.

## Adding a Contact

<ScreenShot
  src={`/docs/screenshots/inbox/add-whatsapp-contact.png`}
  alt="Add new WhatsApp contact"
  width={550}
/>

{/* TODO: re-capture — the form now asks for a Lead stage, not a lifecycle stage */}

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **New Contact** (the blue person + button) in the toolbar. The **Add New WhatsApp contact**
dialog opens.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the **Full Name** and **WhatsApp Number**. **Email Address** is optional.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally pick a **Lead stage** to put the contact straight onto your
[pipeline](/pipeline) board. The field only appears if your board has stages.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Start Conversation**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The new contact opens in the chat window straight away, ready for your first message.

<Callout type="info">
  Starting a conversation with someone who has not messaged you first means there is no open
  24-hour window — your first message must be an approved
  [template](/inbox/send-templates).
</Callout>

## Refreshing

The Inbox updates live, but the **refresh** icon forces a reload and spins while it runs. Use it if
a colleague tells you about a ticket you cannot see, or after a bulk change made elsewhere.
