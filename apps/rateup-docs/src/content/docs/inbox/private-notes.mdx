**Private notes** let your team talk about a ticket without the customer seeing any of it. They
live on the ticket, are visible only to your team, and are never delivered to WhatsApp, Instagram
or any other channel.

---

## Message vs Private Note

The composer has two tabs, and they behave differently:

| | Message | Private Note |
|---|---------|--------------|
| **Goes to** | The customer | Your team only |
| **Colour** | Blue | Amber |
| **Character limit** | 1024 | None |
| **@mentions** | No | Yes |
| **Appears in thread as** | A normal message | Centred, with a padlock |

---

## Write a Private Note

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket and click the **Private Note** tab.
</Step>
<Step>
Type your note in **Add a private note**. The composer turns amber to signal that this is internal.

Use the emoji picker and **Format Message** (T) for bold, italic, strikethrough, code, quotes and
lists — handy for a structured handover.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Send**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The note is added to the thread immediately, labelled **Internal note** with your name and the
time.

## The Notes-Only View

While the **Private Note** tab is selected, the thread above shows **internal notes only**.

This turns the internal discussion into its own readable surface, instead of something you scroll
past customer messages to find.

Switch back to **Message** to see the full conversation again. Opening a different ticket always
starts on the customer conversation.

<Callout type="info">
  This is the fastest way to catch up on a ticket someone handed to you: open it, click **Private
  Note**, and read the internal thread top to bottom.
</Callout>

## Mention a Colleague

<Steps>
<Step>
In the **Private Note** tab, type `@`.
</Step>
<Step>
A list of your organisation's members appears. Keep typing to narrow it, or use the arrow keys.
</Step>
<Step>
Press **Enter** or click a name to insert the mention. It appears highlighted in the note.
</Step>
<Step>
Finish the note and click **Send**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/mention-picker.png" alt="Mentioning a colleague in a private note" />

<Callout type="info">
  You can mention **anyone in your organisation**, not just members of your own team. Mention
  several people in one note if the question concerns all of them.
</Callout>

## What the Mentioned Person Sees

When you are mentioned:

- The ticket appears in your **@Mentions** view
- The **@Mentions** count in the sidebar goes up
- The ticket row shows an **@ Mention** chip — with a number if you were mentioned more than once
- An **@** button appears in that ticket's chat header

Mentions are personal: you only see mentions addressed to you.

---

## Clearing a Mention

Opening or reading a mentioned ticket does **not** clear the mention. It stays until you dismiss it
deliberately, so it cannot disappear because you glanced at the row.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket from **@Mentions**.
</Step>
<Step>
Do whatever was asked — usually a reply in a private note.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **@** button in the chat header, next to the star.
</Step>
<Step>
The ticket leaves **@Mentions** and the sidebar count goes down.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Clearing affects **you only**. The note stays on the ticket, and colleagues mentioned in the same
  note keep their own mention until they clear it.
</Callout>

## What to Use Notes For

| Situation | Note to write |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Handover** | What has been done, what is outstanding, what you promised the customer |
| **Escalation** | Why it is being escalated and what decision you need — mention the decision-maker |
| **Context** | Anything you learned that is not in the messages (a phone call, a system check) |
| **Decisions** | Why you offered the refund, why the exception was approved |

Avoid using notes for anything the customer should be told — switch to **Message** for that.

## Frequently Asked

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="Can the customer ever see a private note?">
No. Private notes are never sent to any channel. They exist only inside RateUp.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I mention someone in a customer message?">
No — `@` mentions work in the **Private Note** tab only.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I mentioned the wrong person. What now?">
Send a new note with the right person mentioned. The wrong recipient can clear their own mention
with the **@** button.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do notes count towards the 1024-character limit?">
No. Private notes have no limit, and the counter is hidden on that tab.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Are private notes included in exports?">
The export lists messages exchanged with customers. It has no column for note text. Still check any
file before sharing it outside the company. See [Exporting](/inbox/toolbar#exporting).
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The Private Note tab has disappeared.">
On WhatsApp, once the customer's 24-hour window closes, the whole composer — notes included — is
replaced by the template button. Notes come back when the customer writes again. Write handover
notes while the window is open.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Are notes deleted with the conversation?">
Yes. [Deleting a conversation](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle#deleting-a-conversation) removes its
private notes along with its messages.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

---

## Best Practices

- Write the handover note *before* reassigning, not after
- Mention people instead of messaging them outside RateUp — the context stays on the ticket
- Clear your mentions once handled, so **@Mentions** stays a real to-do list
- Keep notes factual; they are part of the permanent ticket record
