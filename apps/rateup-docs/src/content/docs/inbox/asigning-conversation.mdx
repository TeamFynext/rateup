Assignment decides **who is responsible for a ticket**. It is not optional bookkeeping: a ticket
with no owner cannot be replied to.

---

## Why Assignment Is Required

The composer stays locked until a ticket has an owner. This prevents the two failure modes every
shared inbox suffers from:

- **Duplicate replies** — two agents answering the same customer minutes apart
- **Silent drops** — everyone assuming somebody else picked it up

An unassigned ticket is visible to everyone but owned by nobody. It carries a **New** status pill
until that changes, and while it is recent it also sits in the **New** view with an amber edge.

---

## The Three Ways to Assign

| Route | Best for | Where |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Quick assign** | Taking a ticket without reading it first | The **person +** button on a list row (**Assign to me**) |
| **Assign bar** | Taking a ticket you have just read | Above the composer in an unassigned ticket |
| **Ticket Details** | Assigning to someone else, or reassigning | Panel → **Assignment** → **Change** |
| **Bulk assign** | Handing many tickets to one person | Selection mode → **Assign selected chats** |

## Assign a Ticket to Yourself

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **New** view — or find any row with an amber left edge.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **person +** button on the right of the row.

Alternatively, open the ticket and click **Assign to me** above the composer.
</Step>
<Step>
The status pill turns to **Assigned**, the ticket moves to your **Mine** view, and the composer
unlocks.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/assign-to-me.png" alt="Messaging is blocked until the ticket has an owner" />

A system note is added to the thread recording who took the ticket, so ownership history is always
visible.

## Assign to a Colleague or Team

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Assign** next to **Assign to me** in the bar above the composer, or open **Ticket Details**
and click **Change** under **Assignment**.
</Step>
<Step>
The picker, *"Assign to team or individual"*, lists your teams. Click a team to expand it.
</Step>
<Step>
Click people to tick or untick them. Each shows **Online** or **Offline**, and you are marked
**( You )**.
</Step>
<Step>
Close the picker. The change saves by itself about half a second after your last click, with no
Save button, and a system note records it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/assign-panel.png" alt="Choosing between a team and an individual" />

Once people are assigned, the button shows their avatars — up to three, then `+N` — and the first
name. Hover it for *"Assigned N staff"*.

## Assigning a Whole Team

Expand a team and click **Assign entire team**. Every current member of that team is ticked in one
go. Click **Unassign entire team** to untick them all.

<Callout type="info">
  **Assign entire team** assigns each **member individually**, not the team as a unit. Every one of
  them sees the ticket in their own **Mine** view. Someone who joins the team later is not added to
  tickets assigned before they joined.
</Callout>

A team row shows a ✓ when every member is ticked, and a dashed circle when only some are.

### One person, one team

A person in several teams is assigned **under one team at a time**. If you tick them under a second
team, they move across. The picker shows a grey chip with the other team's name beside anyone
already assigned elsewhere. Hover it for *"Assigned under …"*.

| | **Assign entire team** | Assign one person |
|---|-----------------|------------------------|
| **Who is assigned** | Every current member | That person |
| **Shows in Mine for** | Every one of those members | That person |
| **Use when** | The whole team should see it and anyone may pick it up | The work needs a specific skill or owner |

<Callout type="info">
  You can assign several people to one ticket. The list row then shows the first assignee's avatar
  with a `+N` badge — hover it for the full list.
</Callout>

## Reassigning

Reassignment changes the owner of an already-assigned ticket. Reach for it when:

- An agent is going offline or on leave
- The issue needs escalating
- Another team is better placed to handle it

<Steps>
<Step>
Leave a **private note** explaining where things stand. This is the handover.
</Step>
<Step>
**@mention** the new owner in that note so it reaches their **@Mentions** view.
</Step>
<Step>
Open **Ticket Details → Assignment → Change** and pick the new owner.
</Step>
<Step>
The ticket leaves your **Mine** view and enters theirs. A system note records the change.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Reassigning without a note is the most common reason customers have to repeat themselves. Always
  leave the context behind — it takes ten seconds and stays on the ticket forever.
</Callout>

## Picking Up a Closed Ticket

Closed tickets cannot be reassigned in place — they are a finished record.

A closed ticket's composer reads *"This chat has been closed by … at …. Assign it to start the
conversation again."*, with **Assign to me** and **Assign** beside it. Assigning from there, or
clicking **Reopen** in the header, opens a **new ticket** for the same customer. **Reopen** carries
the previous assignees across. The closed ticket keeps its number, close time and result.

## Bulk Assignment

To hand several tickets to one person at once:

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the **Select Chats** (✅) icon in the toolbar to enter selection mode.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the tickets you want to move — or tick one, then use the select-all checkbox in the selection
bar.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Assign selected chats**. In the dialog, pick a team under **Select Team** and a person under
**Select Member**, then click **Assign Chats**. Or click **Assign To Me** to take them all.
</Step>
</Steps>

Useful at shift changes, or when redistributing an absent colleague's queue. Bulk assign is not
offered in **Mine** or **Spam**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Bulk assign **replaces** the existing assignees. Each ticket ends up with only the person you
  picked, so anyone who was on it before is taken off.
</Callout>

[Bulk actions in detail →](/inbox/toolbar#assign-many-tickets-at-once)

## Permissions

Assignment is not gated by role. Anyone who can open the Inbox can assign any ticket they can see,
to anyone — the limit is **visibility**, not permission:

| Role | Can assign |
|------|-----------|
| **Super Admin** | Any ticket, to anyone — no channel scoping applies |
| **Admin** | Any ticket on the channels their teams grant |
| **Staff** | The same channel scope, minus any assigned tickets their organisation hides from them |

---

## Best Practices

- Assign as soon as a ticket arrives — an unowned ticket is nobody's problem
- Route to a **team** when you are unsure who should take it
- Reassign when availability changes, rather than letting a queue stall
- Avoid ping-ponging tickets between agents; it fragments the history
- Close tickets once resolved, so ownership lists stay honest
