Assignment decides **who is responsible for a ticket**. It is not optional bookkeeping: a ticket
with no owner cannot be replied to.

---

## Why Assignment Is Required

The composer stays locked until a ticket has an owner. This prevents the two failure modes every
shared inbox suffers from:

- **Duplicate replies** — two agents answering the same customer minutes apart
- **Silent drops** — everyone assuming somebody else picked it up

An unassigned ticket is visible to everyone but owned by nobody, and it sits in **New** with an
amber edge until that changes.

---

## The Three Ways to Assign

| Route | Best for | Where |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Quick assign** | Taking a ticket without reading it first | The **person +** button on a list row |
| **Assign bar** | Taking a ticket you have just read | Above the composer in an unassigned ticket |
| **Ticket Details** | Assigning to someone else, or reassigning | Panel → **Assignment** → **Change** |

## Assign a Ticket to Yourself

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **New** view — or find any row with an amber left edge.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **person +** button on the right of the row.

Alternatively, open the ticket and click **Assign to me** above the composer.
</Step>
<Step>
The status pill turns to **Assigned**, the ticket moves to your **Mine** view, and the composer
unlocks.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Assign to a Colleague or Team

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **dropdown** next to **Assign to me**, or open **Ticket Details** and click **Change**
under **Assignment**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a **team** or an **individual**. Online / offline availability is shown next to each person.
</Step>
<Step>
The ticket is assigned instantly and appears in that person's **Mine** view.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Team vs Individual

| | Assign to a team | Assign to an individual |
|---|-----------------|------------------------|
| **Who can respond** | Any member, subject to permissions | That person |
| **Shows in Mine for** | Nobody in particular | That person |
| **Use when** | You want the fastest available person | The work needs a specific skill or owner |

<Callout type="info">
  You can assign several people to one ticket. The list row then shows the first assignee's avatar
  with a `+N` badge — hover it for the full list.
</Callout>

## Reassigning

Reassignment changes the owner of an already-assigned ticket. Reach for it when:

- An agent is going offline or on leave
- The issue needs escalating
- Another team is better placed to handle it

<Steps>
<Step>
Leave a **private note** explaining where things stand. This is the handover.
</Step>
<Step>
**@mention** the new owner in that note so it reaches their **@Mentions** view.
</Step>
<Step>
Open **Ticket Details → Assignment → Change** and pick the new owner.
</Step>
<Step>
The ticket leaves your **Mine** view and enters theirs. A system note records the change.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Reassigning without a note is the most common reason customers have to repeat themselves. Always
  leave the context behind — it takes ten seconds and stays on the ticket forever.
</Callout>

## Picking Up a Closed Ticket

Closed tickets cannot be reassigned in place — they are a finished record.

Assigning one, or clicking **Reopen**, opens a **new ticket** for the same customer and carries the
previous assignees across. The closed ticket keeps its number, close time and result.

[More on reopening →](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle)

## Bulk Assignment

To hand several tickets to one person at once:

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the **select** (✅) icon in the toolbar to enter selection mode.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the tickets you want to move.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the assignee and confirm.
</Step>
</Steps>

Useful at shift changes, or when redistributing an absent colleague's queue.

## Permissions

| Role | Can assign |
|------|-----------|
| **Business Admin** | Any ticket, to anyone, across all teams |
| **Manager** | Tickets within the teams they manage |
| **Agent** | To themselves; reassigning to others depends on role settings |

---

## Best Practices

- Assign as soon as a ticket arrives — an unowned ticket is nobody's problem
- Route to a **team** when you are unsure who should take it
- Reassign when availability changes, rather than letting a queue stall
- Avoid ping-ponging tickets between agents; it fragments the history
- Close tickets once resolved, so ownership lists stay honest
