Assignment and reassignment allow you to control **who is responsible for a conversation**.  
This helps teams manage workload, avoid duplicate replies, and ensure timely responses.

Assignment actions are available across Inbox views such as **New**, **Assigned**, and **Mine**, based on your role permissions.

---

## Assigning a Conversation

Assigning a conversation means assigning ownership to **yourself**, a **specific team member**, or a **team**.

---

### When to Assign a Conversation

You should assign a conversation when:
- A new message arrives in the **New** tab
- A conversation needs a clear owner
- You want to route the chat to the correct team

---

### Step-by-Step: Assigning a Conversation

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/assignDropdown.png`}
  alt="Assigning a new conversation from the New tab"
  width={650}
/>

1. Open a conversation from the **New** tab
2. In the chat window, locate the assignment options at the bottom
3. Click **Assign to Me**  
   **or**
4. Click the **dropdown icon** next to Assign
5. Select:
   - A **team**, or
   - An **individual team member**
6. The conversation is assigned immediately

Once assigned, the conversation moves out of **New** and appears under **Assigned** (and **Mine**, if assigned to you).

---

## Assigning to a Team vs Individual

- **Assign to Team**
  - The conversation is visible to the entire team
  - Any team member can pick up the conversation

- **Assign to Individual**
  - Ownership is clearly defined for one person
  - The conversation appears in that user’s **Mine** view

---

## Reassigning a Conversation

Reassignment allows you to **change the owner** of an already assigned conversation.

This is useful when:
- An agent is unavailable
- The issue needs escalation
- The conversation should be handled by another team

---

### Step-by-Step: Reassigning a Conversation

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/reassign.png`}
  alt="Reassigning a conversation to another team or user"
  width={450}
/>

1. Open an assigned conversation
2. Click the **assigned user name** at the top-right of the chat window
3. The **Assign to team or individual** panel opens
4. Select another:
   - Team, or
   - Team member
5. The conversation is reassigned instantly

The new assignee becomes responsible for responding and closing the conversation.

---

## Reassigning Closed Conversations

Closed conversations can also be reassigned if follow-up is required.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/closed-reassign.png`}
  alt="Reassigning a closed conversation"
  width={600}
/>

To reopen a closed conversation:

1. Open the conversation from the **Closed** tab
2. Click **Assign to Me** or assign to another user
3. The conversation becomes active again
4. It moves out of the Closed view

> ℹ️ Reassigning a closed conversation does **not** override the WhatsApp 24-hour messaging window.

---

## Role-Based Assignment Permissions

### Business Admin
- Can assign and reassign any conversation
- Can assign across all teams and users

### Manager
- Can assign and reassign conversations within their teams

### Agent
- Can assign conversations to themselves
- Reassignment permissions depend on role settings

---

## Best Practices

- Assign conversations as soon as they arrive
- Ensure every conversation has a clear owner
- Reassign when availability changes
- Avoid frequent reassignment to reduce confusion
- Close conversations once resolved
