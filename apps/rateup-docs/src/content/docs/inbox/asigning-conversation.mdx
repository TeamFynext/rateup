Assignment decides **who is responsible for a ticket**. Every ticket has **one owner**. It is not
optional bookkeeping: a ticket with no owner cannot be replied to.

---

## Why Assignment Is Required

The composer stays locked until a ticket has an owner. This prevents the two failure modes every
shared inbox suffers from:

- **Duplicate replies** — two agents answering the same customer minutes apart
- **Silent drops** — everyone assuming somebody else picked it up

An unassigned ticket is visible to everyone but owned by nobody. It carries a **New** status pill
until that changes, and while it is recent it also sits in the **New** view with an amber edge.

---

## The Ways to Assign

| Route | Best for | Where |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Quick assign** | Taking a ticket without reading it first | The **person +** button on a list row (**Assign to me**) |
| **Assign bar** | Taking a ticket you have just read | **Assign to me** or **Assign to…** above the composer in an unassigned ticket |
| **Ticket Details** | Assigning to someone else, or reassigning | Click the assignee under **Assignment** — or press **A** |
| **Bulk assign** | Handing many tickets out at once | Selection mode → **Assign selected chats** |

## Assign a Ticket to Yourself

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **New** view — or find any row with an amber left edge.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **person +** button on the right of the row.

Alternatively, open the ticket and click **Assign to me** in the bar above the composer.
</Step>
<Step>
The status pill turns to **Assigned**, the ticket moves to your **Mine** view, and the composer
unlocks.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/assign-to-me.png" alt="An unassigned ticket with the Assign to me and Assign to… bar above the composer" />

A system note is added to the thread recording who took the ticket, so ownership history is always
visible.

## Assign to a Colleague in One Click

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the assignee under **Assignment** in the [Ticket Details](/inbox/ticket-details) panel — it
reads **Unassigned** if nobody has it. Or click **Assign to…** in the bar above the composer of an
unassigned ticket.

Shortcut: press **A** anywhere in the open ticket (not while typing). The panel opens with the
picker ready.
</Step>
<Step>
Type in **Search people or teams** to narrow the list.
</Step>
<Step>
Click a name. The ticket is assigned straight away, *"Assigned to …"* confirms it, and a system note
records the change.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/assign-panel.png" alt="The assignee picker open in the Ticket Details panel, with people and Auto-assign in a team" />

The picker has three groups:

| Group | Contains |
|-------|----------|
| *(top)* | **Assign to me** (unless you already own it) and **Unassign** (if someone owns it) |
| **People** | Members of the teams you can see — you first, then whoever is online, then everyone else by name. The current owner is marked **· Owner** |
| **Auto-assign in a team** | Your teams. Picking one hands the ticket to one of its members — see below |

Assigning **replaces** the current owner: the ticket leaves their **Mine** view and enters the new
owner's. A person who is in several teams is recorded under the ticket's current team when they
belong to it.

<Callout type="info">
  Tickets assigned to several people before one-owner assignment keep showing every assignee, with
  a `+N` badge, until someone reassigns them. Picking one person then leaves just that person.
</Callout>

## Let a Team Pick Whose Turn It Is

Pick a team under **Auto-assign in a team** when you know which team should have the ticket but not
who. RateUp hands it to **one** member, turn by turn:

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Taking turns** | Members are given tickets in rotation, so nobody gets every one |
| **Available first** | Only members who are **Online** or **Busy** are in the rotation. If nobody is, every member is |
| **Its own rotation** | Manual team picks keep their own turn order, separate from any [automation](/automation) that assigns round-robin |

The ticket then shows the member it landed on, like any other assignment.

## Reassigning

Reassignment changes the owner of an already-assigned ticket. Reach for it when:

- An agent is going offline or on leave
- The issue needs escalating
- Another team is better placed to handle it

<Steps>
<Step>
Leave a **private note** explaining where things stand. This is the handover.
</Step>
<Step>
**@mention** the new owner in that note so it reaches their **@Mentions** view.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the assignee under **Assignment** (or press **A**) and pick the new owner.
</Step>
<Step>
The ticket leaves your **Mine** view and enters theirs. A system note records the change.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Reassigning without a note is the most common reason customers have to repeat themselves. Always
  leave the context behind — it takes ten seconds and stays on the ticket forever.
</Callout>

## Picking Up a Closed Ticket

Closed tickets cannot be reassigned in place — they are a finished record.

A closed ticket's composer reads *"This chat has been closed by … at …"*, followed by *"Their
24-hour window is still open, so you can message them straight away."* or *"Start a new
conversation with a template."* Beside it are **New conversation** (which assigns you) and
**Assign to…**. Either one, or **Reopen** in the header, opens a **new ticket** for the same
customer. **Reopen** carries the previous assignees across. The closed ticket keeps its number,
close time and result.

[More on reopening →](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle)

## Bulk Assignment

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the **Select Chats** (✅) icon in the toolbar to enter selection mode.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the tickets you want to move — or tick one, then use the select-all checkbox in the selection
bar.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Assign selected chats**. The dialog, *"Assign N chats"*, holds the same searchable list as
the picker.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Assign to me**, a person, or a team. A progress bar — *"Assigning chats…"* — counts the
tickets as they are done.
</Step>
</Steps>

Picking a **team** gives each ticket to the next member in turn, so a batch is spread across the
team rather than landing on one person.

Useful at shift changes, or when redistributing an absent colleague's queue. Bulk assign is not
offered in **Mine** or **Spam**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Bulk assign **replaces** the existing assignees. Each ticket ends up with only the person it was
  given to, so anyone who was on it before is taken off.
</Callout>

[Bulk actions in detail →](/inbox/toolbar#assign-many-tickets-at-once)

## Permissions

Changing who owns a ticket needs the **Assign conversations** ability. Without it, you see the owner
under **Assignment** but cannot click it. Within that, the limit is **visibility**:

| Role | Can assign |
|------|-----------|
| **Owner**, **Admin** | Any ticket, to anyone — no channel scoping applies |
| **Manager** | Any ticket on the channels their teams grant |
| **Staff**, **Agent** | The same channel scope, minus any assigned tickets their organisation hides from them |

Abilities are set per role, and can be added or removed for one person — see
[Users](/settings/users).

---

## Best Practices

- Assign as soon as a ticket arrives — an unowned ticket is nobody's problem
- Pick a **team** when you are unsure who should take it, and let the rotation choose
- Reassign when availability changes, rather than letting a queue stall
- Avoid ping-ponging tickets between agents; it fragments the history
- Close tickets once resolved, so ownership lists stay honest
