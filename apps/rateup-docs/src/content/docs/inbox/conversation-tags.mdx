The Inbox classifies work at two different grains, and mixing them up is the usual mistake.

| | **Conversation tag** | **Contact label** |
|---|---|---|
| Describes | One ticket | The customer |
| Shows on | Only the conversation you tagged | Every ticket that customer ever opens |
| Good for | *Bug Report*, *Escalated*, *Follow-up*, *Refund* | *VIP*, *Wholesale*, *Churn risk* |
| Sidebar section | **Conversation Tags** | **Contact Labels** |

A tag describes what *this* conversation was about. A label describes who you are talking to.

## Create a Tag or a Label

Tags and labels are created in different places, because they belong to different things.

<Steps>
  <Step>For a **conversation tag**, open **Inbox → Settings** and choose the **Conversation tags** tab. For a **contact label**, open [Contacts → Labels](/contacts/labels).</Step>
  <Step>Click **Create tag** (or the Labels page's create button).</Step>
  <Step>Give it a **Name** and a **Colour**, then **Save**.</Step>
</Steps>

Each tag row shows how many conversations currently carry it, so you can see what is actually in
use before renaming or deleting. An empty tab reads *"No conversation tags yet."*

Use **Edit** to rename or recolour, and **Delete** to remove one. Deleting takes the tag off every
conversation carrying it.

---

## Tag a Conversation

<Steps>
  <Step>Open the ticket and expand the details panel on the right.</Step>
  <Step>Under **Conversation tags**, click **+ Add**.</Step>
  <Step>Type in **Search tags** and click a tag to add it. Click the **✕** on a tag chip to take it off.</Step>
</Steps>

Contact labels sit in the same panel under **Contact labels**, and are added the same way, with
**Search labels** — but they attach to the customer, so they will appear on their next ticket too.

Tags also show as chips on the conversation row in the list, so you can see them without opening
the ticket.

## Filter by Tag or Label

The Inbox sidebar carries a section for each, listed under the views:

| Section | Filters to |
|---------|-----------|
| **Contact Labels** | Every ticket belonging to a customer carrying the label |
| **Conversation Tags** | Only the conversations carrying the tag |

Each section only appears once at least one tag or label exists, and has its own clear control once
a filter is active. Both combine with the rest of the sidebar — views, channel, teams, users and
lifecycle — see [Filtering the Inbox](/inbox/inbox-filter).

<Callout type="info">
  Filtering by a contact label is the wider of the two: it pulls in that customer's whole history,
  not just the conversation you had in mind.
</Callout>
