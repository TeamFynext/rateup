everyone: **quick replies** and **auto-close rules**.

## Quick Replies

Quick replies are saved answers agents insert instead of retyping the same thing all day.

Agents reach them by clicking the **lightning** button in the composer, or by typing `/` in the
message box.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/quick-replies-list.png`}
  alt="Quick replies list"
  width={"70%"}
/>

### Add a quick reply

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **Quick Replies** tab and click **Add Quick Reply**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **key** — a short, unique handle agents will type after `/`. Keep it memorable:
`refund-policy` beats `qr-14`.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a **category** — a high-level grouping such as Billing, Technical or General.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a **topic** for more specific context.
</Step>
<Step>
Write the **reply text**, then click **Save Changes**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/add-quick-reply.png`}
  alt="Add quick reply"
  width={650}
/>

The reply is available in every conversation straight away.

### Manage quick replies

From the list you can **edit** and **delete** replies, and organise them by category and topic.
Changes apply instantly across the Inbox.

### Writing good quick replies

- Keep them short — agents will edit them before sending anyway
- Leave the greeting out, so agents can personalise the opening line
- Cover the questions you answer daily, not the rare ones
- Review them quarterly; outdated policies in a quick reply spread fast
- Remember customer messages cap at **1024 characters**

## Automation: Auto-Close Rules

Tickets that go quiet forever are noise. Auto-close rules end them for you.

<ImagePlaceholder
  file="inbox/auto-close-rules.png"
  shot="The Automation tab showing the two auto-close rule cards — New / Unassigned Chats and Assigned Chats — each with its inactivity period and a toggle."
  caption="Auto-close rules."
/>

{/* TODO: capture the Automation tab */}

There are two rules, each switched on or off independently:

| Rule | Applies to |
|------|-----------|
| **New / Unassigned Chats** | Tickets nobody has taken |
| **Assigned Chats** | Tickets with an owner that have gone quiet |

Each reads: **inactive for `<time>` → Close Conversation.**

### Edit a rule

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **Automation** tab and click the rule you want to change.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm the **chat type** — New / Unassigned, or Assigned.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a **close after inactivity** period from the presets, or choose **Custom** and enter a number
of hours.
</Step>
<Step>
The **action** is **Close Conversation** — the thread is marked closed and agents can reopen it at
any time.
</Step>
<Step>
Save.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Choosing sensible periods

| Rule | Typical | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| **New / Unassigned** | Shorter — 24–48 hours | If nobody claimed it in two days, nobody will |
| **Assigned** | Longer — 72 hours or more | An owner may legitimately be waiting on the customer |

<Callout type="warn">
  Set the assigned-chat period too short and you will close tickets agents are still working on.
  Start generous and tighten it once you have seen the effect.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  Auto-closed tickets behave exactly like manually closed ones: read-only, no assignees, and
  reopenable. If a customer writes again, a new ticket is created.
</Callout>
