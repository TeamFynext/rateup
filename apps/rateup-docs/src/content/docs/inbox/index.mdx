## The One Thing to Understand First

**Every row in the Inbox is a ticket.**

A ticket is one topic with one customer — a delivery question, a refund request, a complaint. It
has its own number, title, owner and outcome.

When a customer comes back next month with a different question, they get a **new ticket**. Their
old ticket stays closed, exactly as your team left it.

| | Ticket |
|---|---|
| **Identity** | `#1042` + a short title |
| **Owner** | One agent, several agents, or a whole team |
| **Status** | New → Assigned → Closed |
| **Contents** | Only the messages belonging to that topic |

<Callout type="info">
  A customer with three topics has **three rows** in your Inbox. Opening one shows only that
  topic's messages — the rest are one click away in the ticket's history bar.
</Callout>

## Supported Channels

- **WhatsApp Business**
- **Instagram**

Messages from every connected channel land in the same Inbox, and each ticket shows a channel
badge so agents always know where the customer is writing from.

---

## Who Uses the Inbox

| Role | Typical use |
|------|-------------|
| **Staff** | Works from **Mine**, replies to customers, closes their own tickets |
| **Admin** | Watches **Open** and **New**, balances workload across the team |
| **Super Admin** | Full visibility, configures automation and quick replies |

What you can *see* depends on your role and the channels your teams grant. What you can *do* inside
a ticket you can see is largely not role-gated.
