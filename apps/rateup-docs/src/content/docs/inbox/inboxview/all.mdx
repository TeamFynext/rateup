**All** shows every ticket with no status restriction: open, closed, assigned and unassigned
together. The one exception is contacts flagged as spam, who appear only in
[**Spam**](/inbox/inboxview/spam).

It is not a working queue. Use it when you need to **find** something rather than act on a list.

---

## When to Use It

- A customer refers to "the conversation we had last month" and you do not know how it ended
- You are auditing how a topic was handled across several tickets
- A ticket has gone missing from the view you expected it in
- You want to export a full history for a customer

## Narrowing It Down

**All** is most useful combined with search and filters:

| Tool | Use |
|------|-----|
| **Search** | Find a customer by name or phone number |
| **Channel** | Narrow to one WhatsApp number, Instagram account or Facebook page |
| **Lifecycle / labels** | Narrow by how the contact is tagged |
| **Users / teams** | See one person's or one team's history |
| **Sort** | Switch between newest and oldest first |
