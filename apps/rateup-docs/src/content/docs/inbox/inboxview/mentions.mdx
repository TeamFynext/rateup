**@Mentions** collects every ticket where a colleague mentioned you by name in a **private note**.

Treat it as your personal to-do list for internal requests: questions, handovers and escalations
that were addressed to you specifically.

---

## What You See

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/view-mentions.png" alt="Tickets where you were mentioned" />

- Each row shows an **@ Mention** chip beside the status pill
- If you were mentioned more than once on a ticket, the chip shows the count
- The sidebar count tells you how many tickets are waiting on you

Mentions are personal — you only ever see mentions addressed to **you**.

---

## Reading a Mention Does Not Clear It

Opening a mentioned ticket leaves the mention exactly where it is.

This is deliberate. A mention should not vanish because you clicked the row while scanning the
Inbox — it stays until you dismiss it on purpose.

---

## Clear a Mention

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket from the **@Mentions** view.
</Step>
<Step>
Read the note and do whatever was asked of you.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **@** button in the chat header, next to the star.
</Step>
<Step>
The ticket leaves **@Mentions** and the sidebar count goes down.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/clear-mention-button.png" alt="The clear-mention button appears only when you have an unread mention on the ticket" />

<Callout type="info">
  Clearing a mention only affects **you**. The private note stays on the ticket, and any colleague
  mentioned in the same note keeps their own mention until they clear it themselves.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Explanation |
|---------|-------------|
| The **@** button is not in the header | You have no uncleared mention on this ticket |
| A ticket reappeared in @Mentions | Someone mentioned you again in a newer note |
| The count does not match the rows | Refresh the list — one ticket can hold several mentions |
| You cannot see a mention a colleague swears they sent | Check they mentioned *you* and not a similarly named teammate |

---

## Best Practices

- Work @Mentions before your own queue — someone is waiting on you
- Clear a mention only once you have actually acted on it
- Reply in a private note on the same ticket, so the answer stays with the context
- Mention colleagues instead of messaging them outside RateUp
