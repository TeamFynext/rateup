The Inbox provides multiple **tabs and filters** to help organize customer conversations efficiently.  
These controls allow users—especially **Business Admins**—to monitor, assign, and manage conversations across teams and staff.

---

## Conversation Tabs (Status-Based Views)

The tabs at the top of the Inbox categorize conversations based on their current status.

---

### New

- Shows **new incoming conversations**
- Includes chats that are **not yet assigned** to any team member
- This is the first place admins and agents should check

**Purpose:**  
Ensure no customer message goes unanswered.

---

### Assigned

- Displays conversations that are **assigned to any staff member**
- Includes conversations handled by other agents or teams

**Purpose:**  
Allow admins and managers to track workload distribution.

---

### Mine

- Shows conversations **assigned to the currently logged-in user**
- Helps agents focus only on their own conversations

**Purpose:**  
Improve agent productivity and avoid confusion.

---

### Closed

- Contains conversations that have been **resolved and closed**
- Closed chats are read-only and used for reference

**Purpose:**  
Maintain a clean Inbox and track completed interactions.

---

## Filters (Admin & Manager Controls)

Filters allow you to narrow down conversations based on **staff and teams**.

> 🔐 These filters are especially useful for **Business Admins** and **Managers**.

---

### All Staff Filter

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/staff-filter.png`}
  alt="All Staff Filter"
  width={200}
/>

- Allows viewing conversations handled by:
  - All staff members
  - Business admins
  - Individual agents

**Business Admin View:**  
Admins can monitor conversations across the entire organization or focus on a specific staff member.

---

### All Teams Filter

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/inbox-teamfilter.png`}
  alt="All Teams Filter"
  width={200}
/>

- Allows filtering conversations by team
- Useful when multiple teams handle different types of customer queries

**Business Admin View:**  
Admins can analyze performance and workload at the team level.

---

## Role-Based Visibility

Since you are logged in as a **Business Admin**:

- You can view conversations from **all staff members**
- You can switch between **individual staff and teams**
- You have full visibility across the Inbox

Other roles may have limited access depending on permissions.

---

## Why These Filters Matter

Using tabs and filters helps you:

- Monitor team performance
- Balance workload across staff
- Quickly locate specific conversations
- Maintain operational clarity at scale
