**Closed** holds tickets that have been **closed, resolved, marked unresolved or marked as spam**.

They are finished work: no longer active, but kept as a permanent record for reference and
reporting.

## Closed Tickets Are Read-Only

Opening a closed ticket replaces the composer with a banner:

> **This chat has been closed**  
> by *RateUp Admin* at *16/12/25 11:01 PM*.

You cannot send a message or a private note on a closed ticket. The record stays exactly as it was
left.

You can still:

- Read the whole message history
- See the close result and who closed it
- Open the **Ticket Details** panel
- Star the ticket
- Export the conversation

## Reopening

Click **Reopen** in the chat header.

<Callout type="warn">
  Reopening does **not** revive the old ticket. It opens a **new ticket** for the same customer and
  carries over the people who were assigned when the original closed.
</Callout>

| | Original ticket | New ticket |
|---|-----------------|-----------|
| **Number** | Unchanged | A fresh number |
| **Status** | Stays Closed | Assigned |
| **Close time and result** | Frozen | Not set yet |
| **Where it lives** | Closed view + history bar | Open / Mine |

Both remain visible in the customer's **previous conversations** bar, so the full story is never
lost.

---

## When a Customer Replies After Closure

You do not need to do anything. Their message creates a **new ticket**, which lands in **New**.
The closed ticket stays closed, so its resolution time and result stay accurate.

---

## When to Reopen

<Steps>
<Step>
**The ticket was closed by mistake** — reopen and carry on.
</Step>
<Step>
**The fix did not work** — reopen so the follow-up is tracked as active work rather than buried in
a closed thread.
</Step>
<Step>
**A customer asks about something unrelated** — do *not* reopen. Let their message open its own
ticket.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Role-Based Behaviour

| Role | In the Closed view |
|------|--------------------|
| **Business Admin** | Views and reopens any closed ticket |
| **Manager** | Views and reopens within their teams |
| **Agent** | Views closed tickets; reopening depends on permissions |
