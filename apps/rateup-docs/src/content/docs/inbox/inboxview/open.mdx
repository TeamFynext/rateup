**Open** shows every **active** ticket: those already assigned and those still waiting for an
owner. Nothing closed appears here.

It is the default view when you enter the Inbox, and the one managers live in.

---

## What You See

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/view-open.png" alt="Assigned and unassigned tickets side by side" />

- Every ticket is still active
- Each row shows **New** (unassigned) or **Assigned**
- Unassigned rows carry an **amber left edge**
- The assignee's avatar sits on the right; `+2` means several people are assigned

---

## Open vs New vs Mine

| View | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Open** | All active tickets, assigned or not |
| **New** | Active tickets with **no owner** |
| **Mine** | Active tickets assigned **to you** |

**New** and **Mine** are both subsets of **Open**.

---

## Reading Ownership at a Glance

| Where | What it shows |
|-------|---------------|
| Conversation row | Assignee avatar, `+N` for extras — hover for the full list |
| Chat header | Status pill: **Assigned** or **New** |
| Ticket Details panel | Assignee name, their team, and a **Change** action |

---

## Reassign a Ticket

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **panel icon** at the far right of the chat header to open **Ticket Details**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Assignment**, click **Change**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick another team or team member. The ticket is reassigned instantly and a system note records it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Assigning a **team** makes the ticket available to every member of it. Assigning an
  **individual** puts it in exactly one person's **Mine** view. Use teams when you want the fastest
  available person to pick it up.
</Callout>

---

## Using Open as a Manager

- Sort by **oldest first** to surface tickets that have been waiting
- Use the **staff** and **team** filters to check one person's or one team's load
- Watch for rows with an amber edge — those have nobody on them
- Reassign when someone goes offline rather than letting their tickets stall

---

## Role-Based Behaviour

| Role | In the Open view |
|------|------------------|
| **Business Admin** | Sees every open ticket; can reassign across all teams |
| **Manager** | Sees and reassigns within their teams |
| **Agent** | Sees open tickets; can respond and reassign subject to permissions |
