**Spam** holds tickets marked as junk — promotional blasts, automated noise, and anything that is
not a genuine customer query.

Marking a ticket as spam closes it and removes it from every working view.

---

## Mark a Ticket as Spam

Two routes, same result:

| From | How |
|------|-----|
| **The Close menu** | Open the ticket → **Close** → **Mark as Spam** |
| **Ticket Details panel** | Open the panel → **Mark as spam** at the bottom |

## What Happens

- The ticket is treated as **closed** — the composer is hidden
- It disappears from **Open**, **New** and **Mine**
- Its assignees are cleared
- A system note records who marked it and when
- It remains available in the **Spam** view

---

## Recovering a Ticket Marked by Mistake

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket from the **Spam** view.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Reopen** in the chat header.
</Step>
<Step>
A fresh ticket is opened for the customer and you can carry on as normal.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Spam vs Blocking

| | Mark as spam | Block contact |
|---|-------------|---------------|
| **Scope** | This one ticket | The contact, permanently |
| **Effect** | Ticket closes and moves to Spam | Neither side can message the other |
| **Future messages** | Still arrive, as new tickets | Do not arrive at all |
| **Reversible** | Yes, by reopening | Yes, by unblocking |

To block: open the ticket, use the **Close** menu — or the **⋮** overflow menu if the ticket is
already closed — and choose **Block contact**. You will be asked to confirm.

<Callout type="warn">
  Blocking cuts communication in both directions. Use it for repeat offenders, not for a single
  unwanted message.
</Callout>

---

## Best Practices

- Use **Spam** for noise and **Close** for real conversations that are finished
- Review the Spam view occasionally — genuine customers do end up here by accident
- Block only when marking as spam is not enough
