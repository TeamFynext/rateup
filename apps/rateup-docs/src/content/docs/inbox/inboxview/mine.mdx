**Mine** shows the tickets assigned **to you**. For most agents this is where the working day
happens.

- Every ticket is assigned to you
- You are accountable for replying and for closing it
- These tickets also appear in **Open**, but **Mine** filters out everyone else's work

---

## How Tickets Get Here

A ticket appears in **Mine** when:

- You click **person +** on a row in the **New** view
- You click **Assign to me** inside a ticket
- A manager or admin assigns it to you
- A team ticket is narrowed down to you specifically

---

## Your Daily Loop

<Steps>
<Step>
**Clear @Mentions first.** Colleagues are blocked on those.
</Step>
<Step>
**Work through Mine**, oldest or most urgent first. Watch the 24-hour countdown on WhatsApp
tickets.
</Step>
<Step>
**Close each ticket with the right outcome** as soon as its topic is finished.
</Step>
<Step>
**Top up from New** when your queue is short.
</Step>
</Steps>

## What You Can Do From Here

- Read and reply to customer messages
- Leave [private notes](/inbox/private-notes) and mention colleagues
- Edit the [ticket title and description](/inbox/ticket-details)
- Star the ticket into **Favourites**
- [Close it with a result](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle)
- Reassign it, if your role permits

---

## Handing a Ticket Over

<Steps>
<Step>
Leave a **private note** summarising where things stand — this is the handover, and it stays on the
ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
**@mention** the person taking over, so it lands in their **@Mentions** view.
</Step>
<Step>
Open **Ticket Details → Assignment → Change** and pick the new owner.
</Step>
<Step>
The ticket leaves your **Mine** view and appears in theirs.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Reassigning without a note is the most common cause of a customer repeating themselves. Always
  leave the context behind.
</Callout>
