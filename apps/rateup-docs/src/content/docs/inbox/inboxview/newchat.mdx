**New** shows every open ticket that is **not yet assigned to anyone** and has had a message in the
**last 24 hours**.

It is the queue of fresh, unowned work, which makes it the first place any agent or manager should
look.

<Callout type="info">
  **New means unassigned *and* recent.** A ticket leaves this view the moment someone is assigned —
  even if nobody has replied yet. It also drops out once 24 hours pass without a message. It is
  still open and unowned, but you now find it only in **Open**.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  An empty **New** view does not mean nothing is waiting. Unowned tickets older than a day are only
  in [**Open**](/inbox/inboxview/open). Look for rows with a **New** status pill there, and sort by
  **Oldest First** to find them.
</Callout>

## What You See

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/view-new.png" alt="Unassigned tickets in the New view" />

- Every ticket is **unassigned**
- Rows carry a cream background, an **amber left edge** and a filled **Unassigned** badge
- Each row shows the customer, ticket number and title, last message and time
- The **person +** button on the right assigns the ticket to you instantly

---

## Assign a Ticket to Yourself

The fastest route does not require opening the ticket at all.

<Steps>
<Step>
Hover the row in the **New** view.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **person +** button on the right of the row.
</Step>
<Step>
The ticket moves to **Mine** and disappears from **New**. A system note records that you took it.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can also open the ticket and use the **Assign to me** bar shown above the reply box.

---

## Assign to Someone Else

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket from the **New** view.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the dropdown next to **Assign to me**, or open the **Ticket Details** panel and click
**Change** under **Assignment**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a **team** or an **individual**. Availability (online / offline) is shown to help you choose.
</Step>
<Step>
The ticket is assigned immediately and moves to that person's **Mine** view.
</Step>
</Steps>

[Full assignment guide →](/inbox/asigning-conversation)

## Why Replying Is Blocked Here

Until a ticket has an owner, the composer is replaced by an **Assign to me** bar.

This is deliberate: it prevents two agents answering the same customer at the same time, and it
guarantees every conversation has someone accountable for it.

---

## Keeping New Empty

**New** works best as a queue you actively drain:

- Check it frequently — a ticket sitting here has nobody watching it
- Assign first, investigate second; ownership can always be changed later
- Route to a **team** when you are not sure who should handle it
- Use [auto-close rules](/inbox/inbox-settings) so genuinely dead tickets do not pile up
