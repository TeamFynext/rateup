**Favourites** collects the tickets you have starred — a VIP customer, a live escalation, or
anything you need to come back to today.

---

## Star a Ticket

There are two ways:

| Where | How |
|-------|-----|
| **Conversation list** | Hover a row and click the star next to the customer's name |
| **Chat header** | Click the star at the top of an open ticket |

A starred ticket shows a filled amber star everywhere it appears. Click the star again to unstar —
the ticket leaves **Favourites** immediately.

## Stars Belong to Tickets, Not Customers

If a customer has three tickets, you can star one and leave the other two alone. Each ticket keeps
its own star.

<Callout type="info">
  This is different from the old Inbox, where starring applied to the whole contact. Starring one
  ticket no longer marks everything that customer has ever written.
</Callout>

---

## What Appears Here

- Any ticket you have starred, whatever its status
- Open and closed tickets alike
- Only your own stars

---

## Favourites vs @Mentions

| | Favourites | @Mentions |
|---|-----------|-----------|
| **Who puts it there** | You | A colleague |
| **Why** | You want to keep an eye on it | Someone needs something from you |
| **How it leaves** | You unstar it | You clear the mention |

---

## Best Practices

- Keep the list short — Favourites works as a shortlist, not a second inbox
- Unstar as soon as a ticket no longer needs watching
- Star before escalating, so you can follow the outcome without hunting for it
