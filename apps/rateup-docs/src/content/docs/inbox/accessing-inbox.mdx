Before you can work on tickets, you need access to the Inbox. This page covers where to find it,
what has to be connected, and what each role is allowed to see.

---

## Before You Start

You need:

- A RateUp account in a team with the **Inbox** feature — Owners and Admins always have it
- At least one connected channel — a **WhatsApp Business** number or an **Instagram** account
- Membership of at least one **team**, if your organisation routes tickets by team

<Callout type="warn">
  If the Inbox is missing from your sidebar, it is almost always one of two things: none of your
  teams has the **Inbox** feature, or no channel has been connected yet. Ask an Owner or Admin to check
  both under [Teams](/settings/teams) and [Channels](/settings/channels).
</Callout>

## Open the Inbox

<Steps>
<Step>
**Log in** to your RateUp account.

If you have not set a password yet, see [Login](/getting-started/login).
</Step>
<Step>
In the left sidebar, click **Inbox**.
</Step>
<Step>
The Inbox opens on the **Open** view, showing every active ticket you have access to.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Who Can Access the Inbox

RateUp has six roles, set per member under [Settings → Members](/settings/users):

| Role | Inbox reach |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Every ticket on every channel |
| **Admin** | Every ticket on every channel |
| **Manager** | Tickets on the channels their teams reach; also blocks, marks spam, deletes and exports |
| **Staff** | Tickets on the channels their teams reach |
| **Agent** | Tickets on the channels their teams reach — replies and assigns |
| **Viewer** | Read-only: sees their teams' tickets but cannot reply |

Two things narrow this further:

- **Channel access comes from teams.** A member's channels are the union of those granted to the
  teams they belong to. A member in **no** team is not restricted — they see every channel. See
  [Teams](/settings/teams).
- **The Inbox itself comes from teams too.** A team-scoped member sees it only if one of their
  teams has the **Inbox** feature under [Feature Access](/settings/teams#feature-access).

<Callout type="info">
  Whether members see tickets assigned to other people is an organisation-level setting. Under its
  strictest state a member sees only the tickets assigned to them, and only the calls they took or
  placed.
</Callout>

## What Your Role Controls

What you can do inside a ticket comes from your role's abilities. Any of them can also be added or
taken away for one person on their page under [Members](/settings/users).

| Ability | Lets you | Roles that have it |
|---------|----------|--------------------|
| **See conversations** | Open the Inbox and read tickets | All six |
| **Reply and send messages** | Reply, write notes, and take or place [WhatsApp calls](/inbox/whatsapp-calls) | All but Viewer |
| **Assign conversations** | Change a ticket's owner | All but Viewer |
| **Block, mark spam, edit conversations** | Block a contact, flag spam | Owner, Admin, Manager |
| **Delete conversations** | Delete a ticket, singly or in bulk | Owner, Admin, Manager |
| **Export chats and logs** | Export chats and message logs | Owner, Admin, Manager |

Channels and teams are configured under [Teams](/settings/teams) and
[Channels](/settings/channels), not in the Inbox.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---------|--------------|------------|
| Inbox is missing from the sidebar | None of your teams has the **Inbox** feature | Ask an Owner or Admin to turn it on under [Teams](/settings/teams) |
| Inbox opens but is empty | No channel connected, or no tickets match your permissions | Check the **All** view; ask an admin to confirm the channel is live |
| You can read but not reply | The ticket is unassigned, or closed | Assign it to yourself, or reopen it |
| You cannot see a colleague's ticket | It belongs to a team you are not in | Ask an admin to assign it to you or your team |
| **Delete** is missing | Your role lacks **Delete conversations** | Ask an Owner or Admin |
| **Unassigned** is empty but customers are waiting | **Unassigned** only holds unassigned tickets from the last 24 hours | Check **Open** for older unassigned tickets |
