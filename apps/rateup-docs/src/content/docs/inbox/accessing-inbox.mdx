Before you can work on tickets, you need access to the Inbox. This page covers where to find it,
what has to be connected, and what each role is allowed to see.

---

## Before You Start

You need:

- A RateUp account with an **Inbox-enabled role**
- At least one connected channel — a **WhatsApp Business** number or an **Instagram** account
- Membership of at least one **team**, if your organisation routes tickets by team

<Callout type="warn">
  If the Inbox is missing from your sidebar, it is almost always one of two things: your role does
  not include Inbox access, or no channel has been connected yet. Ask a Business Admin to check
  both.
</Callout>

---

## Open the Inbox

<Steps>
<Step>
**Log in** to your RateUp account.

If you have not set a password yet, see [Login](/getting-started/login).
</Step>
<Step>
In the left sidebar, click **Inbox**.
</Step>
<Step>
The Inbox opens on the **Open** view, showing every active ticket you have access to.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Who Can Access the Inbox

| Role | What they see |
|------|---------------|
| **Business Admin** | Every ticket in the organisation, plus Inbox settings and automation |
| **Manager** | Tickets belonging to the teams they manage |
| **Agent** | Tickets assigned to them or to their team, subject to permissions |

---

## What Your Role Controls

Permissions affect more than visibility. Depending on your role you may or may not be able to:

- Assign or reassign tickets to other people
- Close and reopen tickets
- Flag a contact as spam, or block them
- Export conversation history
- Edit contact details
- Change Inbox settings and automation rules

These are configured under **Teams & Roles**, not in the Inbox itself.

---

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | What to do |
|---------|--------------|------------|
| Inbox is missing from the sidebar | Role does not include Inbox access | Ask a Business Admin to update your role |
| Inbox opens but is empty | No channel connected, or no tickets match your permissions | Check the **All** view; ask an admin to confirm the channel is live |
| You can read but not reply | The ticket is unassigned, or closed | Assign it to yourself, or reopen it |
| You cannot see a colleague's ticket | It belongs to a team you are not in | Ask a Manager to assign it to you or your team |
