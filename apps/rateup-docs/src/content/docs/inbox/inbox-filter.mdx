Below the eight views in the sidebar sits a **Filter** group. Every section in it narrows whichever
view you are looking at.

| Section | Filters by |
|---------|-----------|
| **Channel** | The number or account the customer wrote to |
| **Lifecycle** | The contact's lifecycle stage |
| **Labels** | Labels on the contact |
| **Users** | The agent the ticket is assigned to |
| **Teams** | The team the ticket is assigned to |

A section only appears if there is something to filter by: no labels defined, no **Labels** section.

<Callout type="info">
  Filtering used to live in an **Advanced Filters** popover above the conversation list. That popover
  has been removed — everything it did is in the sidebar now, where you can see what is active
  without opening anything.
</Callout>

## Channel

Scopes the list to **one channel**: a WhatsApp number, an Instagram account or a Facebook page. Each
entry carries its platform's icon.

Use it when you run more than one number and they mean different things — a support line and a sales
line, or one number per branch.

Channel applies to **every** view, including **Spam**, and stays applied as you move between them.

<Callout type="info">
  On a narrow screen the sidebar is off-screen, so the **Channel** selector appears in the
  conversation list header instead. It is the same filter — changing one changes the other.
</Callout>

---

## Lifecycle & Labels

Both filter on the **contact** rather than the ticket:

- **Lifecycle** — one stage at a time, shown with its emoji
- **Labels** — several at once; a ticket matches if its contact carries any of them

Useful for triage: work through **New Lead** enquiries first, or find everyone tagged **VIP** who is
waiting.

---

## Users & Teams

These answer the question every manager asks a dozen times a day: *whose work is this, and how much
of it is there?*

**Users** filters by assigned agent. Use it to:

- See everything one agent is handling right now
- Check whether someone is overloaded before assigning them more
- Review an individual's work before a coaching conversation

**Teams** filters by assigned team. Use it to:

- Focus on one department's queue — Support, Sales, Billing
- Compare load across teams
- Manage region- or language-based routing

Both accept several selections at once.

---

## Clearing a Filter

Each section header shows a **clear** button once something in it is selected. Clearing one section
leaves the others alone.

<Callout type="warn">
  Filters persist across views **and across sessions** in this browser. A view that looks suspiciously
  empty is usually a filter you set yesterday — check the Filter group before assuming work has gone
  missing.
</Callout>

---

## Combining Them

Applying several narrows the list to the intersection: *tickets on the **Sales** number, assigned to
**Team Support**, whose contact is a **New Lead***.

Useful combinations:

| Goal | View | Filters |
|------|------|---------|
| What is my team sitting on right now? | Open | Team: Support |
| Is this agent overloaded? | Open | User: John |
| What did Sales close last week? | Closed | Team: Sales |
| Who has unowned work on the support line? | New | Channel: Support number |
| Which leads are waiting? | Open | Lifecycle: New Lead |

---

## Who Sees What

| Role | Filter visibility |
|------|-------------------|
| **Super Admin** | Every staff member, team and channel |
| **Admin** | The channels their teams grant, and the people working them |
| **Staff** | The same channel scope, minus any assigned tickets their organisation hides from them |

<Callout type="info">
  The filters never widen your access. They only narrow what you can already see — an agent whose
  team is [restricted to one channel](/settings/teams) cannot reach another channel's conversations
  by picking it here.
</Callout>

---

## Best Practices

- Clear the filters when you switch tasks, or you will wonder where your tickets went
- Combine a team filter with **oldest first** sorting to surface stale work
- Use **New** plus a channel filter to see what nobody has picked up on one number
- Rebalance by reassigning, not by asking people to "check the Inbox"
