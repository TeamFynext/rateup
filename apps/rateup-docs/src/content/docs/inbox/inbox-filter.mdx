Below the eight views in the sidebar sit two filters: **All Staff** and **All Teams**.

They answer the question every manager asks a dozen times a day: *whose work is this, and how much
of it is there?*

## How They Differ From Advanced Filters

| | Staff & team filters | [Advanced filters](/inbox/toolbar) |
|---|---------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Where** | Sidebar, under the views | Toolbar, above the list |
| **Filter by** | Who owns the ticket | What the ticket is — channel, labels, campaign, status |
| **Mainly for** | Managers and admins | Everyone |

Both stack on top of the view you are in. **Open** + *Team: Support* + *Channel: WhatsApp* is a
perfectly normal combination.

---

## All Staff

Filters the list by **assigned agent**.

Use it to:

- See everything one agent is handling right now
- Check whether someone is overloaded before assigning them more
- Review an individual's work before a coaching conversation
- Find out who has been handling a particular customer

Set it back to **All Staff** to see everyone again.

---

## All Teams

Filters the list by **assigned team**.

Use it to:

- Focus on one department's queue — Support, Sales, Billing
- Compare load across teams
- Manage region- or language-based routing

Set it back to **All Teams** to clear.

---

## Combining Them

Applying both narrows the list to the intersection: *tickets assigned to **Agent John** within
**Team Support***.

Useful combinations:

| Goal | View | Staff | Team |
|------|------|-------|------|
| What is my team sitting on right now? | Open | All | Support |
| Is this agent overloaded? | Open | John | All |
| What did Sales close last week? | Closed | All | Sales |
| Who has unowned work in my department? | New | All | Support |

---

## Who Sees What

| Role | Filter visibility |
|------|-------------------|
| **Business Admin** | Every staff member and every team |
| **Manager** | The staff and teams they manage |
| **Agent** | Limited or hidden, depending on permissions |

<Callout type="info">
  The filters never widen your access. They only narrow what you can already see — an agent
  filtering by a colleague still sees only the tickets their role permits.
</Callout>

---

## Best Practices

- Clear the filters when you switch tasks, or you will wonder where your tickets went
- Combine with **oldest first** sorting to surface stale work in one team
- Use **New** + a team filter to find work nobody in that department has picked up
- Rebalance by reassigning, not by asking people to "check the Inbox"
