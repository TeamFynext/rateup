Every ticket ends. This page covers how to end one properly, and what happens afterwards.

---

## The Three States

| Status | Meaning | Set by |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **New** | Nobody is assigned yet | Created automatically with the ticket |
| **Assigned** | At least one agent or team owns it | Assigning someone |
| **Closed** | Closed, resolved or unresolved | Using the **Close** menu |

<Callout type="info">
  Status is derived from assignment and closure — there is no status field to maintain separately.
  Assign a ticket and it becomes **Assigned**; close it and it becomes **Closed**.
</Callout>

## Closing a Ticket

The green **Close** button sits in the top-right of the chat window, next to the panel toggle.

<ScreenShot
  src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/close_status.png"
  alt="The Close menu"
  width="60%"
/>

<Steps>
<Step>
Finish the conversation — make sure the customer has what they need.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Close** in the chat header.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the outcome that describes what actually happened.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Choosing the right outcome

| Option | Use when | Ticket goes to |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **Mark as Resolved** | The customer's problem was solved | Closed |
| **Mark as Closed** | Finished, with no specific outcome to record | Closed |
| **Mark as Unresolved** | You could not solve it, and want that on record | Closed |

<Callout type="info">
  These outcomes feed your reporting. "Resolved" and "Unresolved" are the difference between a
  support team that looks perfect and one that can actually find its weak spots — pick honestly.
</Callout>

An option is greyed out when the ticket already has that status. Hover **Mark as Unresolved** for
a reminder that closing removes every assignee.

Below the outcomes, separated from them, sit the two **contact** actions — **Mark contact as spam**
and **Block contact**. They are not ways of finishing a ticket: they apply to the person, and to
every ticket they have. [Spam in detail →](/inbox/inboxview/spam)

Admins see a third group at the very bottom: **Delete conversation**. See
[Deleting a Conversation](#deleting-a-conversation).

### Closing many tickets at once

To close a whole batch — a backlog after a campaign, say — use selection mode in the list: tick the
tickets, or use the select-all checkbox, then **Close selected chats**. Bulk close always records
**Closed**, never an outcome. [Bulk actions →](/inbox/toolbar#close-many-tickets-at-once)

## What Closing Changes

<Callout type="warn">
  Closing a ticket **removes all its assignees**. This is intentional: closed work should not sit
  in anyone's queue.
</Callout>

Once a ticket is closed:

- It moves to the **Closed** view
- All assignees are cleared
- A system note records **who** closed it and **when**
- The composer is replaced by a banner — the ticket becomes **read-only**
- If it was closed with a result, a green badge appears in the ticket strip and in **Ticket Details**

## Blocking a Contact

**Block contact** sits in the **Close** menu, below **Mark contact as spam**. On an already-closed ticket it moves
into the **⋮** overflow menu next to **Reopen**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **Close** menu (or **⋮** on a closed ticket) and choose **Block contact**.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm in the dialog.
</Step>
</Steps>

A blocked contact cannot message you, and you cannot message them. The composer is replaced by
*"This contact has been blocked by you."* with an **Unblock contact** button. Click it and confirm
to lift the block.

<Callout type="info">
  **Block contact** only appears while the customer's 24-hour window is open, and only for a contact
  who is not already blocked. If the option is missing from the menu, the customer has not written
  in the last 24 hours.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  Blocking cuts communication in both directions — the customer cannot reach you even if they later
  need to. To get junk out of your queue while still receiving whatever arrives, use
  [**Mark contact as spam**](/inbox/inboxview/spam) instead.
</Callout>

## Reopening a Ticket

A closed ticket shows **Reopen** where **Close** used to be.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the closed ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Reopen** in the chat header.
</Step>
<Step>
A **new ticket** is created for the same customer, with the previous assignees carried over.
</Step>
</Steps>

### What reopening actually does

| | Original ticket | New ticket |
|---|-----------------|-----------|
| **Number** | Keeps its own | Gets a fresh one |
| **Status** | Stays **Closed** | **Assigned** |
| **Close time and result** | Frozen | Not set |
| **Assignees** | None | Whoever was assigned when it closed |
| **Where it appears** | Closed view, history bar | Open / Mine |

<Callout type="warn">
  Reopening does not revive the old ticket. Its resolution time and outcome stay exactly as they
  were, so your reporting is not rewritten after the fact.
</Callout>

The same happens if you assign somebody to a closed ticket: a successor is opened rather than the
closed one being brought back to life.

## When a Customer Replies After Closure

Nothing to do. Their message creates a **new ticket** in the **New** view, and the closed ticket
stays closed.

This is the whole point of the ticket model: one topic, one ticket, one honest record of how it
went.

## Deleting a Conversation

Closing keeps a ticket as a permanent record. **Deleting** removes it, along with every message in
it. Use it for test threads, duplicates, or a conversation that should never have been stored.
**Admins and Super Admins only.**

There are three places to delete from:

| From | How |
|------|-----|
| **The Close menu** | Open the ticket → **Close** → **Delete conversation**, at the bottom |
| **The ⋮ menu** | On a closed or spam ticket, where there is no Close menu |
| **Ticket Details panel** | **Delete conversation**, the last button in the panel |

<Steps>
<Step>
Choose **Delete conversation** from any of the places above.
</Step>
<Step>
Read the confirmation — *"Ticket #1042 with Priya Sharma will be deleted."*
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Delete**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting cannot be undone. The ticket and every message in it are removed for everyone. The
  customer's WhatsApp is not affected, since they still have their copy. Nor are the contact
  themselves, their details, labels and pipeline lead, or their other tickets.
</Callout>

| Deleted | Kept |
|---------|------|
| The ticket, its number, title and description | The contact and everything on it |
| Every message, private note and system note in it | The customer's other tickets |
| Its conversation tags | The customer's 24-hour window |

To delete many tickets at once, use selection mode in the list and **Delete selected chats**. See
[Bulk Actions](/inbox/toolbar#delete-many-tickets-at-once).

<Callout type="info">
  If you only want a customer out of your queue, [mark them as spam](/inbox/inboxview/spam)
  instead. It is reversible, and nothing is lost.
</Callout>

## Auto-Close

Tickets that go quiet can be closed automatically. Super Admins configure two rules under
**Inbox Settings → Automation**:

- **New / Unassigned chats** — close after N hours of inactivity
- **Assigned chats** — close after N hours of inactivity

Auto-closed tickets behave like any other closed ticket and can be reopened.

[Configure auto-close →](/inbox/inbox-settings)

## Frequently Asked

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="Can I undo a close?">
Click **Reopen**. That opens a successor ticket rather than undoing the close — the closed record
is permanent by design.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Which close option should I use if I am not sure?">
**Mark as Closed**. It records that the ticket is finished without claiming an outcome that did not
happen.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I closed the wrong ticket.">
Reopen it immediately and carry on. The mistaken close stays in the record, along with a system
note showing you reopened it.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do closed tickets still count towards my conversation limit?">
Limits are counted from conversations used, not from open tickets. Closing does not free capacity.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can two people close the same ticket?">
The first close wins. The second person will see the read-only banner.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Best Practices

- Close only when the topic is genuinely finished
- Pick the outcome that is true, not the one that looks best
- Do not reuse an old ticket for a new question — let a new one be created
- Flag the **contact** as spam for noise, and **Block** only for repeat offenders
- Let auto-close handle the tickets nobody will ever answer
