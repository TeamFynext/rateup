Every ticket ends. This page covers how to end one properly, and what happens afterwards.

---

## The Three States

| Status | Meaning | Set by |
|--------|---------|--------|
| **New** | Nobody is assigned yet | Created automatically with the ticket |
| **Assigned** | At least one agent or team owns it | Assigning someone |
| **Closed** | Closed, resolved, unresolved or spam | Using the **Close** menu |

<Callout type="info">
  Status is derived from assignment and closure — there is no status field to maintain separately.
  Assign a ticket and it becomes **Assigned**; close it and it becomes **Closed**.
</Callout>

The green **Close** button sits in the top-right of the chat window, next to the panel toggle.

<Steps>
<Step>
Finish the conversation — make sure the customer has what they need.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Close** in the chat header.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the outcome that describes what actually happened.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Choosing the right outcome

| Option | Use when | Ticket goes to |
|--------|----------|----------------|
| **Mark as Resolved** | The customer's problem was solved | Closed |
| **Mark as Closed** | Finished, with no specific outcome to record | Closed |
| **Mark as Unresolved** | You could not solve it, and want that on record | Closed |
| **Mark as Spam** | Not a genuine customer query | Spam |

<Callout type="info">
  These outcomes feed your reporting. "Resolved" and "Unresolved" are the difference between a
  support team that looks perfect and one that can actually find its weak spots — pick honestly.
</Callout>

## What Closing Changes

<Callout type="warn">
  Closing a ticket **removes all its assignees**. This is intentional: closed work should not sit
  in anyone's queue.
</Callout>

Once a ticket is closed:

- It moves to the **Closed** view (or **Spam**)
- All assignees are cleared
- A system note records **who** closed it and **when**
- The composer is replaced by a banner — the ticket becomes **read-only**
- If it was closed with a result, a green badge appears in the ticket strip and in **Ticket Details**

## Blocking a Contact

**Block contact** sits at the bottom of the **Close** menu. On an already-closed ticket it moves
into the **⋮** overflow menu next to **Reopen**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **Close** menu (or **⋮** on a closed ticket) and choose **Block contact**.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm in the dialog.
</Step>
</Steps>

A blocked contact cannot message you, and you cannot message them.

<Callout type="warn">
  Blocking cuts communication in both directions and applies to the **contact**, not just this
  ticket. For one-off junk, **Mark as Spam** is the right tool.
</Callout>

## Reopening a Ticket

A closed ticket shows **Reopen** where **Close** used to be.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the closed ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Reopen** in the chat header.
</Step>
<Step>
A **new ticket** is created for the same customer, with the previous assignees carried over.
</Step>
</Steps>

### What reopening actually does

| | Original ticket | New ticket |
|---|-----------------|-----------|
| **Number** | Keeps its own | Gets a fresh one |
| **Status** | Stays **Closed** | **Assigned** |
| **Close time and result** | Frozen | Not set |
| **Assignees** | None | Whoever was assigned when it closed |
| **Where it appears** | Closed view, history bar | Open / Mine |

<Callout type="warn">
  Reopening does not revive the old ticket. Its resolution time and outcome stay exactly as they
  were, so your reporting is not rewritten after the fact.
</Callout>

The same happens if you assign somebody to a closed ticket: a successor is opened rather than the
closed one being brought back to life.

## When a Customer Replies After Closure

Nothing to do. Their message creates a **new ticket** in the **New** view, and the closed ticket
stays closed.

This is the whole point of the ticket model: one topic, one ticket, one honest record of how it
went.

## Auto-Close

Tickets that go quiet can be closed automatically. Business Admins configure two rules under
**Inbox Settings → Automation**:

- **New / Unassigned chats** — close after N hours of inactivity
- **Assigned chats** — close after N hours of inactivity

Auto-closed tickets behave like any other closed ticket and can be reopened.

[Configure auto-close →](/inbox/inbox-settings)

## Frequently Asked

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="Can I undo a close?">
Click **Reopen**. That opens a successor ticket rather than undoing the close — the closed record
is permanent by design.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Which close option should I use if I am not sure?">
**Mark as Closed**. It records that the ticket is finished without claiming an outcome that did not
happen.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="I closed the wrong ticket.">
Reopen it immediately and carry on. The mistaken close stays in the record, along with a system
note showing you reopened it.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Do closed tickets still count towards my conversation limit?">
Limits are counted from conversations used, not from open tickets. Closing does not free capacity.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can two people close the same ticket?">
The first close wins. The second person will see the read-only banner.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Best Practices

- Close only when the topic is genuinely finished
- Pick the outcome that is true, not the one that looks best
- Do not reuse an old ticket for a new question — let a new one be created
- Use **Spam** for noise and **Block** only for repeat offenders
- Let auto-close handle the tickets nobody will ever answer
