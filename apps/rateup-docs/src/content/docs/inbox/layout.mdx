The RateUp Inbox is designed to help you manage customer conversations efficiently.  
It is divided into three main sections, each serving a specific purpose.

---

## Inbox Sections Overview

The Inbox layout consists of:

1. Left Panel – Conversation Filters  
2. Middle Panel – Conversation List  
3. Right Panel – Chat Window

---

## Left Panel: Conversation Filters

The left panel helps you organize conversations by status and ownership.

Available filters include:

- **New** – New or unassigned conversations
- **Assigned** – Conversations assigned to team members
- **Mine** – Conversations assigned to you
- **Closed** – Resolved conversations

You can quickly switch between these views to manage your workload.

---

## Middle Panel: Conversation List

The middle panel displays a list of customer conversations.

From here, you can:
- View recent conversations
- Search by **phone number** or **contact name**
- See conversation status and assignment

Clicking a conversation opens it in the chat window.

---

## Right Panel: Chat Window

The chat window shows the selected customer conversation.

You can:
- View message history
- Send replies to customers
- Use quick reply buttons (if available)
- Assign or close the conversation using top controls

This is where most interaction with customers happens.

---

## Navigation Tips

- Use filters to focus on relevant conversations
- Assign conversations to avoid duplicate replies
- Close conversations once they are resolved
