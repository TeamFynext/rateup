The Inbox is split into four regions. This page names every one of them, so the rest of the
documentation can point at things without describing them again.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/layout-annotated.png" alt="The four regions of the Inbox" />

| # | Region | Purpose |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Sidebar** | Choose which tickets you are looking at |
| 2 | **Conversation list** | One row per ticket, with search, filters and sort |
| 3 | **Chat window** | The selected ticket: history, header controls, composer |
| 4 | **Ticket Details** | Everything about the ticket and the customer (collapsible) |

---

## 1 · Sidebar

The sidebar holds the eight Inbox **views**, each with a live count so you can see where work is
waiting without opening anything.

| View | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Open** | Every active ticket |
| **New** | Tickets with no owner yet |
| **Closed** | Finished tickets |
| **All** | Every ticket, any status |
| **Mine** | Tickets assigned to you |
| **@Mentions** | Tickets where a colleague mentioned you |
| **Favourites** | Tickets you have starred |
| **Spam** | Contacts you have flagged as spam — one row each |

Below the views sits the **Filter** group: **Channel**, **Lifecycle**, **Labels**, **Users** and
**Teams**. Each narrows whatever view you are in and stays applied as you move between them.

## 2 · Conversation List

Each row is a **ticket**, not a customer. A customer with three tickets appears three times.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/ticket-row-annotated.png" alt="Anatomy of a ticket row" />

### What a row tells you

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Avatar + channel badge** | The customer and where they wrote from |
| **Star** | The ticket is a favourite. Hover any row to star it |
| **`#1042` + title** | The ticket's number and subject |
| **Message preview** | The most recent message on this ticket |
| **Status pill** | **New**, **Assigned** or **Closed** |
| **@ Mention chip** | A colleague mentioned you on this ticket |
| **Coloured chips** | Contact labels and lifecycle stage |
| **Green badge** | Number of unread messages |
| **Avatar on the right** | Who the ticket is assigned to (`+2` if several) |
| **Amber left edge** | Nobody owns this ticket yet |
| **Person + button** | Assign the ticket to yourself in one click |
| **Red Spam pill** | The contact is flagged as spam — only shown in **Spam** |

A deleted message reads *"This message was deleted"* in the preview, in place of its text.

### Above the list

- The **view name** and the toolbar: search, select, export, refresh, **New**
- A second row with the **count** for the view and the **sort** control

## 3 · Chat Window

The chat window shows the selected **ticket** — only its messages, never the customer's whole
history.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/chat-window-annotated.png" alt="The chat window, top to bottom" />

### Header row

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Customer name and sub-line** | Name, channel, account and phone number |
| **★ Star** | Add or remove this ticket from **Favourites** |
| **@** | Clear your mention on this ticket (only shown when you have one) |
| **Countdown** | Time left in WhatsApp's 24-hour reply window |
| **Status pill** | New, Assigned or Closed |
| **Close / Reopen** | End the ticket, or start a successor once it is closed |
| **Not spam** | Replaces Close / Reopen when the contact is flagged as spam |
| **⋮** | Contact actions on a closed or spam ticket — where the **Close** menu would otherwise host them |
| **Panel icon** | Show or hide the Ticket Details panel |

### Ticket strip

Directly under the header: the ticket icon, `#number`, the ticket title, and — once the ticket has
been closed with an outcome — a green result badge on the right.

### Previous conversations

If the customer has other tickets, an amber bar appears: **"3 previous conversations · last on
Mar 12, 2026"**. Expand it to see each earlier ticket with its number, title, status, assignees
and result, and click any of them to jump straight there.

### Message thread

- Customer messages on the left, your replies on the right
- **Internal notes** appear centred, with a padlock and the author's name
- **System notes** record assignments, closures, reopenings and spam flags
- Delivery ticks show **Sent → Delivered → Read**
- A message deleted on WhatsApp keeps its place, shown as *"This message was deleted"*

### Composer

Two tabs: **Message** (goes to the customer) and **Private Note** (internal only). To their right
sit the quick-replies button and the character counter.

<Callout type="info">
  The composer is replaced by a banner when you cannot reply — because the ticket is unassigned,
  closed, the contact is blocked or flagged as spam, or you are outside WhatsApp's 24-hour window.
</Callout>

## 4 · Ticket Details Panel

Open it with the panel icon at the far right of the chat header.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/ticket-details-full.png" alt="The Ticket Details panel" />

It contains, top to bottom:

- **Ticket #number** and a **copy link** button
- **Assignment** — the owner and team, with **Change**
- **Lifecycle Stage** — the customer's stage
- **Labels** — add or remove contact labels
- **Contact** — name, phone, channel, customer since, consent
- **Ticket Info** — channel, account, created date, close result
- **Title & Description** — editable with the pencil icon
- **Mark contact as spam** — or **Not spam**, if they are already flagged

[Ticket Details in full →](/inbox/ticket-details)

## On Smaller Screens

Below roughly 1024px wide, the list and the chat window take turns instead of sitting side by side.
Opening a ticket fills the screen, and a **back arrow** appears in the header to return to the list.

Narrower still — below roughly 768px — the sidebar is off-screen entirely, so the conversation list
carries what you would otherwise lose: a scrollable **strip of the eight views** with their counts,
and the **Channel** selector. Both are the same controls as the sidebar's, not a reduced version.
