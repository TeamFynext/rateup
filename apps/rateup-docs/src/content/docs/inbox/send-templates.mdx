WhatsApp **templates** are pre-approved messages. They are the only way to write to a customer
once the 24-hour window has closed — and they are useful inside it too.

---

## When You Need a Template

| Situation | Free-form reply | Template |
|-----------|-----------------|----------|
| Customer messaged in the last 24 hours | ✅ | ✅ |
| More than 24 hours since their last message | ❌ | ✅ |
| You are starting the conversation | ❌ | ✅ |

<Callout type="info">
  The countdown in the chat header shows how long the current window has left. When it runs out,
  the composer switches to template-only options automatically.
</Callout>

---

## Send a Template

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket in the Inbox.
</Step>
<Step>
Open the composer menu and choose **Send WhatsApp Template**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick an **approved** template from the list.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in any variables the template requires, and review the preview.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Send**. The message is delivered immediately and appears in the thread.
</Step>
</Steps>

## After the Window Has Closed

When the 24-hour window has expired:

- The normal message box is disabled
- Only **Send WhatsApp Template** remains available
- Only templates approved by WhatsApp can be sent

Once the customer replies to your template:

- A **new 24-hour window** opens automatically
- Free-form replies, quick replies and flows become available again

---

## Inside the Window

Templates still work while the window is open. Use one when you want:

- Consistent, approved wording
- A structured update — order status, appointment reminder
- A message with buttons or a defined layout

Manual replies and templates can sit side by side in the same ticket.

## Templates vs Manual Messages

| | Manual message | Template |
|---|----------------|----------|
| **Availability** | Inside the 24-hour window only | Any time |
| **Approval required** | No | Yes, by WhatsApp |
| **Personalisation** | Full | Limited to variables |
| **Best for** | Live conversation | Re-engagement and structured messages |

---

## Consent

A customer who has not given consent cannot be sent template messages. Their name carries a **DND**
badge in the chat header, and consent is shown in the **Ticket Details** panel.

## Frequently Asked

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="My template is not in the list.">
It is probably not approved yet. Templates must be approved by WhatsApp before they can be sent —
check its status under **WhatsApp → Templates**.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I send a template to reopen a closed ticket?">
Sending any message requires an active ticket. Click **Reopen** first — that opens a successor
ticket — then send the template from there.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Does sending a template open a new 24-hour window?">
No. Only a message **from the customer** opens a window. Your template may prompt one.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

---

## Best Practices

- Use templates for re-engagement, not as a substitute for a real reply
- Keep the content relevant to that specific customer — generic templates get reported as spam
- Get templates approved before you need them, not during an incident
- Watch for the reply, so you can continue in free-form while the window is open
