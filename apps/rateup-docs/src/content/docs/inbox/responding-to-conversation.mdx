This page covers everything about answering a customer: the composer, its limits, and the four
situations where you cannot reply.

---

## Before You Reply

<Steps>
<Step>
**Check the ticket has an owner.** If the composer is replaced by an **Assign to me** bar, take
the ticket first.
</Step>
<Step>
**Read the ticket strip** — the number and title tell you what this ticket is about.
</Step>
<Step>
**Check the history bar.** If the customer has previous conversations, expand it and see what your
team already told them.
</Step>
<Step>
**Check the countdown** in the header on WhatsApp tickets. It shows how long you can still send a
free-form reply.
</Step>
</Steps>

## The Composer

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/composer-annotated.png" alt="The composer and its controls" />

Two tabs decide where your text goes:

| Tab | Delivered to | Colour |
|-----|--------------|--------|
| **Message** | The customer | Blue |
| **Private Note** | Your team only | Amber |

<Callout type="warn">
  Always check which tab is active before sending. The composer changes colour precisely so this is
  hard to get wrong — but it is worth the half-second glance.
</Callout>

## Send a Reply

<Steps>
<Step>
Make sure the **Message** tab is selected.
</Step>
<Step>
Type your reply in the message box.
</Step>
<Step>
Press **Enter**, or click the **send** button.
</Step>
</Steps>

The message appears in the thread immediately and is delivered to the customer.

---

## Character Limit

Customer messages are limited to **1024 characters**.

A counter sits to the right of the composer tabs, showing how much you have used — for example
`184 / 1024`.

| State | What happens |
|-------|--------------|
| Under the limit | Counter shown in grey |
| At the limit | Counter turns **red** and **Send** is disabled |

Shorten the message, or split it into two, to send again.

<Callout type="info">
  Private notes have **no** character limit, so the counter is hidden on that tab.
</Callout>

## Quick Replies

Quick replies are saved answers to questions you get constantly.

Two ways to insert one:

| Method | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Button** | Click the **lightning** icon next to the character counter |
| **Keyboard** | Type `/` in the message box to open the list, and keep typing to filter |

Pick a reply and it is inserted into the composer, where you can edit it before sending.

Quick replies are created under [Inbox Settings](/inbox/inbox-settings).

## When You Cannot Reply

The composer is replaced by a banner in four situations:

| Situation | What you see | How to continue |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Unassigned ticket** | Amber **Assign to me** bar | Take the ticket, or assign it to someone |
| **Closed ticket** | "This chat has been closed by … at …" | Click **Reopen** in the header |
| **Blocked contact** | Blocked-contact banner | Unblock the contact |
| **Contact flagged as spam** | "This contact is marked as spam." | Click **Not spam** in the banner |
| **24-hour window expired** | Template-only options | Send an approved template |
| **Conversation limit reached** | Amber upgrade callout | Upgrade the plan, or wait for the reset |

## The WhatsApp 24-Hour Window

WhatsApp — not RateUp — restricts when you may send free-form messages.

### How it works

- The window opens when the **customer** sends you a message
- It lasts **24 hours** from that message
- The countdown in the chat header shows the time remaining

### Inside the window

- Send free-form replies, attachments and quick replies
- Continue the conversation normally

### Outside the window

- Free-form messaging is disabled
- Only **approved templates** can be sent
- Once the customer replies to your template, a **new 24-hour window** opens and normal replying
  resumes

<Callout type="info">
  This limit applies to WhatsApp only. Other channels have their own rules.
</Callout>

[How to send a template →](/inbox/send-templates)

## Message Status

Outgoing messages show delivery ticks:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Sent** | Accepted by the channel |
| **Delivered** | Reached the customer's device |
| **Read** | Opened by the customer |

Hover a message to see the exact timestamps.

## Deleted Messages

If a WhatsApp user deletes a message they had sent — "delete for everyone" — the message stays in
the thread but its content is withheld. In its place you see, in italics:

> 🚫 This message was deleted

This applies both ways: a customer deleting their own message, and your business deleting a message
it sent from the WhatsApp Business app.

| Where | What it shows |
|-------|---------------|
| The thread | The placeholder, in the same position in the timeline |
| The conversation list preview | *"This message was deleted"* |
| An [exported](/inbox/toolbar) chat | The row is kept; the text is replaced by the placeholder |

<Callout type="info">
  Deletions are reported by WhatsApp only for accounts using the **WhatsApp Business app**
  (coexistence). On a Cloud API-only number, a customer deleting a message on their phone changes
  nothing on your side — you keep seeing what they sent.
</Callout>

A deletion is an edit to history, not a new message: it starts no automations, opens no ticket and
does not touch the 24-hour window.

## Automated vs Manual Messages

A ticket can contain both:

- **Automated messages** come from flows and automation rules, and often carry buttons. They are
  visually distinct from an agent's reply.
- **Manual replies** are sent by people and show the sender's name.
- **System notes** record assignments, closures and reopenings.
- **Private notes** are centred, padlocked, and never leave your team.

## Frequently Asked

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="Why is my Send button greyed out?">
Either you are over the 1024-character limit, or the ticket is unassigned, closed or outside the
24-hour window. The banner above the composer tells you which.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I edit or delete a message I already sent?">
Not from RateUp — sent messages are part of the record here. Send a correction as a follow-up
message. If you delete the message from the WhatsApp Business app instead, RateUp marks it as
deleted in the thread rather than removing the row.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The customer replied but my ticket did not update.">
Click the **refresh** icon in the toolbar. If the reply came after the ticket was closed, it has
opened a **new ticket** — check the **New** view.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I reply to two tickets from the same customer separately?">
Yes, and you should. Each ticket has its own thread and the customer sees one continuous chat.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Best Practices

- Reply fast — the 24-hour window is shorter than it sounds
- Check the history bar before answering a returning customer
- Use quick replies for repeat questions, and personalise the opening line
- Keep long explanations under the character limit, or split them deliberately
- Close the ticket once the topic is done
