This page covers everything about answering a customer: the composer, its limits, and the four
situations where you cannot reply.

---

## Before You Reply

<Steps>
<Step>
**Check the ticket has an owner.** If the composer is replaced by an **Assign to me** bar, take
the ticket first.
</Step>
<Step>
**Read the ticket strip** — the number and title tell you what this ticket is about.
</Step>
<Step>
**Check the history bar.** If the customer has previous conversations, expand it and see what your
team already told them.
</Step>
<Step>
**Check the countdown** in the header on WhatsApp tickets. It shows how long you can still send a
free-form reply.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Instagram Comment Threads

A ticket that starts from an Instagram comment opens with **Commented on your post**, followed by a
**View post** link. It opens the original post on instagram.com in a new tab, so you can read what
the customer is replying to before you answer.

The link only appears when Instagram supplies the post's permalink.

## The Composer

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/composer-annotated.png" alt="The composer and its controls" />

Two tabs decide where your text goes:

| Tab | Delivered to | Colour |
|-----|--------------|--------|
| **Message** | The customer | Blue |
| **Private Note** | Your team only | Amber |

<Callout type="warn">
  Always check which tab is active before sending. The composer changes colour precisely so this is
  hard to get wrong — but it is worth the half-second glance.
</Callout>

## Send a Reply

<Steps>
<Step>
Make sure the **Message** tab is selected.
</Step>
<Step>
Type your reply in the message box.
</Step>
<Step>
Press **Enter**, or click the **send** button.
</Step>
</Steps>

The message appears in the thread the moment you send it, carrying a **clock** instead of a tick. The
clock means RateUp has the message but WhatsApp has not confirmed it yet; it becomes a normal
outgoing message, with delivery ticks, once it does.

This applies to everything you send from the composer — a typed reply, an attachment, a
[template](/inbox/send-templates) drawn as the card you previewed, and a flow shown with its
invitation message and button.

<Callout type="info">
  If a send fails, the bubble is taken back down and an error appears saying so — most often *"Could
  not send the message"*. A send that vanishes without an error has not failed; it has been replaced
  by the confirmed message.
</Callout>

## Character Limit

Customer messages are limited to **1024 characters**.

A counter sits to the right of the composer tabs, showing how much you have used — for example
`184 / 1024`.

| State | What happens |
|-------|--------------|
| Under the limit | Counter shown in grey |
| At the limit | Counter turns **red** and **Send** is disabled |

Shorten the message, or split it into two, to send again.

<Callout type="info">
  Private notes have **no** character limit, so the counter is hidden on that tab.
</Callout>

## Quick Replies

Quick replies are saved answers to questions you get constantly.

Two ways to insert one:

| Method | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Button** | Click the **lightning** icon next to the character counter |
| **Keyboard** | Type `/` in the message box to open the list, and keep typing to filter |

Pick a reply and it is inserted into the composer, where you can edit it before sending.

Quick replies are created under [Inbox Settings](/inbox/inbox-settings).

## The Composer Toolbar

Under the message box sits a row of tools. Which ones you see depends on the channel.

| Tool | What it does | Channels |
|------|--------------|----------|
| 😀 **Emoji** | Insert an emoji | All |
| **Format Message** (T) | Bold, italic, strikethrough, **Clear formatting**, code, quote, bulleted and numbered lists | All |
| **Undo / Redo** | Step back or forward through your edits | All |
| 📎 **Insert Media** | Attach a file — see below | All |
| **Template** | Send an approved WhatsApp template | WhatsApp |
| **Flow** | Send a WhatsApp Flow | WhatsApp |
| **Automation** | Start one of your chatbots for this customer — see below | WhatsApp |
| 🎙️ **Voice note** | Record and send audio | All but Webchat |
| **From** selector | Which of your numbers the reply is sent from — see below | All but Webchat |

On a phone, **Template**, **Flow**, **Automation**, **Undo** and **Redo** fold into a single **⋯**
button.

Formatting is converted to WhatsApp's own markup when the message is sent, so **bold** arrives as
bold on the customer's phone.

### Attachments

**Insert Media** (📎) offers:

| Type | Accepted | Not offered on |
|------|----------|----------------|
| **Document** | PDF, TXT, Word, Excel, PowerPoint | Instagram |
| **Image** | Image files | — |
| **Video** | Video files | — |
| **Audio** | AAC, AMR, MP3, MP4 audio, OGG | — |
| **Contacts** | Share contact cards — pick **Individual** contacts or a whole **Group** | Instagram, Messenger |
| **Location** | Share a pin — drop it on the map, or use your current location | Instagram, Messenger |

You can also **drag files** straight onto the message box.

Attached files appear as thumbnails above the box. Click the red **✕** on one to remove it, or click
an image or video to preview it first. Then click **Send**.

<Callout type="info">
  **Location** uses your browser's location to centre the map. If you have blocked location access
  for RateUp, the picker reads *"Browser location access denied"*. Allow it in your browser's site
  settings, or drop the pin by hand.
</Callout>

### Choose which number you reply from

The **From** selector at the right of the toolbar shows the number or account this reply will be
sent from. It is set automatically to the number **the customer wrote to**, and switches back each
time you open a different ticket.

Only change it if you have a reason to. A reply sent from a number the customer has not written to
has no open 24-hour window, and WhatsApp rejects it. If the customer's number is no longer
connected, the selector is left empty and you must pick one before sending. Sending without one
gives *"Select a account to send the message"*.

On Instagram and Messenger the selector is locked to the account the conversation is on.

### Start a chatbot for this customer

**Automation** (the robot icon) hands the customer to one of your
[WhatsApp chatbots](/whatsapp/chatbots).

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Automation**. The **Initiate Automation** dialog lists your chatbots.
</Step>
<Step>
Select one under **Select an automation**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Send**. The chatbot's first message goes out and it takes the conversation from there.
</Step>
</Steps>

Use it to hand a customer to a self-service flow — booking, order tracking, a feedback survey —
without typing the steps out yourself. With no chatbots set up, the dialog reads *"No Automations
found"*. Automation needs an open 24-hour window, so it is not offered once the window has closed.

## When You Cannot Reply

The composer is replaced by a banner in four situations:

| Situation | What you see | How to continue |
|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| **Unassigned ticket** | *"This conversation is new and unassigned."* with **Assign to me** | Take the ticket, or use **Assign** to give it to someone |
| **Closed ticket** | *"This chat has been closed by … at …. Assign it to start the conversation again."* | Click **Assign to me**, or **Reopen** in the header |
| **Blocked contact** | *"This contact has been blocked by you."* | Click **Unblock contact** |
| **Contact flagged as spam** | *"This contact is marked as spam."* | Click **Not spam** in the banner |
| **WhatsApp 24-hour window expired** | Only a pulsing **+** button | **+ → Send WhatsApp Template** |
| **Instagram / Messenger window expired** | No composer at all | Wait for the customer to write again |
| **Conversation limit reached** | *"Conversation limit reached (used/limit)"* with **Upgrade Plan** | Upgrade the plan, or wait for the reset |

<Callout type="warn">
  Once a WhatsApp window expires, the **Private Note** tab goes too. The whole composer is replaced
  by the template button, so you cannot leave an internal note on that ticket until the customer
  writes again. Write handover notes before the window closes.
</Callout>

## The WhatsApp 24-Hour Window

WhatsApp — not RateUp — restricts when you may send free-form messages.

### How it works

- The window opens when the **customer** sends you a message
- It lasts **24 hours** from that message
- The countdown in the chat header shows the time remaining

### Inside the window

- Send free-form replies, attachments and quick replies
- Continue the conversation normally

### Outside the window

- Free-form messaging is disabled
- The composer shrinks to a single **+** button with a pulsing green dot. It opens a menu with just
  **Send WhatsApp Template**
- Only **approved templates** can be sent
- Once the customer replies to your template, a **new 24-hour window** opens and normal replying
  resumes

<Callout type="info">
  This limit applies to WhatsApp only. Other channels have their own rules.
</Callout>

## Message Status

Outgoing messages show delivery ticks:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Sent** | Accepted by the channel |
| **Delivered** | Reached the customer's device |
| **Read** | Opened by the customer |

Hover a message's time to see the exact timestamps. A message still waiting for WhatsApp shows a
**clock** instead of a tick.

## Working With Messages in the Thread

### Copy a message

Every message except a deleted one has a **Copy** button beside its time. Click it to copy the
text to your clipboard. A *"Copied to clipboard"* notice confirms it.

### React to a customer's message

<Steps>
<Step>
Find the customer's message and click the **emoji** button beside its time.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick an emoji. It is sent to the customer as a reaction on that message.
</Step>
</Steps>

Reactions work on messages the **customer** sent, not on your own. A reaction the customer sends
shows as a small emoji on the bubble. Hover it to see who reacted.

<Callout type="info">
  Instagram and Messenger accept only a limited set of reactions. Picking one they do not support
  gives *"… not supported"*, and nothing is sent.
</Callout>

### Download an image

Every image in a conversation carries a **Download image** button, on the thumbnail and again in
the full-size view. Click the image to open it full size.

### Messages you did not type

| Label on the bubble | Sent by |
|---------------------|---------|
| Your colleague's name and avatar | A person on your team |
| 🤖 **Automation** | A chatbot, flow or automation rule — in a lighter bubble |
| 📣 **Campaign** | A [campaign](/campaign) |
| Centred grey chip | A system note — assigned, reassigned, closed, reopened, spam |
| Centred amber bubble with 🔒 | A private note — never sent to the customer |

## Deleted Messages

If a WhatsApp user deletes a message they had sent — "delete for everyone" — the message stays in
the thread but its content is withheld. In its place you see, in italics:

> 🚫 This message was deleted

This applies both ways: a customer deleting their own message, and your business deleting a message
it sent from the WhatsApp Business app.

| Where | What it shows |
|-------|---------------|
| The thread | The placeholder, in the same position in the timeline |
| The conversation list preview | *"This message was deleted"* |
| An [exported](/inbox/toolbar) chat | The row is kept; the text is replaced by the placeholder |

<Callout type="info">
  Deletions are reported by WhatsApp only for accounts using the **WhatsApp Business app**
  (coexistence). On a Cloud API-only number, a customer deleting a message on their phone changes
  nothing on your side — you keep seeing what they sent.
</Callout>

A deletion is an edit to history, not a new message: it starts no automations, opens no ticket and
does not touch the 24-hour window.

## Frequently Asked

<Accordions type="single">
<Accordion title="Why is my Send button greyed out?">
Either you are over the 1024-character limit, or the ticket is unassigned, closed or outside the
24-hour window. The banner above the composer tells you which.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I edit or delete a message I already sent?">
Not from RateUp — sent messages are part of the record here. Send a correction as a follow-up
message. If you delete the message from the WhatsApp Business app instead, RateUp marks it as
deleted in the thread rather than removing the row.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="The customer replied but my ticket did not update.">
Click the **refresh** icon in the toolbar. If the reply came after the ticket was closed, it has
opened a **new ticket** — check the **New** view.
</Accordion>
<Accordion title="Can I reply to two tickets from the same customer separately?">
Yes, and you should. Each ticket has its own thread and the customer sees one continuous chat.
</Accordion>
</Accordions>

## Best Practices

- Reply fast — the 24-hour window is shorter than it sounds
- Check the history bar before answering a returning customer
- Use quick replies for repeat questions, and personalise the opening line
- Keep long explanations under the character limit, or split them deliberately
- Close the ticket once the topic is done
