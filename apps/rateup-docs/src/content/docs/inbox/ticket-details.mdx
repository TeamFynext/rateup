The **Ticket Details** panel is everything about a ticket and its customer that does not belong in
the message thread.

Open it with the **panel icon** at the far right of the chat header (**Toggle details panel**); close
it with the chevron in the panel's own header. On a wide screen it sits beside the conversation. On
a narrower one it opens over it.

## Panel Header

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Ticket #number** | The ticket's identity |
| **Copy link to ticket** (🔗) | Copies a link to this ticket to your clipboard; the icon briefly reads **Copied** |
| **Collapse Panel** (›) | Hides the panel to give the conversation more room |

## Assignment

Shows the first assignee and their team, with `+N` if there are more, or **Unassigned**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Change**.
</Step>
<Step>
Open the **Assign** picker, expand a team, and tick people — or use **Assign entire team**.
Online / offline status is shown next to each person.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Done** to collapse the section.
</Step>
</Steps>

[Full assignment guide →](/inbox/asigning-conversation)

## Title & Description

The ticket's own words, directly under Assignment because they are what an agent reads first. A
ticket with no title shows **Untitled ticket**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the **pencil** (**Edit title and description**).
</Step>
<Step>
Write a short, specific **Ticket title**. It is what your team reads in the conversation list.
</Step>
<Step>
Add a **Description** for context the messages do not make obvious.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**, or **Cancel** to discard.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Good title | Weak title |
|-----------|------------|
| Refund not received for order 8821 | Question |
| Wrong size delivered — needs exchange | Customer issue |
| Bulk pricing request, 200 units | Follow up |

Hover a title in the conversation list to read its description without opening the ticket.

## Lead Stage

One row showing where this customer sits on your [pipeline](/pipeline) board. It shows the current
column in that column's colour, or **Not on the board**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the stage row to open the picker. It lists every column on the board, each marked as open,
won or lost.
</Step>
<Step>
Click a column. The customer moves there — a lead is opened for them if they had none.
</Step>
</Steps>

To take the customer off the board, open the picker and choose **Remove from the board** at the
bottom, then confirm with **Remove**.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Remove from the board** deletes the lead. Its notes, tasks, timeline and assignees go with it,
  and the confirmation counts each of them first. To record a deal that did not happen, mark it
  [lost](/pipeline/leads#closing-a-lead) instead.
</Callout>

This belongs to the **contact**, so it applies to all of their tickets and shows as a chip on each
of their rows. The section is absent for organisations without the **Pipeline** feature, and on a
board with no columns. Columns are configured in [Pipeline → Stages](/pipeline/stages).

## Conversation Tags

Tags on **this ticket only** — *Bug Report*, *Escalated*, *Refund*.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **+ Add**.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the tags that apply; untick to remove. Or click the **✕** on a tag chip.
</Step>
</Steps>

If no tags exist yet, the picker reads *"No conversation tags yet — create them in Inbox
Settings."* [Tags & labels →](/inbox/conversation-tags)

---

## Contact Labels

Colour-coded labels on the **customer** — VIP, wholesale, churn risk.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **+ Add**.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the labels to apply; untick to remove. Or click the **✕** on a label chip.
</Step>
</Steps>

Labels show on every conversation row for that customer, and filter the Inbox from the sidebar's
**Contact Labels** section.

## Contact

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Name** | Click the **pencil** (**Edit name**), type, then **Save** |
| **Phone** | The customer's number |
| **Customer since** | Month and year the contact was created |
| **Consent** | **Given** or **Not given** |

Renaming here updates the contact everywhere in RateUp — campaigns, contact lists and every one of
their tickets. For other fields, open the contact under [Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts).

<Callout type="warn">
  **Consent** controls whether this customer may receive template messages. A contact without
  consent shows a **DND** badge next to their name in the chat header.
</Callout>

## Ticket Info

Read-only facts about this ticket:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Channel** | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or Webchat |
| **Account** | Which of your numbers or accounts the ticket came in on — marked *(no longer connected)* if you have since disconnected it |
| **Created** | When the ticket was opened |
| **Result** | The outcome it was closed with, if any |

**Account** is read from the ticket's own messages, so it stays correct even if the customer has
since written to a different number of yours.

## Spam and Delete

Two buttons at the foot of the panel.

**Mark contact as spam** — the same action as **Close → Mark contact as spam**. It flags the
**contact**, closes their open tickets and moves them into the **Spam** tab. If the contact is
already flagged, the button reads **Not spam** and takes them back out.
[More about spam →](/inbox/inboxview/spam)

**Delete conversation** — **admins only**. Permanently deletes this ticket and every message in it,
after a confirmation. The contact and their other tickets are untouched.
[More about deleting →](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle#deleting-a-conversation)

## Ticket Fields vs Contact Fields

Knowing which is which saves confusion:

| Belongs to the **ticket** | Belongs to the **contact** |
|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| Title and description | Name, phone, email |
| Assignment | Lead stage and lifecycle |
| Conversation tags | Contact labels |
| Status and result | Consent |
| Created and closed dates | Custom fields |
| Favourite star | Spam and blocked flags |

Change a contact field and it applies to every ticket that customer will ever have. Change a
ticket field and only this ticket is affected.
