The **Ticket Details** panel is everything about a ticket and its customer that does not belong in
the message thread.

Open it with the **panel icon** at the far right of the chat header; close it with the chevron in
the panel's own header.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/ticket-details-full.png" alt="The Ticket Details panel" />

---

## Panel Header

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Ticket #number** | The ticket's identity |
| **Copy link** | Copies a direct link to this ticket — paste it to a colleague and they land here |
| **Chevron** | Collapses the panel to give the conversation more room |

---

## Assignment

Shows the current owner and their team, or **Unassigned**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Change**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a team or an individual — availability is shown next to each person.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Done** to collapse the picker.
</Step>
</Steps>

[Full assignment guide →](/inbox/asigning-conversation)

---

## Lifecycle Stage

The customer's stage in your funnel — lead, customer, churned, and whatever else your organisation
has configured.

Click a stage to set it. This belongs to the **contact**, so it applies to all of their tickets.

Stages are configured under **Contacts → Lifecycle**.

---

## Labels

Labels are colour-coded tags on the **contact** — VIP, refund pending, wholesale, and so on.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **+** in the **Labels** section.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the labels to apply, untick to remove.
</Step>
</Steps>

Labels show on every conversation row for that customer and can be used as a filter in the
toolbar.

---

## Contact

The customer's own details:

- Name and avatar
- Phone number
- Channel
- Customer since
- Consent status

<ScreenShot
  src={`/docs/screenshots/inbox/edit-contact.png`}
  alt="Editing contact details"
  width={420}
/>

Editing here updates the contact everywhere in RateUp — campaigns, contact lists and every one of
their tickets.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Consent** controls whether this customer may receive template messages. A contact without
  consent shows a **DND** badge next to their name in the chat header.
</Callout>

## Ticket Info

Read-only facts about this ticket:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Channel** | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or Webchat |
| **Account** | Which of your numbers or accounts the customer wrote to |
| **Created** | When the ticket was opened |
| **Result** | The outcome it was closed with, if any |

---

## Title & Description

The only fields that belong to **this ticket alone**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the **pencil** icon next to **Title & Description**.
</Step>
<Step>
Write a short, specific title. It is what your team reads in the conversation list.
</Step>
<Step>
Add a description for context the messages do not make obvious.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**, or **Cancel** to discard.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Good title | Weak title |
|-----------|------------|
| Refund not received for order 8821 | Question |
| Wrong size delivered — needs exchange | Customer issue |
| Bulk pricing request, 200 units | Follow up |

## Mark Contact as Spam

At the bottom of the panel — the same action as **Close → Mark contact as spam**. It flags the
**contact**, closes their open tickets and moves them into the **Spam** tab.

If the contact is already flagged, the button reads **Not spam** and takes them back out again.

[More about spam →](/inbox/inboxview/spam)

---

## Ticket Fields vs Contact Fields

Knowing which is which saves confusion:

| Belongs to the **ticket** | Belongs to the **contact** |
|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| Title and description | Name, phone, email |
| Assignment | Lifecycle stage |
| Status and result | Labels |
| Created and closed dates | Consent |
| Favourite star | Custom fields |
| | Spam and blocked flags |

Change a contact field and it applies to every ticket that customer will ever have. Change a
ticket field and only this ticket is affected.
