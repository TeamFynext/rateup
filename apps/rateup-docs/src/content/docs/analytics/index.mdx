**Analytics** turns your conversation history into numbers you can act on: how fast your team
replies, how many conversations resolve, and whether your WhatsApp messages are delivered.

## The Filter Bar

All four views share one filter bar. The range and team you pick stay
put as you move between those four views.

| Control | What it does | Shown on |
|---------|--------------|----------|
| **Date range** | **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 Days** (the default), **Last 30 Days** or **Custom Range** — a two-month calendar | Every view |
| **Team** | One team, or every team | Every view |
| **Channel type** | **Campaign & Inbox**, **External** (your [API](/external-api) sends) or **All** | Overview and Template Delivery |
| **Refresh** | Fetches fresh numbers. The figures are cached for about a minute, so use it when you need the latest | Every view |
| **PDF report** | Downloads a PDF of the current view, named like `rateup-overview-2026-10-09.pdf`. It reads *"Preparing report…"* while it is built | Every view except Message log |

A control a view cannot use is hidden rather than shown and ignored.

<Callout type="info">
  Comparisons only mean something over equal periods. Every tile that shows a change compares the
  range you picked with the same length of time just before it — *"vs previous period"*.
</Callout>

## Who Can See Analytics

The **Analytics** sidebar entry appears only when your team has the **Analytics** feature. Inside
it, your role decides what loads:

| Ability | Lets you see |
|---------|--------------|
| **See analytics** | Overview, Team Performance, Template Delivery and Message log — and the **Analytics** tab on [Inbox → Calls](/analytics/calls) |
| **Export chats and logs** | **Export Excel** on Message log |

The [audit log](/analytics/team-activity) needs **See the audit log** and is under **Settings →
Audit log**.

Abilities come from a member's role, and can be added or removed per person — see
[Members & Roles](/settings/users).
