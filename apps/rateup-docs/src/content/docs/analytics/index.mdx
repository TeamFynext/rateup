## Date Ranges

Every tab shares the same period selector:

- **Today**
- **Last 7 Days**
- **Last 14 Days**
- **Last 30 Days**
- **Last 60 Days**

<Callout type="info">
  Comparisons only mean something over equal periods. A "bad week" against a month-long average is
  a reporting artefact, not a problem.
</Callout>

---

## Exporting

Most tabs support **Export**, producing a PDF of the current view. Tabs that do not will tell you
*"Export not supported for this tab"*.

[Message Logs](/analytics/message-logs) exports raw data rather than a report.

---

## Who Can See Analytics

Analytics requires the **Analytics** feature on your team, plus the *"Access reports and data
insights"* permission on your user.

Without both, the section does not appear in the sidebar.
