**Message Analytics** is about delivery, not conversation. It answers one question: are your
messages actually reaching people?

---

## The Metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Total Messages Sent** | Everything dispatched in the period |
| **Successful Delivery** | Messages that reached the recipient |
| **Failed Messages** | Messages that did not |
| **Avg Delivery Time** | How long delivery took on average |

Alongside these, the standard message states:

**Sent → Delivered → Read**

---

## Filtering by Source

Messages are grouped by where they originated:

| Source | Sent by |
|--------|---------|
| **Campaign** | A bulk campaign send |
| **Template** | A template sent from a conversation |
| **External API** | Your own integration — see [External API](/external-api) |
| **All Sources** | Everything together |

This split matters. Campaign failures and conversation failures have completely different causes.

---

## Why Messages Fail

| Cause | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Invalid or unreachable number | Clean the contact list |
| No consent for template messages | Check consent before campaigning |
| Template not approved | Get approval before sending |
| Outside the 24-hour window with a free-form message | Use a template |
| Rate or quality limits on the account | Reduce volume, improve message relevance |

<Callout type="warn">
  A rising failure rate on campaigns is an early warning about list quality. Repeatedly messaging
  people who do not want it damages your WhatsApp account's quality rating, which is much harder to
  repair than a bad campaign.
</Callout>

---

## Delivered Is Not Read

Delivery only means the message reached the device. **Read** is the number that indicates
attention, and the gap between the two is the honest measure of whether your messages are worth
sending.
