**Template Delivery** is about delivery, not conversation. It answers one question: are your outbound
WhatsApp messages reaching people — by template and team?

## The Delivery Tiles

| Tile | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Messages sent** | Outbound WhatsApp messages in the range, with the change *"vs previous period"* |
| **Delivered** | Share that reached the customer's phone, with the count underneath. **Nothing sent** when there is nothing to measure |
| **Failed** | Messages WhatsApp did not deliver, with their share of sent |
| **Avg delivery time** | How long delivery took on average. Under a second shows as `<1s` |

---

## Narrow by Where Messages Came From

The **Channel type** filter in the bar at the top decides which messages count:

| Option | Counts |
|--------|--------|
| **Campaign & Inbox** | Messages sent from [campaigns](/campaign) and from the Inbox |
| **External** | Messages your own systems sent through the [External API](/external-api) |
| **All** | Both |

This split matters. Campaign failures and API failures have completely different causes.

## The Charts and Tables

| Card | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Delivery trend** | Delivered against failed, per day — or per hour for **Today** and **Yesterday** |
| **Teams** | Messages each team sent, with *"N% delivered"*. Always shows **every** team, whatever the team filter says, because it is the comparison between them |
| **Templates** | Delivery for each WhatsApp template sent in the range |

The **Templates** card opens with a box per category — **Marketing**, **Utility**,
**Authentication** — giving each one's count and its share of template messages. The table below
has:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Template** | The template's name. A red **Failing** badge marks one with a failure rate of 20% or more |
| **Category** | Marketing, Utility or Authentication |
| **Sent** | How many went out |
| **Delivered** | Delivery rate |
| **Failed** | Failure rate |
| **Avg delivery** | Average time to deliver |
| **Last used** | When it was last sent, e.g. *"2 hours ago"* |

With nothing sent, the card reads *"No templates sent in this range"*.

## Why Messages Fail

| Cause | Fix |
|-------|-----|
| Invalid or unreachable number | Clean the contact list |
| No consent for template messages | Check consent before campaigning |
| Template not approved | Get approval before sending |
| Outside the 24-hour window with a free-form message | Use a template |
| Rate or quality limits on the account | Reduce volume, improve message relevance |

To see the actual error on a failed message, open [Message logs](/analytics/message-logs) and
filter by **Failed**.

<Callout type="warn">
  A template marked **Failing** on campaign sends is an early warning about list quality.
  Repeatedly messaging people who do not want it damages your WhatsApp account's quality rating,
  which is much harder to repair than a bad campaign.
</Callout>
