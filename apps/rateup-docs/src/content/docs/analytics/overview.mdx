## See What Is Waiting Right Now

The **Right now** card at the top carries a pulsing **Live** badge and the note *"Not affected by
the date range"*. Its counts are as of this moment:

| Count | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Unassigned** | Tickets nobody owns yet. Always counts every team — with a team picked, it says **All teams** underneath |
| **In progress** | Tickets that have an owner and are still open |
| **Agents online** | Members online now, out of everyone, e.g. `4 / 9` |
| **Resolved to date** | Tickets resolved so far, with the change in resolved rate against last week in points |

The team filter narrows **In progress**, **Agents online** and **Resolved to date**.

---

## Read the Conversation Tiles

The **Conversations** tiles cover the range you picked. Each shows its change *"vs previous
period"* — the same length of time just before your range — coloured green when the move is good
news.

| Tile | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Conversations** | Tickets in the range |
| **First response** | Average time to the first reply of any kind — *"Any reply, including automations"* |
| **Agent response** | Average time to the first reply from a person — *"First reply from a person"* |
| **Resolution rate** | Share of tickets that reached a resolved outcome. Its change is in points |

<Callout type="info">
  **First response** and **Agent response** differ whenever journeys or automations answer first.
  A fast **First response** with a slow **Agent response** means customers get an instant
  automated reply and then wait for a person.
</Callout>

A tile with no replies to measure reads **No replies yet**.

## Read the Delivery Tiles

The **WhatsApp delivery** tiles are the same four that open [Template Delivery](/analytics/template-delivery):
**Messages sent**, **Delivered** (as a percentage, with the count underneath), **Failed** (with its
share of sent) and **Avg delivery time**. They follow the **Channel type** filter.

---

## The Charts

| Chart | Shows |
|-------|-------|
| **Resolved per day** | Tickets closed or resolved each day |
| **Open conversations** | Tickets still waiting on a resolution at the end of each day |
| **Response time** | Average first response, by the day the ticket started — or by hour for **Today** and **Yesterday** |
| **Delivery trend** | Outbound WhatsApp messages per day (or per hour), delivered against failed |

Use **Response time** to find the hours or days when customers wait longest. That is usually a rota
problem rather than a performance problem.

---

## Find Where to Look First

**Needs attention**, at the foot of the page, picks out the worst of the range:

| List | What it picks | Click a row to open |
|------|---------------|---------------------|
| **Slowest first response** | The three agents with the slowest average first response | That agent's page |
| **Highest template failure rate** | The three templates with the highest failure rate, among templates sent at least 20 times | [Template Delivery](/analytics/template-delivery) |

When nothing qualifies, it reads *"Nothing stands out"*.

---

## Read the Overview Well

| Pattern | Likely meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| Rising **Conversations**, steady **Agent response** | The team is keeping up |
| Steady volume, rising **Agent response** | A staffing or process problem |
| **Unassigned** climbing during the day | Nobody is picking up new tickets — check **Agents online** |
| High **Resolution rate**, rising **Open conversations** | Tickets are arriving faster than they are finished |

<Callout type="warn">
  **Resolution rate** is only as honest as your closing habits. If agents close everything as
  **Resolved** regardless of what happened, it tells you nothing. See
  [Closing & Reopening](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle).
</Callout>
