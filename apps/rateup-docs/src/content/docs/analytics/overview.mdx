The **Overview** tab is the daily health check: how much is coming in, how fast it is being
answered, and how much of it is getting finished.

## Conversation Trends

A chart of conversation volume over the period. Use it to find your peaks — the days and weeks when
demand spikes — and to check that staffing follows them.

---

## Response Time Pattern

Average response time across the hours of the day.

This is the most operationally useful chart in Analytics: it shows exactly when customers wait
longest, which is usually a rota problem rather than a performance problem.

---

## Channel Distribution

Where conversations come from — WhatsApp, Instagram, and any other connected channel.

Use it to decide where to invest. A channel producing a tenth of your volume rarely deserves half
your attention.

---

## Resolved Conversation Stats

How conversations ended, drawn from the outcome chosen when each ticket was closed.

<Callout type="warn">
  These numbers are only as honest as your closing habits. If agents close everything as
  **Resolved** regardless of what happened, this chart tells you nothing. See
  [Closing & Reopening](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle).
</Callout>

---

## Reading the Overview Well

- **Rising volume with a stable response time** is a team keeping up
- **Stable volume with a rising response time** is a staffing or process problem
- **A high resolution rate with a rising reopen count** usually means tickets are being closed too
  early
- Check the response-time pattern before adding people — the fix is often the rota
