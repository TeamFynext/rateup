**Team Performance** aggregates the agent metrics by team. It is the view for deciding where to
put people, rather than how any one person is doing.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/analytics/team-performance.png" alt="Team Performance" />

---

## The Metrics

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Team** | The team |
| **Members** | How many people are in it |
| **Conversations** | Tickets the team handled |
| **Avg Response** | The team's average time to reply |
| **Resolution Rate** | Share closed with a resolved outcome |

---

## Load per Head

The number that matters is rarely the raw total — it is conversations divided by members.

A team of eight handling 400 conversations is doing half the work per person of a team of three
handling 300. The totals hide it; the ratio does not.

---

## What This View Answers

| Question | How to read it |
|----------|----------------|
| Which team is stretched? | High conversations per member, rising response time |
| Where should the next hire go? | The team whose response time degrades as volume grows |
| Is routing working? | A team with very low volume may not be receiving what it should |
| Which team needs process help? | Reasonable volume, poor resolution rate |

---

## Before Drawing Conclusions

<Callout type="warn">
  Teams handle different work. A billing team's conversations are longer and slower than a
  first-line support team's by nature. Compare a team against its own history, not against a
  different team.
</Callout>
