The **Teams** tab of **Conversations** groups the numbers by team. It is the view for deciding
where to put people, rather than how any one person is doing.

## The Teams Table

Headed **Teams** — *"Conversations assigned to each team in this range"*. Sort by any column, or use
**Search by name**.

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Team** | The team, with its colour |
| **Members** | How many people are in it, with *"N online"* underneath |
| **Messages sent** | Messages the team sent in the range |
| **Active conversations** | Tickets currently assigned to the team |
| **Avg first response** | The team's average time to a first reply |

With no teams yet, it reads *"No teams yet — Create a team in Settings to see its numbers here."*

---

## Open a Team's Page

<Steps>
<Step>
Click a team's row, or its name.
</Step>
<Step>
The team's page opens with *"N members · N online"* under its name. The back arrow returns to
**Teams**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a range — **Today** to **Last 60 Days**. Like an agent's page, it has its own range.
</Step>
</Steps>

The tiles show **Members** (with how many are online), **Active chats** (*"Being handled now"*),
**Avg first response** and **Resolution rate**, with changes *"vs last week"*.

The **Members** table lists what each member handled in the range: **Agent**, **Status**,
**Messages**, **Avg first response** and **Active chats**. Click a member to open their
[agent page](/analytics/agent-performance#open-an-agents-page).

**PDF report** downloads the page as `rateup-team-<name>-<date>.pdf`.

## Look at Load per Head

The number that matters is rarely the raw total — it is the work divided by **Members**.

A team of eight with 40 **Active conversations** is carrying half the load per person of a team of
three with 30. The totals hide it; the ratio does not.

| Question | How to read it |
|----------|----------------|
| Which team is stretched? | Many active conversations per member, rising response time |
| Where should the next hire go? | The team whose response time degrades as volume grows |
| Is routing working? | A team with very few conversations may not be receiving what it should |

<Callout type="warn">
  Teams handle different work. A billing team's conversations are longer and slower than a
  first-line support team's by nature. Compare a team against its own history, not against a
  different team.
</Callout>
