## The Metrics

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Agent** | The team member |
| **Conversations** | Tickets they handled in the period |
| **Avg Response** | Their average time to reply |
| **Resolution Rate** | Share of their tickets closed with a resolved outcome |
| **Status** | Whether they are online now |

---

## Filtering

Filter by **team** to compare like with like, and change the date range to match the period you
care about. If no agents match, the table shows *"No staff found"*.

---

## Using This Fairly

Agent metrics are easy to misread. A few cautions worth holding onto:

<Callout type="warn">
  **Volume is not productivity.** An agent handling complex escalations will show fewer
  conversations and slower response times than one answering "what are your hours?" all day.
</Callout>

- **Compare within a role**, not across the whole organisation
- **Response time depends on shift** — someone covering quiet hours looks fast for reasons that
  have nothing to do with them
- **Resolution rate depends on closing habits** — an agent who closes honestly will look worse
  than one who marks everything resolved
- Use the numbers to start a conversation, not to end one

---

## What to Actually Look For

| Pattern | Likely meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| One agent far slower than peers on the same shift | Workload, tooling or training |
| Everyone slower at the same hours | A rota gap — see [Response Time Pattern](/analytics/overview) |
| High volume, low resolution rate | Tickets are being touched but not finished |
| Low volume, high resolution rate | Possibly cherry-picking easy tickets |
