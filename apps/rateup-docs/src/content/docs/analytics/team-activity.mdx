**Team Activity** is the live view. Where the other tabs report on a period, this one shows the
present moment.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/analytics/team-activity.png" alt="Team Activity" />

---

## What It Shows

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Members** | Everyone in the selected team |
| **Online indicator** | Who is available now |
| **Status** | What each member is currently doing |
| **LIVE badge** | The view is updating in real time |

Filter by **team** to focus on one group.

---

## When to Use It

| Situation | What it tells you |
|-----------|-------------------|
| A spike in incoming conversations | Who is available to absorb it |
| Before assigning an urgent ticket | Who is actually at their desk |
| At shift handover | Whether the incoming shift has arrived |
| A quiet [New](/inbox/inboxview/newchat) queue that is not being cleared | Whether anyone is online to clear it |

---

## Pairing It With the Inbox

Team Activity tells you *who is available*; the Inbox's
[Users filter](/inbox/inbox-filter) tells you *what they are holding*.

Use them together before reassigning: moving work to someone who is offline just moves the delay.

---

## A Note on Presence

<Callout type="info">
  Online status reflects activity in RateUp, not whether someone is working. Treat it as a signal
  for routing decisions, not as attendance monitoring — teams notice quickly when it is used the
  second way, and they stop leaving the tab open.
</Callout>
