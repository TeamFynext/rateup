**Team activity** is the second tab under **Analytics → Logs**: *"What your team created, changed or
deleted, newest first"*. Use it to answer "who changed this, and when?"

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/analytics/team-activity.png" alt="Team activity log with Action, User, Details and Time columns" />

---

## The Columns

| Column | Contains |
|--------|----------|
| **Action** | What was done, e.g. **Channel added**, **Channel updated**, **Channel removed** |
| **User** | Who did it |
| **Details** | What was sent with the change. Click it to read all of it |
| **Time** | When, to the second |

Sort by **User** or **Time** from their headings. Rows come 25 to a page.

---

## Find a Change

<Steps>
<Step>
Set the **Date range** in the filter bar. The **Team** filter is hidden here — this tab covers the
whole organisation.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a person in the first select (**Everyone** by default).
</Step>
<Step>
Pick an area in the second select (**All features** by default).
</Step>
</Steps>

With nothing to show, it reads *"No activity found"* — *"Nothing matches these filters in this date
range."* when you have filtered, or *"Nothing your team did falls in this date range."* when you
have not.

This tab needs the **See the audit log** ability. It has no PDF report.

---

## When to Use It

| Situation | What to look for |
|-----------|------------------|
| A channel stopped receiving messages | A **Channel updated** or **Channel removed** row, and who made it |
| A setting changed and nobody remembers why | Filter by the feature, then read **Details** |
| Reviewing a new admin's first week | Filter by that person |

<Callout type="info">
  Team activity records changes, not presence. To see who is online right now, use the **Right
  now** card on [Overview](/analytics/overview) or the **Status** column on
  [Conversations → Agents](/analytics/agent-performance).
</Callout>
