A **trigger** is the event that starts a rule. Every automation has exactly one, chosen on the
builder's **Trigger** step — *"What starts it?"* — under **Event**. The same step sets the
**Channels** the rule runs on and the accounts it uses.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/automation/trigger-select.png" alt="The Trigger step with WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger switched on, the Event cards and the Listen on accounts" />

---

## Pick the Trigger on the Trigger Step

<Steps>
<Step>
Under **Channels**, switch on every platform the rule should run on — **WhatsApp**, **Instagram**,
**Messenger**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Event**, pick a card. Each card names the event in plain words and carries a one-line hint.
</Step>
<Step>
For a store event, pick **Which Shopify event** or the **Order status**.
</Step>
<Step>
Tick the accounts under **Listen on** — or **Send from**, for the store events, which only send.
</Step>
</Steps>

The **Event** list shows only the events that every switched-on channel can fire. Switch on
WhatsApp and Instagram together and the events of a single platform — Instagram, Messenger or the WhatsApp ad trigger — and the store events drop out of the list.

## Message and Conversation Triggers

| On the rule card | In the builder | Hint | Channels |
|------------------|----------------|------|----------|
| **Message Received** | **A customer messages you** | *"Reply to keywords, or to any message."* | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Chat Assigned** | **A chat is assigned** | *"Tell the customer who is helping them."* | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Chat Closed** | **A chat is closed** | *"Follow up once your team closes a chat."* | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |

**Message Received** is the workhorse, and the one trigger that **requires a condition** — a
**Message Body** keyword, or **Reply to every message** ticked. See
[Conditions](/automation/conditions). Use **Chat Closed** for follow-ups, such as a satisfaction
survey after resolution.

## Contact Triggers

| On the rule card | In the builder | Hint | Channels |
|------------------|----------------|------|----------|
| **Contact Created** | **A new contact is created** | *"Welcome someone the first time they write."* | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Lead Stage Updated** | **A lead moves to a new stage** | *"React when a lead moves on your board."* | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Lifecycle Updated (legacy)** | **A contact's lifecycle changes (legacy)** | *"Use lead stages for new automations."* | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |

**Lifecycle Updated (legacy)** is listed only on a rule that already uses it. New rules use **Lead
Stage Updated**, which fires when a contact moves to another stage of a [pipeline](/pipeline).

## Store Triggers

| On the rule card | In the builder | Then pick | Fires when |
|------------------|----------------|-----------|-----------|
| **Shopify Event** | **Something happens on Shopify** | **Which Shopify event** | The chosen event happens in your connected Shopify store |
| **WooCommerce Event** | **A WooCommerce order changes status** | **Order status** | An order reaches the chosen status. *"The message goes to the phone number on the order."* |

Store events message the buyer from one account, so they need a **single channel**. With two or
more channels switched on they are missing from the **Event** list; with a store event chosen, the
other channel chips are dimmed with *"Store events send from one channel. Pick a single channel"*.
The account list is headed **Send from** rather than **Listen on**.

Shopify events under **Which Shopify event**:

| Event | Fires on |
|-------|----------|
| **On Orders create** | A new order |
| **On Orders fulfilled** | The whole order fulfilled |
| **On Orders paid** | Payment captured |
| **On Orders cancelled** | An order cancelled |
| **On Draft Orders created** | A draft order created |
| **On orders partially fulfilled** | Part of the order fulfilled |
| **On fulfilled orders cancelled** | A fulfilment cancelled |
| **On abandoned checkouts** | A checkout left untouched — see [Abandoned-Cart Reminders](/automation/abandoned-cart-reminders) |
| **On refund issued** | A refund |
| **On store credit issued** | The part of a refund paid out as store credit |

WooCommerce statuses under **Order status**: **Order processing (paid)**, **Order completed**,
**Order on hold**, **Order cancelled**, **Order refunded**, **Payment failed**.

Both store triggers take an optional **Only for these products (optional)** list on the
**Conditions** step: the rule fires only if the order (or, for abandoned checkouts, the cart)
contains any selected product.

<Callout type="info">
  **Continue** stays disabled until the event is complete: *"Pick a Shopify event to continue"* or
  *"Pick an order status to continue"*.
</Callout>

## Instagram Triggers

Listed only when **Instagram** is the rule's one channel.

| On the rule card | In the builder | Hint |
|------------------|----------------|------|
| **Instagram Comment (Private Reply)** | **Someone comments on your post** | *"Reply privately to comments on your posts."* |
| **Instagram Story Mention** | **Someone mentions you in a story** | *"Thank people who tag you in their story."* |
| **ig.me Link Opened** | **Someone opens your ig.me link** | *"Greet people who arrive from a link you shared."* |

- **Instagram Comment (Private Reply)** fires on comments on your posts, reels, ads or live videos.
  The first message the rule sends arrives as a private reply to the comment. Which comments it
  answers, and any public reply, are set on the next steps.
- **Instagram Story Mention** fires when someone mentions your account in their story.
- **ig.me Link Opened** fires when someone opens a chat from an ig.me link. You name the link on
  the **Conditions** step.

With an Instagram trigger chosen, the **WhatsApp** and **Messenger** chips are dimmed with *"This
event doesn't happen on WhatsApp"* (or Messenger). See
[Comments & Story Mentions](/instagram/automations) for comment sources, specific posts and public
replies.

## Messenger Triggers

Listed only when **Messenger** is the rule's one channel.

| On the rule card | In the builder | Hint |
|------------------|----------------|------|
| **Facebook Page Comment** | **Someone comments on your Page's post** | *"Reply privately in Messenger to comments on your Page's posts."* |
| **m.me Link Opened** | **Someone opens your m.me link** | *"Greet people who open your Page from a link."* |

- **Facebook Page Comment** fires on comments on any post of the Page. The **Conditions** step takes
  **Message Body** keywords on the comment text, or none to answer every comment. The first message
  the rule sends arrives as a private reply in Messenger, so put label and assign actions after it.
  The **Actions** step also offers **Reply in public (optional)**. See
  [Facebook Comments](/messenger/comments).
- **m.me Link Opened** fires when someone opens a chat with your Page from an m.me link. You name
  the link on the **Conditions** step; someone new starts the rule only when they tap the Page's
  Get Started button. See [m.me Links](/messenger/m-me-links).

Taps on the Get Started button, ice breakers and the persistent menu are not triggers of their own:
each sends a keyword that a **Message Received** rule answers — see
[Welcome & Menu](/messenger/welcome-menu).

## WhatsApp Triggers

Listed only when **WhatsApp** is the rule's one channel.

| On the rule card | In the builder | Hint |
|------------------|----------------|------|
| **Came From a Click-to-WhatsApp Ad** | **Someone taps your click-to-WhatsApp ad** | *"Show the products an ad promoted to everyone who taps it."* |

It runs on the first message after someone taps a click-to-WhatsApp ad, before any keyword rule.
On the **Conditions** step, **Ad IDs (optional)** — *"Find an ad's ID in Ads Manager. Press Enter
after each one."* — and **Or ads whose headline or link contains** narrow it to certain ads. *"Leave
both empty to answer every ad. A rule naming an ad goes before one for every ad."* Pair it with
[**Send products to buy**](/automation/actions#send-products-to-buy) to show what the ad promoted.

## Why a Channel Chip Is Dimmed

A chip you cannot switch on is dimmed, and its reason shows under the chips and when you hover it.

| Reason shown | Cause | Fix |
|--------------|-------|-----|
| *"This event doesn't happen on …"* | An event of one platform is chosen — Instagram, Messenger or WhatsApp | Pick another event, or keep the rule on that platform |
| *"Store events send from one channel. Pick a single channel"* | A Shopify or WooCommerce event is chosen | Make a separate rule per channel |
| *"“Send Template” can't run on …"* | An action does not run on that platform — such as a WhatsApp-only action, or **Send Products to Buy** on Messenger | Remove the action — see [Actions](/automation/actions) |
| *"“Trigger Journey” runs on one channel. Remove it, or pick a single channel"* | A journey action is on the rule | Remove it, or keep one channel |

The last channel that is on cannot be switched off — a rule needs at least one. Switching a channel
off removes its accounts from the rule.

<Callout type="warn">
  Changing the **Event** clears what belonged to the old one — the Shopify event and products, the
  WooCommerce status and products, the Instagram post choices, the ig.me and m.me link names and the
  ad IDs — and, for every event except **Message Received** and Instagram and Facebook comments,
  removes the condition groups.
  Check the **Conditions** step again after switching events.
</Callout>

## Choose the Right Trigger

<Callout type="info">
  Pick the **narrowest trigger that covers your case**. A rule triggered on every message and then
  filtered down works, but it is harder to reason about than one triggered on the right event in
  the first place.
</Callout>

| You want to… | Trigger |
|-------------|---------|
| Route incoming questions | Message Received |
| Follow up after resolution | Chat Closed |
| Welcome a new contact | Contact Created |
| React when a lead moves on | Lead Stage Updated |
| React to social activity | Instagram Comment / Story Mention / ig.me Link Opened, Facebook Page Comment |
| Greet people from a link you shared | ig.me Link Opened / m.me Link Opened |
| Answer people who tapped your ad | Came From a Click-to-WhatsApp Ad |
| Confirm or update an order | Shopify Event / WooCommerce Event |
| Win back an abandoned cart | Shopify Event → **On abandoned checkouts** |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| An event is missing from the **Event** list | Not every switched-on channel can fire it, or it is a store event on a multi-channel rule | Switch the other channels off |
| **Continue** says *"Tick a … account, or turn … off"* | A channel is on with no account ticked | Tick an account for it, or switch it off |
| **Continue** says *"Tick at least one account to continue"* | No account is ticked | Tick one under **Listen on** or **Send from** |
