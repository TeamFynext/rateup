**Actions** are what a rule does when it fires. They are set on the builder's **Actions** step —
*"What should it do?"* — and *"Actions run in order, top to bottom."* A rule needs at least one.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/automation/actions.png" alt="The Actions step with three numbered action cards: assign to a team, reply, and label" />

---

## Add and Order Actions

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add action** and pick from the menu. Actions are grouped under **Reply to the customer** and
**Behind the scenes**.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in the card that opens — a team, a label, a template.
</Step>
<Step>
Drag a card by its handle to change the order. Click a folded card to open it again.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each folded card reads as a line of the rule — *assign to Support*, *label it “VIP”*. An unfinished
one reads in grey italics, such as *assign to a team …*.

| Message next to **Continue** | Cause |
|------------------------------|-------|
| *"Add at least one action"* | The rule has no actions — the list shows **No actions yet** |
| *"Two actions are the same — remove one"* | The same action with the same value is on the rule twice |
| *"Finish the “…” action"* | A card is missing its value |

## Actions and Channels

The **Add action** menu offers only the actions every switched-on channel can run.

| Action | Name in messages | Channels |
|--------|------------------|----------|
| **Send a message** | Send Message | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Send products to buy** | Send Products to Buy | WhatsApp, Instagram |
| **Send a template** | Send Template | WhatsApp only |
| **Send a flow** | Send Flow | WhatsApp only |
| **Send a web survey** | Send Web Survey | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Start a journey** | Trigger Journey | Any one channel — not on a rule with two or more |
| **Assign to a team** | Assign Team | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Assign to a teammate** | Assign User | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Add a label** | Attach Label | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Move to a lead stage** | Assign Lead Stage | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Close the chat** | Close Chat | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |
| **Call an external API** | Call External API | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger |

Adding an action also limits the channels. With **Send a template** on the rule, the
**Instagram** and **Messenger** chips on the **Trigger** step are dimmed with *"“Send Template”
can't run on Instagram or Messenger"*; with **Send products to buy**, the **Messenger** chip reads
*"“Send Products to Buy” can't run on Messenger"*. With **Start a journey** on it, every other chip is dimmed
with *"“Trigger Journey” runs on one channel. Remove it, or pick a single channel"*. Remove the
action to free the channel.

## Reply to the Customer

| Action | Sends |
|--------|-------|
| **Send a message** | A plain message — *"Write the reply your customer will get"* |
| **Send products to buy** | Products from your [store](/commerce) — see [below](#send-products-to-buy) |
| **Send a template** | An approved [template](/whatsapp/templates) |
| **Send a flow** | A [WhatsApp flow](/whatsapp/flows) |
| **Send a web survey** | A [survey](/surveys), listed as *survey name (team name)* |
| **Start a journey** | Hands the conversation to a [journey](/automation/journeys). **Select journey** lists only the journeys of the rule's channel, newest first — see [Instagram](/instagram/journeys) and [Messenger](/messenger/journeys) for what those channels add. A journey switched off on the Journeys list is not started |

Bold, italic and other formatting in **Send a message** work on WhatsApp. On a rule that also runs
on Instagram or Messenger, *"Instagram and Messenger get the same words as plain text."*

### Fill a Template From the Event

Once you pick a template, **Variable Mapping** lists its variables — **Variable `{{1}}`**, with the
template's sample value beside it. Map each one to a field:

| Rule | Prompt | Pick from |
|------|--------|-----------|
| Shopify | *"Map your WhatsApp template variables to Shopify data."* | **Select Shopify Field** |
| WooCommerce | *"Map your WhatsApp template variables to WooCommerce order data."* | **Select WooCommerce Field** |
| Any other | *"Map your WhatsApp template variables to contact fields."* | **Select Contact Field** |

Anything left unmapped falls back to the template's sample value.

| Option | Shown when | Does |
|--------|------------|------|
| **Button “…” link ending** | The template has a URL button with a variable | Fills the `{{1}}` at the end of the button's address. Left empty, it uses the template's sample value |
| **Use Shopify product image as header** | A Shopify or WooCommerce rule, and a template with an image header | Swaps the template's header image for the ordered product's image |

On a Shopify rule, pick **Checkout Recovery Link — button code** for the button to send a tracked
cart link — see [Abandoned-Cart Reminders](/automation/abandoned-cart-reminders).

<Callout type="warn">
  Outside WhatsApp's 24-hour window, only **Send a template** will actually deliver. A **Send a
  message** action on a stale conversation fails silently — check
  [Message Analytics](/analytics/template-delivery) if replies are not arriving.
</Callout>

### Send Products to Buy

**Send products to buy** sends up to **10** products from your [store](/commerce). Click **Choose
products**, tick them in the picker — *"Up to 10 products per reply."* — and click **Use … products**.
**Message (optional)** adds up to 600 characters of your own above them.

| Rule runs on | What the customer gets |
|--------------|------------------------|
| WhatsApp | *"Sent as catalog cards the customer can add to a cart from. Products must be live in your WhatsApp catalog."* |
| Instagram | One message listing the products with a link — *"One product with one option gets a direct checkout link. More than that links to your storefront."* |

On Instagram the action also offers **Limited drop** — see
[Sell from a Comment](/instagram/automations#sell-from-a-comment). Pair it with an Instagram comment
rule for comment-to-buy, or with **Came From a Click-to-WhatsApp Ad** to show what an ad promoted.
The template gallery has **Comment to buy**, **Live drop** and **Ad to products** ready-made.

<Callout type="warn">
  The action sends nothing if Commerce is not set up, or if none of its products is still for sale.
  On Instagram with several products, turn the storefront on in **Commerce → Settings**, or the reply
  can only list the products without a link.
</Callout>

### Reply in Public to a Comment

On an **Instagram Comment (Private Reply)** or **Facebook Page Comment** rule, the Actions step also
shows **Reply in public (optional)**: *"Posted under the comment so others see you answered. One is picked at random each
time, so add a few."* Click **Add a public reply**, then **Add a variation** — up to 10, each up to
2,200 characters. See [Comments & Story Mentions](/instagram/automations) and
[Facebook Comments](/messenger/comments).

## Behind the Scenes

| Action | Does |
|--------|------|
| **Assign to a team** | Gives the ticket to a team — anyone in it can pick it up |
| **Assign to a teammate** | Gives it to a specific person |
| **Add a label** | Adds a label to the contact |
| **Move to a lead stage** | Puts the contact on a column of your Leads [pipeline](/pipelines) board, opening a lead if they are not on it yet. A **Won** or **Lost** column is marked *· closes as won* / *· closes as lost* |
| **Close the chat** | Closes the ticket as **Resolved**, **Closed** or **Unresolved**. **Spam** flags the contact as spam instead, which moves them to the [Spam](/inbox/inboxview/spam-contacts) tab |
| **Call an external API** | Sends a `POST` with a JSON body to a full URL you enter |

Prefer **Assign to a team** over **Assign to a teammate** in automation. Team routing keeps working
when someone is on leave; individual routing quietly piles up in an absent person's queue.

Labels and lead stages make later filtering and reporting possible. A rule that labels every
refund enquiry costs nothing and makes "how many refund requests last month?" answerable.

**Move to a lead stage** needs a pipeline: without one the card reads *"No pipeline columns yet —
create a pipeline before using this action."*

<Callout type="info">
  **Set lifecycle (legacy)** — *Assign Lifecycle (legacy)* — is kept only on rules that already use
  it, so they keep running and can still be saved. It is not in the **Add action** menu; new rules
  use **Move to a lead stage** — see [Leads that open themselves](/pipelines/automatic-leads).
</Callout>

### Call Your Own Systems

With **Call an external API** on the rule, an **Example API request** box shows the shape of the
call. A value that is not a full URL is rejected: *"Enter a valid full URL (e.g.
https://example.com/webhook)"*.

Use it to notify your own systems — create a record in your CRM, trigger a fulfilment job, post to
a team channel. See [Integrations](/settings/integrations) for webhook setup and payloads.

## Combine Actions

Actions run in order, so a single rule can do a complete piece of work. The **Review** step reads it
back as one sentence:

> When a customer messages you and it mentions “refund”, label it “Refund”, assign to Billing and
> send the refund_received template.

## Keep Automations Safe

- **Start narrow.** One keyword, one account, then widen.
- **Avoid loops.** A rule that sends a message on message-received can re-trigger itself if the
  conditions are too broad.
- **Do not auto-close on a customer message.** Closing what someone just wrote in about is the
  fastest way to lose them.
- **Name your rules properly.** The **Name** on the Review step is all you see on the rule card six
  months later.
- **Review quarterly.** Rules outlive the campaigns that justified them.
