An abandoned-cart reminder is an [automation rule](/automation) on the Shopify event **On abandoned
checkouts**. You choose how long a checkout sits untouched before the WhatsApp message goes out, and
RateUp stops the reminder the moment the order comes through. The **Results** tab on the Shopify
integration then shows the orders and revenue those reminders recovered.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/automation/abandoned-cart-rule.png" alt="The Conditions step of an abandoned-checkout rule: Send after, Only buyers who accepted marketing, and Only for these products (optional)" />

## Before You Start

| You need | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Shopify connected under [Integrations](/settings/integrations) | The checkouts come from your store's webhooks |
| An approved **Marketing** [template](/whatsapp/templates) | WhatsApp treats cart reminders as marketing; outside the 24-hour window only a template delivers |
| The buyer's phone number on the checkout | A checkout with no phone number is skipped |

## Set Up a Reminder

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Automation → Rules** and click **Create rule**. On **Template**, pick **Abandoned cart
reminder** (marked **Needs Shopify**), or **Start from scratch**.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Trigger**, keep **WhatsApp** under **Channels**, choose the **Shopify Event** event and
**On abandoned checkouts** under **Which Shopify event**, then tick the number under **Send from**.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Conditions**, set **Send after** — **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **3 hours**, **12 hours**, **1 day**,
**3 days**, **7 days**, or **Custom…** for any number of minutes, hours or days. Leave **Only buyers
who accepted marketing** ticked.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally narrow it under **Only for these products (optional)** — *"The rule triggers if the
cart contains any selected product."* Click **Add product**, and the refresh button if a new product is missing.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Actions**, add **Send Template**, pick your template and map its variables. Map the link to
**Checkout Recovery Link** — see [Track the Link](#track-the-link) below.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Review**, name the rule — *"Cart reminder – 1 hour"* — and click **Activate automation**.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Follow Up With a Second Rule

One rule sends one reminder. For a follow-up, create another rule with a longer **Send after** —
the app suggests *1 hour, 1 day and 3 days*. Each rule reminds each checkout at most once.

## When a Reminder Goes Out

| Rule | Behaviour |
|------|-----------|
| The clock | **Send after** counts from the buyer's **last change** to the checkout. Editing the cart pushes a waiting reminder back, so nobody is messaged mid-checkout |
| Limits | Between **15 minutes** and **7 days**. A custom value outside that is rejected — *"Send after at least 15 minutes"* / *"Send after 7 days at most"* |
| The order comes through | Every waiting reminder for that checkout is cancelled |
| The cart changes | A reminder whose product filter no longer matches the cart is cancelled |
| Shopify drops the checkout | Its reminders are cancelled |
| Already sent | An updated checkout is never reminded twice by the same rule |
| **Only during business hours** | A reminder due outside the hours is pushed back in 30-minute steps until they open |

A reminder is **skipped** — not sent — when the checkout has no phone number, when **Only buyers who
accepted marketing** is on and the buyer did not tick Shopify's marketing box, or when the contact
has opted out.

<Callout type="warn">
  Unticking **Only buyers who accepted marketing** messages people who never agreed to marketing.
  Meta allows marketing messages only to people who opted in, and complaints from those who did not
  lower your number's quality rating and sending limits.
</Callout>

Checkouts nobody returns to expire after eight days. Their personal details are deleted later, but
the results of any reminder sent for them are kept, so revenue reports outlive the customer data.

## Track the Link

A reminder only counts as recovering an order if the buyer came back after it was sent. To also see
who **opened** the cart, the link has to go through RateUp's tracked address.

| Where the link sits | What to map |
|---------------------|-------------|
| In the message body | **Checkout Recovery Link** — sent as the tracked link automatically |
| On a URL button | Approve the button with the tracked-link address the builder shows, ending in `{{1}}`, then set **Button "…" link ending** to **Checkout Recovery Link — button code** |

The builder spells out the exact address to approve under **Button "…" link ending**. Tracked links
are tagged `utm_source=rateup` and `utm_medium=whatsapp`, with the rule name as the campaign, so
Shopify's own marketing reports credit WhatsApp as well.

<Callout type="info">
  A URL button's address is fixed when Meta approves the template. If the button was approved with
  your shop's own address, it cannot carry the tracked link — clicks will not be counted. Submit a
  new template with the tracked-link address.
</Callout>

## See What the Reminders Recovered

Two places show results:

| Where | Shows |
|-------|-------|
| The rule's card on the **Automation → Rules** list | Revenue and orders recovered in the last 30 days, or how many reminders were sent |
| **Settings → Integrations → Shopify → Results** | The full report below |

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/automation/shopify-results.png" alt="Shopify Results tab: stat tiles, reminder funnel, revenue by rule and revenue per day" />

The Results tab opens first on the Shopify page. Pick **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Last
90 days**; everything counts reminders **sent** in that period.

| Figure | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Revenue recovered** | Value of orders completed after a reminder, per currency |
| **Orders recovered** | How many — with a note such as *"2 more came back without a reminder"* for orders that needed no nudge |
| **Recovery rate** | Recovered orders as a share of reminders sent |
| **Link click rate** | Share of reminders whose tracked link was opened — and how many reminders failed to send |
| **Reminder funnel** | **Sent → Delivered → Read → Opened the cart link → Completed the order** |
| **Revenue by rule** | Which rule brought each order back, with *"3 of 40 recovered"* per rule |
| **Revenue recovered per day** | By the day the reminder was sent |

An order is credited to the **last reminder sent before it was placed**. A checkout completed with
no reminder sent gets no credit — the buyer came back on their own.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"No reminders sent in this period"* | No **On abandoned checkouts** rule is active, or none came due | Check the rule's switch and its **Send after** |
| Reminders sent to almost nobody | Most buyers did not accept marketing | Expected; do not untick the consent box to compensate |
| **Link click rate** stays at zero | The link is not the tracked one | Map **Checkout Recovery Link**, or approve a button with the tracked address |
