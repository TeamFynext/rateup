Since October 2026 an automation is built one step at a time. A step rail across the top reads
**Basics → When → If → Then → Review**, and each step has to pass its own checks before **Next**
takes you on.

## Build a Rule Step by Step

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Automation** and click **Create Automation**. The header reads **Automations / New
automation** until you give it a name.
</Step>
<Step>
**Basics — *Name and channels*.** Pick the **Platform** (**WhatsApp**, **Instagram** or
**Messenger**), type a **Rule Name** and choose one or more **Channels** — the numbers, Instagram
accounts or Pages the rule runs on.
</Step>
<Step>
**When — *When should this run?*** Choose the **Trigger Event**. A **Shopify Event** asks for the
**Shopify Trigger Condition**, and a **WooCommerce Event** for the **WooCommerce order status**. See
[Triggers](/automation/triggers).
</Step>
<Step>
**If — *Only if…*.** Add the conditions that must match. What appears here depends on the trigger:
condition blocks for messages and contacts, post pickers for Instagram comments, a product filter and
reminder timing for Shopify. See [Conditions](/automation/conditions).
</Step>
<Step>
**Then — *Then do this*.** Add actions with **Add action**. They run in the order listed. See
[Actions](/automation/actions).
</Step>
<Step>
**Review — *Review and save*.** Read the one-line summary — *"Welcome — when message received, if
1 condition match, then 2 actions"* — set any limits, and click **Create Automation**.
</Step>
</Steps>

After the first save you land on the saved rule. A new rule is saved switched on.

## What Each Step Checks

**Next** validates only the step you are on. If something is wrong, the field is marked and a toast
names the first problem.

| Step | Blocks you when |
|------|-----------------|
| **Basics** | *"Name is required"*, or *"Select at least one account"* |
| **When** | *"Select a Shopify event"* or *"Select a WooCommerce order status"* for those triggers |
| **If** | *"At least one condition is required for Message Received trigger"*, *"Duplicate conditions are not allowed"*, *"Add at least one post ID"* for specific Instagram posts |
| **Then** | *"At least one action is required"*, *"Duplicate actions are not allowed"*, an empty value, or an invalid URL for **Call External API** |

Changing the **Platform** on the Basics step resets the channels, the trigger, the conditions and any
Shopify, WooCommerce or Instagram settings — pick the platform first.

<Callout type="info">
  Pressing **Enter** in a text field moves to the next step rather than saving, so a half-built rule
  is never saved by accident.
</Callout>

## Set Limits on the Review Step

| Option | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Send only once per day per contact** | *"Prevents duplicate messages to the same contact within 24 hours."* The app notes it applies only to the chatbot action |
| **Only during business hours** | The rule fires only inside the hours you pick under **Select Business Hours** |

Business hours are the schedules defined on [Settings → Account](/settings/account).

## Edit a Saved Rule

Click a rule's card to open it. On a saved rule every step is unlocked, so you can jump straight to
the one you want from the step rail, and **Update Automation** is available on every step — you do
not have to walk to Review again.

If a save fails validation, the builder jumps to the step holding the problem and shows the message.

## Share a Keyword Rule as a Link

A saved **Message Received** rule with at least one keyword gets a second tab in its header:
**Builder** and **Share**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **Share** tab.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the account under **Select Account** and the keyword under **Select Keyword**.
</Step>
<Step>
Copy the **Share Link**. Opening it starts a chat with the keyword already typed — a `wa.me` link on
WhatsApp, an `m.me` link on Messenger, an Instagram link on Instagram.
</Step>
</Steps>

Put the link on a poster, in a bio or behind a QR code, and the customer only has to press send for
the rule to fire.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **Next** does nothing but show a toast | The current step has an error | Read the toast; the field is marked in red |
| No **Share** tab | The rule is unsaved, not **Message Received**, or has no keyword | Save it with a **Message Body** keyword |
| Channels emptied themselves | You changed the **Platform** | Pick the platform first, then the channels |
| The rule never fires | It is **Inactive**, or the conditions cannot match | Check the switch on its card, then the **If** step |
