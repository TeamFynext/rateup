An automation is built one step at a time. A step rail across the top reads
**Template → Trigger → Conditions → Actions → Review**, and **Continue** stays disabled until the
step you are on is complete — the reason is written next to it.

## Build a Rule Step by Step

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Automation** and click **Create Automation**. The header reads **Automations / New
automation** until you give it a name.
</Step>
<Step>
**Template — *"Start from a template"*.** Pick the closest template, or click **Start from
scratch**. Filter the gallery by **Auto-replies**, **Routing**, **Pipeline**, **Labels**, **Store
orders** or **Instagram**. A template fills in the trigger, conditions and actions; you still pick
the team, label or WhatsApp template it should use. Some are marked **Needs Shopify**, **Needs
WooCommerce** or **Needs Instagram**.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/automation/template-gallery.png" alt="The Template step: category filters, Start from scratch and the template cards" />
</Step>
<Step>
**Trigger — *"What starts it?"*** Under **Channels**, switch on every platform the rule should run
on — **WhatsApp**, **Instagram** and **Messenger** can all be on at once. Choose the **Event**, then
tick the accounts under **Listen on** (or **Send from**, for events that only send). A **Shopify
Event** asks **Which Shopify event**, and a **WooCommerce Event** asks for the **Order status**. See
[Triggers](/automation/triggers).
</Step>
<Step>
**Conditions.** Add what must match. The heading follows the trigger — *"Which messages should it
reply to?"* for **Message Received**, *"Which comments should it answer?"* for Instagram and Facebook comments,
*"Only when…"* otherwise. See [Conditions](/automation/conditions).
</Step>
<Step>
**Actions — *"What should it do?"*** Add actions; they run in order, top to bottom. See
[Actions](/automation/actions).
</Step>
<Step>
**Review — *"Review and switch it on"*.** Give the rule a **Name**, read the sentence that describes
it — click any coloured part to jump back and change it — set any **Limits**, and click **Activate
automation**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Opening the builder from a template card on the Automation page skips the Template step and starts
on **Trigger**. A new rule is saved switched on.

## One Rule on Several Channels

A rule can listen on WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger at the same time and reply on each. Switch
the platforms on under **Channels**, then tick at least one account for each.

| What you see | Meaning |
|--------------|---------|
| A channel chip is dimmed | The chosen event or one of the actions does not run on that platform. Hover it for the reason |
| You cannot switch the last channel off | A rule needs at least one channel |
| Accounts disappear when you switch a channel off | That platform's accounts are removed from the rule |
| *"Tick a … account, or turn … off"* | A channel is on with no account ticked for it |

## Watch It in the Phone Preview

The **Preview** button in the header opens the **Device Preview** panel on the right; it opens
by itself when the builder loads. It plays the rule as the customer will see it: what they send, then what
comes back. Actions the customer never sees — labels, assignments, webhooks — are listed under the
conversation.

When a rule runs on more than one channel, a switch above the phone picks which app it shows. The
same switch drives the **Share** tab, so the phone and the share link always show the same
platform.

## What Each Step Checks

**Continue** checks only the step you are on. While it is disabled, the reason sits next to it:

| Step | Blocks you when |
|------|-----------------|
| **Template** | *"Pick a template, or start from scratch"* |
| **Trigger** | *"Tick at least one account to continue"*, *"Pick a Shopify event to continue"*, *"Pick an order status to continue"*, or a channel with no account |
| **Conditions** | *"Add at least one condition — without one it would answer every message"*, *"Remove the repeated condition"*, *"Fill in every condition"*, *"Pick at least one post"* |
| **Actions** | *"Add at least one action"*, *"Two actions are the same — remove one"*, or *"Finish the “…” action"* |
| **Review** | *"Give it a name to switch it on"*, or *"Pick which business hours"* |

Changing the **Event** clears the settings that belonged to the old one — Shopify and WooCommerce
events and products, Instagram post choices, the ig.me and m.me link names and the ad IDs — so a
hidden value is never saved.

<Callout type="info">
  Pressing **Enter** in a text field moves to the next step rather than saving, so a half-built rule
  is never saved by accident.
</Callout>

## Set Limits on the Review Step

| Option | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Run once a day per contact** | *"Skips anyone this automation already ran for in the last 24 hours."* |
| **Only during business hours** | The rule fires only inside the hours you pick under **Business hours** |

Business hours are the schedules defined on [Settings → Account](/settings/account).

## Edit a Saved Rule

Click a rule's card to open it. A saved rule opens on **Trigger** with every step unlocked, so you
can jump straight to the one you want from the step rail, and **Save changes** is available on every
step — you do not have to walk to Review again.

If a save fails validation, the builder jumps to the step holding the problem and shows the message.

## Share a Keyword Rule as a Link

A saved **Message Received** rule with at least one keyword gets a second tab in its header:
**Builder** and **Share**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **Share** tab and pick the **Keyword**.
</Step>
<Step>
If the rule runs on more than one channel, pick the platform tab — **WhatsApp**, **Instagram** or
**Messenger**. Each has its own **Account**, **Share Link** and QR code.
</Step>
<Step>
Copy the **Share Link**, or download the QR code.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Platform | Link | What the customer does |
|----------|------|------------------------|
| **WhatsApp** | `wa.me/<number>?text=<keyword>` | Presses send — the keyword is already typed |
| **Instagram** | `ig.me/m/<username>` | Types the keyword themselves |
| **Messenger** | `m.me/<page id>` | Types the keyword themselves |

<Callout type="warn">
  Instagram and Messenger links cannot pre-fill a message: they only open the chat. Print the
  keyword next to the link or QR code — *"Send REFUND to start"* — or the rule never fires.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **Continue** is disabled | The current step is incomplete | Read the message next to the button |
| No **Share** tab | The rule is unsaved, not **Message Received**, or has no keyword | Save it with a **Message Body** keyword |
| A channel chip cannot be switched on | The event or an action does not run on that platform | Hover the chip for the reason |
| Instagram or Messenger link opens an empty chat | Those apps cannot pre-fill text | Tell customers which keyword to send |
| The rule never fires | It is **Inactive**, or the conditions cannot match | Check the switch on its card, then the **Conditions** step |
