The **WhatsApp** section is where you build what customers experience on WhatsApp: the messages you
send them, the forms they fill in and the suggestions they see before they type.

Conversations themselves happen in the [Inbox](/inbox); this section is what feeds it.

There is no separate **WhatsApp** entry in the sidebar. Its pages sit in the **WhatsApp** section of
the **Meta** group. Journeys, including WhatsApp ones, are under **Automation → Journeys**:

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/meta-panel.png" alt="The Meta sidebar panel: a WhatsApp section with Templates, Flows and Ice Breakers, and an Instagram & Messenger section with Instagram and Messenger" />

| Sidebar entry | Opens |
|---------------|-------|
| **Meta → WhatsApp → Templates** | [Message Templates](/whatsapp/templates) — `/dashboard/whatsapp/templates` |
| **Meta → WhatsApp → Flows** | [WhatsApp Flows](/whatsapp/flows) — `/dashboard/whatsapp/flows` |
| **Meta → WhatsApp → Ice Breakers** | [Ice Breakers & Commands](/whatsapp/ice-breakers) — `/dashboard/whatsapp/ice-breakers` |
| **Automation → Journeys** | [Journeys](/automation/journeys) — `/dashboard/automation/journeys?channel=whatsapp` shows only WhatsApp ones |

Each entry appears only if your team has that feature: **Templates** and **Flows** follow their own
abilities, and **Ice Breakers** shows if you have either of them or Automation Rules. The **Meta**
group's other section, **Instagram & Messenger**, holds [Instagram](/instagram) and
[Messenger](/messenger). Number settings are under [Settings → Channels](/settings/channels).

## How the Pieces Fit

```
Customer opens your WhatsApp
        │
        ├── sees ice breakers / commands   → Ice Breakers & Commands
        ├── talks to a journey             → Automation → Journeys
        ├── fills in a form                → WhatsApp Flows
        ├── browses products               → Catalog  → Commerce
        └── anything else                  → Inbox    (a human replies)

You message them first                     → Templates → Campaigns
```

---

## Before You Start

Everything here requires a connected **WhatsApp Business account** — see
[Channels](/settings/channels).

Templates additionally require **approval by WhatsApp** before they can be used, so build them
before you need them, not during a rush.

---

## The 24-Hour Rule

WhatsApp lets you send free-form messages only within **24 hours** of the customer's last message.
Outside that window, only approved templates go through.

This single rule shapes most of how the section is used: templates for re-engagement, everything
else for live conversation.

[More on the 24-hour window →](/inbox/responding-to-conversation)
