## Creating a Chatbot

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create Chatbot** and enter a name.
</Step>
<Step>
Build the conversation as a series of steps — a message, then the replies the customer can choose.
</Step>
<Step>
Attach a **flow** or **template** to a step where you need structured input or approved wording.
</Step>
<Step>
Save.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## What a Step Can Do

| Element | Behaviour |
|---------|-----------|
| **Message** | Sends text |
| **Button** | Offers a choice that moves the conversation on |
| **Document** | Sends a file |
| **Open link** | Sends the customer to a URL |
| **Flow** | Opens a [flow](/whatsapp/flows) for structured input |
| **Restart conversation** | Returns the customer to the start |

---

## Testing Before You Publish

The preview runs the bot as a customer would experience it — *Start*, *Type your reply…*,
*Waiting for a reply above…*.

Walk it end to end and check:

- Every button leads somewhere real
- No path dead-ends without an option
- The customer can always reach a human
- The wording sounds like your brand

<Callout type="warn">
  The single worst chatbot failure is the loop with no exit. Before publishing, try deliberately
  giving unexpected answers and confirm you can still get out.
</Callout>

---

## Handover to an Agent

When the bot cannot help, the conversation becomes a normal ticket in the [Inbox](/inbox). The
bot's messages are already in the thread, so the agent has the context and the customer does not
repeat themselves.

---

## Managing Chatbots

| Action | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Clone** | Duplicates as *"Copy of …"* — the safe way to change a live bot |
| **Search** | Find a bot by name |
| **Delete** | Removes it permanently |

<Callout type="info">
  Clone before editing anything customers are currently using. Edit the copy, test it, then switch
  over.
</Callout>
