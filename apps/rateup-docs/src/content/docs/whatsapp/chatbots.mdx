A **chatbot** answers a WhatsApp conversation automatically — handling the questions you get
constantly, and passing anything else to a person. **WhatsApp → Chatbot** lists every WhatsApp
chatbot you have built.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/chatbots-list.png" alt="Your WhatsApp chatbots" />

Instagram and Messenger have chatbots of their own, built in the same editor — see
[Instagram Chatbots](/instagram/chatbots) and [Messenger Chatbots](/messenger/chatbots).

---

## Create a Chatbot

Click **Create Chatbot**. A short wizard opens — *"Start from a ready-made example or a blank
canvas. You can change everything later."*

<Steps>
<Step>
**Starting point.** Pick **Start from scratch** — *"A blank canvas with just the start step"* — or
one of the examples below. Filter them with the chips along the top (**All**, **Welcome**,
**Support**, …). On a wide screen, picking an example plays it in a phone preview on the right, so
you can see the conversation before you create anything.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Next**. **Details.** The **Chatbot Name** is filled in from the example; change it if you
like. It must be at least 3 characters and is only ever seen by your team.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally pick an **Account** — *"Only needed when the flow sends messages from a specific WhatsApp
number."* The **How it starts** box tells you what usually starts this kind of bot.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create Chatbot**. The [builder](/whatsapp/chatbot-builder) opens with the example already
laid out as nodes. Edit the wording, then **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/chatbot-wizard-examples.png" alt="Create Chatbot wizard, Starting point step, with Welcome menu playing in the phone preview" />

When you have no chatbots yet, the empty list offers three examples and **Start from scratch** as
buttons, so you can open the wizard with a starting point already chosen.

## WhatsApp Examples

| Example | Filter | What it does |
|---------|--------|--------------|
| **Welcome menu** | Welcome | Greets people with buttons to explore, read FAQs or get in touch |
| **FAQ list** | Support | Answers common questions from a list, with a way back to the list |
| **Mini store** | Sales | Lets people browse categories and follow a link to your store |
| **Appointment booking** | Bookings | Picks a service, a day and a time, then confirms the booking |
| **Rating with follow-up** | Feedback | Asks how it went and follows up differently on a good or bad answer |
| **Loan eligibility** | Qualification | Checks employment, income and credit score before passing a lead on |
| **Lead capture** | Lead capture | Asks for a name and what they need, then hands the lead to your team |
| **Talk to a person** | Handoff | Lets people ask for a person and assigns the chat to your team |

Examples carry placeholder wording and links (`https://example.com/…`). Replace them before the bot
goes live — a customer who taps **Visit store** and lands on `example.com` is worse off than one who
got no bot at all.

## Make It Start

A chatbot does nothing on its own. An [automation rule](/automation) starts it, with the
**Trigger Chatbot** action.

When you create a chatbot from an example, the builder opens with a **Set up the trigger** card at
the bottom — for example *"Starts when someone sends a keyword like “agent” or “human”."* Click
**Create the rule** to open a new automation with the platform and the **Message Received** trigger
already filled in. Dismiss the card with **×** and it does not come back.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/chatbot-trigger-prompt.png" alt="Chatbot builder after creating from Talk to a person, with the Set up the trigger card" />

<Callout type="warn">
  Creating the chatbot is not the same as switching it on. Until a rule with **Trigger Chatbot**
  points at it, no customer ever reaches it — and the **Set up the trigger** card only appears once.
  If you dismissed it, create the rule from [Automation](/automation) yourself.
</Callout>

## Testing Before You Go Live

The builder's **Preview** runs the bot as a customer would experience it. Walk it end to end and
check:

- Every button leads somewhere real
- No path dead-ends without an option
- The customer can always reach a human
- The wording sounds like your brand

<Callout type="warn">
  The single worst chatbot failure is the loop with no exit. Before you switch the rule on, try
  deliberately giving unexpected answers and confirm you can still get out.
</Callout>

## Handover to an Agent

When the bot reaches an **Assign** step, the conversation becomes a normal ticket in the
[Inbox](/inbox). The bot's messages are already in the thread, so the agent has the context and the
customer does not repeat themselves.

## Manage Your Chatbots

| Action | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Search chatbots...** | Filters the list by name |
| **Sort** | Order the cards, newest first by default |
| **Refresh** | Reloads the list |
| **Clone** | From a card's **More actions** menu. Asks for a name, prefilled *"Copy of …"* |
| **Delete** | From **More actions**. Removes it permanently after you confirm |

<Callout type="info">
  Clone before editing anything customers are currently using. Edit the copy, test it, then point
  the rule at the copy.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| **Create Chatbot** stays disabled on Details | The name is shorter than 3 characters | Type a longer name |
| The bot never runs | No rule starts it | Add a rule with **Trigger Chatbot** in [Automation](/automation) |
| Customers see `example.com` links | Example wording was not replaced | Edit the nodes in the builder and save |
