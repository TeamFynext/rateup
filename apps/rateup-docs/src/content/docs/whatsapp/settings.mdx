## Accounts and Numbers

An account can have more than one phone number — one per brand, region or department.

Each number is a separate identity to your customers. In the [Inbox](/inbox), every ticket shows
which number the customer wrote to, so agents reply as the right business.

---

## Business Profile

The profile customers see when they tap your business name in WhatsApp: the photo, the about line,
description, category, address, email and websites. You edit it per number under
[Channels](/settings/channels), on the number's **Profile** tab.

The business name itself is separate. It is Meta's **display name**, it goes through review before
it can change, and the number has to be re-registered before the new one appears — see
[Change the Display Name](/settings/channels).

<Callout type="warn">
  This is customer-facing and appears before anyone has spoken to you. An out-of-date address or a
  dead website link here costs more trust than it looks like it should.
</Callout>

WhatsApp has no way to empty a profile field once it is set, so plan on replacing values rather
than clearing them.

## Let Customers Call Your Number

Since October 2026 customers can make WhatsApp voice calls to your number, and agents answer them in
the browser from the [Inbox](/inbox). Calling is switched on per number, on the **Calling** tab of the
number's edit dialog under [Channels](/settings/channels).

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/calling-tab.png" alt="Calling tab of a WhatsApp number with voice calls, callback requests and calling hours on" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Settings → Channels → WhatsApp** and edit the number.
</Step>
<Step>
Open the **Calling** tab and turn on **Voice calls**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the options below, then click **Save**. You will see *"Calling settings saved"*.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Voice calls** | *"Customers can call this number on WhatsApp, and agents answer from the inbox in their browser."* The others are greyed out until it is on |
| **Call button in chat** | Shows the call icon at the top of the customer's chat with you |
| **Callback requests** | When a customer calls and nobody answers, WhatsApp asks them whether you may call back |
| **Calling hours** | Outside these hours customers cannot call; they see when you are open. Set a **Time zone** (for example `Asia/Kolkata`) and the hours for each day — a new schedule starts at Monday–Friday, 09:00–18:00 |

These settings are held by WhatsApp, not by RateUp, so the tab always shows what WhatsApp is
enforcing for the number.

<Callout type="info">
  If the tab shows *"Could not load calling settings"*, the number is usually not eligible:
  WhatsApp calling needs a messaging limit of at least 2,000 customers a day, and does not work on
  a number shared with the WhatsApp Business app (coexistence). The full list is in
  [WhatsApp's Calling Limits](/inbox/whatsapp-calls#whatsapps-calling-limits).
</Callout>

Every call — answered, missed, its length and the agent — is listed under [Inbox → Calls](/inbox/calls).

## Account Health

WhatsApp scores business accounts on quality, based on how recipients respond to your messages.
Poor scores reduce how many messages you may send.

The things that damage it:

| Cause | Avoid by |
|-------|----------|
| Messaging people who did not opt in | Respecting [consent](/contacts) |
| Irrelevant campaign blasts | Segmenting with [audiences and labels](/contacts) |
| High block and report rates | Sending less, and sending better |
| Templates that mislead | Saying plainly what the message is |

Watch failure rates in [Template Delivery](/analytics/template-delivery) — a rise there is usually
the first visible symptom.
