## Accounts and Numbers

An account can have more than one phone number — one per brand, region or department.

Each number is a separate identity to your customers. In the [Inbox](/inbox), every ticket shows
which number the customer wrote to, so agents reply as the right business.

---

## Business Profile

The profile customers see when they tap your business name in WhatsApp: the photo, the about line,
description, category, address, email and websites. You edit it per number under
[Channels](/settings/channels), on the number's **Profile** tab.

The business name itself is separate. It is Meta's **display name**, it goes through review before
it can change, and the number has to be re-registered before the new one appears — see
[Change the Display Name](/settings/channels).

<Callout type="warn">
  This is customer-facing and appears before anyone has spoken to you. An out-of-date address or a
  dead website link here costs more trust than it looks like it should.
</Callout>

WhatsApp has no way to empty a profile field once it is set, so plan on replacing values rather
than clearing them.

## Account Health

WhatsApp scores business accounts on quality, based on how recipients respond to your messages.
Poor scores reduce how many messages you may send.

The things that damage it:

| Cause | Avoid by |
|-------|----------|
| Messaging people who did not opt in | Respecting [consent](/contacts) |
| Irrelevant campaign blasts | Segmenting with [groups and labels](/contacts) |
| High block and report rates | Sending less, and sending better |
| Templates that mislead | Saying plainly what the message is |

Watch failure rates in [Message Analytics](/analytics/message-analytics) — a rise there is usually
the first visible symptom.
