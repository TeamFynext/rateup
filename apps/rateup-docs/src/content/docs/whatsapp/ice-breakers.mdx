**Ice breakers and commands** are what WhatsApp shows a customer when they open a chat with you and
have not written anything yet. Open them from **Meta → WhatsApp → Ice Breakers**
(`/dashboard/whatsapp/ice-breakers`). The page is headed **Ice Breakers & Commands**: *"The suggested
questions and slash commands customers see when they open a WhatsApp chat with you."* It was
previously called **Conversational**.

Ice breakers and commands belong to one WhatsApp number. The page opens on your first number; use
the number picker at the top to switch to another.

They are small, cheap to set up, and they deflect a surprising share of routine questions.

## Ice Breakers

Ice breakers are tappable suggested questions shown in an empty chat.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add Ice Breaker**.
</Step>
<Step>
Write the question as a customer would ask it.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save Changes**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  A maximum of **four** ice breakers is allowed (*"Maximum 4 ice breakers allowed"*). Choose the four questions you actually answer most
  — check your [Inbox](/inbox) if you are guessing.
</Callout>

---

## Commands

Commands are shortcuts a customer types with a leading slash. Each has a **command** and a
**description** explaining what it does.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add Command**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the command name and a short description.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save Changes**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Keep descriptions to a few words — they appear in a compact list on the customer's phone.

---

## Ice Breakers vs Commands

| | Ice breakers | Commands |
|---|-------------|----------|
| **Seen by** | Everyone opening an empty chat | Customers who type `/` |
| **Best for** | The obvious first questions | Power users and repeat customers |
| **Limit** | 4 | Several |

Most businesses get more value from ice breakers, because they require nothing of the customer.

---

## Syncing

**Sync from WhatsApp** pulls the current configuration from your WhatsApp account, so RateUp
matches what customers actually see.

<Callout type="info">
  If a change does not appear on a test phone, close and reopen the chat — WhatsApp caches these
  components on the device.
</Callout>
