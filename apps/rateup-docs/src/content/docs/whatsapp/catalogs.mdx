A **WhatsApp catalog** is the product list customers browse inside the chat. Connect one and your
WhatsApp becomes a storefront that [Commerce](/commerce) can work with.

## Connecting a Catalog

<Steps>
<Step>
Make sure your **WhatsApp Business account** is connected in
[Channels](/settings/channels).
</Step>
<Step>
Open **WhatsApp → Catalog** and select the account.
</Step>
<Step>
Connect the catalog associated with that account.
</Step>
<Step>
Sync to pull in the products.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## What a Connected Catalog Enables

| Feature | How it uses the catalog |
|---------|------------------------|
| [Commerce](/commerce) | Products, orders, payments and checkout |
| [Templates](/whatsapp/templates) | Catalog buttons that open the product list |
| [Inbox](/inbox) | Agents can send products into a conversation |

<Callout type="warn">
  Without a connected catalog, Commerce shows *"No catalog connected"* and catalog buttons cannot be
  used on templates.
</Callout>

---

## Keeping Products Current

Products are managed in your catalog source and pulled into RateUp on sync. Sync whenever prices,
stock or the product list change.

Day-to-day product management — stock badges, deleting products, collections — happens in
[Commerce → Catalogs](/commerce/catalogs).
