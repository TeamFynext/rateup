## Signing In

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to [rateup.app](https://rateup.app) and click **Login** in the top-right corner.
</Step>
<Step>
On *"Welcome back"*, click **Google** or **Apple** — or, under *"or with email"*, enter your
**Email** and **Password**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Sign in**. (The **Keep me signed in** box does not change anything in the current build.)
**Forgot password?** starts a [password reset](/getting-started/resetpassword).
</Step>
<Step>
You land on your dashboard.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Before Your First Login

| Requirement | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| **Email verified** | Verification is required before you can sign in the first time |
| **Google account already registered** | Google sign-in works only for an email that has been registered |
| **An invite accepted** | If a colleague added you, use the invite link before trying to log in |

## What You See After Signing In

Your dashboard reflects two things:

- The **business details** you gave during registration
- The **role** an admin has given you, and any abilities they have added or removed for you

If a section is missing from your sidebar, your role does not include it — the sidebar is built
from your role's abilities. Ask an Owner or Admin to check your role under
[Members & Roles](/settings/members). It is not a fault.

With every section showing, the sidebar runs, top to bottom:

| Entry | Holds |
|-------|-------|
| **Get started** | The [setup guide](/getting-started/setup-guide) |
| **Inbox** | The [Inbox](/inbox) views and filters, [Calls](/inbox/calls) and **Inbox settings** |
| **Analytics** | Overview, Team Performance, Template Delivery and the [Message log](/analytics/message-log) |
| **Contacts** | [All Contacts](/contacts), Audiences, Custom Fields and Labels |
| **Campaigns** | [Broadcasts and drips](/campaigns) |
| **Automation** | [Rules](/automation) and [Journeys](/automation/journeys) |
| **Meta AI Agent** | The [AI agent's](/meta-agent) setup steps |
| **Meta** | A **WhatsApp** section — [Templates](/whatsapp/templates), [Flows](/whatsapp/flows), [Ice Breakers](/whatsapp/ice-breakers) — and an **Instagram & Messenger** section — [Instagram](/instagram), [Messenger](/messenger) |
| **Pipelines** | Your [lead boards](/pipelines) |
| **Loyalty** | The [loyalty programme](/loyalty) |
| **Commerce** | Your [shop](/commerce) |
| **Surveys & Links** | [Surveys](/surveys), [QR codes and Link-in-bio Pages](/qr-links) |
| **Settings** | **You**, **Organization** and — for RateUp staff — **RateUp**. See [Settings](/settings) |

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| "Invalid email or password" | Wrong credentials | Use [Reset Password](/getting-started/resetpassword) |
| Google sign-in bounces you back | That email has never been registered | Register with email first, or ask an admin to invite you |
| Login works but the dashboard is empty | No channel connected, or no permissions | See [Accessing the Inbox](/inbox/accessing-inbox) |
| You never received the verification email | Filtered as spam | Check spam and promotions folders, then retry |
