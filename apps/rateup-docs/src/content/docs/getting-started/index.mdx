## The Path

<Steps>
<Step>
**[Create an account](/getting-started/register)** — register, verify your email, and tell RateUp
about your business.
</Step>
<Step>
**[Log in](/getting-started/login)** — with Google or email.
</Step>
<Step>
**[Connect a channel](/settings/channels)** — a WhatsApp Business account or Instagram. Nothing
works until one is live.
</Step>
<Step>
**[Invite your team](/settings/users)** — add people, set their permissions, and group them into
[teams](/settings/teams).
</Step>
<Step>
**[Open the Inbox](/inbox)** — where every customer conversation is handled.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Your First Week

Once the basics are in place, these are the highest-value things to set up next:

| Do this | Why |
|---------|-----|
| [Quick replies](/inbox/inbox-settings) | Stops your team retyping the same answers |
| [Ice breakers](/whatsapp/conversational-components) | Deflects the three questions you get most |
| [Labels and lifecycle stages](/contacts) | Makes filtering and reporting possible later |
| [Templates](/whatsapp/templates) | Approval takes time — start before you need them |
| [Auto-close rules](/inbox/inbox-settings) | Keeps the Inbox from filling with dead conversations |

<Callout type="info">
  Resist configuring everything at once. Get conversations flowing through the Inbox first — a week
  of real messages will tell you which automation is actually worth building.
</Callout>

## Getting Help

- The **book** icon in the sidebar rail opens these docs in a new tab from wherever you are in the
  app
- The **megaphone** next to it lists **What's new** — every user-visible change, newest first. Read
  state is per browser, so it is unread again on a different machine
- Every section of these docs ends with **Related Pages** for the next step
- Most pages have a **Troubleshooting** table for the common failure modes
- If the Inbox is missing from your sidebar, it is a permissions or channel problem — see
  [Accessing the Inbox](/inbox/accessing-inbox)
