## When to Use Password Reset

Use the **Reset Password** option if:
- You forgot your password
- You entered an incorrect password multiple times
- You want to create a new password for security reasons

---

## Resetting Your Password

Follow the steps below to reset your RateUp password.

---

### Step 1: Open Login Page

1. Visit [https://rateup.app](https://rateup.app)
2. Click the **Login** button on the landing page

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/authentication/home.png`} alt="RateUp home page" />
---

### Step 2: Click "Forgot Password"

On the Login page:
- Click **Forgot password?**

This will redirect you to the password recovery screen.

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/authentication/forgot.png`} alt="Login page" width="350px"/>

---

### Step 3: Enter Your Email Address

1. Enter the **email address** associated with your RateUp account
2. Click **Reset Password**

> 📧 A password reset email will be sent to your inbox.

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/authentication/reset.png`} alt="Login page" width="350px"/>

---

### Step 4: Check Your Email

1. Open the email from **RateUp**
2. Click the **Reset Password** link inside the email

> 🔐 The reset link is time-sensitive and can be used only once.

---

### Step 5: Create a New Password

On the Reset Password page:
- Enter your **new password**
- Confirm the password
- Click **Update Password**

Once successful, your password will be updated immediately.

---

After resetting your password, continue to the  
👉 **[Login to RateUp](/gettingStarted/login)** documentation.

---

## Troubleshooting

### Didn’t receive the reset email?
- Check your **Spam / Promotions** folder
- Ensure the email address is correct
- Wait a few minutes and try again

### Reset link expired?
- Request a new reset link from the **Forgot password** page

---

## Security Tips

- Use a **strong password** (mix of letters, numbers, symbols)
- Do not reuse old passwords
- Avoid sharing your login credentials
