Use this when you cannot sign in. If you are already signed in and simply want a new password, use
[Security](/settings/security) instead — it is quicker and does not involve email.

---

## Resetting

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the [login page](/getting-started/login) and click **Forgot password?**
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the **email address** registered to your RateUp account and click **Reset Password**.
</Step>
<Step>
Open the email from RateUp and click the **Reset Password** link.

<Callout type="info">
  The link is time-sensitive and works **once**. Request a new one if it has been sitting in your
  inbox.
</Callout>
</Step>
<Step>
Enter your **new password**, confirm it, and click **Update Password**.
</Step>
<Step>
Sign in with the new password.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No email arrived | Filtered as spam, or the address is not registered | Check spam and promotions; confirm the address, then retry after a few minutes |
| "Reset link expired" | The link timed out or was already used | Request a new reset |
| "Passwords do not match" | The confirmation differs | Retype both fields |
| You signed up with Google | There is no password to reset | Use **Continue with Google** on the login page |

## Choosing a Password

- Unique to RateUp, not reused from another service
- Long rather than complicated — a passphrase beats a short string of symbols
- Never shared; add colleagues as [users](/settings/users) instead of sharing a login
