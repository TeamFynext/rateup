Signing up is three steps: **Account**, **Your store** and **Goals**. The account is created at the
end of the first step; the other two describe your business and choose which features your menu
starts with.

<Callout type="info">
  If a colleague invited you, do **not** sign up here — use the invite link in your email instead.
  Registering separately creates a second, empty organisation rather than joining theirs.
</Callout>

<Steps>

</Step>
<Step>

## Step 1 · Create Your Account

The card is headed *"Create your account"*. Choose **Google** or **Apple** at the top, or fill in
the form under *"or with email"*.

### With email

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Full name** | Your own name |
| **Work email** | Must be valid, and not already registered |
| **Password** | See the rules below |

A strength meter under the password reads **Weak**, **Fair**, **Good** or **Strong**. A password is
accepted only when it has all of these:

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| At least 8 characters | Shorter passwords are rejected. 12 or more is ideal |
| An uppercase and a lowercase letter | `A`–`Z` and `a`–`z` |
| A number | `0`–`9` |
| A special character | One from the list below |

Accepted special characters:

```text
! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) , . ? " : { } | < >
```

The meter never shows **Good** or **Strong** for a password that would be refused, so if it stops
at **Fair**, a rule is still missing. Submitting it anyway shows *"Your password must meet all
security requirements"*.

The terms box — *"I agree to the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy"* — is ticked for you. Click
**Create account**.

### With Google or Apple

After you approve RateUp with the provider, the card shows **Account connected** with your email.
Check your **Full name** and click **Create account**.

## Verify Your Email

Email signups only. RateUp sends a verification link and shows *"Check your inbox"*:

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the email and click the link. *"It works once and expires in 24 hours."*
</Step>
<Step>
RateUp opens **Step 2** of signup.
</Step>
</Steps>

If it does not arrive, click **Resend the email** (available again after a one-minute countdown) and
check your spam folder.

<Callout type="warn">
  An unverified account cannot reach the dashboard — every visit sends you back to the verification
  screen until the link is clicked.
</Callout>

## Step 2 · Tell Us About Your Store

<ScreenShot
  src={`/docs/screenshots/authentication/signup-step2.png`}
  alt="Signup step 2, 'Tell us about your store': business name, WhatsApp business number, website, what you sell and team size."
/>

The card is headed *"Tell us about your store"* — *"What your customers will see in chat."*

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Business name** | Yes | At least 2 characters. Appears across RateUp and on customer-facing pages |
| **WhatsApp business number** | Yes | With country code. Must be a valid phone number |
| **Website** | No | For example `https://yourcompany.com` |
| **What do you sell?** | Yes | E-commerce · Healthcare · Education · Finance · Real Estate · Hospitality · Retail · Technology · Marketing · Other |
| **How big is your team?** | Yes | **1–5**, **6–25**, **26–100** or **100+** |

Click **Continue**.

<Callout type="info">
  Leaving after step 1 is safe: the account already exists, and the next time you sign in RateUp
  brings you back here to finish.
</Callout>

## Step 3 · What Should RateUp Do First?

*"Pick one or more — you can change this later."*

| Option | Described as |
|--------|--------------|
| **WhatsApp Business API** | Verified sender, approved templates |
| **Loyalty inside chat** | Points and tiers in the thread |
| **Campaigns that get opened** | Broadcasts with delivery reporting |
| **Reviews and ratings** | Ask for a rating after delivery |
| **Surveys on the web** | NPS and CSAT forms you can share |
| **One shared inbox** | WhatsApp and Instagram in one queue |
| **Sell products on WhatsApp & Instagram** | A shop with checkout in the chat |

Your picks decide which optional features start in your menu: **Loyalty inside chat** turns on
Loyalty, **Surveys on the web** turns on Surveys, and **Sell products on WhatsApp & Instagram**
turns on Commerce. Pipelines is always on. Pick nothing and every feature starts on. Nothing is
locked — you can change the set at any time in [Customize Menu](/settings/customize-menu).

Click **Finish setup**. **Back** returns to step 2 without losing anything.

## You're In

The last screen says *"… is live"* and lists what was set up — **Email verified**, **Workspace
created for …** and **Free account started — no card on file**. Click **Go to dashboard**.

Owners and Admins land on the [setup guide](/getting-started/setup-guide), which starts with
**Choose features** and then connecting a channel.

## The Flow at a Glance

```
Step 1 · Account
  ├── Email        →  full name, work email, password  →  verify email link
  └── Google/Apple →  full name
        ↓
Step 2 · Your store  →  business name, WhatsApp number, website, what you sell, team size
        ↓
Step 3 · Goals       →  pick one or more (sets your starting features)
        ↓
Success screen  →  Go to dashboard  →  setup guide
```

## Troubleshooting

| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| *"This account already exists. Please sign in to continue"* | That email is already registered | [Log in](/getting-started/login), or [reset your password](/getting-started/resetpassword) |
| *"Sign up process is already completed"* | Registration finished on a previous attempt | Go to the login page and sign in |
| *"Your password must meet all security requirements"* | One or more password rules is unmet | Add whatever is missing — upper and lower case, a number, a special character, 8+ characters |
| *"Enter a valid email"* | The address is malformed | Check for typos |
| *"Please accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy to continue"* | The terms box was unticked | Tick it |
| *"Enter a valid phone number"* | The WhatsApp number is incomplete or has no country code | Pick the country and enter the full number |
| *"Couldn't save. Try again."* | Steps 2–3 did not save | Click **Finish setup** again |
| No verification email | Filtered as spam, or a typo in the address | Check spam, then **Resend the email** |
| A feature you expected is missing from the menu | Your step 3 picks did not include it | Turn it on in [Customize Menu](/settings/customize-menu) |
