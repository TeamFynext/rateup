Signing up takes three short steps. What you are asked for depends on whether you register with
**email** or with **Google / Apple**.

<Callout type="info">
  If a colleague invited you, do **not** sign up here — use the invite link in your email instead.
  Registering separately creates a second, empty organisation rather than joining theirs.
</Callout>

## Getting to the Signup Form

<Steps>
<Step>
Visit [rateup.app](https://rateup.app) and click **Login** in the top-right corner.
</Step>
<Step>
On the login page, click **Create account**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose how to register:

- **Continue with Google**
- **Continue with Apple**

## Step 1 · Create Your Account

What this step asks for depends on the method you chose.

### With email

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Name** | Your own name |
| **Email address** | Must be valid, and not already registered |
| **Password** | See the rules below |

As you type, a checklist appears under the password field and ticks each rule off:

| Rule | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **At least 8 characters** | Shorter passwords are rejected |
| **1 uppercase letter** | `A`–`Z` |
| **1 lowercase letter** | `a`–`z` |
| **1 number** | `0`–`9` |
| **1 special character** | See the accepted list below |

Accepted special characters:

```text
! @ # $ % ^ & * ( ) , . ? " : { } | < >
```

<Callout type="warn">
  All five rules must be green before you can continue. Until then the field shows *"Your password
  must meet all security requirements"* — it does not say which rule is missing, so read the
  checklist.
</Callout>

### With Google or Apple

Your name and email come from the provider, so instead you are asked for a **phone number**. It is
required, and it is verified later with a one-time code.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|-------|----------|-------|
| **Business name** | Yes | Appears across RateUp and on customer-facing pages |
| **Website URL** | No | For example `https://yourcompany.com` |
| **Industry** | Yes | Chosen from the list below |
| **Location** | Yes | Where the business operates |
| **Team size** | Yes, in practice | **1–5**, **6–25**, **26–100** or **100+** |

**Industries:** E-commerce · Healthcare · Education · Finance · Real Estate · Hospitality ·
Retail · Technology · Marketing · Other.

<Callout type="info">
  **Team size** is marked optional but the **Continue** button stays disabled until you pick one. If
  the button looks stuck on this step, that is almost always why.
</Callout>

Your industry choice shapes the templates, automations and onboarding RateUp suggests — so pick the
closest match rather than **Other** where you can.

## Step 3 · What Are You Most Excited to Use?

Select all that apply. This tailors your dashboard and onboarding tour; it does not restrict what
you can use later.

| Option | Covers |
|--------|--------|
| **WhatsApp Business API** | Verified sender and templates |
| **Loyalty on WhatsApp** | Points, tiers and rewards |
| **WhatsApp Campaigns** | Broadcasts |
| **Customer Feedback** | NPS, CSAT and review drives |
| **Web Feedback Forms** | Surveys and forms |
| **Unified Inbox** | WhatsApp and Instagram in one place |

A progress indicator shows **Step 3 of 3**. Use **Back** to revisit earlier steps — nothing is lost.

## Verification

The last stage depends, again, on how you registered.

### Email signup — verify your email

<Steps>
<Step>
RateUp sends a verification email. The page reads **Verify Your Email — check your inbox to
continue**.
</Step>
<Step>
Open the email and click the verification link.
</Step>
<Step>
Your account is activated and you can sign in.
</Step>
</Steps>

If it does not arrive, use **Resend verification email** and check your spam and promotions
folders.

### Google / Apple signup — verify your phone

<Steps>
<Step>
The **Verify your Phone Number** screen asks for the **OTP code** sent to the number you gave.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter it and click **Verify OTP**, or use **Resend OTP** if it does not arrive.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Email verification is required before your first login. An unverified account cannot sign in.
</Callout>

## The Flow at a Glance

```
Create account
  ├── Email        →  name, email, password
  └── Google/Apple →  phone number
        ↓
Business details  →  name, website, industry, location, team size
        ↓
Interests         →  select all that apply
        ↓
Verify            →  email link, or phone OTP
        ↓
Dashboard
```

## Troubleshooting

| Message | Meaning | What to do |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| **"This account already exists"** | That email is already registered | [Log in](/getting-started/login), or [reset your password](/getting-started/resetpassword) |
| **"Sign up process is already completed"** | Registration finished on a previous attempt | Go to the login page and sign in |
| **"Your password must meet all security requirements"** | One or more of the five password rules is unmet | Check the password checklist — the unticked rules are the ones to fix |
| **"Enter a valid email"** | The address is malformed | Check for typos |
| **Continue button will not activate** | A required field is empty — usually **team size** | Fill every field on the step |
| **No verification email** | Filtered as spam, or a typo in the address | Check spam, then **Resend verification email** |
