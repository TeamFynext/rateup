**Set up RateUp** is the checklist an Owner or Admin lands on after [signing up](/getting-started/register).
It walks through the few things an organisation needs before conversations can flow, ticks steps
off as you do them, and goes away once you finish or hide it.

## Open the Guide

The guide lives at `/dashboard/setup`. While it is unfinished:

- Opening the dashboard takes you there, if you can connect channels (the ability *Connect, remove
  and configure channels* — by default Admins and the Owner).
- A **rocket** icon, **Get started**, sits at the top of the sidebar rail. Its panel lists every
  step under **Features**, **Channels**, **WhatsApp** and **Recommended**; click one to jump to its
  card.

The header shows your progress — *"2 of 9 complete"* — and how many minutes the remaining steps
take.

## The Steps

### Start with your features

*"Keep your menu to what your team actually uses."*

| Step | Button | Does |
|------|--------|------|
| **Choose the features you will use** | **Choose features** | Opens *"Which features will you use?"* — the same cards as [Customize Menu](/settings/features), pre-ticked from your signup answers. Click **Save and continue** to save and come back to the guide |

Only people who can change features can save this step; for anyone else **Save and continue** is
disabled.

### Connect a channel — Required

*"Connect at least one — every conversation lands in the same inbox, with the same replies and
rules."* Any one of the three completes the band.

| Step | Button |
|------|--------|
| **Connect WhatsApp Business API** | The Facebook connect button; **Manage connection** once connected |
| **Connect an Instagram Business Account to RateUp** | **Connect Instagram** |
| **Connect Facebook Messenger to RateUp** | **Connect Messenger** |

A channel's step ticks itself as soon as the channel is connected. See [Channels](/settings/channels).

### Finish WhatsApp setup

*"You can't send on WhatsApp until these are done."* These wait for WhatsApp to be connected —
until then they show **Waiting on WhatsApp** — and cannot be skipped.

| Step | Button | Does |
|------|--------|------|
| **Verify your WhatsApp Business account** | **Check Status** | Asks Meta for your business verification status and shows **Verified**, **Under review**, **Not verified** or **Not eligible**. **Verified** completes the step |
| **Add payment in Meta** | **Add payment** | Opens Meta's billing settings in a new tab. Tick **Mark done** when you have added a card |

### Make it work for you

*"Optional — do these in any order, any time."*

| Step | Button | Opens |
|------|--------|-------|
| **Create your first message template** | **Open templates** | [Message Templates](/whatsapp/templates). Needs WhatsApp |
| **Set your business hours** | **Set hours** | [Profile & Business → Business hours](/settings/account) |
| **Set up auto-replies** | **Open automation** | [Automation Rules](/automation) |
| **Launch a loyalty or referral program** | **Set up loyalty** | [Loyalty settings](/loyalty/settings). Only when your plan includes loyalty and Loyalty is on in [Customize Menu](/settings/features) |
| **Invite your team** | **Invite team** | [Members & Roles](/settings/users) |

## Mark Done, Skip and Hide

| Control | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Mark done** | Ticks a step you finished somewhere RateUp cannot see. Untick it to undo |
| **Skip** | Takes a step off the count, marked **Skipped**. Steps that depend on it (the WhatsApp steps on WhatsApp, for example) are skipped with it. **Undo skip** brings it back. You cannot skip every channel — one must stay |
| **Hide setup guide** | Top right. Marks the guide finished, removes **Get started** from the rail and opens the Inbox |
| **Skip setup and explore the app** | Under *"Rather poke around first?"* — the same as hiding the guide |
| **Book a 15-minute setup call** | Books time with the RateUp team |

When every step is done or skipped, the header changes to *"You're live."* with **Open Inbox**, and
the guide is finished.

<Callout type="warn">
  Hiding the guide is permanent — the rocket does not come back. Everything it links to is still in
  the normal menu, so nothing is lost, but note any steps you still mean to do.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No **Get started** rocket in the rail | The guide was finished or hidden, or you cannot connect channels | Ask an Admin or the Owner, or open the pages directly |
| The WhatsApp steps say **Waiting on WhatsApp** | WhatsApp is not connected yet | Connect it in the **Connect a channel** band |
| **Save and continue** is greyed out on **Choose features** | You lack *Edit organisation details and business hours* | Ask a Manager, Admin or the Owner |
| No loyalty step | Loyalty is off in [Customize Menu](/settings/features), or not on your plan | Turn it on, or check [Billing](/settings/billing) |
| **Check Status** shows **Not verified** | Meta has not verified the business | Complete business verification in Meta Business Suite, then check again |
