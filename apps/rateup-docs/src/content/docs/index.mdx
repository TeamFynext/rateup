## How the Pieces Connect

```
Customers reach you        →  WhatsApp · Instagram · QR codes · web forms
Conversations land in      →  Inbox
Repetitive work handled by →  Automation · Chatbots · Flows
You reach out with         →  Campaigns · Templates
They buy through           →  Commerce
They come back because of  →  Loyalty
You learn from it in       →  Analytics · Web Forms · Testimonials
```

Not sure where to begin? Start with the [Inbox](/inbox) — it is where most teams spend their day,
and everything else feeds into it.
