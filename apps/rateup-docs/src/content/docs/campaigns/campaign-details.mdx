## The Header

The title row shows the campaign's name, its [status](/campaigns#campaign-statuses) and the template
it sends. Hover a **Failed** or red **Paused** badge to read why it stopped.

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Refresh** | Re-reads all three tabs. Statuses arrive after the send finishes and retries fire on their own, so an open page goes stale |
| **Edit** | Reopens the wizard on its Review step |
| **Export** | Downloads the delivery report or the analytics report — see [Export a Report](#export-a-report) |
| **Send to more** | Opens the wizard's Audience step to add recipients. Only people never messaged are sent to |
| Bin icon | Deletes the campaign |

To the right of the tabs sit the send controls: **Pause** and **Resume** for a broadcast, or
**Pause Drip**, **Start Drip** and **Resume Drip** for a drip — see
[Pausing, Resuming & Retrying](/campaigns/pausing-and-retrying).

## Overview

### The five numbers

| Card | Counts | Rate shown against |
|------|--------|--------------------|
| **Dispatched** | Recipients handed to Meta at least once, including any Meta then rejected | Recipients |
| **Delivered** | Messages that reached the phone | Dispatched |
| **Read** | Messages opened | Delivered |
| **Pending** | Recipients not messaged yet | Recipients |
| **Failed** | Recipients whose latest attempt failed | Recipients |

**Failed** is where recipients stand *now*: someone a retry later reached is no longer counted.
**Delivered** is the number that means success — **Dispatched** is only what went out.

### The cards below

| Card | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Next batch** | The send that has not gone out yet — a scheduled start or a booked retry — with its attempt number, start time and how many recipients it covers. Offers **Cancel schedule**, or **Retry now** and **Cancel pending retries** for a retry |
| **Drip schedule** / **Next stage** | Drips only. Every stage with its template, how many are waiting and how many were sent, and *"N of N stages complete"* |
| **Activity** | Each send as it was scheduled, started, finished (*"N sent · N failed"*) or cancelled |
| **Setup** | **Type**, **Team**, **Audience**, **Sent**, **Retries**, **Opt-in filter** and **Timezone** |
| **Replies** | How many *"conversations started"*. *"Replies land in the inbox, assigned to the campaign's team."* |

## Participants

Everyone on the campaign and what happened to each of them, 25 to a page.

| Column | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **Contact** | Name and phone |
| **Status** | **SENT**, **DELIVERED**, **READ**, **PENDING** or **FAILED** |
| **Attempts** | One chip per attempt — hover for its outcome |
| **Last attempt** | When the latest attempt went out |
| **Reason** | Why the latest attempt failed, with Meta's error code |
| **Read** | When it was read |

Filter with the pills — **All**, **Delivered**, **Read**, **Sent**, **Pending**, **Failed** — and
search with **Search name or phone**. On a drip, a stage picker narrows the list to **All stages** or
one **Stage N**.

| Action | What it does |
|--------|--------------|
| **Retry now** (on a failed row) | Re-sends to that one recipient |
| **Open** (on any other row) | Opens the contact's conversation in the [Inbox](/inbox) |
| **Retry all failed** | Re-sends to every failed recipient |
| **Retry selected** | Re-sends to the failed recipients you ticked |
| **Remove from campaign** | Takes the ticked recipients off the campaign. The contacts are not deleted |

Retry buttons appear on broadcasts only. More on what a retry does in
[Retry failed recipients](/campaigns/pausing-and-retrying#retry-failed-recipients-yourself).

## Analytics

| Section | Shows |
|---------|-------|
| **Delivery funnel** | **Attempted → Dispatched → Delivered → Read → Replied**, all attempts combined, with how many dropped off at each stage |
| **Why messages failed** | Each failure reason with its **Code**, **Outcome**, **Count** and **Share**. *"N failed · N still retryable"* summarises it; **See failed recipients** lists them |
| **Send attempts** | One numbered row per send — the first send, any scheduled send, each automatic or manual retry — with its **Trigger** (**Manual**, **Scheduled**, **Automatic** or **Drip**), how many it re-attempted, delivered, recovered and failed, and its **Recovery rate**. Click a row to see who it went to |

The **Outcome** column says what a retry could do about a failure:

| Outcome | Means |
|---------|-------|
| **Retryable** | A later attempt may succeed — automatic retries pick these up |
| **Limited** | Meta is limiting your sending |
| **Permanent** | The number cannot receive the message |
| **Needs a fix** | The template is the problem — edit or replace it |
| **Unclassified** | No specific reason was recorded |

When retries recovered anything, the tab says *"Retries recovered N messages that would otherwise
have failed."*

## Export a Report

**Export** in the header offers two reports:

| Menu item | Downloads |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Delivery Report → Download as Excel** | The delivery report, as `.xlsx` |
| **Delivery Report → Download as OpenDocument (ODS)** | The same, as `.ods` |
| **Analytics → Download as PDF** | The Analytics tab as a report — funnel, failure reasons and send attempts — fetched fresh, with the template and team in the heading |

On a drip with a stage picked on **Participants**, the delivery report covers that stage.

Exports contain participant phone numbers — treat them as personal data.

## Delete a Campaign

Click the bin icon and confirm *"Are you sure you want to delete this campaign? This action cannot
be undone."*

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting destroys the delivery record and its analytics. If you want the history for future
  comparison, export both reports first.
</Callout>

A draft is deleted from the wizard instead, with **Discard draft**.

## Reading the Numbers

| Pattern | Likely meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| Many **Permanent** failures | List quality — numbers not on WhatsApp, or contacts who opted out |
| Many **Retryable** failures with retries off | Meta's marketing frequency cap. Turn on retries next time |
| High delivery, low read | The message is not compelling, or arrived at a bad time |
| High read, few replies | The call to action is unclear |
| Replies flooding the Inbox | Check staffing before the next send |

Read this alongside [Message Analytics](/analytics/template-delivery), which puts the campaign in the
context of everything else you send.
