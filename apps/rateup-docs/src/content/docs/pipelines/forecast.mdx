**Forecast** answers one question: how much is likely to land, and when. It groups your open leads
by their **expected close** month and weights each one by the win probability of the stage it is in.

The **Forecast** tab exists on Leads boards only. Support, Projects and Custom boards have no win
probabilities to weight by, so the tab is hidden there — see [Board Types](/pipelines/board-types).

## Reading a Column

| Element | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| The month | Formatted from each lead's **Expected close** date |
| **{`{n}`} leads** | How many open leads fall in that month |
| The big number | **Expected to convert** — the probability-weighted count |
| The bar | That number against the strongest month showing |
| Each row | A lead, with its stage and that stage's probability. Click to open it. A column carries up to twenty cards — the count above them is the answer, the cards are the evidence |

The headline number is deliberately not the raw lead count. Five leads sitting at 20% are one
expected win, and the column says **1** rather than **5**.

---

## The Unscheduled Column

Open leads with no **Expected close** date collect in a trailing **Unscheduled** column rather than
being dropped from the view.

<Callout type="warn">
  An unscheduled lead is invisible to your forecast in every other respect, so the column is
  usually the most important one on this screen. If it is the tallest column you have, the forecast
  is not measuring your pipeline — it is measuring the part of it somebody remembered to date.
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  The lead window shows **Expected close** but cannot change it: on a Leads board there is no date
  picker for it anywhere in the dashboard. The way to set it is the **Expected close** column of a
  spreadsheet [import](/pipelines/import-export), which only applies to the new cards that import
  creates. Leads added by hand or by a rule stay **Unscheduled**.
</Callout>

---

## What It Includes

Only **open** leads. Won and lost leads have already landed, so they belong in
[Insights](/pipelines/insights) rather than a forecast.

Every open lead on the board, counted on the server. The sidebar's facets and the search box narrow
the **Board** and **List** views only — they do not narrow the forecast, which answers *"what is
this pipeline expected to close"* rather than *"what is it expected to close under the filter I left
on"*. To read one person's forecast, open [Insights](/pipelines/insights) for their win rate, or the
[List](/pipelines/list) filtered to them.
