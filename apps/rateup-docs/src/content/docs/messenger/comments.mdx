The **Comments** tab on **Messenger** lists your **Comment Automations** — *"Reply privately in
Messenger when someone comments on your Page's posts."* Each one is an
[automation rule](/automation) with the **Facebook Page Comment** trigger, for the Page selected at
the top of the page.

## Answer Comments Automatically

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create** (or **Create rule** when the list is empty). The rule builder opens with
**Messenger**, the **Facebook Page Comment** trigger and this Page selected. On **Template**, click
**Start from scratch** to keep that trigger and Page.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Conditions** — *"Which comments should it answer?"* — add **Message Body** keywords such as
`price`, or leave it empty to answer every comment.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Actions**, add **Send a message**, or **Start a journey** to run a
[Messenger journey](/messenger/journeys). The first message arrives as a private reply in
Messenger.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally fill in **Reply in public (optional)** under the actions (below). On **Review**, name the
rule and click **Activate automation**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The rule answers comments on every post of the Page — there is no post picker, unlike
[Instagram comments](/instagram/automations). Comments the Page itself writes are ignored, and each
comment is answered once.

## How the Private Reply Works

A comment does not tell RateUp who wrote it in Messenger terms. The first message the rule sends goes
out as a **private reply** to the comment; Meta's answer names the person, and only then do the
rule's later actions know who they are acting on. The Conditions step says so — *"a comment only
tells us who someone is once that reply reaches them, so put label, assign and lifecycle actions
after it."*

| Order of actions | Result |
|------------------|--------|
| Reply first, then **Add a label**, **Assign to a team** | The label and team land on the person who commented |
| **Add a label** first, then reply | The label has nobody to go on yet |

Once the private reply reaches someone, the comment is filed in their conversation in the
[Inbox](/inbox). A comment no rule answered stays out of the Inbox.

<Callout type="warn">
  Meta allows a private reply to a comment for **7 days** after it was posted, and only one until the
  person writes back. Keep the first message to a single text that invites a reply — *"Thanks!
  Want the price list? Reply YES."*
</Callout>

## Reply in Public

**Reply in public (optional)** posts a comment under theirs, so other people see you answered —
*"Sent you a DM 📩"*. Click **Add a public reply**, then **Add a variation** for more.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Variations | Up to **10**. One is picked at random each time |
| Length | Up to **2200** characters each |
| When | Posted after the rule's actions have run |
| Empty variations | Dropped when you save |

A public reply that fails does not stop the private reply or the rest of the rule.

## Turn a Rule On or Off

Each rule appears as a card. Use the card's switch to pause or resume it, the delete action to
remove it, or click the card to edit it in [Automation Rules](/automation).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Comments get no answer at all | The Page's connection does not include the permissions Meta needs to send comments to RateUp | Reconnect the Page under [Channels](/settings/channels) and allow everything Meta asks for |
| The private reply arrives but the public reply does not | Meta refused the public reply — it needs the permission to manage the Page's engagement | Reconnect the Page and allow every permission |
| A label or assignment did not apply | The action ran before the private reply | Move it below the reply action |
| Old comments get no answer | More than 7 days old | Nothing to fix — Meta no longer allows a private reply |
| A rule you made is not on the tab | It belongs to another Page | Switch the Page selector at the top |
