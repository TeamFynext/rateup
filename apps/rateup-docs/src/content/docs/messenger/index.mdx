**Messenger** holds everything that runs on your Facebook Page — *"Chatbots and links for messages
sent to your Facebook Page."* Messenger conversations land in the [Inbox](/inbox) like any other
channel, and a Page connects on its own: no WhatsApp number is needed.

The page has one tab per tool:

| Tab | What it holds |
|-----|---------------|
| **Chatbots** | Your Messenger chatbots — see [Messenger Chatbots](/messenger/chatbots) |
| **Comments** | Rules with the **Facebook Page Comment** trigger — see [Facebook Comments](/messenger/comments) |

**Messenger** appears in the sidebar under **Instagram** for anyone whose team has access to
chatbots.

## Work with Several Pages

With more than one Page connected, a **Select Page** list sits beside the tabs. **Comments**,
**m.me Links** and **Welcome & Menu** belong to the Page selected there — the first Page until you
pick another. Switch Pages before you edit.

Chatbots are the exception: a chatbot is not tied to a Page. The [rule](/automation) that starts it
decides which Page it runs on.

Nothing on Messenger needs a sync step: rules take effect when you save them, and **Welcome & Menu**
changes Messenger as soon as you click **Save**.

## Connect a Facebook Page

Until a Page is connected, the page shows *"Connect a Facebook Page"* — *"Messenger chatbots run on
a connected Facebook Page. Connect one to get started."* — with a **Connect Facebook Page** button.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Connect Facebook Page**, or open **Settings → Channels** and click **Connect** on **Facebook
Messenger**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Continue with Facebook** and log in. Meta asks only for Messenger permissions.
</Step>
<Step>
In **Choose Facebook Pages**, tick the Pages you want — *"Each Page becomes its own Messenger
channel."* A Page already linked to another RateUp organisation is marked *"Connected to another
organization"* and cannot be picked.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Connect**. The Pages appear under **Facebook Messenger connections**.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can connect as many Pages as you like. If the list is empty — *"No Facebook Pages were shared.
Log in again and choose at least one."* — Meta was not given access to any Page during login.

<Callout type="warn">
  Disconnecting a Page stops its conversations reaching the Inbox — *"Messenger conversations from
  this Page will stop arriving in the inbox."* Chatbots and rules built for it stop answering too.
</Callout>

## Where Conversations Go

Every message to a connected Page becomes a ticket in the [Inbox](/inbox), with a Messenger badge.
A chatbot's messages are already in the thread when an agent picks it up. A button tap shows as the
button's title, an m.me link visit as *"Opened a link (ref: …)"*, and a Page comment joins the
person's conversation once a [comment rule](/messenger/comments) has replied to it.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"Connect a Facebook Page"* although you connected one | The Page is not listed under **Facebook Messenger** — an Instagram account is a separate channel | Connect the Page under **Settings → Channels → Facebook Messenger** |
| A Page is missing from **Choose Facebook Pages** | It was not shared with RateUp during login | Log in again and pick it |
| A Page is greyed out in **Choose Facebook Pages** | *"Connected to another organization"* | Disconnect it there first |
| **Messenger** is not in the sidebar | Your team has no access to chatbots | Ask an admin to give your team access to chatbots |
| A comment rule, link or menu you set up is not shown | Another Page is selected | Switch the Page selector |
