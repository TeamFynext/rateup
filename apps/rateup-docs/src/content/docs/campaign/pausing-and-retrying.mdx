A campaign can be stopped and restarted without messaging anyone twice, and recipients whose message
failed can be tried again — automatically, on a schedule you set in the wizard, or by hand from the
campaign page.

## Pause a Broadcast

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the campaign. **Pause** sits to the right of the tabs while messages are still queued.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm **Pause campaign**: *"Recipients who have not been messaged yet are taken off the queue.
Messages already sent cannot be recalled. You can resume the campaign later."*
</Step>
</Steps>

The badge reads **Pausing** while the queue empties, then **Paused**. Recipients who were not
reached stay **Pending**.

A campaign scheduled for later can be taken off its schedule with **Cancel schedule** on the
Overview tab's **Next batch** card.

<Callout type="warn">
  Pause the moment you spot a mistake in a live campaign — a wrong link, a broken variable. You
  cannot recall what has already gone, but you can stop the rest.
</Callout>

## Resume a Broadcast

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Resume** — its badge shows how many recipients are still waiting. It only appears while
someone is left to message.
</Step>
<Step>
In **Resume campaign**, choose when it goes out:

| Option | Sends |
|--------|-------|
| **Keep its schedule** | At the time the campaign was scheduled for before you paused it. Offered, and selected, only while that time is still ahead |
| **Send now** | Immediately |
| **Pick a new date and time** | At a date and **Time** you choose, in your own timezone |
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Resume**.
</Step>
</Steps>

*"Only the N recipients who were never messaged are sent to."* Nobody who already received the
message gets it again, and **failed recipients are not included** — retry those separately.

A time that has already passed is refused: *"That time has already passed. Pick a later one, or
choose Send now."*

## Pause or Restart a Drip

A drip has one toggle in the same place:

| Button | Shown when | Effect |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| **Pause Drip** | The drip is running | Stops further stages. *"Drip campaign paused"* |
| **Start Drip** | You paused it | Starts it again. *"Drip campaign started"* |
| **Resume Drip** | RateUp paused it | Starts it again once the cause is fixed |

## When RateUp Pauses a Campaign

If your **account** cannot send, a campaign is paused instead of failing every remaining recipient
one by one. The badge turns red, the campaign's team is notified, and a banner on the campaign page
says why:

| Banner | Cause | Fix it with |
|--------|-------|-------------|
| **Campaign paused: campaign credits used up** | Your campaign credits are spent | **Get more credits** — buy a credit pack, upgrade your plan or wait for the monthly reset |
| **Campaign paused: conversation limit reached** | Your plan's conversation allowance is used up | **Get more credits** — upgrade or wait for the allowance to reset |
| **Campaign paused: WhatsApp payment issue** | Meta rejected the payment method on your WhatsApp Business account (error 131042) | **Open Meta billing** — add or fix the payment method in Meta's billing settings |
| **Campaign paused** | Another account-level stop | The reason shown in the banner |

Each banner ends with *"N recipients have not been messaged yet and will be sent when you resume."*
Those recipients stay **Pending**, so once the cause is fixed, **Resume** in the banner sends to
exactly the people who were missed.

## Retry Messages Meta Blocked Automatically

Broadcasts can retry failures on their own. Set it up on the wizard's **Retries** step by turning on
**Retry blocked messages**.

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| Strategy | **Smart retries** (**Recommended**) — *"Retries soon, then spreads the rest across your window — most caps lift within the first hour."* Or **Every 24 hours** — *"Same time each day. Predictable, but slower to recover."* |
| **Max attempts** | 1 to 3 retries after the first send. Default 3 |
| **Retry window** | 1 to 7 days. Anything still blocked at the end is given up. Default 3 days |
| **Quiet hours** | Off by default. A start and end time when no retry runs — Smart retries space around it; Every 24 hours waits for the morning |

The step draws the resulting timeline and sums it up, for example *"Messages Meta refused with a
retryable error will be retried up to 3 times over 3 days."*

While a retry is booked the campaign shows **Retrying**, and the Overview's **Next batch** card shows
when it fires and how many *"Eligible messages"* it will take. **Cancel pending retries** drops it.
If nothing is left to retry when it fires, it cancels itself and the campaign completes.

### What is retried automatically

Only a recipient whose **latest** attempt failed with a retryable error:

| Retried | Examples |
|---------|----------|
| Yes — Meta's limits | Marketing frequency cap (131049), sending and spam rate limits |
| Yes — temporary errors | Meta or WhatsApp temporarily unavailable, RateUp could not reach WhatsApp |
| Yes — conditions that lift later | Outside the 24-hour window (131047), a throttled Flow (132069), a template still syncing (134101) |
| No | The number could not receive the message (131026), the contact opted out of marketing (131050), a template error, a payment-method problem |

Each retry uses a campaign credit, which is why permanent failures are never retried — waiting does
not change them.

## Retry Failed Recipients Yourself

On a broadcast's **Participants** tab:

| Button | Re-sends to |
|--------|-------------|
| **Retry now** on a failed row | That one recipient |
| **Retry selected** | The failed recipients you ticked |
| **Retry all failed** | Every failed recipient — the button shows how many |

**Retry now** on a booked retry in **Next batch** fires it straight away.

A manual retry re-sends to failed recipients **whatever the reason**, including failures an
automatic retry would skip. Check the **Reason** column first: a number that could not receive the
message will fail again, and spend another credit doing so.

Retry buttons are disabled while the campaign is still queuing or pausing — *"Wait for the
campaign's current send to finish."* Drips have no retry.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| No **Resume** button | Everyone has already been messaged | Nothing is left to send; retry failures instead |
| Resume sent nothing to failed recipients | Resume only targets recipients never messaged | Use **Retry all failed** |
| No **Pause** button | Nothing is left in the queue, or the campaign is a drip | Use **Pause Drip** on a drip |
| **Retry now** on Next batch is disabled | *"Nothing to retry — no recipient failed with a retryable error."* | Nothing to do — remaining failures are permanent |
| Campaign keeps pausing straight after resuming | The cause is not fixed yet | Check [Billing](/settings/billing) or Meta's billing settings |
