A **campaign** sends an approved [template](/whatsapp/templates) to a set of contacts. It is how
you start conversations at scale rather than one at a time. Campaigns are open on every plan,
including Free.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/campaign/list.png" alt="Broadcast campaign cards: a draft with Continue setup and completed campaigns with delivery and read rates" />

---

## Two Kinds of Campaign

| Type | Sends |
|------|-------|
| **Broadcast** | *"One template to everyone, once."* You can pace it with a daily send limit and have Meta's blocks retried automatically |
| **Drip** | *"A sequence of messages spread over days."* Each **stage** is a template sent once, a set delay after the contact joins the campaign |

Broadcasts suit announcements and offers. Drips suit onboarding sequences and gradual re-engagement.
The type is chosen when you create the campaign and cannot be changed afterwards — create a new
campaign to switch.

## The Campaigns List

**Campaign** in the sidebar opens the list. Broadcasts and drips sit on separate **Broadcast** and
**Drip** tabs.

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Create Campaign** | Opens the [creation wizard](/campaign/creating-a-campaign) |
| **Search campaigns...** | Find a campaign by name |
| Sort | Order the cards, newest first by default |
| **Filters** | Narrow by **Team** and **Status**, then **Apply** — **Reset** clears both |

The line under the title counts your campaigns and drafts and, on a metered plan, shows how many
**campaign credits** you have left.

Each campaign is a card. A **Draft** card shows **Continue setup** and reopens the wizard where you
left off; any other card opens the [campaign page](/campaign/campaign-details). A campaign that has
gone out shows *"N% delivered · N% read"*; one that has not shows *"Not sent yet"*.

## Campaign Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Being set up in the wizard. Nothing has been sent |
| **Scheduled** | Launched with a future send time, and waiting for it |
| **Sending** | Messages are going out. Has a moving dot |
| **Retrying** | The first send has finished and an automatic retry is booked for later. Nothing is going out in the meantime |
| **Pausing** | You pressed **Pause** and the queue is being emptied |
| **Paused** (amber) | You paused it. Recipients not yet messaged are waiting for you to resume |
| **Paused** (red) | RateUp paused it because your account could not send — see [auto-pause](/campaign/pausing-and-retrying#when-rateup-pauses-a-campaign) |
| **Completed** | Sending is finished. Delivery and read receipts keep arriving afterwards |
| **Failed** | The campaign stopped on an error. Hover the badge to see why |

The **Status** filter uses the same labels, so **Sending** also catches a campaign that is still
being queued, and **Paused** catches both kinds.

## Before You Send

<Callout type="warn">
  Campaigns are the fastest way to damage a WhatsApp account. Sending an irrelevant message to a
  large list produces blocks and reports, which lower your quality rating and reduce how much you
  may send afterwards — a cost that outlasts the campaign by months.
</Callout>

| Check | Why |
|-------|-----|
| The template is **approved** | Only approved templates are listed in the wizard |
| The audience has given **consent** | Leave **Only send to contacts who opted in** switched on |
| The list is **segmented** | Relevance is what stops people blocking you |
| The variables map to the right fields | Use **Send test to me** on the Review step first |
