## Overview

The headline view of the campaign:

| Shown | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Total Participants** | How many contacts are in it |
| **Template** | Which template is being sent |
| **Status** | Draft, Scheduled, Active, Completed or Inactive |
| **Team** | The owning team |

**Back to Campaigns** returns to the list; **Refresh** pulls the latest figures.

---

## Participants

The per-contact list, with **name**, **phone**, **consent** and delivery **status**.

Search by name or phone to find one person, and filter by status to see only failures.

| Action | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Add Participant** | Add someone to a campaign already built |
| **Resend** | Try again for a contact whose message failed |
| **Delete Participants** | Remove people from the campaign |

<Callout type="info">
  Check **consent** in this list before sending, not after. A participant without consent will fail
  — and every failure counts against your account.
</Callout>

## Delivery Report

The **Delivery Report** breaks the send down by outcome: delivered, read and failed.

Read it alongside [Message Analytics](/analytics/message-analytics), which puts this campaign in the
context of everything else you send.

### What the numbers tell you

| Pattern | Likely meaning |
|---------|----------------|
| High failure rate | List quality, or missing consent |
| High delivery, low read | The message is not compelling, or arrived at a bad time |
| High read, no replies | The call to action is unclear |
| Replies flooding the Inbox | Good problem — check staffing before the next send |

## Controlling a Drip

Running drip campaigns can be paused and resumed:

| Control | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Pause Drip** | Stops sending; already-sent messages are unaffected |
| **Start Drip** | Resumes from where it stopped |

<Callout type="warn">
  Pause immediately if you spot a mistake in a live drip — a wrong link, a broken variable. You
  cannot recall what has already gone, but you can stop the rest.
</Callout>

---

## Exporting

| Format | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Download as Excel** (.xlsx) | Analysis in a spreadsheet |
| **Download as OpenDocument** (.ods) | Open-format alternative |

Exports contain participant phone numbers — treat them as personal data.

---

## Deleting a Campaign

**Delete Campaign** removes it and its report.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting destroys the delivery record. If you want the history for future comparison, export it
  first.
</Callout>
