## Before You Start

You need:

- An **approved** [template](/whatsapp/templates)
- A connected [WhatsApp account](/settings/channels)
- An audience — a [group](/contacts/groups), a label filter, or selected contacts
- **Consent** from the people you are about to message

---

## Creating It

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create Campaign** and give it a name you will recognise in a report three months from now.
</Step>
<Step>
Select the **team** the campaign belongs to.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the **template**. Only approved templates appear.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in the template's **variables** — map them to contact fields so each person gets their own
name, order number or date.
</Step>
<Step>
Build the **audience**: pick a group, filter by label or lifecycle stage, or add participants
individually with **Add Participant**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose **Broadcast** or **Drip**.

For a drip, set the **daily send limit** — or **No limit** to send as fast as possible.
</Step>
<Step>
**Send** now, or schedule it for later.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Choosing the Audience Well

Relevance is the whole game. A message that lands wrong does not just underperform — it generates
blocks that make every future campaign worse.

| Segment by | Using |
|-----------|-------|
| Explicit list | [Groups](/contacts/groups) |
| A trait | [Labels](/contacts/labels) |
| Funnel position | [Lifecycle stages](/contacts/lifecycle) |
| Custom data | [Custom fields](/contacts/custom-fields) |

<Callout type="info">
  Three targeted campaigns of 200 people almost always beat one campaign of 600. You get better
  results and you protect your account.
</Callout>

## Variables

Templates carry variables that personalise each message. Map every one before sending.

<Callout type="warn">
  A mis-mapped variable goes out to your entire list at once and cannot be recalled. Send the
  campaign to yourself, or to a group of one, before running it for real.
</Callout>

## Daily Send Limits

For drip campaigns, the daily limit paces the send.

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **A number** | At most that many messages per day |
| **No limit** | Sends as fast as the channel allows |

Pacing is not just about volume. A steady drip is easier for your team to handle — replies arrive
in a manageable stream instead of a flood no one can answer.

---

## Scheduling

Scheduled campaigns show as **Scheduled** until they run.

Send when your audience will actually read it, and — more importantly — when your team is around to
handle the replies. A campaign that goes out at 6pm on a Friday produces a Monday morning backlog.
