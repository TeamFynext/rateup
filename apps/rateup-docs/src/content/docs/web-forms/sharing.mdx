## Link

**Copy URL** gives you the form's address. It works anywhere a link does — email, SMS, social,
your website.

The form must be [published](/web-forms/settings) first.

---

## QR Code

**Download QR Code** produces a printable code. **Download all QRs** gets every code at once when
you have several.

Use QR codes for anything physical: receipts, table tents, packaging, posters, event badges.

<Callout type="info">
  For anything printed, consider pointing a [QR Studio code](/qr-studio/qr-creator) at the form
  instead. The printed code stays valid even if you later replace the form — a QR printed on 5,000
  receipts is not something you want tied to one survey forever.
</Callout>

## WhatsApp

Send the form from a WhatsApp conversation, a [template](/whatsapp/templates) button, or a
[campaign](/campaign).

Response rates over WhatsApp are typically much higher than email — the message arrives where the
customer already is, and the link opens in one tap.

Configure the **start message** and **template message** so the invitation reads like your brand
rather than a bare URL.

---

## Embedding

**Generate IFrame** produces embed code for your own site.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Embed as iframe** or **Create an iframe to embed survey**.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the **width** and **height**, with their units.
</Step>
<Step>
**Copy code** and paste it into your page.
</Step>
</Steps>

Check the embed on a phone. An iframe with a fixed pixel height is the usual cause of a form that
scrolls awkwardly inside a box on mobile.

## URL Tags

**Manage URL Tags** lets you add parameters to the form's link so you know where each response came
from.

| Placement | Tag |
|-----------|-----|
| Receipt QR | `source=receipt` |
| Email campaign | `source=email-june` |
| Table tent | `source=table` |

The tag arrives with the response, so *"which placement actually works?"* becomes a question with
an answer instead of a guess.

<Callout type="info">
  Tag every placement from day one. Adding tags later means the first months of responses are
  permanently unattributable.
</Callout>

---

## Redirects

**Edit Redirect** sends respondents to a URL of your choosing after submitting — your site, a
reward page, or a [Google review](/web-forms/automations).
