## Responses

Every individual submission, with its answers, timestamp and any
[URL tags](/web-forms/sharing) it carried.

Read these when you need the detail: a specific complaint, a lead's contact details, the wording
someone used.

---

## Analytics

### Happiness Index

An overall satisfaction score across responses, from **Poor** through **Okay**, **Good**,
**Great** and **Excellent**.

### NPS breakdown

Net Promoter Score responses split three ways:

| Group | Score | Meaning |
|-------|-------|---------|
| **Promoters** | 9–10 | Will recommend you |
| **Passives** | 7–8 | Satisfied but not enthusiastic |
| **Detractors** | 0–6 | Unhappy, and may say so |

<Callout type="info">
  **Passives are the opportunity.** Promoters already advocate and detractors are a rescue job;
  passives are the group closest to becoming promoters if you find out what is missing. Use a
  [logic jump](/web-forms/logic-and-variables) to ask them specifically.
</Callout>

### Per-question charts

Each question gets its own visualisation — distributions for choice and rating questions, response
listings for free text.

## AI Summaries

| Feature | Produces |
|---------|----------|
| **Generate Summary by AI** | A written summary of the responses |
| **Generate Team wise summary** | The same, broken down by team |
| **Large Text Summary** | A digest of long free-text answers |

This is most valuable on free text, where hundreds of paragraphs are otherwise unreadable.

<Callout type="warn">
  Treat a summary as a starting point, not a finding. Read a sample of the raw responses before
  acting — a summary smooths over exactly the sharp, specific complaint you most needed to see.
</Callout>

## Exporting

| Format | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Excel / CSV** | Analysis elsewhere |
| **Export to PDF** | A shareable report |
| **Google Sheet** | Live sync to a spreadsheet |

<Callout type="warn">
  Exports contain whatever respondents told you, including contact details. Treat them as personal
  data.
</Callout>

Filter before exporting — by team, date or response content — rather than exporting everything and
trimming later.

## Acting on Results

A survey nobody acts on trains customers not to answer the next one.

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Detractor response | Follow up personally — see [Automations](/web-forms/automations) |
| Repeated complaint about one thing | Fix the thing; the survey has done its job |
| Promoters | Ask for a [Google review](/web-forms/automations) or a [testimonial](/testimonials) |
| Falling response rate | The form is too long, or you are asking too often |
