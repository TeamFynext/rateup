## Responses

Every individual submission, with its answers, timestamp and any [URL tags](/web-forms/sharing) it
carried. Search it, narrow it with the column filters and the date-range picker, and **Refresh** to
pull in new submissions.

Read these when you need the detail: a specific complaint, a lead's contact details, the wording
someone used.

## Analytics

*"No data to analyse yet"* shows until the first response arrives — *"Charts appear here once the
first responses come in."*

### The headline numbers

| Card | Counts |
|------|--------|
| **Total Responses** | Completed submissions |
| **Started** | Visitors who began answering |
| **Total Visits** | How many times the survey was opened |
| **Google Review Redirects** | Respondents sent on to a review page — see [Automations](/web-forms/automations) |
| **Response Rate** | Responses as a share of visits, with a band: **Great** (70% or more), **Good** (50%+), **Low** (30%+) or **Too Low** |

### Happiness Index

*"How respondents felt, from their scale answers."* A score **Out of 10**, labelled by band:

| Score | Label |
|-------|-------|
| 9 to 10 | **Very Happy** |
| 7 to under 9 | **Happy** |
| 5 to under 7 | **Neutral** |
| 3 to under 5 | **Unhappy** |
| Under 3 | **Very Unhappy** |

Below the score, the same five moods show how many responses fell into each.

### NPS Overview

*"Promoters minus detractors, from −100 to +100."* A gauge shows the score, labelled **Excellent**
(50 or more), **Great** (30+), **Okay** (0+) or **Poor** (below 0), with the three groups beneath:

| Group | Score given | Meaning |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Promoters** | 9–10 | Will recommend you |
| **Passives** | 7–8 | Satisfied but not enthusiastic |
| **Detractors** | 0–6 | Unhappy, and may say so |

<Callout type="info">
  **Passives are the opportunity.** Promoters already advocate and detractors are a rescue job;
  passives are the group closest to becoming promoters if you find out what is missing. Use a
  [logic jump](/web-forms/logic-and-variables) to ask them specifically.
</Callout>

### Question breakdown

*"How each answer was distributed."* Every question gets its own chart — vote counts for choice and
rating questions, with the **Most voted** answer marked, and response listings for free text.

**Export to PDF** saves the Analytics tab as a report.

## Compare Teams

**Team Analytics** shows the same result set **between teams** — useful when several branches or
departments run the same survey and you need to know which is ahead.

| Chart | Shows |
|-------|-------|
| **Team Response Activity** | How many responses each team collected |
| **Team Question Activity** | Answers to each question, team by team — as a **Bar**, **Pie** or **Radar** chart |

*"No responses found for comparison"* means the selected teams have nothing to compare yet.

## Summarise Long Answers with AI

| Feature | Produces |
|---------|----------|
| **Generate Summary by AI** | A written summary of the responses |
| **Generate Team wise summary** | The same, broken down by team |
| **Large Text Summary** | A digest of long free-text answers |

This is most valuable on free text, where hundreds of paragraphs are otherwise unreadable.

<Callout type="warn">
  Treat a summary as a starting point, not a finding. Read a sample of the raw responses before
  acting — a summary smooths over exactly the sharp, specific complaint you most needed to see.
</Callout>

## Export Responses

**Export** on the Responses tab offers:

| Menu item | Gives you |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Download as Excel** | The responses as `.xlsx`, with the columns currently shown |
| **Download as OpenDocument (ODS)** | The same, as `.ods` |
| **Sync with Google Sheet** | Creates a spreadsheet in a **RateUp** folder in your connected Google Drive, with one tab per language (one tab for a WhatsApp Flow survey), and fills it with every response. After that, each new response is added as a row as it arrives |
| **Resync with Google Sheet** | Shown once a sheet exists. Rewrites the whole sheet from the current responses, after a confirmation |

Once a survey is synced, its **Sheet Url** appears under **GoogleSheet** in the survey's
[Settings](/web-forms/settings). Google Sheets must be connected under
[Integrations](/settings/integrations) first.

**Export** is not offered on an encrypted survey.

<Callout type="warn">
  Exports contain whatever respondents told you, including contact details. Treat them as personal
  data.
</Callout>

Filter before exporting — by team, date or response content — rather than exporting everything and
trimming later.

## Acting on Results

A survey nobody acts on trains customers not to answer the next one.

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Detractor response | Follow up personally — see [Automations](/web-forms/automations) |
| Repeated complaint about one thing | Fix the thing; the survey has done its job |
| Promoters | Ask for a [Google review](/web-forms/automations) or a [testimonial](/testimonials) |
| Falling response rate | The survey is too long, or you are asking too often |
