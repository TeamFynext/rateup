## Start Blank or From a Template

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create Survey**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose **Start Blank**, or **Create Survey from Template** to begin with a ready-made form.
</Step>
<Step>
Name it and select the **team** it belongs to.
</Step>
<Step>
The editor opens on the **Questions** tab.
</Step>
</Steps>

Templates are worth browsing even if you end up starting blank — **Try this Template** shows how a
well-structured form is put together. If none match, you will see *"No templates found"*.

---

## Adding Questions

The editor has three parts: the **question list** down the left, the **Page Editor** in the centre
with a **Preview** button in its header, and the **Add Element** pane you pick new questions from.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/web-forms/editor.png" alt="The form editor" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add Question**, then pick a [type](/web-forms/question-types) from **Add Element**. The tiles
are grouped by category — type in **Search elements…** to jump straight to one.
</Step>
<Step>
Write the question in **Enter question here**.
</Step>
<Step>
Add a **description** if the question needs context — but if it needs a paragraph, rewrite the
question.
</Step>
<Step>
Configure the type-specific options.
</Step>
<Step>
Reorder questions from the sidebar by dragging.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Writing Questions People Answer

Completion rate is decided almost entirely by the questions, not the design.

| Do | Instead of |
|----|-----------|
| Ask one thing per question | Bundling two into one sentence |
| Use the customer's words | Internal jargon |
| Make optional questions optional | Requiring everything |
| Put the easy questions first | Opening with a long free-text box |
| Stop at five or six questions | Twenty "while we have you" extras |

<Callout type="warn">
  Every extra question costs completions. Before adding one, ask what decision its answer will
  change — if there is no answer to that, cut it.
</Callout>

---

## Previewing

Use **Preview** to take the form as a customer would. Check it on a phone: most responses arrive on
one, and layouts that work on a laptop often do not.

The editor flags problems with an **Issue with your survey** warning — resolve those before
sharing.

---

## Status

A form is **Active** or **Paused**. Paused forms do not accept responses, which is the safe way to
stop collection without deleting anything.
