## Themes

A **theme** is a saved set of colours, fonts and logo you can reuse across forms.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create New Theme** and enter a **theme name**.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the colours: **background**, **button**, **input**, **title**, **subtitle**.
</Step>
<Step>
Upload your **logo**, and set its **width** and **height**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose a **font weight** — Thin through Extra Bold.
</Step>
<Step>
**Save & Apply**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Preview in both **Desktop** and **Mobile**. Most responses arrive on a phone, so if you only check
one, check mobile.

<Callout type="warn">
  Keep text and background contrast high. A pale grey question on a white background looks elegant
  on your monitor and is unreadable outdoors on a phone — which is exactly where a QR-code
  respondent will be.
</Callout>

## The Pages

A form is made of pages, each configurable:

| Page | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| **Welcome page** | The first screen — sets expectations |
| **Survey page** | Where the questions live |
| **Thank-you page** | What people see after submitting |
| **Language page** | Lets respondents choose a language |
| **Custom HTML page** | Your own markup, for full control |

---

### Welcome page

The welcome page decides whether someone starts at all.

- Say what the form is about in one line
- Say **how long it takes** — this single sentence lifts completion more than anything else on the
  page
- Say what happens with the answers, if that is a concern

Keep it to a title, a sentence and a button.

---

### Thank-you page

The most under-used page in any survey tool. Do not waste it on the word "Thanks".

| Use it to | Example |
|-----------|---------|
| Route happy respondents onward | A [Google review](/web-forms/automations) link |
| Deliver a promised reward | A coupon code |
| Continue the relationship | A link to your WhatsApp |
| Celebrate | **Enable Confetti** |

---

### Language page

Lets respondents pick their language before starting. Worth adding wherever you serve more than one
— people abandon forms in a language they are not comfortable in rather than struggling through.

---

### Custom HTML page

For when the built-in pages are not enough and you need full control of the markup.

---

## Consistency

Build one theme that matches your brand and apply it to every form. A form that looks like your
website is trusted; one that looks like generic software gets treated as spam.
