The **Meta AI Agent** answers your customers on WhatsApp using Meta's own Business Agent. It runs at
Meta, not in RateUp — you configure what it knows, what it may do and who it is allowed to talk to,
and Meta does the answering.

Each area is its own page in the sidebar under **Meta AI Agent**, and every page works against one
WhatsApp number, chosen from the **Select WhatsApp number** picker at the top.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/meta-agent/overview.png" alt="The Meta AI Agent Overview page" />

<Callout type="info">
  The section only appears if your plan includes the AI chatbot feature. Until a number is
  onboarded, the header shows **Not onboarded** and the other pages have nothing to configure.
</Callout>
