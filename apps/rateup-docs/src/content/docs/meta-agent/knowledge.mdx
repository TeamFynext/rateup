**Knowledge** is what the agent answers from: your business details, FAQs, documents and website
pages. It sits on four tabs — **Business info**, **FAQs**, **Files** and **Websites** — and the step
is ticked on the setup guide as soon as any one of them has something in it.

## Fill In Business Info

Structured facts about the business, kept as fields rather than prose so the agent quotes them
consistently. *"Every field is optional — the agent uses whichever ones you fill in."*

| Field | What goes in it |
|-------|-----------------|
| **Business description** | What you sell and who you sell it to. The agent leans on this most |
| **Payment methods** | For example: cards, UPI, net banking, cash on delivery |
| **Return policy** | For example: unopened items can be returned within 14 days |
| **Purchase info** | How customers order, minimums, bulk pricing |
| **Delivery and shipping** | Dispatch times, courier partners, shipping charges |
| **Contact email**, **Address**, **Hours of operation** | Under **Contact info** — where and when customers reach a human |

Click **Save & push to Meta** to store it.

**Reset** asks *"Reset business info at Meta?"* and clears every business info field Meta holds for
this number. FAQs, files and websites are untouched.

---

## Add FAQs

Question-and-answer pairs the agent can quote verbatim. Click **Add FAQ**, fill in **Question** and
**Answer**, then **Create FAQ**. Editing one reopens it as **Edit FAQ**.

Anything a customer asks weekly and that has one correct answer belongs here rather than in a
skill — an FAQ is the answer itself, a skill is the instruction for finding one.

---

## Upload Files

Documents Meta indexes for the agent: price lists, policy PDFs, product sheets. Click **Upload** and
choose a `.pdf`, `.doc`, `.docx`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.csv` or `.xlsx` file of up to 100MB.
Anything larger is refused before it is sent.

---

## Add Websites

Pages Meta crawls and indexes for the agent. Enter a **Page URL** and click **Add**. It must be a
public `https` page — Meta's crawler does not sign in.

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Not started** / **Queued** | Meta has not read the page yet |
| **Crawling** | Meta is reading it now |
| **Crawled** | Read and indexed |
| **No content found** | Meta reached the page but found nothing to index |
| **Failed** | Meta could not read it |

Crawl status does not refresh by itself — click **Sync** to fetch the latest.

<Callout type="warn">
  Everything here is customer-facing by proxy. An out-of-date return policy or price list is worse
  in the agent's mouth than on a web page, because the customer will read it as a promise.
</Callout>

---

## Keep RateUp and Meta in Step

Every tab has a **Sync** button that pulls Meta's copy into RateUp. Use it after changing anything
directly at Meta, or when a save timed out. **Business info** also says when it was last synced —
*"Synced 5m ago"*, or *"Never synced with Meta"*.
