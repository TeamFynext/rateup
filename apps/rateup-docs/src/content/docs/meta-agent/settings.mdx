**Settings** is where the agent goes live, and where you bound what it does once it is.

---

## Rollout & Behaviour

| Control | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Rollout enabled** | Whether the agent answers at all |
| **AI audience** | **Everyone**, or **Allowlisted numbers only** |
| **Handoff message** | Shown when the agent hands a conversation to a human |
| **Custom handoff message** | Off means Meta sends its own translated message instead |
| **Follow up when a customer goes quiet** | Whether the agent nudges an abandoned conversation |
| **Follow-up message** | What that nudge says |
| **Never-say phrases** | Exact phrases the agent must never produce — press Enter after each one |

Follow-up timing is chosen from a fixed set: Meta only supports those intervals.

---

## Roll Out Safely

<Callout type="warn">
  **Allowlisted numbers only** is how you test against real conversations without exposing every
  customer to a half-configured agent. Switch to **Everyone** once you have watched it answer.
</Callout>

Never-say phrases are the cheapest safeguard here. Add the promises your business cannot keep —
guaranteed delivery dates, refund commitments, medical or legal advice — as exact phrases.
