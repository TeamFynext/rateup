**Go live** is where the agent is switched on, and where you bound what it does once it is. It has
four panels: **Rollout & behaviour**, **Inbox handover**, **Allowlist** and **Usage caps**.

## Switch the Agent On

<Steps>
  <Step>Set **AI audience** to **Allowlisted numbers only**, and add your own team's numbers under **Allowlist**.</Step>
  <Step>Turn on **Agent is on**.</Step>
  <Step>Click **Save & push to Meta**.</Step>
  <Step>Message the number from an allowlisted phone and watch it answer. When you are happy, switch **AI audience** to **Everyone** and save again.</Step>
</Steps>

The header badge turns **Live** and the setup guide shows **Your agent is live.**

| Control | What it does |
|---------|-------------|
| **Agent is on** | The master switch. Off, the agent answers nobody, whatever else is configured. Turning it back on only affects conversations that start afterwards |
| **Release the conversation after a handoff** | On, the agent gives up the conversation when it hands a customer to your team, so your team can reply. Off keeps the agent in control after it sends the handoff message |
| **Handoff message** | What the customer is told at the handoff — see below |
| **Follow up when a customer goes quiet** | Whether the agent nudges a conversation the customer has abandoned |
| **After** | How long to wait: **5 minutes**, **15 minutes**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **2 hours**, **8 hours** or **24 hours**. Meta only supports these intervals |
| **Follow-up message** | What that nudge says, up to 1,024 characters |
| **AI audience** | **Everyone**, or **Allowlisted numbers only** |
| **Never-say phrases** | Exact phrases the agent must never produce — press Enter after each one, up to 50 |

Nothing on this panel takes effect until you click **Save & push to Meta**.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Everyone** needs a payment method on your Meta Business Agent account — Meta refuses to switch
  it on without one, and the allowlist is then ignored. **Allowlisted numbers only** needs no
  payment method, and is how you test against real conversations without exposing every customer to
  a half-configured agent.
</Callout>

## Choose the Handoff Message

| Option | What the customer gets |
|--------|------------------------|
| **Meta's standard message** | Meta's own wording, translated for the customer |
| **Written by the agent** | The agent writes its own line for the moment |
| **My own message** | Your text, up to 1,024 characters — for example *"Let me get a colleague to help with that — one moment."* |

Sending the message and releasing the conversation are separate: with **Release the conversation
after a handoff** off, the customer is told they are being handed over but the agent keeps the
conversation.

## Decide What Happens After a Handover

Once Meta AI hands a customer to your team, it stays silent for them until the conversation is
handed back. The **Inbox handover** panel decides whether closing the ticket does that.

| Setting | Default | What it does |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| **Hand back to Meta AI when a ticket is closed** | On | Closing or resolving the customer's ticket hands the conversation back to Meta AI. Spam is never handed back |

This switch saves straight away and is not sent to Meta. Your team can also hand a conversation back
by hand — from the conversation in the [inbox](/inbox/responding-to-conversation), or from
[Test → Thread control](/meta-agent/test#hand-a-conversation-back-to-meta-ai).

<Callout type="warn">
  Any message RateUp sends to a customer takes the conversation off Meta AI — not only a teammate's
  reply, but an automation, a campaign or a message sent through the API. WhatsApp gives the
  conversation to whoever sends. A broadcast to customers the agent is serving silences it for each
  of them until they are handed back.
</Callout>

## Add Numbers to the Allowlist

The **Allowlist** is the numbers the agent answers while **AI audience** is **Allowlisted numbers
only**. Enter a **Consumer phone number** with its country code and click **Add**; remove one with
the cross on its chip.

Meta allows **20 numbers**. With the allowlisted-only audience and an empty list, the agent answers
no one.

## Cap the Agent's Usage

**Usage caps** limit how much the agent does so costs stay predictable. With no caps, usage is
unlimited.

<Steps>
  <Step>Click **Add a cap**.</Step>
  <Step>Choose the **Limit**: **AI replies per conversation** or **Tokens across the business**.</Step>
  <Step>Choose the **Window**: **Rolling 1 day**, **Rolling 7 days**, **Rolling 14 days** or **Rolling 30 days**.</Step>
  <Step>Enter the **Cap**, then click **Save & push to Meta**.</Step>
</Steps>

When a cap is reached, the agent finishes its current reply, then hands new messages to your team
until the window rolls over. Saving replaces the whole set at Meta, so removing a row and saving
deletes that cap.

Only people with the **Connect systems and credentials** ability can change caps; everyone else sees
*"Only admins can change usage caps."*

---

## Roll Out Safely

Never-say phrases are the cheapest safeguard here. Add the promises your business cannot keep —
guaranteed delivery dates, refund commitments, medical or legal advice — as exact phrases.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Meta refuses to switch **AI audience** to **Everyone** | No payment method on the Meta Business Agent account | Add one at Meta, or stay on **Allowlisted numbers only** |
| The agent answers nobody | **Agent is on** is off, or the audience is allowlist-only with an empty list | Switch it on and save; add numbers or choose **Everyone** |
| The agent stopped answering one customer | The conversation was handed over, or RateUp sent them a message | Hand it back from the inbox or **Test → Thread control** |
| Customers are being sent to your team mid-day | A usage cap was reached | Raise the cap, or wait for the window to roll over |
| **Add** is disabled on the allowlist | The list is full at 20 numbers | Remove one first |
