A **board** is one workflow. Each board has its own columns, labels and team access.

## Creating a Board

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create Board**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **board name** — the placeholder suggests the idea: *Customer Support*, *Bug Tracker*.
</Step>
<Step>
Answer **"What kind of work lives here?"** with a short description, so people joining later know
what belongs on it.
</Step>
<Step>
**Select teams** that should have access. Use **All Teams** for a board everyone works from.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Columns

Columns are the stages work moves through. A ticket sits in exactly one column at a time, and
moving it between columns is how progress is recorded.

Common shapes:

| Board | Columns |
|-------|---------|
| **Bug tracker** | Reported → Confirmed → In progress → Fixed |
| **Refund approvals** | Requested → Under review → Approved → Paid |
| **Feature requests** | Received → Considering → Planned → Shipped |

<Callout type="info">
  Keep columns to four or five. More than that and people stop moving cards, which makes the board
  a worse record than no board at all.
</Callout>

---

## Board Labels

Each board has its own **board labels** — a cross-cutting way to mark tickets that columns cannot
express: severity, product area, customer tier.

Manage them from the board; if none exist yet you will see *"No labels found on this board"*.

---

## Team Access

Boards are scoped to teams. Someone outside the selected teams does not see the board at all.

Use this to keep sensitive workflows — refunds, HR, escalations — away from the whole
organisation, without creating a separate system for them.

---

## Editing and Deleting

| Action | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Edit Board** | Change the name, description or team access |
| **Search boards** | Find a board by name |
| **Delete board** | Removes the board and everything on it |

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a board deletes its tickets, comments and attachments. If a board is finished rather
  than wrong, archive the work by moving live tickets elsewhere first.
</Callout>
