## Tickets vs Inbox Tickets

The two are different things with an unfortunate name overlap:

| | [Inbox tickets](/inbox/tickets) | Ticket boards |
|---|-------------------------------|---------------|
| **What it is** | One customer conversation | A unit of internal work |
| **Created by** | A customer messaging you | A person, deliberately |
| **Lives in** | The Inbox | A board column |
| **Ends when** | The conversation is closed | The work is done |

A customer reports a bug in the Inbox; you create a **board ticket** to get it fixed. The two link
together — see **Open Chat In Inbox** on any ticket that came from a conversation.

## When to Use a Board

| Situation | Board? |
|-----------|--------|
| A customer question you can answer now | No — handle it in the Inbox |
| A bug that needs a developer | Yes |
| A refund awaiting approval | Yes |
| A follow-up in three weeks | Yes |
| A conversation waiting on the customer | No — leave it in the Inbox |

<Callout type="info">
  If the work will outlive the conversation, or needs someone outside the support team, it belongs
  on a board.
</Callout>
