**Messages** (`/dashboard/loyalty/messages`) is *"Everything your program says on WhatsApp — what
sends automatically, how it replies, and the templates behind them."* It has four tabs:
**Automatic**, **Replies**, **Chatbot** and **Templates**. The sidebar entry counts templates still
awaiting Meta. *When* scheduled messages send is set on [Reminders](/loyalty/reminders).

## The Automatic Messages

The **Automatic** tab lists every message the programme can send, in three groups. Each card has an
on/off switch and a status badge.

| Group | Message | Sent |
|-------|---------|------|
| **New customers** | **Welcome a new customer** | The first time someone messages you. Its **Join** button signs them up |
| | **Welcome a new member** | When staff sign a customer up at the counter |
| **Sent by a transaction** | **When points are earned** | After every purchase that earns points |
| | **When points are adjusted** | When staff correct a balance and the change is approved |
| | **When a gift is given** | When staff hand a customer a free gift with a code |
| | **When a reward is redeemed** | When a customer spends points on a reward |
| | **Tier upgrade** | The moment someone reaches a new tier |
| **Reminders** | **Birthday gift**, **Inactivity reminder**, **Points expiry warning**, **Points expired**, **VIP status at risk**, **Reward expiring**, **Joining anniversary** | On a schedule — **Set when these send** opens [Reminders](/loyalty/reminders) |

| Badge | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Approved** | Meta approved the template — it reaches everyone |
| **Awaiting Meta** | In review, usually a few hours |
| **Rejected by Meta** | Rewrite it and submit again |
| **Draft — not submitted** | Saved in the template editor but never sent to Meta |
| **Not written** | *"Nothing written yet — customers get no message for this."* |
| **Off** | Switched off |

With no messages yet, **Set up the standard notifications** writes points earned, reward redeemed,
tier upgrade and birthday for your programme and submits them to Meta in one go.

## Write or Change a Message

<Steps>
<Step>
On the message's card, click **Write it** (or **Edit message**).
</Step>
<Step>
Edit the words. The details filled in when it sends — the customer's name, their points — are shown
under the box.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save and submit to Meta**. *"Submitted to Meta — usually approved within a few hours."*
</Step>
</Steps>

For an image, a footer or buttons, click **Open in WhatsApp template editor** — the same editor as
[WhatsApp › Templates](/whatsapp/template-editor). Nothing is saved until you save there; a draft you
started shows on the card, ready to carry on. A message with an image, footer or buttons can only be
edited in that editor. **Advanced** lets you pick an existing loyalty template instead.

<Callout type="info">
  Until Meta approves it, a message still reaches customers who messaged you in the last 24 hours —
  *"Sent as plain text to customers with an open chat."* That depends on **Send as a plain message
  inside the 24-hour window** in [Settings](/loyalty/settings).
</Callout>

## Set Up Keyword Replies

The **Replies** tab holds what the programme answers when a customer texts a keyword — *"Plain
replies — they work the moment your program is live. No approval needed."*

| Keyword | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| `join` | **Joining the program** — the welcome sent the moment someone signs up |
| `points` | **Checking their balance** — what they have, spent and earned |
| `refer` | **Referring a friend** — their referral code and joining link |
| `rewards` | **Browsing rewards** — what they can redeem right now |
| `card` | **Getting their card** — their QR card to show at the counter |
| `stop` | **Leaving the program** — removes them, and says how to come back |

<Steps>
<Step>
With none set up, click **Add the standard replies** — *"Join, points, rewards, refer and stop —
written for your program, editable afterwards."*
</Step>
<Step>
To change one, click **Edit**. Set **They text** and **You reply**, or **Write it from my program**.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally pick a **Template for customers who haven't messaged yet**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  A plain reply *"Only reaches customers who have messaged you in the last 24 hours."* A customer
  registered at the till who has never written to you gets nothing unless the reply has a template —
  use **Create a template for the rest**.
</Callout>

A keyword already used by another automation or command is not saved — *"is already in use
elsewhere in your inbox."* Pick another word.

## Turn Replies Into a Tappable Menu

The **Chatbot** tab turns your keyword replies into a WhatsApp list, so customers pick from a menu
instead of having to know the words. *"Every keyword below becomes a row customers can tap."*

<Steps>
<Step>
Write your keyword replies on **Replies** first — the menu is built from them.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the **WhatsApp account** and a **Trigger word** customers text to open the menu. It cannot be one
of your keywords.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create the chatbot**. This *"Adds a chatbot under WhatsApp › Chatbot, and a rule that opens
it."*
</Step>
</Steps>

When you change your keywords later, the tab says *"The menu is behind your keywords"* — click
**Update the menu**. **Open in flow editor** opens the chatbot in the
[chatbot builder](/whatsapp/chatbots).

## Manage the Templates

The **Templates** tab is the library of loyalty templates.

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Template** | Its name |
| **Account** | The WhatsApp number it belongs to |
| **Status** | **Approved**, **Awaiting Meta**, **Rejected** or **Draft** |
| **Used by** | Which messages use it — **Not used** if none |
| **Created** | When it was made |

**New template** starts from one of the programme's messages (**Automatic messages** or **Keyword
replies**) or **Start blank**, and opens the WhatsApp template editor. **Sync with Meta** refreshes
the statuses. A template can only be deleted when nothing uses it, and deleting removes it from
WhatsApp too.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Customers registered at the till get no welcome | No approved template, and they have not messaged you | Submit **Welcome a new member** to Meta and wait for **Approved** |
| A message never sends | Switched **Off**, **Not written**, or **Send WhatsApp notifications** is off in Settings | Check the card, then [Settings](/loyalty/settings) |
| *"messages could not be submitted"* | Meta refused the submission | Open the message and submit again |
| A keyword does not answer | It is in use elsewhere, or the programme is paused | Pick another word; resume in Settings |
