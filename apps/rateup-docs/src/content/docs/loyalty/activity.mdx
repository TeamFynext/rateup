**Activity** (`/dashboard/loyalty/activity`) is the ledger — *"Every point in and out"* — and the
place you approve requests. The sidebar entry shows how many requests are waiting.

## Read the Ledger

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **When** | Date and time of the movement |
| **Customer** | The member — click the name to open their record on [Members](/loyalty/customers) — and what it was for, such as a purchase amount |
| Points | The change, named after your points |
| **Type** | **Earned**, **Redeemed**, **Adjusted**, **Expired** or **Refunded** |

Filter with **All**, **Earned** or **Redeemed**. **Full history** on a customer in the
[Cashier](/loyalty/cashier-portal) opens Activity filtered to **One customer**; **Show every
customer** clears it.

The page's address keeps the filter and page, so a view can be bookmarked, reloaded and shared.

**Export** downloads the ledger with **Export as CSV** or **Export as Excel**.

## Approve or Reject a Request

**Pending** shows what is waiting on you — *"requests waiting on you"*. Each row says what it is,
the customer, and who asked.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Pending**.
</Step>
<Step>
Check the request. Look at the customer's history if the amount looks unusual.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Approve** to post it. *"Approving posts the points immediately and tells the customer on
WhatsApp."*
</Step>
<Step>
Or click **Reject**, answer *"Why is this being rejected?"*, and confirm. The note goes back to
whoever asked.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Request type | What it is |
|--------------|------------|
| **Points adjustment** | A correction to a balance, including **Undo** on a Cashier receipt |
| **Reward redemption** | A redemption that needs approval before it is handed over |
| **Tier override** | A member's tier set by hand |
| **Account suspension** / **Account reactivation** | Suspending or restoring a member |
| **Bulk operation** | A change to many members at once |

With **Auto-approve staff adjustments** on in [Settings](/loyalty/settings), staff corrections post
at once — *"only tier overrides and bulk operations land under Pending."*

<Callout type="warn">
  Auto-approval means a mistyped correction goes straight onto a customer's balance and messages
  them. If you turn it on, read the ledger regularly.
</Callout>
