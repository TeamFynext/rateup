## The Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Customer** | Name and contact details |
| **Tier** | Their current tier |
| **Points** | Current spendable balance |
| **Lifetime** | Total ever earned — this does not go down on redemption |
| **Last Active** | When they last did something |
| **Referral Code** | Their personal code |

Use **Filter** to narrow by tier or activity.

<Callout type="info">
  **Points** and **Lifetime** answer different questions. Points is what they can spend now;
  lifetime is how valuable they have been. Tier thresholds are based on the lifetime figure, which
  is why redeeming never demotes anyone.
</Callout>

---

## Customer Details

Open a member to see their full record — balance, tier, date of birth, transaction history and
referral information. **Back to Customers** returns to the list.

### Referral tools

| Action | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Copy referral code** | Give the customer their code over the phone or in chat |
| **Copy referral link** | Send them something they can forward directly |

If copying fails you will see *"Failed to copy referral code"* — usually a browser clipboard
permission.

## Exporting

| Format | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Export as CSV** | Import elsewhere |
| **Export as Excel** | Analysis in a spreadsheet |

<Callout type="warn">
  Member exports contain personal data including dates of birth. Handle and delete accordingly.
</Callout>

---

## Using the List Well

- Sort by **Last Active** to find members drifting away, and target them before they are gone
- Look at members just below a tier threshold — a nudge converts them
- Check high-lifetime members are actually redeeming; a big saver who never spends is disengaged
- Use **date of birth** with [birthday reminders](/loyalty/settings)
