**Customers** (`/dashboard/loyalty/customers`) is the member list for your programme. The sidebar
entry shows how many members you have.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/loyalty/customers.png" alt="Loyalty Customers table with tier, points, lifetime points and referral code" />

---

## The Columns

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Customer** | Name and phone number |
| **Tier** | Their current tier |
| **Points** | What they can spend now |
| **Lifetime** | Every point they have ever earned — it does not go down when they redeem |
| **Date of Birth** | Used by the birthday reminder |
| **Last Active** | When they last did something |
| **Referral Code** | Their personal code, with **Copy referral code** and **Copy referral link** |

Search with **Search by name or phone...**.

---

## Open a Member

Click a member's name to open their record — the same view staff see in the
[Cashier](/loyalty/cashier-portal): balance, lifetime points, how far they are from the next tier,
total spend and their last visits. **Customers** at the top goes back to the list.

Customer names in the [Activity](/loyalty/activity) ledger open the same record, and the link can be
bookmarked or shared.

## Edit a Member's Details

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the member and click **Edit details**.
</Step>
<Step>
Change the **Name**, **Date of birth**, **Gender** or **Email**. The number they joined on cannot be
changed here.
</Step>
<Step>
If the programme sends on more than one channel, pick their **Preferred channel**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Field | Note |
|-------|------|
| **Date of birth** | *"Needed for the birthday reminder. Leave it empty to remove it."* A birthday cannot be in the future |
| **Gender** | **Male**, **Female**, **Other** or **Not given** |
| **Preferred channel** | *"Where they hear from you. The programme falls back to its other channels if this one cannot reach them."* |
| **Email** | Optional |

## Export Members

**Export Data** offers **Export as CSV**, **Export as Excel** and **Export as OpenDocument (ODS)**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Member exports contain personal data, including dates of birth. Store and delete them accordingly.
</Callout>

---

## Use the List Well

| Look for | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Old **Last Active** dates | Members drifting away — the [quiet-customer reminder](/loyalty/reminders) can bring them back |
| High **Lifetime**, high **Points** | A big saver who never spends is disengaged |
| Missing **Date of Birth** | No birthday message can go out for them |
