**Notifications** are what keeps a loyalty programme alive. A customer who never hears about their
points forgets they have any.

## Point Activity

| Notification | Sent when |
|--------------|-----------|
| **Points Issued** | Points are awarded |
| **Points Adjusted** | Staff manually change a balance |
| **Points Earned** | A purchase earns points |

Toggle these individually:

- **Notify on purchase points**
- **Notify on manual adjustments**
- **Notify on manual reward grant**

<Callout type="info">
  The points-earned message is the one that matters most. It closes the loop at the moment of
  purchase, while the customer still remembers why.
</Callout>

---

## Rewards

| Notification | Sent when |
|--------------|-----------|
| **Reward Issued** | A reward is granted |
| **Reward Redeemed** | A reward is claimed |
| **Reward Assigned** | A reward is assigned to them |
| **Tier Upgraded** | They reach a new tier |

Tier upgrades are worth celebrating properly — it is the moment a member feels the programme
noticed them.

---

## Expiry

| Notification | Sent when |
|--------------|-----------|
| **Points Reminder** | Points are approaching expiry |
| **Points Expiry Alert** | Expiry is imminent |
| **Points Expired** | Points have gone |

Controlled by **Enable Points Reminder**, **Enable Expiry Alert** and **Enable Points Expired
Notification**.

<Callout type="warn">
  If you expire points, warn people first. Silently deleting a balance a customer was saving is the
  single most reliable way to turn a member into a detractor.
</Callout>

---

## Re-engagement

| Notification | Sent when |
|--------------|-----------|
| **Inactivity Reminder** | After a set number of **days of inactivity** |
| **Birthday Reminder** | On the member's birthday |

Birthday messages are enabled with **Enable Birthday Reminders**, and pair naturally with a
**Birthday Gift** reward. They need the member's **date of birth** — collect it at registration or
you have nothing to trigger on.

---

## Channels

| Channel | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| **Customer Notifications** | WhatsApp messages to members |
| **Email Notifications** | Where an email address is on file |

WhatsApp notifications use approved [templates](/whatsapp/templates) — set one per notification
type. A notification with no template selected will not send.

---

## How Much Is Too Much

Every one of these can be turned off, and some should be.

| Send | Do not send |
|------|-------------|
| Points earned — at the moment of purchase | A message for every trivial adjustment |
| Tier upgrades | Repeated expiry warnings in one week |
| Expiry warnings — once, with time to act | Birthday messages with no gift attached |
| Birthday gifts | Inactivity reminders more than occasionally |

<Callout type="warn">
  Notification fatigue ends in blocks, and a blocked customer is lost across every channel — not
  just loyalty.
</Callout>
