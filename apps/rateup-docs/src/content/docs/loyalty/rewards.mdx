**Rewards** are what customers spend points on. Without attractive rewards, points are just a
number that goes up.

## The Three Reward Types

| Type | Customer gets | Best for |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **Point Reward** | A points credit | Bonuses and goodwill gestures |
| **Coupon** | A discount — fixed amount or percentage | Driving the next purchase |
| **Free Gift** | A physical item | Memorable, high-perceived-value rewards |

## Create a Point Reward

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create Reward** → **Create Point Reward**.
</Step>
<Step>
Name it, and set the **cost** in points.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the points the customer receives.
</Step>
<Step>
Save.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Create a Coupon

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create Coupon**.
</Step>
<Step>
Name it and set the **cost** in points.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the discount: **Fixed Amount** or a percentage.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the **expiry date**, if it should not last forever.
</Step>
<Step>
Save. Redemption produces a **redemption code** for the customer to present.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Steps>
<Step>

</Step>

## Pricing Rewards

The point cost is where a programme succeeds or fails.

| Problem | Symptom | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Priced too high | Points issued far exceeds points redeemed | Lower the cost, or add a cheap entry reward |
| Priced too low | Redemptions constant, margin suffering | Raise the cost, or reduce the earning rate |
| Nothing reachable early | New members drop out | Add one small reward reachable in a visit or two |

<Callout type="info">
  Always have one **cheap, attainable reward**. The first redemption is what converts a sign-up
  into a member — after that, saving for something bigger feels worth it.
</Callout>

Check **Top Redeemed Rewards** on the [dashboard](/loyalty/dashboard) to see what people actually
choose, and retire what nobody takes.

## Referral Programme

The **Referral Programme** rewards members for bringing in new customers.

Every member has a **referral code** and a **referral link**, both copyable from their record in
[Customers](/loyalty/customers).

<Steps>
<Step>
Configure the referral rewards — what the referrer gets, and what the new member gets.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the [referral notification template](/loyalty/notifications) so both sides are told.
</Step>
<Step>
Make sure members know their code exists — most never look for it unless told.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Reward **both sides**. A referral bonus that only pays the referrer feels like being used; one
  that gives the friend something too is an easy thing to send.
</Callout>

---

## Managing Rewards

Rewards can be set **Active** or **Inactive**. Deactivating is better than deleting for a seasonal
reward you will bring back.
