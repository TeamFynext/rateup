## The Four Kinds of Reward

**Create reward** asks *"What kind of reward is it?"*

| Kind | What it is | Costs points? |
|------|------------|---------------|
| **Points reward** | *"Customers spend their points on it — a discount, cashback or a free item."* | Yes |
| **Coupon** | *"A code any customer can use. No points needed."* | No |
| **Free gift** | *"Handed to a customer by you or by the referral program, each with its own code."* | No |
| **Review reward** | *"Thanks a customer for leaving a Google review after the feedback survey."* | No — set up on [Feedback](/loyalty/feedback) |

## Create a Points Reward

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create reward** › **Points reward**. **Start from an example** fills in a reward suited to
your kind of business.
</Step>
<Step>
Give it a **Name** — *"e.g. Free coffee, 20% off"* — and a **Description**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick **What they get**: **Cash off**, **Percentage off**, **Free item** or **Free service**, and set
the amount.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the **Cost** in points, and **What it normally sells for** — used to show what the reward costs
you.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally, under **Rules (optional)**, set a **Minimum spend** or **Days and times** (**Days**,
**From**, **Until**) it can be used.
</Step>
<Step>
To limit it to some tiers, switch on **Only some tiers** under **Available to** and tick them.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create reward**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Create a Coupon

A coupon is one code any customer can use, without spending points.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create reward** › **Coupon**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the **Name**, the **Coupon code** (*"The code customers show or type to use it."*) and a
**Description**.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the **Discount** — **Percentage off** or **Amount off** — its **Value**, and any **Minimum
bill**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Limits**, set **Total uses** across all customers and **Uses per customer**.
</Step>
<Step>
Set when it **Starts** (**Straight away** by default) and **Ends** (**No end date** by default), and
any **Terms and conditions**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create reward**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Create a Free Gift

A free gift is handed to a named customer — by staff with **Grant a gift** in the
[Cashier](/loyalty/cashier-portal), by the referral bonus, or by a birthday, anniversary or
quiet-customer [reminder](/loyalty/reminders).

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create reward** › **Free gift**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the **Name** — *"e.g. Coffee mug, Tote bag"* — and a **Description**.
</Step>
<Step>
Set **How many you have**. *"Each one handed out gets its own code."*
</Step>
<Step>
Set **What it normally sells for**, and any **Terms and conditions**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create reward**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Offer a Reward to One Tier Only

Any points reward, coupon or free gift can be limited to some tiers with **Only some tiers**. On its
tile the reward then shows the tiers it is for instead of **All members**, and in the Cashier it is
marked as tier-only. A Gold-only perk is a reason to reach Gold.

## Set the Joining and Referral Bonuses

| Bonus | Who gets it | When |
|-------|-------------|------|
| **Joining bonus** | **New members** | *"Paid once, the moment a customer joins, however they joined."* |
| **Referral bonus** | **The customer who shares** and **The friend who joins** | When the friend joins with the referral code |

Click **Edit** on either card to choose what it pays — **Points** or a **Free gift** — and the
**Amount**. *"Set it to 0 to give nothing for joining."* Switch a bonus off with its toggle.

<Callout type="info">
  Reward both sides of a referral. A bonus that only pays the referrer feels like being used; one
  that gives the friend something too is an easy thing to send.
</Callout>

## Manage Rewards

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| Hide a reward without losing its history | Switch it off on the tile, or turn off **Offered to customers** in the editor |
| Change it | **Edit** on the tile |
| Remove it | **Delete** on the tile |
| Find one | Filter by **Type** and **Status** (**On and off**, **On**, **Off**) |

Tiles show how many have been redeemed or given out, and **Out of stock** or **Expired** when that
applies.

## Redeem Online With Shopify or WooCommerce

With a store connected in [Settings → Integrations](/settings/integrations), a points reward or
coupon that takes money or a percentage off can be used at checkout: switch on **Use at Shopify
checkout** or **Use at WooCommerce checkout**. *"Each customer gets their own one-use code when they
redeem — there is no code to set here."* Set **Code expires after** a number of days, or leave it
blank for no expiry.

A free item or service cannot be synced — it needs a product to give away.

## Price Rewards Properly

| Problem | Symptom | Fix |
|---------|---------|-----|
| Priced too high | Points issued far exceeds points redeemed | Lower the cost, or add a cheap entry reward |
| Priced too low | Redemptions constant, margin suffering | Raise the cost |
| Nothing reachable early | New members drop out | Add one small reward reachable in a visit or two |

Always have one **cheap, reachable** reward — the first redemption is what turns a sign-up into a
member. Check **Most redeemed rewards** on the [Overview](/loyalty/dashboard) and retire what nobody
takes.
