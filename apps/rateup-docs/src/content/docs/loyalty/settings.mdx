## Basic Settings

| Setting | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Program Name** | What customers see the programme called. Required |
| **Points Currency** | What you call your points — *Stars*, *Coins*, *Points*. Required |
| **Money Currency** | The currency spend is measured in. Required |
| **Default Country** | Default for phone numbers at registration. Required |
| **Program Status** | **Program Active** enables or disables the whole programme |

<Callout type="info">
  Name your points something of your own. *"You have 340 Beans"* is memorable in a way
  *"340 points"* never is — and it makes the programme feel like yours rather than software.
</Callout>

## Points Expiration

| Setting | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **Enable Expiration** | Turns expiry on |
| **Expiration Period** | How long points last before expiring. Required when expiry is on |

Expiry limits your liability and creates urgency. It also, done badly, destroys goodwill.

<Callout type="warn">
  If you enable expiry, enable the [reminder notifications](/loyalty/notifications) at the same
  time. Expiring points silently is the most damaging thing a loyalty programme can do to a
  customer who was saving.
</Callout>

Give people a realistic window. A period shorter than a typical purchase cycle means points expire
before anyone could reasonably spend them.

## Approvals

**Auto-approve Adjustments** decides whether staff adjustments take effect immediately or wait for
review in [Approvals](/loyalty/approvals).

Leave it **off** unless your team is small and trusted.

---

## Birthday Reminders

**Enable Birthday Reminders** sends a message on each member's birthday. Pair it with a
**Birthday Gift** reward so the message arrives with something attached.

Requires a **date of birth** on the member record — collect it during registration.

## Danger Zone

Two irreversible actions.

### Reset Points

Sets every member's balance to zero.

<Callout type="warn">
  This wipes points your customers earned and expect to spend. There is almost no situation where
  this is the right answer to a live programme — export
  [transactions](/loyalty/transactions) first if you proceed.
</Callout>

### Delete Program

Removes the loyalty programme and all its data — members, balances, transactions and rewards.
**Remove all data** does exactly what it says.

<Callout type="warn">
  Permanent and irreversible. Export member and transaction data before doing this.
</Callout>

## Launch Checklist

<Steps>
<Step>
Program name, points currency, money currency and default country set.
</Step>
<Step>
[Earning rules and tiers](/loyalty/earning-rules) configured and modelled.
</Step>
<Step>
At least one **cheap, reachable** [reward](/loyalty/rewards) available.
</Step>
<Step>
[Notifications](/loyalty/notifications) enabled, with approved templates selected.
</Step>
<Step>
[Commands](/loyalty/commands) configured so customers can self-serve.
</Step>
<Step>
Staff trained on the [cashier portal](/loyalty/cashier-portal).
</Step>
<Step>
**Program Active** switched on.
</Step>
</Steps>
