**Reminders** (`/dashboard/loyalty/reminders`) holds *"The messages your programme sends on a
schedule rather than on a purchase. Each one goes out at the hour you choose, in your own time."*
[Messages](/loyalty/messages) is what each one says; this page is when it goes.

## The Reminders

| Reminder | When it sends | Timing you set | Can carry a gift |
|----------|---------------|----------------|------------------|
| **Birthday** | On the customer's birthday | Hour | Yes |
| **Joining anniversary** | Each year on the day they joined | Hour | Yes |
| **Quiet for a while** | Once a customer has not visited for a stretch — once per lapse | **After this many quiet days** (30 to start) and hour | Yes |
| **VIP status at risk** | Before a member drops out of their tier, while they can still keep it | **Days before they lose it** (30) and hour | No |
| **Reward expiring** | Before a reward they already claimed runs out of time | **Days before it lapses** (3) and hour | No |
| **Points expiring** | Before a customer's points run out | **Days before they expire** (14) and hour | No |
| **Points expired** | Once points have gone, so the balance is never a surprise | Hour | No |

## Set When a Reminder Sends

<Steps>
<Step>
Write the message first on [Messages](/loyalty/messages) › **Automatic**. Until then the card reads
**Not written yet**.
</Step>
<Step>
Switch on **Send this reminder**.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the number of days, where the reminder has one.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the hour under **Send at** — 00:00 to 23:00, in the programme's time zone.
</Step>
<Step>
For a birthday, anniversary or quiet-customer reminder, pick a **Gift** — a free gift from
[Rewards](/loyalty/rewards) — or **No gift**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each card shows *"How it reads, with sample details"*. **Edit wording** takes you to the message on
Messages.

## Before You Switch Them On

| Reminder | Needs |
|----------|-------|
| **VIP status at risk** | *"Needs a tier window, set on the Program page."* — **Spend counted within** a number of months, not **Lifetime** |
| **Points expiring**, **Points expired** | *"Needs points expiry, switched on in Settings."* |
| **Birthday** | A date of birth on the member — staff can add one in the Cashier or on [Members](/loyalty/members) |

<Callout type="warn">
  If you switch on points expiry in [Settings](/loyalty/settings), switch on **Points expiring** at
  the same time. Letting points lapse without a warning is the most damaging thing a loyalty
  programme can do to a customer who was saving.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"No timezone set for this programme"* | No time zone on the profile | **Set the timezone** — until then reminders go out in UTC, so the hour you pick may not be the hour customers see |
| A birthday message never arrives | No date of birth on the member, or no approved template | Add the date; check the message on [Messages](/loyalty/messages) |
| **VIP status at risk** cannot send | Tiers count **Lifetime** spend | Set a window in [Program](/loyalty/program) › Tiers |
