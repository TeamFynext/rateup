**Cashier** (`/dashboard/loyalty/cashier`) is the counter-side view of the programme. Staff search a
customer, act, and move on to the next one — every action ends on a receipt that says what changed
and whether the customer was told on WhatsApp.

## Find a Customer

Type in **Name or phone number**. Pick the customer to open them, or tap a name under **Recent** to
serve someone again.

| Shown | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Available** | Points they can spend now |
| **Lifetime** | Every point they have ever earned |
| **Can redeem now** | How many rewards their balance covers |
| **To** the next tier | How far they are from moving up |
| **Total spend** | What they have spent with you |
| **Last visits** | Their latest visits, with **Full history** for the rest |

Their referral code is shown too, with **Copy code** and **Copy referral link**.

## Add a Purchase

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the customer and click **Add purchase**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter *"What did they spend?"*. The form shows an estimate of the points — the base rate, any
earning rule that applies, and the tier multiplier — and what their balance becomes.
</Step>
<Step>
Add a **Note (optional)**, such as the bill number.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Give** with the number of points. The receipt shows the balance before and after, any tier
change, and whether the WhatsApp message was sent.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Made a mistake? **Undo** on the receipt does not reverse the purchase on the spot — it sends a
  correction to [Activity](/loyalty/activity) › Pending for approval (*"Sent to Activity › Pending
  for approval"*).
</Callout>

## Redeem a Reward

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the customer and click **Redeem a reward**. Each reward shows its cost and what it leaves, or
how many points short they are.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Redeem** on the one they want.
</Step>
<Step>
Hand it over. The customer gets a code on WhatsApp.
</Step>
</Steps>

If a reward needs approval, the receipt says *"This reward needs approval before it can be handed
over — it is waiting in Activity."*

A reward the customer was given but has not used yet appears on their record as **Waiting**, with
**Mark as used**.

## Check a Coupon Code

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Coupon code** — at the top of the Cashier, or on an open customer.
</Step>
<Step>
Type the **Code** from the customer's WhatsApp message and click **Check code**.
</Step>
<Step>
If it is valid, click **Mark as used** so the code cannot be used again.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Message | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| *"No redemption with that code."* | The code does not exist |
| *"Already used."* | Someone marked it used before |
| *"Waiting for approval in Activity."* | Approve it in [Activity](/loyalty/activity) first |

## Grant a Gift

**Grant a gift** gives a free gift without the customer spending points — for service recovery, a
birthday, a goodwill gesture. Pick the gift, add a **Note (optional)**, and click **Give**. Free gifts
are set up on [Rewards](/loyalty/rewards); with none, the form says so.

---

## Register a Customer

<Steps>
<Step>
Search their number. If nobody has it, click **Register** with the number — or click **Add
customer** › **New**.
</Step>
<Step>
Fill in the **Phone number** and **Name**. **Date of birth (optional)**, **Gender (optional)** and
**Referral code (optional)** can be added too.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Register**. They get the joining bonus and a WhatsApp welcome.
</Step>
</Steps>

To enrol someone already in your contacts, click **Add customer** › **From Contacts**, search, and
click **Add**. Existing members are marked **Member**.

<Callout type="info">
  Self-registration from the counter QR is better wherever possible: fewer typos, and the customer's
  own consent. Print it from [Materials](/loyalty/materials).
</Callout>

## Run It on a Staff Device

**Open full screen for staff** opens the same screen at `/cashier`, without the dashboard around it.
It stays signed in on that device until someone clicks **Sign out**.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"WhatsApp is not connected"* banner | No WhatsApp number | Points still work; connect a number so customers are told |
| Receipt says *"Not sent — the program is not live"* | The programme is paused | Resume it in [Settings](/loyalty/settings) |
| Receipt says *"Not sent — no approved template, and they have not messaged you today"* | Outside the 24-hour window and no approved template | Check the message on [Messages](/loyalty/messages) |
| *"The program is not live, so nobody can join yet."* | Paused or not activated | Activate or resume it in [Settings](/loyalty/settings) |
| *"This number is already a member."* | They registered before | Open the existing member |
