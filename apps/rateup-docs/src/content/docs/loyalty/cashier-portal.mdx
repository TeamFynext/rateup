The **Cashier Portal** is the counter-side view of the loyalty programme. It is deliberately
simple — staff need to find a customer and act in seconds, with a queue behind them.

## Finding a Customer

Search by **name or phone number**. Open the result to see their record.

| Shown | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Current Tier** | Their tier |
| **Available Points** | What they can spend now |
| **Lifetime Points** | Total ever earned |
| **Joined** | When they enrolled |
| **Referral Code** | Copyable, with a link |

---

## Issuing Points

<Steps>
<Step>
Find the customer.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Issue Points** and **enter the amount**.
</Step>
<Step>
Add a note in **Add any notes…** — this becomes the description in
[Transactions](/loyalty/transactions), so make it useful.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm. Depending on your settings this may go to [Approvals](/loyalty/approvals) first.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Redeeming a Reward

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Available Rewards for Customer** — this lists only what they can actually afford.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the reward and **Claim Reward**.
</Step>
<Step>
Hand over the gift, or apply the discount.
</Step>
<Step>
Confirm the redemption so the points are deducted.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Confirm **after** the customer has what they came for, not before. Confirming first and then
  discovering the item is out of stock leaves them with neither the reward nor their points.
</Callout>

## Coupon Codes

For a customer presenting a coupon:

<Steps>
<Step>
**Enter coupon code**.
</Step>
<Step>
Apply the discount.
</Step>
<Step>
**Mark as Used** so the code cannot be reused.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Granting a Reward Directly

**Assign Reward** or **Grant reward** gives a reward without the customer spending points — for
service recovery, or a goodwill gesture.

<Callout type="info">
  A granted reward after something went wrong is worth far more than the points it costs you.
  Give staff explicit permission to use this and a clear limit.
</Callout>

---

## Registering a New Customer

Staff can enrol customers at the counter. The customer can also register themselves via the
**Customer Registration page link** from [Commands](/loyalty/commands) — a QR code at the till is
the usual way to share it.

<Callout type="info">
  Self-registration is better wherever possible: fewer typos, and the customer's own consent, given
  by them.
</Callout>

---

## Training Staff

Cover these five things and nothing else:

1. Search by phone number — it is more reliable than by name
2. Always note *why* when issuing points
3. Confirm redemption only after handing over
4. Mark coupon codes used, every time
5. When unsure, issue nothing and ask — corrections are visible and awkward
