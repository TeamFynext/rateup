**Program** (`/dashboard/loyalty/program`) holds what the programme promises: its **Profile**, how
points are **Earning**, and its **Tiers**. What customers can redeem is on
[Rewards](/loyalty/rewards); what the programme says is on [Messages](/loyalty/messages).

## The Page at a Glance

The sidebar's **Program** entry carries a chip: **Live**, **Paused** or **Draft**. On a draft the
page has a **Continue setup** button that reopens [the setup](/loyalty/onboarding).

The strip at the top shows **Members**, the points **outstanding** (and *"≈ … if all redeemed"*),
the **Giveback** share **of revenue**, and anything that **Needs attention** — WhatsApp not
connected, or templates awaiting Meta.

Each section card sums itself up and has an **Edit** button that opens its full editor at full
width.

| Card | Summary | Editor |
|------|---------|--------|
| **Profile** | *"Name, brand, contact, hours"* — logo set or not | Business and programme names, currency, time zone, contact, logo, brand colour, hero image |
| **Earning** | The rate per unit spent, and *"about … a visit"* | The base earning rule, extra rules, typical spend, and the full tier list |
| **Tiers** | **On ·** a number of tiers, or **Off · one flat rate** | The tier ladder, up to three tiers |

<Callout type="info">
  *"Edits apply to new transactions the moment you save. Nothing on this page can put a live
  program back into draft — pausing it is a Settings decision, and it keeps every balance intact."*
</Callout>

## Edit the Profile

**Edit** on **Profile** opens the same form as the first setup step: **Your business name**, **Your
loyalty program name**, **Currency**, **Time zone** (*"Sets when birthday and reminder messages go
out."*), the **Primary contact** and the branding — **Logo**, **Brand colour** and **Hero image**.

The logo, colour and hero photo are what [Materials](/loyalty/materials) prints on the QR card and
the brochure.

## Change How Points Are Earned

**Edit** on **Earning** opens **Point Earning Rules**.

### The base earning rule

**Base earning rule** — *"What every customer earns on every purchase."* Edit it to change the rate:
**Spend** an amount, **earn** a number of points. The form reads it back to you, for example *"A
₹100 purchase earns 10 Beans."* The base rule applies to every purchase, so it cannot be switched
off.

Without one, the page shows **No base earning rule** — *"Customers earn nothing on a purchase until a
default rule exists."* — with **Open setup**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Changing the earning rate after launch changes what every point already issued is worth. Model it
  before you go live; this is the number you least want to change in public.
</Callout>

### Add an extra earning rule

**Additional Earning Rules** *"Replace the base rate at set times, like a happy hour or a weekend."*

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add earning rule**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the **Rule Name** — *"e.g., Happy Hour Bonus, Weekend Multiplier, Birthday Bonus"* — and a
**Description**.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the **Rule Type**: **Points Multiplier (2x, 3x points)**, **Fixed Bonus Points** or
**Percentage Bonus**, and set the **Earn rate while this rule applies**.
</Step>
<Step>
Narrow it with any of **Specific Days/Times** (**Available Days**, **Start Time**, **End Time**),
**Customer Tier Restriction** (**Applicable Tiers**) or **Limited Time Period** (**Valid From**,
**Valid To**).
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create Earning Rule**. Switch a rule off with **Enable Rule** without deleting it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  An extra rule's rate *"Replaces the default rate, it does not add to it."* A weekend rule earning 1
  point per unit while the base earns 2 halves the points at weekends.
</Callout>

## Set Up Tiers

**Edit** on **Tiers** opens the tier ladder.

<Steps>
<Step>
Switch **Offer tiers** on. Off, *"everyone is simply a Member earning the same rate."*
</Step>
<Step>
Name the tier **Everyone starts as**, and pick its **Colour**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Add a tier above**. Give it a **Tier name**, the **Spend to reach it**, a **Points
multiplier** (1 to 10) and its **Perks**.
</Step>
<Step>
Set **Spend counted within** — **Lifetime**, or within 1 to 12 months.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

On save, every member is re-placed on the tier their spend earns, and a toast says how many moved up
and down. Promotions are messaged as usual.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| At most three tiers | The starting tier plus two above it — *"Three tiers is the most this programme can run."* |
| Multiplier | A tier cannot earn slower than everyone else, and stays at 10× or under |
| Names | Each tier needs its own name |
| Window | **VIP status at risk** on [Reminders](/loyalty/reminders) needs a window, not **Lifetime** |

A ladder of more than three tiers — built in the **Tiers** list inside the Earning editor, with
**Add tier** — cannot be edited here: the section says so and sends you to the full editor.

<Callout type="warn">
  Removing a tier that still has members asks **Move members down a tier?** — confirming with
  **Downgrade and remove** moves them to the tier below, where they earn at its rate. Tell them
  before you do it.
</Callout>
