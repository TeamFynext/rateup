## Reading It Properly

The single most important relationship is **points issued vs points redeemed**.

| Pattern | What it means | What to do |
|---------|---------------|-----------|
| Issued far exceeds redeemed | Points are piling up unspent — the rewards are not attractive or not reachable | Lower a reward's cost, or add one people want |
| Redeemed close to issued | Healthy circulation | Nothing |
| Both low | Members are not engaging at all | Check notifications are reaching people |
| Members growing, activity flat | You are enrolling people who never return | Look at the first-week experience |

<Callout type="warn">
  A large unredeemed point balance is a liability, not a success. Every one of those points is a
  promise you will eventually have to honour — and a customer who never redeems is a customer who
  stopped caring.
</Callout>

---

## Tier Distribution

Most programmes should look like a pyramid: many members at the base tier, fewer at each step up.

If almost everyone sits in the base tier, your thresholds are too high to be motivating. If
everyone reaches the top, they are too low to mean anything.
