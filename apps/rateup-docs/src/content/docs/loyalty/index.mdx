**Loyalty** runs a points programme for your customers. They earn points, climb tiers, redeem
rewards and refer friends — all through WhatsApp, with no app to install.

## How It Works

```
Customer registers          →  Registration page or WhatsApp command
Customer spends             →  Points earned, per your earning rules
Points accumulate           →  Tier upgrades as thresholds are crossed
Customer redeems            →  Points, coupons or free gifts
Customer refers a friend    →  Referral rewards for both
```

Everything the customer does happens in WhatsApp. Everything you configure happens here.

## Before You Launch

<Callout type="warn">
  Get the earning rate right before customers join. Changing it later devalues points people have
  already earned, and that is the fastest way to lose trust in a loyalty programme.
</Callout>

Set up in this order:

1. [Settings](/loyalty/settings) — program name, points currency, money currency
2. [Earning rules and tiers](/loyalty/earning-rules) — how points accrue
3. [Rewards](/loyalty/rewards) — what points are worth
4. [Notifications](/loyalty/notifications) — what customers hear from you
5. [Cashier portal](/loyalty/cashier-portal) — train your staff before going live
