**Loyalty** runs a points programme for your customers. They join by sending a keyword on
WhatsApp, earn points when they buy, climb tiers, redeem rewards and refer friends — with no app to
install. Your staff serve them from the **Cashier**; everything else is configured here.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/loyalty/overview.png" alt="The Loyalty sidebar group and the Overview page of a live programme" />

---

## How It Works

| Customer does | What happens |
|---------------|--------------|
| Scans your counter QR, or texts your join keyword | They become a member, and get any joining bonus |
| Buys something | Staff add the purchase in the Cashier and points are earned by your earning rule |
| Keeps spending | They move up a tier, if tiers are on |
| Spends points | Staff redeem a reward and the customer gets a code on WhatsApp |
| Refers a friend | Both sides get the referral bonus |
| Goes quiet, has a birthday, has points about to expire | A reminder goes out at the hour you set |
| Answers the post-visit survey happily | They are asked for a Google review, and can be rewarded for it |

Everything the customer does happens in WhatsApp. Nothing reaches customers until you activate the
programme.

## Set Up in This Order

<Steps>
<Step>
Connect a WhatsApp number in [Settings → Channels](/settings/channels). The join QR can only be made
once a number is connected.
</Step>
<Step>
Run the [setup](/loyalty/onboarding) — profile, earning, tiers, rewards and messages — and
**Activate program** on the last step.
</Step>
<Step>
Check the WhatsApp templates were approved on [Messages](/loyalty/messages), and set when each
reminder goes out on [Reminders](/loyalty/reminders).
</Step>
<Step>
Print the counter QR card from [Materials](/loyalty/materials).
</Step>
<Step>
Train your staff on the [Cashier](/loyalty/cashier-portal) before the first customer joins.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally, set up the post-visit survey on [Feedback](/loyalty/feedback).
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="warn">
  Get the earning rate right before customers join. Changing it after launch changes what every
  point already issued is worth, and that is the fastest way to lose trust in a loyalty programme.
</Callout>
