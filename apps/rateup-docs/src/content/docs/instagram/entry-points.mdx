they see when they do.

## Ice Breakers

Ice breakers are the suggested questions Instagram shows before a customer types anything.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/instagram/overview.png" alt="Ice breaker questions" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **Ice Breakers** section and click **Add question**.
</Step>
<Step>
Write the **question text** as the customer would ask it — *"What are your business hours?"*, not
*"Business hours"*.
</Step>
<Step>
Set what happens when they tap it.
</Step>
<Step>
Save and **Sync with Meta**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Ice breakers are the cheapest support you will ever build. Look at your Inbox, find the three
  questions you answer most, and make them tappable.
</Callout>

## Persistent Menu

The persistent menu sits in the DM thread permanently, so customers can reach your key options at
any point in the conversation.

Each entry has:

| Field | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Title** | What the customer sees — *"Talk to an agent"* |
| **Type** | What it does |
| **URL / Payload** | Where it goes, or which bot step it triggers |

| Type | Behaviour |
|------|-----------|
| **Web URL** | Opens a link |
| **Postback** | Triggers a step in your chatbot |

Keep it short. A menu of three useful entries gets used; a menu of ten gets ignored.

### Why the referral parameter matters

It arrives with the conversation, so you know which campaign, poster or page sent that person. Use
a different parameter per placement and you can tell which one actually works.

  Pair an ig.me link with a [QR code](/qr-studio/qr-creator): the printed code stays the same while
  the destination behind it changes with each campaign.
</Callout>
