An **Instagram journey** handles a DM conversation automatically — answering the questions you get
constantly, collecting details, rewarding followers, and handing over to a person when it cannot.
They live on the **Journeys** tab of **Instagram**.

Journeys were previously called Chatbots.

## Create a Journey

Click **Create Journey**. The wizard opens on **Starting point** — the channel is already Instagram.

<Steps>
<Step>
Pick **Start from scratch** or one of the [examples](#instagram-examples). The chips along the top
filter them by use (**Welcome**, **Support**, **Growth**, …). On a wide screen, the selected example
plays in a phone preview on the right.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Next**. Check the **Journey Name** — at least 3 characters, only your team sees it.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally pick an **Account** — *"Optional. Used to set up the rule that starts this journey."* The
journey itself is not tied to an account; the rule decides which account it answers on.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create Journey**. The editor opens with the example laid out. Change the wording, then
**Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/instagram/chatbot-wizard.png" alt="Instagram Create Journey wizard, Growth filter, Follow to unlock a code playing in the preview" />

With no journeys yet, the tab offers three examples and **Start from scratch** as buttons.

## Instagram Examples

| Example | Filter | What it does | Usually started by |
|---------|--------|--------------|--------------------|
| **DM welcome** | Welcome | Greets people with quick replies to shop, ask a question or talk to you | A DM that matches a rule |
| **FAQ quick replies** | Support | Answers your most common questions with one tap each | A DM that matches a rule |
| **Lead capture** | Lead capture | Asks for a name and a phone number or email, then hands the lead to your team | A DM that matches a rule |
| **Product cards** | Sales | Shows products as swipeable cards with a link to buy each one | A DM that matches a rule |
| **Booking request** | Bookings | Picks a service, asks for a preferred time and passes the request to your team | A DM that matches a rule |
| **Comment to DM a link** | Growth | Replies privately to anyone who comments on a post, with the link they asked for | **Instagram Comment** |
| **Follow to unlock a code** | Growth | Sends a discount code to followers and asks everyone else to follow first | A DM that matches a rule |
| **Story mention thank-you** | Growth | Thanks people who mention you in their story | **Instagram Story Mention** |
| **ig.me link campaign** | Growth | Welcomes people who open your ig.me link from an ad, bio or QR code | **ig.me Link Opened** |
| **Talk to a person** | Handoff | Lets people ask for a person and assigns the chat to your team | A keyword like “agent” or “human” |

Examples use placeholder links and codes — `https://example.com/offer`, `WELCOME10`. Replace them
before you switch the rule on.

## Make It Start

A journey runs only when an [automation rule](/automation) starts it with **Trigger Journey**. A
rule can only start a journey of its own platform, so an Instagram rule lists only Instagram
journeys.

After you create from an example, the editor shows a **Set up the trigger** card. **Create the
rule** opens a new Instagram automation with the right trigger already chosen — **Instagram
Comment**, **Instagram Story Mention**, **ig.me Link Opened** or **Message Received** — and, if you
picked an account in the wizard, that account too. Dismiss the card and it does not come back.

<Callout type="warn">
  A journey with no rule never runs. If you dismissed **Set up the trigger**, create the rule from
  [Automation Rules](/automation) or from the **Comments**, **Story Mentions** or **ig.me Links** tab.
</Callout>

## What an Instagram Journey Can Send

The editor is the same one WhatsApp uses — see the [Journey Builder](/whatsapp/chatbot-builder) for
the canvas itself. On Instagram, the **Components** panel offers:

| Group | Components |
|-------|-----------|
| **Basic Messages** | Text · Image · Video · Audio |
| **Interactive** | Input |
| **Templates** | Cards · Quick Reply · Product Template · Follow Check |
| **Flow Control** | Pause · Start |
| **None Interactive** | Assign |

| Component | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Quick Reply** | A **Message Text** with up to **13** tappable replies, each up to 20 characters |
| **Cards** | Up to **10** swipeable cards — a title and subtitle (80 characters each), an **Image URL**, and up to **3** buttons per card. A button is a **URL** or an **Action** |
| **Product Template** | Up to **10** products from your catalogue |
| **Input** | Asks a question and saves the answer under **Save Answer to Variable** — for an email address, a phone number or a name |
| **Follow Check** | Branches on whether the person follows you — see below |
| **Assign** | Hands the conversation to your team in the [Inbox](/inbox) |

WhatsApp-only parts — Reply Buttons, List, Document, templates, catalogs, flows, Location and
Contacts — do not appear.

## How a Conversation Continues

A **Quick Reply**, **Cards** or **Input** step stops and waits for the person. When they answer, the
journey carries on down the matching branch:

| They… | The journey… |
|-------|----------|
| Tap one of the step's own buttons | Follows that button's branch |
| Type text that matches a button's title (case does not matter) | Treats it as that button |
| Type anything else at a **Quick Reply** step | Keeps waiting; the message goes to your rules instead |
| Answer an **Input** step | Saves the answer and moves on |

A reply that the waiting journey consumes does not also fire your keyword rules. The journey waits for up to
**24 hours** — Instagram only lets a business message someone within 24 hours of their last
message — and after that the next DM is treated as a fresh message.

<Callout type="info">
  A tap on an old button from earlier in the thread, or on the persistent menu, is not an answer to
  the current step. It goes to your rules instead.
</Callout>

## Reward Followers with Follow Check

**Follow Check** sends the person down one of two branches: **Follows you** or **Doesn't follow
yet**. The **Follow to unlock a code** example shows the pattern — the code goes out on the first
branch, a *"Follow us, then send “code” again"* nudge on the second.

<Callout type="warn">
  Instagram only shares who follows you once the person has messaged you. A Follow Check straight
  after a comment always takes **Doesn't follow yet**. Put it after a quick reply — *"Follow us,
  then tap Done"* — so the person has written to you first.
</Callout>

---

## Journeys Started by a Comment

When a comment rule starts a journey, the journey's first message goes out as a **private reply** to
that comment — the only way to reach someone who has never messaged you. Instagram allows one per
comment, and nothing more until the person writes back:

- Only a **Text** step is sent as the private reply. Make the journey's first step a Text.
- An image, video or audio cannot be a private reply. If one comes first, the journey sends its caption
  (or *"Thanks for your comment!"*) as the private reply, then tries the media as a normal DM.
- Every step after the private reply is a normal DM. Instagram refuses those to someone who has
  never messaged you, and a refused message stops the journey at that step.

Keep a comment journey to one Text message — the **Comment to DM a link** example is built exactly that
way.

## Test It

Click **Preview** in the editor to walk the conversation in a phone frame. Check that every reply
leads somewhere, that nothing loops, and that a person is reachable within a step or two.

## Manage Journeys

| Action | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Search journeys...** | Filters by name |
| **Sort** | Orders the cards |
| **Clone** | From **More actions**. Asks for a name, prefilled *"Copy of …"* |
| **Delete** | From **More actions**, after you confirm |
| **Settings** | In the editor toolbar. Renames the journey |

<Callout type="warn">
  Editing a journey changes it for everyone already part-way through it. Clone, edit the clone, test
  it, then point the rule at the clone.
</Callout>

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| The journey never runs | No rule starts it | Add **Trigger Journey** to an Instagram rule |
| The journey does not move on after a quick reply | The person typed something that matches no button | Word the buttons so people tap them; a typed message is handled by your rules |
| Everyone gets the "follow first" branch | The Follow Check runs before the person has messaged you | Put a quick reply before it |
| A comment journey sends nothing, or stops after one message | Its first step is not a Text, or it sends more than the one private reply Instagram allows | Start with a single Text step |
| The rule's **Trigger Journey** list is empty | The rule's platform is not Instagram | Set the rule's platform to Instagram |
