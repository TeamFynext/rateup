An **Instagram chatbot** handles a DM conversation automatically — answering the questions you get
constantly, and handing over to a person when it cannot.

## Create a Chatbot

<Steps>
<Step>
Select the **Instagram account** the bot belongs to.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create Chatbot** and give it a name.
</Step>
<Step>
Build the conversation — the messages the bot sends and the replies it offers.
</Step>
<Step>
**Save**, then **Sync with Meta** so it goes live.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## What a Bot Can Send

| Element | Use |
|---------|-----|
| **Message** | Plain text |
| **Buttons** | Choices that move the conversation on |
| **Web URL** | Opens a link — *Visit the website*, *Open in browser* |
| **Postback** | Triggers the next step inside the bot |
| **Live chat with a person** | Hands the conversation to your team |

<Callout type="info">
  Always include a route to a human. A bot that cannot be escaped turns a small question into a
  complaint — the **Live chat with a person** action is what prevents that.
</Callout>

---

## Handover to an Agent

When a customer chooses the live-chat option, the conversation becomes a normal ticket in the
[Inbox](/inbox). The bot's messages are already in the thread, so the agent can see what was
already said and does not ask the customer to repeat it.

---

## Testing

Use the preview to walk the conversation before syncing. Check that:

- Every button leads somewhere
- The wording reads like your brand, not like a form
- The handover option is reachable within a step or two
- Nothing loops back on itself endlessly

---

## Managing Chatbots

| Action | Notes |
|--------|-------|
| **Clone** | Copies a bot as *"Copy of …"* — the safe way to iterate on a live bot |
| **Search** | Find a bot by name once you have several |
| **Delete** | Removes it; sync afterwards so Instagram stops serving it |

<Callout type="warn">
  Editing a live bot changes what customers get mid-conversation. Clone, edit the clone, test it,
  then switch — rather than editing the one currently in use.
</Callout>
