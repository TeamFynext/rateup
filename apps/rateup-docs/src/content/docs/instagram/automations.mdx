Not every Instagram interaction starts as a DM. **Automations** let you respond to public activity
— comments and story mentions — and pull it into a private conversation.

---

## Comment Automations

When someone comments on your post, RateUp can reply automatically.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/instagram/comment-automations.png" alt="Comment automations" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create automation** in the **Comment Automations** section.
</Step>
<Step>
Set the trigger — the keyword or phrase in a comment that fires it.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the response. A **private reply** moves the conversation into DMs, where you can answer
properly.
</Step>
<Step>
Save and **Sync with Meta**.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Private Replies

A private reply turns a public comment into a DM thread. This is the pattern behind
"comment PRICE and we'll send you the details" — the comment is public marketing, the answer is a
private conversation you can actually work in.

<Callout type="warn">
  Instagram limits when a private reply may be sent after a comment. If replies stop going out,
  the window has usually closed rather than the automation being broken.
</Callout>

## Story Mention Automations

When someone mentions your account in their story, RateUp can respond automatically.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/instagram/story-automation.png" alt="Story mention automations" />

Customers who tag you in a story are your best advocates and the easiest to lose — they get no
acknowledgement unless someone happens to see it. An automatic thank-you closes that gap.

<Steps>
<Step>
Create an automation in the **Story Mention Automations** section.
</Step>
<Step>
Write the response — a thank-you, an offer, or a route into a chatbot.
</Step>
<Step>
Save and **Sync with Meta**.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Keeping Automations Honest

- Reply as a person would, not as a form
- Keep the automatic part short and hand over quickly
- Do not automate a reply to a complaint — route those to a human immediately
- Re-read your automations occasionally; wording that felt clever six months ago rarely still does
