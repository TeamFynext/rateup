Not every Instagram conversation starts as a DM. The **Comments** and **Story Mentions** tabs
respond to public activity and pull it into a private conversation. Both are
[automation rules](/automation) underneath — the tabs list the rules for the account selected at
the top of the page, and **Create** opens the rule builder with the trigger already chosen.

---

## Answer Comments Automatically

The **Comments** tab lists your **Comment Automations** — *"Automatically send private replies when
someone comments on your posts."*

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/instagram/comment-automations.png" alt="Comment automations" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create** (or **Create rule** when the list is empty). The rule builder opens with
**Instagram** as the channel, the **Instagram Comment (Private Reply)** trigger, and this account
selected. On **Template**, click **Start from scratch** to keep that trigger and account.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Conditions**, choose **Comments on**: **Posts & reels**, **Live videos** or **Both**.
</Step>
<Step>
For posts and reels, choose **Apply to**: **All posts**, or **Specific posts** and pick them from
**Select posts / reels**. Add conditions on the comment text — a keyword such as `PRICE` — if only
some comments should get an answer.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Actions**, add the actions — **Send Message**, or **Trigger Journey** to start an
[Instagram journey](/instagram/chatbots). The first message they send arrives as a private reply
to the comment.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally fill in **Reply in public (optional)** under the actions (below). On **Review**, name the rule and
click **Activate automation**. No sync is needed — the rule is live once it is saved and active.
</Step>
</Steps>

### Private Replies

The first message a comment rule sends is a **private reply**: a DM tied to that comment. It
reaches people who have never messaged you, which an ordinary DM cannot. It is the pattern behind
*"comment PRICE and we'll send you the details"* — the comment is public marketing, the answer is a
private conversation you can work in.

<Callout type="warn">
  Instagram allows **one** private reply per comment and nothing more until the person writes back.
  Keep the first message to a single text that invites a reply. A second message, or a journey whose
  first step is not a Text, is refused for anyone who has not messaged you before.
</Callout>

### Reply in Public

**Reply in public (optional)** posts a comment under theirs, so other people see you answered —
*"Sent you a DM 📩"*. Click **Add a public reply**, then **Add a variation** for more.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Variations | Up to **10**. One is picked at random each time, so the replies under a busy post do not all read the same |
| Length | Up to **2200** characters each — *"Instagram comments stop at 2200 characters"* |
| When | Posted after the rule's actions have run |
| Empty variations | Dropped when you save |

A public reply that fails does not stop the private reply or the rest of the rule.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/instagram/comment-public-replies.png" alt="Then step of an Instagram comment rule with a Trigger Journey action and three public replies" />

### Comments on Live Videos

Choose **Live videos** or **Both** under **Comments on** to answer comments made during your
Instagram lives. Picking **Live videos** switches **Apply to** off and back to all posts — a live
video is never in the post list, so a post filter would block it.

## Sell from a Comment

A comment rule can answer with the product itself. When someone comments a keyword — *buy*,
*link*, *mine* — on a post, reel or live, the private reply lists the product with a link to buy
it. This needs [Commerce](/commerce) set up with the products in your store.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/instagram/comment-to-buy.png" alt="Actions step of an Instagram comment rule (keywords buy, link, price) with a Send products to buy card open: one product chip, Message (optional) filled in, Limited drop switched on with Units available 20; below it, Reply in public (optional) with 'Sent you the link 📩'" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Create a comment rule as [above](#answer-comments-automatically) — or, on **Template**, pick
**Comment to buy** (*"Someone comments “buy” → DM them the product with a link to check out."*) or
**Live drop** (*"Someone comments “mine” on your live → the first 20 get a checkout link."*).
</Step>
<Step>
On **Conditions**, set the keywords — the templates use `buy`, `link` and `price`, or `mine` and
`buy` for lives.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Actions**, add **Send products to buy** and click **Choose products** — up to 10.
Optionally write a **Message (optional)**, up to 600 characters; without one the reply opens with
*"Thanks for your comment! Here it is:"* (or *"Thanks! Here's the edit:"* for several products).
</Step>
<Step>
For a drop, switch on **Limited drop** and set **Units available** (below).
</Step>
<Step>
Add a public reply such as *"Sent you the link 📩"*, then name the rule and click **Activate
automation**.
</Step>
</Steps>

What the link opens depends on what you picked — *"One product with one option gets a direct
checkout link. More than that links to your storefront."*

| You picked | The reply ends with |
|------------|---------------------|
| One product with one option that can be sold | *"Buy it here:"* and a checkout link, starting at the address step |
| One product with several options | *"Shop here:"* and the product's storefront page |
| Several products | *"Shop here:"* and your storefront |
| Anything but a direct checkout, storefront off | *"Reply here and we'll help you order."* — no link |

The reply lists up to 5 products with their prices, and is kept within Meta's 1,000-character limit
for a private reply: your message is shortened before the link ever is.

<Callout type="warn">
  With several products — or one with several options — turn the storefront on in **Commerce →
  Settings** first. Otherwise the
  builder warns *"Turn on your storefront in Commerce → Settings, or the reply can only list these
  products without a link."* — and every commenter gets a list they cannot buy from.
</Callout>

### Sell a Limited Drop

**Limited drop** — *"Sell only so many, one per person, first come. Later commenters hear it sold
out."* — caps the rule at **Units available** (20 when you switch it on).

| Situation | What happens |
|-----------|--------------|
| A unit is free | The commenter gets the reply and their checkout link |
| The same person comments again | They get their first checkout back, not a second unit |
| Every unit is taken | The reply reads *"Sorry, … has sold out."*, plus a link to your storefront when it is on |
| A checkout lapses unpaid | Its unit goes back to the drop and someone else can take it |

A unit is held while its checkout is still open, not by stock: stock is taken only when the order
is placed, and an order beyond the limit is refused.

<Callout type="info">
  Run a drop on a live by choosing **Live videos** under **Comments on** — the **Live drop** template
  does this for you. Set **Units available** to what you can actually ship, not what you hope to
  sell.
</Callout>

On a **Story Mention** rule, **Send products to buy** sends the same message as a DM, followed by
the product cards.

## Thank People Who Mention You in a Story

The **Story Mentions** tab lists your **Story Mention Automations** — *"Triggered when someone
mentions your account in their Instagram story."*

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/instagram/story-automation.png" alt="Story mention automations" />

Customers who tag you in a story are your best advocates and the easiest to lose — they get no
acknowledgement unless someone happens to see it. An automatic thank-you closes that gap.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Create**. The rule builder opens with the **Instagram Story Mention** trigger and this
account selected. On **Template**, click **Start from scratch** to keep that trigger and account.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Actions**, add a **Send Message** with your thank-you, or **Trigger Journey** — the **Story
mention thank-you** journey example is a ready-made start.
</Step>
<Step>
On **Review**, name the rule and click **Activate automation**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Every story mention matches — there are no post or keyword filters for this trigger.

## Turn a Rule On or Off

Each rule appears as a card on its tab. Use the card's switch to pause or resume it, the delete
action to remove it, or click the card to edit it in [Automation Rules](/automation).

---

## Keep Automations Honest

- Reply as a person would, not as a form
- Keep the automatic part short and hand over quickly
- Do not automate a reply to a complaint — route those to a human immediately
- Re-read your public replies occasionally; wording that felt clever six months ago rarely still does

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| A rule you made is not on the tab | It belongs to another account | Switch the account selector at the top |
| Comments on a live get no answer | **Comments on** is **Posts & reels** | Choose **Live videos** or **Both** |
| Some posts get no answer | **Apply to** is **Specific posts** | Add the post, or choose **All posts** |
| The private reply arrives but nothing after it | Instagram allows one private reply until the person writes back | Make the first message ask them to reply |
| *"Instagram comments stop at 2200 characters"* | A public reply is too long | Shorten it |
| A comment-to-buy reply has no link | Several products and the storefront is off, or nothing picked is still for sale | Turn the storefront on, or check the products |
| Nothing is sent for a comment-to-buy rule | Commerce is not set up, or none of the products is active | Set up [Commerce](/commerce) and check the products |
| Commenters hear *"has sold out"* too early | **Units available** is lower than you meant, or open checkouts still hold units | Raise the number; lapsed checkouts free their units |
