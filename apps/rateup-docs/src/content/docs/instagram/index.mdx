## Before You Start

You need an **Instagram professional account** connected under
[Channels](/settings/channels). Until then, the page shows *"No Instagram accounts connected"* with
a **Go to Settings** link.

If you manage several accounts, use the **account selector** at the top to choose which one you are
configuring — every setting on the page belongs to the selected account.

---

## Syncing with Meta

Instagram settings live on Meta's side as well as in RateUp. Two actions keep them aligned:

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Sync with Meta** | Pushes your RateUp configuration to Instagram |
| **Clear from Meta** | Removes the configuration from Instagram |

<Callout type="warn">
  Changes are not live for customers until you sync. Editing ice breakers or the persistent menu in
  RateUp and forgetting to sync is the most common reason "nothing changed".
</Callout>

---

## Where Conversations Go

Anything a customer sends by DM becomes a ticket in the [Inbox](/inbox), with an Instagram badge on
the row. Agents reply exactly as they would on WhatsApp.
