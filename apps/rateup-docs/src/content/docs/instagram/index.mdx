RateUp connects to your Instagram professional account so DMs land in the [Inbox](/inbox)
alongside your other channels — and so the routine parts of Instagram can answer themselves.
Instagram does not need WhatsApp: you can connect it on its own.

## Connect an Instagram Account

You need an **Instagram professional account** linked to a Facebook Page. Until one is connected, the
page shows *"No Instagram accounts connected"* with a **Go to Settings** button.

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Settings → Channels** and click **Connect** on **Instagram Messaging**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Continue with Facebook** and log in. Meta asks only for Instagram permissions — no WhatsApp
number is involved.
</Step>
<Step>
In **Choose Instagram accounts**, tick the accounts you want. *"Each account becomes its own
Instagram channel."* An account already linked to another RateUp organisation is marked
*"Connected to another organization"*.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Connect**. The accounts appear under **Instagram connections**.
</Step>
</Steps>

You can connect as many accounts as you like. If none show up, Meta was not given access to them —
*"No Instagram professional accounts were shared. Log in again and choose the Facebook Page each
account is linked to."* See [Channels](/settings/channels) for disconnecting and the other channels.

## Work with Several Accounts

The **account selector** at the top right lists every connected account, with a count beside it.
Everything on the page — comment rules, story rules, the menu, ice breakers and ig.me links —
belongs to the account selected there. Switch accounts before you edit.

Chatbots are the exception: a chatbot is not tied to an account. The [rule](/automation) that starts
it decides which account it runs on.

## Syncing with Meta

The **Persistent Menu** and **Ice Breakers** live on Instagram's side. Editing them in RateUp does
not change Instagram until you push them:

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Save** | Stores your changes in RateUp |
| **Sync with Meta** | Pushes the saved menu or ice breakers to Instagram |
| **Clear from Meta** | Removes them from Instagram |

<Callout type="warn">
  Changes to the menu and ice breakers are not live for customers until you sync. Saving and
  forgetting to sync is the most common reason "nothing changed". Chatbots, comment rules and ig.me
  links need no sync — they take effect when you save them.
</Callout>

## Where Conversations Go

Anything a customer sends by DM becomes a ticket in the [Inbox](/inbox), with an Instagram badge on
the row. Agents reply exactly as they would on WhatsApp.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"No Instagram accounts connected"* | No Instagram channel yet | Connect one under **Settings → Channels** |
| An account is missing from **Choose Instagram accounts** | Its Facebook Page was not shared with RateUp during login | Log in again and pick that Page |
| A rule or link you made is not listed | Another account is selected | Switch the account selector |
