# Accessing the Inbox

This section explains how to access the Inbox from the RateUp dashboard and who can use it.

---

## Open Inbox from Dashboard

To access the Inbox:

1. **[Log in to your RateUp](/gettingStarted/login) account** .
2. From the left sidebar, click **Inbox**
3. The Inbox dashboard will open with your available conversations

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/inbox.png`} alt="Inbox" />

Inbox is accessible from anywhere within the RateUp platform using the sidebar menu.

---

## Who Can Access the Inbox?

Access to Inbox depends on your **user role and permissions**.

### Users with Inbox Access

Typically, the following roles can access Inbox:

- **Admins** – Full access to all conversations and settings
- **Managers** – View and manage team conversations
- **Agents** – Access assigned and personal conversations

---

## Permission-Based Access

- Users can only see conversations assigned to them or their team
- Some actions (assignment, closing chats, filters) may be limited based on role
- Inbox visibility and actions are controlled through **Team & Role settings**

---

## Important Notes

- Inbox access requires an active WhatsApp integration
- If Inbox is not visible, check your assigned role or permissions
