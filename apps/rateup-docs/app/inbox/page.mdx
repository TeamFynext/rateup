# Inbox Overview

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/inbox-overview.png`} alt="Inbox-overview" />

The **Inbox** is RateUp’s central hub for managing customer conversations.  
It allows teams to receive, respond to, and organize WhatsApp, Instagram and chat bot messages in one shared workspace.

---

## What Inbox Does

With Inbox, you can:

- View all customer messages in one place
- Assign conversations to team members
- Use automated and manual replies
- Track conversation status from start to closure

---

## Why Inbox Matters

Inbox helps teams:
- Respond faster to customers
- Avoid missed or duplicate replies
- Collaborate efficiently across teams
- Keep conversations organized

- **WhatsApp Business**
