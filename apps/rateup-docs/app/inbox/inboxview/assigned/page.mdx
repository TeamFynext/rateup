# Assigned Conversations

The **Assigned** view displays conversations that have been **assigned to a team or a specific staff member**.  
These conversations already have ownership and are actively being handled or monitored.

The Assigned view is primarily used by **Business Admins and Managers** to track workload and ensure accountability.

---

## Purpose of Assigned Conversations

Assigned conversations help teams:

- Track who is responsible for each conversation
- Monitor active conversations across teams
- Avoid duplicate or missed responses
- Balance workload effectively

## What Appears in Assigned Conversations

A conversation appears in **Assigned** when:

- It is assigned to a **team**
- It is assigned to **another staff member**
- It has not been closed yet

Assigned conversations remain visible until they are closed or reassigned.

---

## Assigned vs Mine

Understanding this difference is important:

| View | Meaning |
|-----|--------|
| Assigned | Conversations assigned to any team or staff member |
| Mine | Conversations assigned specifically to you |

> ℹ️ A conversation assigned to you will appear in **both Assigned and Mine**.

---

## Assigned User Visibility

When a conversation is assigned, the assigned user is clearly shown in multiple places.

### Conversation List

- The assigned user’s name or label appears in the conversation list
- This helps quickly identify ownership

### Chat View (Top-Right)

- The assigned user is shown at the **top-right corner** of the chat window
- This ensures clear accountability while responding

---

## Opening an Assigned Conversation

To open an assigned conversation:

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/assign.png`}
  alt="open an assigned conversation"
/>

1. Click a conversation from the **Assigned** tab
2. The chat opens in the conversation panel
3. You can view:
   - Assigned user or team
   - Conversation lifecycle status
   - Full message history

Your ability to respond depends on your role permissions.

---

## Reassigning an Assigned Conversation

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/reassign.png`}
  alt="Reassign conversation to another team or individual"
  width={300}
/>

Assigned conversations can be reassigned at any time.

To reassign a conversation:

1. Open the assigned conversation
2. Click the **assigned user name** at the top-right
3. The **Assign to team or individual** panel opens
4. Choose:
   - Another team, or
   - A specific team member
5. The conversation is reassigned instantly

---

## Team vs Individual Assignment

When reassigning, you can:

- Assign the **entire team**  
  → Any team member can respond based on access rules

- Assign a **specific individual**  
  → Ownership is clearly defined for one person

User availability (Online / Offline) is shown to help with smart assignment.

---

## Role-Based Behavior

### Business Admin
- Can view all assigned conversations
- Can reassign across all teams
- Can monitor organization-wide workload

### Manager
- Can view and reassign conversations within their teams

### Agent
- Can view assigned conversations
- Can respond if permitted
- Reassignment permissions depend on role settings

---

## Best Practices

- Regularly review the Assigned view
- Reassign conversations if an agent is unavailable
- Avoid frequent reassignment to prevent confusion
- Close conversations once they are fully resolved
