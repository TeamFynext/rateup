# Mine (My Conversations)

The **Mine** view displays conversations that are **assigned specifically to you**.  
This view represents your **personal workload** and is where agents focus on handling customer conversations.

The Mine view helps you:

- Focus only on conversations you own
- Manage your daily workload efficiently
- Respond to customers without distraction
- Track conversations you are responsible for closing

In the **Mine** view:
- All conversations are assigned to **you**
- You are the primary owner of each conversation
- These conversations also appear in **Assigned**, but Mine is your personal view

---

## How Conversations Appear in Mine

A conversation appears in **Mine** when:

- You click **Assign to Me**
- A conversation is assigned to you by an admin or manager
- A team conversation is explicitly assigned to you

---

## Mine vs Assigned

| View | Meaning |
|-----|--------|
| Mine | Conversations assigned specifically to you |
| Assigned | Conversations assigned to any team or staff |

> ℹ️ All conversations in **Mine** are also part of **Assigned**, but not all Assigned conversations appear in Mine.

---

## Working with Conversations in Mine

From the Mine view, you can:

- Open and read customer messages
- Send replies and use quick actions
- Update conversation lifecycle (Resolved / Closed)
- Create tickets if required
- Reassign conversations (if permitted)

---

## Reassigning from Mine

If needed, you can reassign a conversation from Mine:

1. Open the conversation
2. Click the assigned user name at the top-right
3. Select another team or team member
4. The conversation moves out of your Mine view

Reassignment is useful when:
- You are unavailable
- The issue needs escalation
- Another agent is better suited to handle it

---

## Role-Based Behavior

### Agent
- Primarily works from the **Mine** view
- Handles customer communication
- Closes or resolves conversations

### Manager / Business Admin
- May also see Mine conversations
- Can reassign or monitor conversations

---

## Best Practices

- Regularly review your Mine view
- Respond promptly to assigned conversations
- Close conversations once resolved
- Reassign conversations when you cannot continue handling them
