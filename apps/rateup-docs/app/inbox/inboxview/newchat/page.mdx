# New Conversations (New Tab)

The **New** tab displays all **newly received customer messages** that are **not yet assigned** to any team member or staff.

This view helps ensure every incoming message is noticed and assigned quickly.

---

## Purpose of the New Tab

The New tab is used to:

- View newly received customer messages
- Identify conversations that are not yet assigned
- Assign conversations to yourself or other team members
- Manage incoming workload efficiently

---

## New Tab Overview

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/new-chat.png`} alt="New tab showing unassigned conversations" />

In the **New** tab:
- All conversations are **unassigned**
- Conversations are sorted by recent activity
- Each conversation shows the customer name, last message, and time received

---

## Opening a New Conversation

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/new-chat.png`} alt="Unassigned conversation view" />

To open a conversation:

1. Click on any conversation listed under **New**
2. The conversation opens in the chat window on the right
3. At this stage, the conversation is still unassigned

You can review the customer’s message before deciding how to assign it.

---

## Assigning a Conversation

At the bottom of the chat window, assignment options are available.

---

### Assign to Me

- Click **Assign to Me** to take ownership of the conversation
- The conversation moves from **New** to **Mine**
- You are now responsible for responding and closing the chat

This is useful when you want to handle the conversation immediately.

---

### Assign to Team or Individual

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/inbox/assignDropdown.png`} alt="Assign conversation to team or individual" />

Next to **Assign to Me**, click the **dropdown icon** to assign the conversation to others.

From the **Assign to team or individual** panel, you can:

- Assign the conversation to a **specific team**
- Assign it to an **individual staff member**
- Assign the entire team at once

The list of available teams and users depends on your role and permissions.

---

## Team vs Individual Assignment

- **Team assignment**
  - Conversation becomes visible to the selected team
  - Any team member can respond based on access rules

- **Individual assignment**
  - Conversation is assigned directly to one staff member
  - It appears under their **Mine** tab

---

## Business Admin Capabilities

As a **Business Admin**, you can:

- View all new incoming conversations
- Assign conversations to any team or staff member
- Monitor and control conversation distribution across the organization

Other roles may have limited assignment options based on permissions.

---

## Best Practices

- Check the **New** tab frequently
- Assign conversations as soon as possible
- Use **Assign to Me** for quick handling
- Use team assignment for shared responsibility
