# Testimonials

The response from **testimonial question** in surveys will be displayed here as Testimonial card. This feature provide a facility to share
or embed these testimonial in your site.

### Testimonial Collection

You can create a collection to group testimonials and give proper name. So that, you can categorize certain type of testimonials.

#### Create collection

 - To create collection, Navigate to `Testimonial` in sidebar. Then, click **Create Collection**.
 - Click on the created collection and select testimonials
 - click on **Save and Next**
 
### Customize Testimonials

In edit tab of collection, you can click on customize button to change color or font of the testimonial card.

### Share Testimonial collection

Once all set, In share tab, if you click on share button, you will get an iframe to embed these in any website.
