# WhatsApp Catalog

The WhatsApp Catalog allows businesses to manage products, sync catalogs from Meta, and enable product-based conversations and orders directly on WhatsApp.

import ScreenShot from "../../../components/Screenshot";
import { CDN } from "../../../lib/cdn";

---

## Accessing Catalogs

Catalogs are managed from **WhatsApp → Catalog**.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/catalog-list.png`}
  alt="WhatsApp catalogs list"
/>

This page displays all catalogs connected to your WhatsApp Business Account.

---

## Catalogs Overview

Each catalog appears as a card showing:

- **Catalog name**
- **Catalog ID**
- **Catalog vertical** (for example: Commerce)

This view helps you identify and select the correct catalog when managing products or orders.

---

## Syncing Catalogs from Meta

To fetch the latest catalogs from Meta:

1. Click **Sync Catalogs** (top-right)
2. Wait for the sync to complete

This ensures your catalog list stays up to date with Meta.

---

## Opening a Catalog

Click on any catalog card to open it.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/catalog-details-products.png`}
  alt="Catalog products view"
/>

Once opened, the catalog is divided into three sections:
- **Products**
- **Orders**
- **Settings**

---

## Products Section

The **Products** tab displays all products inside the selected catalog.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/products-list.png`}
  alt="Catalog products list"
/>

From here, you can:
- View products
- Add new products
- Edit existing products
- Sync products from Meta

---

## Adding a Product

To add a new product:

1. Click **Add Product**
2. The **Add Item** form opens

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/add-product-modal.png`}
  alt="Add product modal"
  width={500}
/>

---

## Product Details Configuration

Fill in the required product information:

### Item Type
- Predefined product type (read-only)

### Product Retailer ID
- Unique identifier for the product

### Product Title
- Name shown to customers

### Product Description
- Description of the product

### Internal Labels
- Used for internal categorization and filtering

---

## Product Attributes

Configure product attributes to improve discoverability:

- **Product Condition** (for example: new)
- **Availability** (in stock / out of stock)
- **Brand**
- **Website URL**

---

## Pricing Information

Provide pricing details:

- **Price**
- **Sale Price**
- **Currency**

These values are shown to customers in WhatsApp product messages.

---

## Uploading Images and Videos

Products support media uploads.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/whatsapp/product-media-upload.png`}
  alt="Product image and video upload"
/>

### Image
- Upload or provide a link
- Minimum size: **500 × 500**

### Video
- Upload or provide a link

Tags can be added to both images and videos for internal reference.

---

## Saving a Product

- Click **Save** to add the product
- Click **Cancel** to discard changes

Once saved, the product appears in the Products list.

---

## Syncing Products from Meta

To fetch the latest products from Meta:

1. Click **Sync Products**
2. Wait for synchronization to complete

This updates product availability and details automatically.

---

## Catalog Information

## Disconnecting WhatsApp Business Account

To disconnect the linked WhatsApp Business Account:

1. Click **Disconnect WhatsApp Business Account**
2. Confirm the action

Disconnecting stops catalog syncing and product messaging.

## When to Use Catalogs

Catalogs are used when:
- Sharing products in WhatsApp chats
- Enabling product-based conversations
- Managing orders from WhatsApp
- Integrating Shopify checkout flows

---

## Best Practices

- Keep product titles and descriptions clear
- Use high-quality images
- Sync catalogs and products regularly
- Use product sets for better organization
- Review order status frequently

---

## Why WhatsApp Catalog Matters

WhatsApp Catalog enables businesses to showcase products, manage orders, and drive conversions directly inside WhatsApp, creating a seamless commerce experience.
