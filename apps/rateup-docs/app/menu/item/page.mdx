# Menu Item

### Before you begin:

- **Create a menu Category:** Head over to the "Menu" tab to set the stage.

### Creating your first Item:

1. Click "Add" to button to at the end of the each category named banner.
2. **Essential details:**
   - **Name:** Enter a clear and descriptive name for easy identification.
   - **Visual flair (optional):** Add an image to make it shine (*upto 3 images*).
   - **Video Url:** Provide Video url from any site -optional
   - **Price:** We can give price in two ways
        * Fixed: You give only one price
        * Variant: You give multiple price with each name and its price
   - **Food Label:** You can assign multiple food labels, including ingredients like milk, eggs, and others, through a multi-select option.
3. Click "Create Item" to create the item.

### Editing existing item:

1. Find the item needing a touch-up and click the pencil icon to edit.
2. Modify the details confidently to ensure they reflect your current needs.
3. Click "Save  Item" to update the data.

### Deleting a item (proceed with caution):

1. Locate the item to delete and click the trash icon to delete.
2. **Remember, it's permanent:** Acknowledge that deleted item can't return, then click the confirmation button to finalize the removal.

### Bonus features:

- **Disable Item:** Click the icon with a blue circle containing a checkmark to disable the item. Clicking it again will re-enable the item.

- **Swap order:** Rearrange your item:
   - Click on the swap icon, located as the last icon in the row, following the trash icon.
   - A modal awaits! Grab and hold the 6-dot icon next to each item name, drag and drop to your desired order, then click "Save" to make it official.
