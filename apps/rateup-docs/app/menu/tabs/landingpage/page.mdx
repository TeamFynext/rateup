# Menu Landing page

Customizing the landing page allows you to create a branded and engaging introduction to your menu. It helps in making respondents feel more comfortable and sets a positive tone for their menu-taking experience. Additionally, the customization options ensure that the landing page aligns with your brand aesthetics and menu objectives.

#### Background Customization
   - You have the option to choose the background for the landing page. This can be an image. Choosing a background helps set the tone and visual appeal of the landing page.
   - Upload separate backgrounds for mobile devices and "big screen" devices, such as tablets or larger screens using different buttons

#### Logo Upload
   - You can upload a logo for the menu on the landing page. This logo could be your brand logo or any relevant image. Additionally, you can adjust the size of the logo to ensure it fits well with the overall design.

#### Icon Change
   - Customize the menu icon by selecting your preferred icon from the available options in the icon tab. You can switch it to a catalogue icon or any other icon of your choice with a simple click.

#### Header Customization
   - Add a concise, welcoming text and style it to improve readability and visual appeal.

#### Title Customization
   - The title of the landing page is a crucial element. You can give your menu a catchy or informative title and style it according to your preferences. This helps in conveying the purpose or theme of the menu.

#### Menu Page Toggle
   - This switch enables or disables the presence of the menu icon on the landing page.

#### Survey Page Toggle
   - This switch controls the presence or absence of the survey icon on the landing page.
