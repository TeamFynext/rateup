## Welcome Page

 The welcome page is the first impression respondents have when they access your survey. 
 To create welcom page click on `Add Content` in the survey editor and click on **Welcome Page**.
 The welcome page will be created by by default when creating survey. You can delete it if you don't need a welcome page.

#### Customizing Welcome Page

You can customize welcome page according to your preference. You can either use survey theme or individually change each items. By default, it will use survey theme. You can disable it by unticking the `Use Survey Theme`.

To change contents of title, subtitle or button text click on these items or pencil icon right to this. To style welcome page, click on Edit button on the top right. An editor will open in the right side.

You can customize following:

##### 1. Background
  
  - Click on `Background`.
  - Choose the background,either color or image: If you have choosed image, then click on upload button and upload an image.    

##### 2. Title

Click on pencil icon on the right of title **Welcome**.  Give a suitable title according to your preference.

To change the font styles, 
  - Click on `Title` from the right panel
  - Select the font weight from dropdown
  - Change the font size
  - Change the font color 
  

##### 3. SubTitle

Click on pencil icon on the right of subtitle **welcome to the survey**.  Give a suitable subtitle according to your preference.

To change the font styles, 
  - Click on `SubTitle` from the right panel
  - Select the font weight from dropdown
  - Change the font size
  - Change the font color 

##### 4. Button

Click on pencil icon on the right of button **Start survey**.  Give a suitable text for the button according to your preference.

To change the button styles, 
  - Click on `Button` from the right panel
  - Select a background color
  - Select a text color
  - Change position of button by selecting X axis and Y axis position.


#### Mobile View

In the top left corner, switch to the mobile view and repeat all the above process for changing mobile theme.

After making all the changes make sure to save the changes by clicking the `Save` button.
