The **Create Point Reward** page allows administrators to configure rewards that customers can redeem using their loyalty points.

## Creating a Point Reward

To create a new point-based reward:

1. Navigate to **Loyalty** from the sidebar.
2. Click **Rewards**.
3. Select the **Point Rewards** tab.
4. Click **Create Reward**.

The **Create Point Reward** form will open.

---

### Reward Name

Enter the name of the reward customers will see.

Examples:

- AED 10 Cashback
- Free Coffee
- 20% Weekend Discount

---

### Description

Provide a short explanation of what the customer receives when redeeming the reward.

Example:
Redeem 2,000 points and receive AED 10 cashback on your next order.


---

### Reward Type

Choose how the reward will be applied.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/reward-type-options.png`}
  alt="Reward type options"
  width={300}
/>

Available options include:

- **Fixed Cashback** – Customer receives a fixed cashback value.
- **Percentage Discount** – Customer receives a percentage discount.
- **Free Item** – Customer receives a complimentary product.
- **Free Service** – Customer receives a complimentary service.

---

### Points Cost

Specify how many loyalty points are required to redeem the reward.

Example:

- **2000 points**

---

### Cashback Amount

Defines the cashback amount returned to the customer.

Example:

- ₹200 cashback

This field is mainly used when **Fixed Cashback** is selected.

---

### Terms and Conditions

Enter any restrictions or conditions related to the reward.

Examples:

- Valid only for dine-in orders
- Cannot be combined with other offers
- Valid during weekends only

---

### Redemption Rules (Optional)

You can configure additional restrictions for redeeming the reward.

**Minimum Spend**  
Requires customers to spend a minimum amount before redeeming the reward.

**Time Restrictions**  
Limits when the reward can be redeemed.

**Tier Requirements**  
Restricts the reward to specific loyalty tiers.

---

### Creating the Reward

After configuring the reward:

1. Review the reward details.
2. Click **Create Reward**.

The reward will appear in the **Point Rewards** list and can be enabled or disabled anytime.
