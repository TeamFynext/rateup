# Registering a Customer from Cashier Portal

This guide explains how to register a new loyalty customer directly from the Cashier Portal.

---

## Open the Cashier Portal

To start customer registration:

1. Log in to your RateUp account
2. From the left sidebar, open **Loyalty**
3. Click **Cashier Portal**

The Cashier Portal allows cashiers or store staff to quickly search and register loyalty customers.

  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-portal-home.png`}

---

## Start Customer Registration

1. Click the **Register Customer** button at the top-right corner
2. The loyalty registration form will open

<ScreenShot 
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/customer-registration-form.png`} 
  alt="Customer Registration Form" 
/>

---

## Fill Customer Details

Enter the customer information in the registration form.

### Required Fields

- Customer Name
- Phone Number

### Optional Fields

- Email Address
- Date of Birth
- Referral Code

Make sure the phone number is entered correctly because loyalty rewards and notifications are linked to the customer’s number.

---

## Complete Registration

After entering all details:

1. Review the customer information
2. Click **Register**

Once registration is successful:

- The customer will be added to the loyalty program
- Loyalty points can now be earned and redeemed
- The customer will appear in the Customers section

---

## Important Notes

- Duplicate phone numbers may not be allowed
- Referral codes are optional and depend on your loyalty configuration
- Customer registration may trigger welcome messages or reward flows

---

## What’s Next?

Continue to the **Customers** section to manage registered loyalty customers and view their activity.
