# Quick Actions

The **Quick Actions** section in the Cashier Portal allows staff to perform common loyalty operations instantly after locating a customer. These actions help manage loyalty points, rewards, and coupons during a transaction.

The Quick Actions panel provides the following operations:

- **Issue Points**
- **Issue Reward**
- **Redeem Coupon**
- **Redeem Reward**

These tools allow staff to quickly manage customer loyalty activities during checkout.

---

## Issue Points

The **Issue Points** feature allows staff to add loyalty points to a customer’s account based on a purchase or activity.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-issue-points.png`}
  alt="Issue points modal"
  width={450}
/>

To issue points:

1. Click **Issue Points** from the Quick Actions section.
2. Enter the **Purchase Amount** in the input field.
3. Points will be calculated automatically based on configured **earning rules**.
4. Optionally add **Notes** for the transaction.
5. Click **Issue Points** to confirm.

This ensures that customers receive loyalty points for eligible purchases.

---

## Issue Reward

The **Issue Reward** feature allows staff to manually grant a reward to a customer.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-issue-reward.png`}
  alt="Issue reward modal"
   width={450}
/>

To issue a reward:

1. Click **Issue Reward** from the Quick Actions section.
2. Select the reward to grant from the available list.
3. Optionally add **Notes** explaining the reward.
4. Click **Assign Reward**.

This feature is commonly used for promotions, customer service recovery, or special offers.

---

## Redeem Coupon

The **Redeem Coupon** action allows staff to apply a coupon code provided by the customer.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-redeem-coupon.png`}
  alt="Redeem coupon modal"
   width={450}
/>

To redeem a coupon:

1. Click **Redeem Coupon**.
2. Enter the **coupon code** in the input field.
3. Click **Redeem Coupon** to apply the coupon.

This applies the coupon discount or offer during checkout.

---
## Use Coupon

The **Use Coupon** action marks a coupon as consumed or used.

This is typically done after successfully applying a coupon during checkout.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-portal/use-coupon-modal.png`}
  alt="Use Coupon Modal"
/>

### Steps to Use a Coupon

1. Click **Use Coupon**
2. Enter the coupon code provided by the customer
3. Verify the coupon details
4. Click **Mark as Used**

After confirmation, the coupon cannot be reused if configured as single-use.

---
## Redeem Reward

The **Redeem Reward** feature allows customers to claim available rewards using their loyalty points.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/loyalty/cashier-claim-reward.png`}
  alt="Claim reward modal"
   width={450}
/>

To redeem a reward:

1. Click **Redeem Reward** from the Quick Actions section.
2. Select the reward the customer wants to claim.
3. Review the reward details.
4. Click **Claim Reward**.

The required points will be deducted from the customer's loyalty account after redemption.

---

## Why Quick Actions Matter

Quick Actions help businesses:

- Process loyalty activities faster during checkout
- Improve customer experience with instant rewards
- Reduce manual effort for staff
- Provide seamless loyalty interactions at the point of sale

These actions ensure that loyalty benefits are easily applied during customer transactions.
