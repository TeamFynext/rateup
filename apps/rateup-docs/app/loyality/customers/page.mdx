# Customers

The **Customers** page allows administrators to view and manage members enrolled in the loyalty program. From this page, teams can search for customers, review loyalty points, track customer tiers, and export customer data for reporting.

## Opening the Customers Page

To access the Customers page:

1. Open the **Loyalty** module from the sidebar.
2. Click **Customers**.

The page loads with a table displaying all loyalty program members.

---

## Searching for Customers

The **Search bar** at the top of the page allows staff to quickly locate customers.

To search for a customer:

1. Click the **Search field**.
2. Enter the **customer name or phone number**.
3. Matching results will appear instantly in the table.

This helps staff quickly find specific loyalty members.

---

## Exporting Customer Data

The **Export Data** button allows downloading the customer list for reporting or analysis.

To export customer data:

1. Click **Export Data** in the top-right corner.
2. Select the preferred format.

Available export formats:

- **CSV** – Useful for data processing and integrations.
- **Excel** – Suitable for spreadsheet-based analysis.

---

## Understanding the Customer Table

The customer table provides a detailed overview of loyalty members.

### Customer

Displays:

- Customer **name**
- Registered **phone number**
- Profile avatar or initials

---

### Tier

Shows the customer's current loyalty level.

Examples include:

- **Bronze**
- **Silver**
- **Platinum**

Tiers represent the customer's engagement level in the loyalty program.

---

### Points

Displays the number of **available loyalty points** that the customer can redeem.

---

### Lifetime

Shows the **total number of points earned** by the customer throughout their loyalty history.

---

### Date of Birth

Displays the customer's **birth date**, which can be used for birthday rewards or special campaigns.

---

### Last Active

Shows the most recent date when the customer interacted with the loyalty system.

This helps identify active and inactive members.

---

### Referral Code

Each customer has a **unique referral code** used to invite new members to the loyalty program.

Additional options include:

- **Copy icon** – Copies the referral code.
- **Link icon** – Generates a shareable referral link.

---

### Actions

The **Actions** column provides quick access to customer details.

Available action:

- **View** – Opens the detailed customer profile.

Customer profile functionality will be documented in the **Customer Details page**.

---

## Pagination

At the bottom of the table, pagination controls allow navigation through large customer lists.

Options include:

- **Rows per page selector**
- **Page navigation controls**

This ensures smooth navigation when managing many customers.

---

## Why the Customers Page Matters

The Customers page helps teams:

- Manage loyalty program members
- Track loyalty tiers and engagement
- Monitor customer points
- Access referral codes
- Export loyalty data for reporting

This page provides a centralized overview of all customers participating in the loyalty program.
