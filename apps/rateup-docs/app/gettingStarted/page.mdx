# Register & Login to RateUp

This guide explains how to **register a new account** on RateUp and **log in after registration**.

---

### Step 1: Open RateUp

1. Visit [https://rateup.app](https://rateup.app)
2. Click the **Login** button in the top-right corner of the landing page

> ℹ️ RateUp uses a unified entry point. New users can register from the Login page.

---

### Step 2: Choose Registration Method

On the Login page, click **Create account**.

You can register using either:
- **Continue with Google** (recommended for faster setup)
- **Continue with Email**

---

### Step 3: Account Details (Step 1 of 3)

Enter your basic account information:

- **Name**
- **Email address**
- **Password**
- **Confirm password**
- Accept the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy**

Click **Continue** to proceed.

---

### Step 4: Business Information (Step 2 of 3)

Provide details about your business to personalize your RateUp experience:

- **Business Name** *(required)*
- **Website URL** *(optional)*
- **Phone Number** *(optional)*
- **Industry** *(required)*
- **Location** *(required)*

> 🔧 Your dashboard and features will be customized based on this information.

Click **Continue**.

---

### Step 5: Select Your Goals (Step 3 of 3)

Choose one or more goals that best match your needs:

- Improve Customer Support  
- Generate More Leads  
- Automate Sales Process  
- Increase Customer Retention  
- Boost Social Media Engagement  
- Build Loyalty Programs  

Click **Continue** to complete registration.

---

### Step 6: Email Verification

1. A verification email will be sent to your registered email address
2. Open the email and click **Verify Email**
3. Your account will be activated after verification

---

## Logging In to RateUp

Once your account is registered and verified:

1. Visit [https://rateup.app](https://rateup.app)
2. Click **Login**
3. Sign in using one of the following options:
   - **Continue with Google**
   - **Continue with Email** (enter your email and password)
4. Click **Sign In**

You will be redirected to your RateUp dashboard.

---

## Important Notes

- 🔐 Email verification is required before first login
- 🔁 Use **Forgot password** if you cannot access your account
- 🧠 Business details and selected goals help personalize your RateUp experience

---

## Registration Flow Summary

**Register → Verify Email → Login → Access Dashboard**
