# Login to RateUp

This guide explains how to **log in to your RateUp account** after registration.

---

## Step 1: Open RateUp

1. Visit [https://rateup.app](https://rateup.app)
2. Click the **Login** button in the top-right corner

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/authentication/home.png`} alt="RateUp login page" />

---

## Step 2: Choose Login Method

You can sign in using either:

- **Continue with Google**
- **Continue with Email**

### Email Login
1. Enter your **email address**
2. Enter your **password**
3. Click **Sign In**

<ScreenShot src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/authentication/login.png`} alt="Email login form" width="350px"/>

---

## Step 3: Access Dashboard

After successful authentication, you will be redirected to your **RateUp dashboard**.

---

## Forgot Password

If you can’t access your account:

1. Click **Forgot password**
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Follow the reset instructions sent to your email

---

## Important Notes

- 🔐 Email verification is required before first login
- 🔁 Google login works only if the email is already registered
- 🧠 Your dashboard experience is based on business details provided during registration
