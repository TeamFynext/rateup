# Campaign Details

The Campaign Details page helps you monitor campaign performance, manage participants, resend messages, and review delivery statistics.

---

# Open Campaign Details

To open a campaign:

1. Open the **Campaigns** page
2. Locate the campaign card
3. Click the campaign

---

# Campaign Overview

The **Overview** tab displays campaign information and delivery performance.

The overview includes:

- Campaign name
- Campaign ID
- Team
- Template used
- Campaign status
- Total participants
- Delivery metrics

Metrics shown:

- Sent
- Delivered
- Read
- Pending
- Failed

# Open Participants Tab

To manage recipients:

1. Open the campaign details page
2. Click the **Participants** tab

The Participants section allows you to:

- View participants
- Search participants
- Check consent status
- Resend messages
- Add participants

  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/campaign/campaign-participants-tab.png`}

# Add Participants

Participants can be added from:

- Individual contacts
- Groups
- Existing campaigns

## Add New Participants

1. Open the **Participants** tab
2. Click **Add Participant**
3. Select:
   - Individual
   - Group
   - Campaign
4. Search and select contacts
5. Click **Add Participants**

  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/campaign/campaign-add-participants.png`}

# Resend Campaign Messages

Campaign messages can be resent to selected participant statuses.

Supported statuses:

- Failed
- Pending
- Sent
- Delivered
- Read
- Completed

---

## Resend Immediately

To resend messages instantly:

1. Open the campaign details page
2. Click **Resend**
3. Select **Send Now**
4. Choose participant statuses
5. Click **Resend Campaign**

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/campaign/campaign-resend-send-now.png`}
  alt="Resend campaign immediately"
  width={400}
/>

---

## Schedule Resend

To resend messages later:

1. Open the **Resend** modal
2. Select **Schedule Later**
3. Choose:
   - Date
   - Time
4. Select participant statuses
5. Click **Resend Campaign**

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/campaign/campaign-resend-schedule-later.png`}
  alt="Schedule campaign resend"
  width={400}
/>

# Delivery Report

To review delivery statistics:

1. Open the campaign details page
2. Click **Delivery Report**

The report helps analyze:

- Delivery status
- Failed messages
- Read rate
- Campaign engagement

---

# Important Notes

- Campaign performance updates automatically
- Participants can be managed anytime
- Messages can be resent to selected statuses
- Delivery reports help track engagement
- Consent-enabled contacts are recommended
