# Managing Campaigns

This guide explains how to manage campaigns from the Campaigns dashboard.

The Campaigns page allows you to:

- View campaigns
- Search campaigns
- Filter campaigns
- Sort campaigns
- Enable or disable campaigns
- View campaign details
- Edit campaigns
- Delete campaigns

---

## Open Campaigns

To access Campaigns:

1. Open the RateUp dashboard
2. From the sidebar, click **Campaigns**

The Campaigns dashboard will open.

  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/campaign/campaigns-list-page.png`}

/>

---

## View Campaigns

The Campaigns page displays all available campaigns in card format.

Each card displays:

- Campaign name
- Channel type
- Assigned team
- Status
- Created date
- Audience or participant count

---

## Switch Between Broadcast and Drip Campaigns

At the top of the page, use the tabs to switch between:

- **Broadcast**
- **Drip**

This allows you to manage different campaign types separately.

---

## Search Campaigns

To search campaigns:

1. Use the search bar in the top-right corner
2. Enter the campaign name or keyword

Matching campaigns will appear automatically.

---

## Sort Campaigns

To sort campaigns:

1. Click the **Newest** dropdown
2. Select a sorting option

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/campaign/campaign-sort-options.png`}
  alt="Campaign sort options"
  width={300}
/>

Available options include:

- Newest
- Oldest
- Name (A-Z)
- Name (Z-A)

---

## Filter Campaigns

To filter campaigns:

1. Click **Filters**
2. Configure filter options
3. Click **Apply**

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/campaign/campaign-filters.png`}
  alt="Campaign filters"
  width={350}
/>

---

## Filter by Team

Use the **Team** dropdown to display campaigns belonging to a specific team.

Example:
- All Teams
- Main
- Calicut

---

## Filter by Status

Use the **Status** dropdown to filter campaigns by status.

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/campaign/campaign-filter-status-dropdown.png`}
  alt="Campaign status filter"
  width={300}
/>

Available statuses include:

- Active
- Inactive
- Draft
- Scheduled
- Completed

Reset Filters

To clear all applied filters:

1. Click **Reset**
2. The campaign list will return to default view


---

## Enable or Disable a Campaign

Each campaign card includes a toggle switch.

### To Enable a Campaign

1. Turn the toggle ON
2. The campaign status will change to **Active**

### To Disable a Campaign

1. Turn the toggle OFF
2. The campaign status will change to **Inactive**

---

## Open Campaign Actions

Each campaign card contains an action menu.

To open campaign actions:

1. Click the three-dot menu on the campaign card

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/campaign/campaign-card-actions.png`}
  alt="Campaign card actions"
  width={300}
/>

---

## View Campaign Details

To view campaign information:

1. Open the campaign actions menu
2. Click **View Details**

You can review campaign configuration, templates, participants, and performance.

---

## Edit a Campaign

To modify a campaign:

1. Open the campaign actions menu
2. Click **Edit**
3. Update campaign details
4. Save changes

---

## Delete a Campaign

To remove a campaign:

1. Open the campaign actions menu
2. Click **Delete**
3. Confirm deletion

Deleted campaigns cannot be recovered.

---

## Important Notes

- Only active campaigns can send messages
- Filters help organize large campaign lists
- Broadcast and drip campaigns are managed separately
- Campaigns can be paused or resumed anytime
