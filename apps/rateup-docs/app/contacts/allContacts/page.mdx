# All Contacts Overview

The **All Contacts** section provides a complete list of every contact stored in RateUp. It acts as the primary workspace for viewing, managing, and organizing customer contact data collected from multiple channels.

## What Is All Contacts?

All Contacts is a unified contact directory that brings together contacts created from:

- WhatsApp
- Web Chat
- Manual entry

Each contact appears as a row in a structured table, making it easy for teams to scan, filter, and take action on customer data.

---

## What You Can Do Here

Using the All Contacts section, you can:

- View all customer contacts in one place  
- Search contacts by name, phone number, or email  
- Filter contacts by lifecycle stage and source  
- Identify how and when a contact was created  
- Add new contacts manually  
- Import or export contact data  
- Perform actions on individual or multiple contacts  

---

## How All Contacts Is Organized

The page is designed around a table layout that displays essential contact information such as:

- Contact name or identifier  
- Phone number or system ID  
- Labels for categorization  
- Source of contact creation  
- Date the contact was added  

Additional actions can be accessed through the action menu available for each contact.

---

## When to Use All Contacts

All Contacts is most useful when you need to:

- Quickly locate a specific customer  
- Review contacts from a particular source  
- Manage large volumes of customer data  
- Prepare contact lists for follow-ups or exports  
- Maintain a clean and organized contact database
