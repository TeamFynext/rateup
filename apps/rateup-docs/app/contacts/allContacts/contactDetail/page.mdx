# Contact Details

The **Contact Details** view allows you to view and manage all information related to a specific contact. This includes contact information, group membership, activity history, and internal notes.

## Opening Contact Details

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/view-contactdetail.png`}

To open the Contact Details view:

1. Go to the **Contacts List**
2. Locate the required contact
3. Click the three-dot **Action Menu (⋮)**
4. Select **View Details**

The Contact Details modal opens for the selected contact.

## Tabs Overview

The Contact Details view is organized into four tabs:

- **Details**
- **Groups**
- **Activity**
- **Notes**

Each tab focuses on a specific aspect of the contact.

---

## Viewing Contact Details

To verify basic contact information:

1. Open the **Details** tab
2. Review the displayed information

This tab shows:
- Phone number
- Primary contact identifier

Use this tab to quickly confirm the contact’s identity.

---

## Managing Groups

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/contact-groups-tab.png`}
  alt="Contact groups tab"
  width={450}
/>

To view or update group membership:

1. Open the **Groups** tab
2. Review the list of groups the contact belongs to
3. If the contact is not part of any group, a placeholder message is shown
4. Click **Add to Group** to add the contact to an existing group

Groups help organize contacts into structured collections.

## Adding and Editing Notes

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/contact-notes-tab.png`}

  width={450}

To add or update internal notes:

1. Open the **Notes** tab
2. Enter notes related to the contact
3. Click **Edit Notes** to modify existing notes

Notes are internal-only and help teams share important context.

---

## Actions

At the bottom of the Contact Details modal, the following actions are available:

- **Send Message** – Start a conversation with the contact
- **Close** – Exit the Contact Details view

These actions allow quick follow-ups without leaving the contact context.

---

## Why Contact Details Matter

The Contact Details view helps teams:

- Access complete contact information in one place  
- Manage group membership efficiently  
- Review contact activity and engagement  
- Maintain internal notes for collaboration  
- Quickly message contacts when needed
