# Contact Details

The **Contact Details** view allows you to view and manage all information related to a specific contact. This includes contact information, group membership, activity history, and internal notes.

## Opening Contact Details

You can open the Contact Details view by clicking **View Details** from the contact action menu in the Contacts List.

This opens a modal where you can manage all aspects of the selected contact.

## Tabs Overview

The Contact Details view is organized into four tabs:

- **Details**
- **Groups**
- **Activity**
- **Notes**

Each tab focuses on a specific aspect of the contact.

---

## Details Tab

The **Details** tab displays the basic contact information.

This includes:
- Phone number
- Primary contact identifier

The Details tab helps quickly identify and verify the contact.

---

## Groups Tab

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/contact-groups-tab.png`}
  alt="Contact groups tab"
  width={450}
/>

The **Groups** tab shows the group membership of the contact.

- Displays all groups the contact belongs to
- If the contact is not part of any group, a placeholder message is shown
- Use **Add to Group** to add the contact to an existing group

Groups help organize contacts into meaningful collections.

---

## Activity Tab

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/contact-activity-tab.png`}
  alt="Contact activity tab"
  width={450}
/>

The **Activity** tab displays interaction history related to the contact.

- Shows past activities when available
- Displays a message when no activity history exists

This tab helps track engagement and previous actions.

---

## Notes Tab

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/contact-notes-tab.png`}
  alt="Contact notes tab"
  width={450}
/>

The **Notes** tab allows internal users to add private notes about the contact.

- Add notes to capture important context
- Click **Edit Notes** to update or modify notes
- Notes are for internal use and are not visible to customers

---

## Actions

At the bottom of the Contact Details modal, the following actions are available:

- **Send Message** – Start a conversation with the contact
- **Close** – Exit the Contact Details view

These actions allow quick follow-ups without leaving the contact context.

---

## Why Contact Details Matter

The Contact Details view helps teams:

- Access complete contact information in one place  
- Manage group membership efficiently  
- Review contact activity and engagement  
- Maintain internal notes for collaboration  
- Quickly message contacts when needed
