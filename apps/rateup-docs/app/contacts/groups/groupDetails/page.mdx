# Group Details

The **Group Details** page is used to view and manage a specific contact group. From this page, you can review group information, manage members, and perform group-level actions.

## Opening a Group

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/groups-overview.png`}
  alt="Opening a Group"

To open a group:

1. Navigate to the **Groups List**
2. Click on the required group card

The Group Details page opens for the selected group.

## Group Header

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/group-header.png`}
  alt="Group details header showing group name and total members"

At the top of the page, the group header displays:

- **Group Name**
- **Team** associated with the group
- **Total Members** count
- **Back to Groups** button
- **Delete Group** action

Use the header to quickly understand the group context or navigate back.

---

## Viewing Group Members

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/group-members-list.png`}
  alt="Group members list"
/>

To view contacts in the group:

- Scroll through the **Group Members** list
- Each row represents one contact in the group

For each contact, the list shows:

- Contact name and avatar
- Phone / IGSID
- Label (if assigned)
- Source (Manual, Imported, WhatsApp, etc.)
- Date added to the group
- Available actions

---

## Searching Group Members

To find a specific member within the group:

1. Use the search input above the members list
2. Enter a contact **name** or **phone number**
3. Review the filtered results as they update in real time

This is especially useful for groups with many members.

---

## Adding Members to a Group

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/add-members-modal.png`}
  alt="Add members to group modal"
  width={450}
/>

To add members:

1. Click **Add Members**
2. Choose one of the following options:
   - Add a **new contact**
   - Select **existing contacts**
   - Import contacts using **CSV / XLSX**
3. Fill in any optional fields if required:
   - Labels
   - Lifecycle stage
   - Assignee
   - Custom fields (for example, coupon)
   - Notes
4. Save to add contacts to the group

New members appear immediately in the group list.

## Customizing the Members List View

<ScreenShot
  src={`${CDN}/docs/screenshots/contacts/group-view-settings.png`}
  alt="Toggle columns in group members list"
  width={200}
/>

To customize visible columns:

1. Click **View Settings**
2. Enable or disable required columns

Available columns include:

- Name
- Phone / IGSID
- Label
- Source
- Date Added
- Last Contact
- Action

Changes apply instantly and affect only your current view.

---

## Removing Members from a Group

To remove a contact from the group:

1. Locate the contact in the members list
2. Click **Remove member**

The contact is removed from the group but remains available in the Contacts List.

---

## Group-Level Actions

You can perform the following actions from the Group Details page:

- **Delete Group** – Permanently deletes the group
- **Back to Groups** – Returns to the Groups List

> Deleting a group does not delete the contacts inside it.

---

## Why Group Details Matter

The Group Details page allows teams to:

- Manage group membership effectively
- Add or remove contacts as needed
- Search within large groups
- Customize member list views
- Keep contact groups organized
