#+title: Architecture - Loyalty

* Types of Loyalty Campaigns
** 1. Transaction
In this each transaction (or an action) is rewarded to the one who made the transaction.
** 2. Referral
Participants (users who registered for the referral campaign program) are given a unique Referral Code.
When a new user signs up using this code, both the new user & referrer are rewarded.
** 3. Referral Transaction
Participants (users who registered for the referral campaign program) are given a unique Referral Code.
When a user makes a transaction with a Referral Code, both the user & the owner of the Referral Code are rewarded.

* Schema doc
** LCampaignRewardEvent
Records all reward-worthy events occured in a campaign.


*** fields
**** referrer | LoyaltyCustomer
the one who referred the customer
**** customer | LoyaltyCustomer
could be any of the one listed below
- the one who is being referred in ReferralCampaign.
  No transaction is involved. The customer just registered.

- the one who made the transaction (Transaction | ReferralTransaction)
**** transactionAmount | number
transaction amount in events having a transaction. NULL otherwise

*** Sample Data
| id | referrer? | customer? | transactionAmount? |
|  0 | bob       | jane      |               NULL |
|  1 | bob       | jane      |                100 |
|  2 | NULL      | alice     |                1000 |

**** id 0 (CampaignType: Referral)
This is how a record from campaignType Referral would look like. In this both referrer & referree(customer) are recorded.
**** id 1 (CType: ReferralTransaction)
Just like above, but also includes the transactionAmount
**** id 2 (CType: Transaction)
Does not have a referrer. It is the event of a customer making a transaction in campaign period.

** LCustomerRewardEvent
How much points are alloted to the customer is calculated & recorded here on each LCampaignRewardEvent-record.

For a Referral or ReferralTransaction event, 2 rows will be created (one for referrer & one for referree).

*** Sample Data
| id | LCampaignRewardEvent | customer | type     |    pointsEarned |
|----+----------------------+----------+----------+-----------------|
|  0 |                    0 | bob      | Referrer |               1 |
|  1 |                    0 | jane     | Customer |               2 |
|  2 |                    2 | alice    | Customer | 10 (1% of 1000) |
