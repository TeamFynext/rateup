**Create Campaign** opens a guided wizard. Every step saves to a **Draft** campaign as you move
through it, so you can leave at any point and pick up where you stopped — nothing is sent until you
press the launch button on the last step.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/campaign/wizard-review.png" alt="Campaign wizard Review step with per-step summary cards and the launch rail" />

---

## Before You Start

| You need | Where |
|----------|-------|
| An **approved** template | [Message Templates](/whatsapp/templates) — only approved templates are listed |
| A connected WhatsApp number | [Channels](/settings/channels) |
| An audience | [Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts), [audiences](/contacts/audiences) or a past campaign |
| Campaign credits | [Billing](/settings/billing) — one credit per message to one recipient |

---

## How the Wizard Works

The rail across the top shows the steps and ticks each one off as it is completed. Click a step to
jump to it. A **Drip** campaign has no **Retries** step, so it runs to five steps instead of six.

| Footer button | What it does |
|---------------|--------------|
| **Back** | The previous step |
| **Save & exit** | Saves the step you are on and returns to the campaigns list, with a *"Draft saved"* toast |
| **Next** | Saves the step and moves on. Not shown on **Review** |
| **Discard draft** | Deletes the draft and everything set up on it, after a confirmation. Only shown while the campaign is still a draft |

The badge in the top right reads **Draft · saved just now** after each save. Only **Basics** and
**Template** are required — every other step can be left at its defaults.

## Create a Campaign

<Steps>
<Step>
### Basics

Click **Create Campaign**. On *"Let's start with the basics"*, fill in:

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| **Campaign name** | *"Internal only — recipients never see this."* Required |
| **Team** | *"Controls who can edit and see results."* Required |
| **Campaign type** | **Broadcast** or **Drip** — fixed once the draft is created |

Click **Next** to create the draft.
</Step>
<Step>
### Template

**For a broadcast**, pick one template under **Choose a template**. Search with **Search
templates**, or narrow the list to **All**, **Marketing** or **Utility**. Beside the list sit
**Variable mapping** and **Template details**.

The message itself is shown in the **Device Preview** panel on the right, which opens by itself on
this step: the template as the recipient will see it in WhatsApp, with the variables filled in. The
**Preview** button in the header hides and shows it. The panel closes when you move on to
**Audience**.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/campaign/template-step.png" alt="The campaign Template step: template list, Variable mapping and Template details, with the template shown in the Device Preview phone" />

Under **Variable mapping**, every placeholder in the template body — `{{1}}`, `{{2}}` — must be
mapped to **Contact name** or one of your [custom fields](/contacts/custom-fields). Until they are,
**Next** stops with *"Map every placeholder to a contact field to continue."*

**For a drip**, *"Build the sequence"*: each **Stage** has a template, a delay in **minutes**,
**hours** or **days**, and a time of day. *"Each stage sends once, the given delay after the contact
joins."* Use **Add stage** for more and **Remove stage** to drop one. Every stage needs a template. The
**Device Preview** panel shows the first stage's template.
</Step>
<Step>
### Audience

*"Who should receive this?"* Pick recipients from three tabs:

| Tab | Adds |
|-----|------|
| **Individual** | Contacts you tick. **Filters** narrows the list by label, pipeline stage (or lifecycle), source and a custom-field value |
| **Audience** | Everyone in an [audience](/contacts/audiences). Filters set on screen also narrow which audience members are added |
| **Campaign** | The audience of a past campaign, filtered by **Message Status** — **COMPLETED**, **NOT COMPLETED**, **PENDING** or **FAILED** |

When a search or filter is active, **Add all matching** enrols every contact that matches, across
all pages — not just the page you can see. Anyone already on the campaign is skipped.

Only **WhatsApp** contacts are listed, and an audience's count reflects the WhatsApp members it holds.
Audiences and campaigns are added as individual recipients when you save, so later changes to the audience
do not change the campaign.

This step is optional: *"Optional — you can add recipients after saving."*
</Step>
<Step>
### Schedule

*"When should it go out?"* Choose **Send now** or **Schedule**. A scheduled send takes a date,
**Time** and **Timezone**.

| Setting | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Daily send limit** | Off by default. Turn it on and set **Messages per day** to spread the send. The step shows when it will finish — *"N recipients at N/day → finishes"* a date |
| **Only send to contacts who opted in** | On by default. Skips contacts with no recorded WhatsApp opt-in. *"Recommended — required for marketing templates in several countries."* |

On a metered plan the step also says how many campaign credits the send will use and how many you
have left.
</Step>
<Step>
### Retries

Broadcasts only. *"Recover messages Meta blocks"* — turn on **Retry blocked messages** to re-send
messages Meta refused for a reason that can clear on its own, such as a marketing frequency cap
(error 131049). See [Automatic retries](/campaigns/pausing-and-retrying#retry-messages-meta-blocked-automatically)
for the strategies and what is retried.
</Step>
<Step>
### Review

*"Everything ready?"* One card per step, each with a badge — **Set**, **Needed**, **Empty**,
**Scheduled**, **Immediate**, **On** or **Off** — and an **Edit** link back to it.

The right-hand rail shows the recipients, template, start time, pace and retries, and on a metered
plan **Campaign credits after launch**. Click **Launch campaign** — or **Schedule campaign** when a
send time is set.
</Step>
</Steps>

When you launch, the wizard opens the [campaign page](/campaigns/campaign-details).

## Send a Test First

**Send test to me** on the Review step sends the real template to one number before you commit the
whole audience.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Send test to me**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Send a test to**, search your contacts by name or number, or enter a number with its
country code.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Send test**.
</Step>
</Steps>

If the number belongs to one of your contacts, the test is filled in with their own name and fields;
otherwise the template's example values are used. A template with a Flow button sends the button too.

The test is not a campaign send: it adds no participant and moves none of the campaign's numbers.
It is still a real WhatsApp message — *"Meta charges for it like any other message."*

<Callout type="warn">
  A mis-mapped variable goes out to your entire list at once and cannot be recalled. Send a test to
  yourself and read it on your phone before you launch.
</Callout>

## Campaign Credits

| Message on Review | Meaning |
|-------------------|---------|
| *"Uses N of N credits left"* | What the launch will spend |
| *"Uses N of N credits left — N stages each"* | A drip spends one credit per stage per recipient |
| *"N campaign messages are beyond your credits and will not be sent."* | The audience is bigger than your remaining credits |
| *"Your campaign credits are used up, so nothing would be sent."* | Launch is disabled until you **Buy credits** or **Upgrade** |

Retries use a credit too. If credits run out mid-send, the campaign
[pauses](/campaigns/pausing-and-retrying#when-rateup-pauses-a-campaign) rather than failing the rest.

## Edit or Add to a Campaign Later

**Edit** on the campaign page reopens the wizard on its Review step; **Send to more** opens it on
**Audience**. On a campaign that has already gone out, Review says *"This campaign has already been
sent. Only the N recipients it has never messaged will be sent to — nobody is messaged twice."* and
the button reads **Send to N new recipients**.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| The template is not in the list | It is not approved, or not available to the campaign's team | Check its status in [Message Templates](/whatsapp/templates) |
| **Next** will not leave the Template step | A placeholder has no contact field | Map every row under **Variable mapping** |
| **Launch campaign** is disabled | No template, or no campaign credits | *"Add a template before launching — everything else is optional."* or buy credits |
| A test never arrives | The number has no country code | Use the country picker — *"N's number has no country code. Fix it above to send."* |
| Fewer people received it than were added | **Only send to contacts who opted in** skipped those without consent | Expected — check consent on [Contacts](/contacts/managing-contacts) |
