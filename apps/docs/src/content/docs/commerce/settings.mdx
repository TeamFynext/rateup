**Commerce → Settings** covers *"How you get paid and deliver, what checkout asks, and your store's
details."* Each tab keeps its edits while you look at another; click **Save changes** in the bar at
the bottom of a tab to keep them, or **Discard**. **Customer messages** has no bar: each change there
saves on its own.

| Tab | Covers | Page |

## Set Delivery Rates

**Shipping rates** — *"Customers see the rates that match their address."* With no rates, *"Checkout
skips the delivery step until you add one."*

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add rate**.
</Step>
<Step>
Give it an **Option name** (such as Standard) and a **Zone** name, and pick **Countries** — *"Leave
empty to offer this rate everywhere."*
</Step>
<Step>
Set **Price**, an optional **Free over** amount, and **Fastest (days)** / **Slowest (days)**. Keep
**Offer this rate** on and click **Save rate**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The table lists **Zone**, **Option**, **Delivery time** (*"2–5 days"*, or **Same day** for 0–0),
**Price** (**Free** at zero) and **Free over**. A rate that is switched off shows **Off**.

| Checkout | Which rates apply |
|----------|-------------------|
| RateUp | These rates |
| WooCommerce | These rates — customers pick one before paying, *"since WooCommerce's payment page has no delivery step"* |
| Shopify | *"Shopify's own shipping rates apply there. These rates apply only to RateUp checkout."* |

### Buy Shipping Labels With EasyPost

**Shipping labels** — *"Connect EasyPost to compare carriers and buy labels from an order. Customers
get tracking on WhatsApp automatically."* **Connect** asks for the **API key** and the address
parcels ship from. Once connected, each order's **Ship this order** gets a **Buy a label** tab — see
[Ship an Order](/commerce/orders#ship-an-order).

## Connect Your Meta Catalog

The **Store** tab's **Meta catalog** card: *"WhatsApp and Instagram show your products from this
catalog. Picking one saves straight away."*

<Steps>
<Step>
Connect a WhatsApp number first — the card shows **Connect WhatsApp** until you do.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Find my catalogs**. RateUp pulls the catalogs on your Meta account.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Use this catalog** on the one to use. It is marked **Used by your shop**, and *"Your
products are being published to it now."*
</Step>
</Steps>

Connecting links the catalog to every WhatsApp Business account in your organisation and publishes
every product you own to it. With one catalog (or one already linked to WhatsApp), RateUp picks it
for you. *"Products you add or change are published to this catalog automatically."*

| Message | Means |
|---------|-------|
| *"No catalog on your Meta account yet"* | Create one in Meta Commerce Manager (**Open Meta Commerce Manager**), then **Find my catalogs again** |
| *"Could not reach Meta. Try again."* | Meta did not answer |
| **Connected to WhatsApp** on a catalog | It is already linked to a WhatsApp number |

## Choose What Customers Hear

**Customer messages** lists every message the shop sends on its own, in two cards: **Order
updates** and **Reminders**. WhatsApp only lets a business send free-form messages to a customer
who has written in the last 24 hours, so each message has two parts:

| Part of the card | Goes to | What is sent |
|------------------|---------|--------------|
| **Customer wrote in the last 24 hours** | Customers in an open WhatsApp chat, and every Instagram and Messenger customer | Your words, as a chat message |
| **No message from them in 24 hours** | Everyone else on WhatsApp | The approved template you pick — or nothing, with **None: open chats only** |

The badge by the message's name sums it up: **Reaches every customer** (an approved template is
picked), **Open chats only**, or **Off**.

### Write a Message in Your Own Words

<Steps>
<Step>
On the message's card, click **Edit message**. The box opens on the words customers get now — the
default until you change it.
</Step>
<Step>
Write the message. The buttons above the box add emoji, WhatsApp's bold, italic, strikethrough and
monospace, and the message's variables: hover a variable such as `{{2}}` to see what it stands for,
and click it to insert it. The line under the message lists them, for example `{{1}}` Customer's
first name · `{{2}}` Order number · `{{3}}` Order total · `{{4}}` Items ordered · `{{5}}` Delivery
address.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**. The words are used straight away. Nothing is sent to Meta.
</Step>
</Steps>

To go back to RateUp's wording, click **Edit message**, then **Use the default words**.

| Writing rule | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Use only the message's own variables | A message with five variables refuses `{{6}}` — *"This message has 5 variables; remove the extra one."* |
| Stay under 1,024 characters | WhatsApp's limit. The counter under the box turns red past it |
| Words inside `[[ ]]` are optional | They are left out when the value inside is empty: the Shipped default `Your order {{2}} is on its way.[[ Carrier: {{5}}.]]` drops the carrier sentence when there is no carrier |
| The total already has its currency | Write `{{3}}`, not `₹{{3}}`, or customers see the symbol twice |

The **Confirm order** / **Cancel** buttons on **Confirm a cash order**, the rating buttons on **Ask
for a review** and **Buy now** on **Back in stock** are added for you.

### Reach Customers Who Haven't Written in 24 Hours

Under **No message from them in 24 hours**, pick one of your approved
[WhatsApp templates](/whatsapp/templates). The card then shows that template's text — what these
customers will read — and what fills its variables: *"Filled with `{{1}}` Customer's first name ·
`{{2}}` Order number …"*. A template's `{{1}}`, `{{2}}` … get the message's variables in the order
the card lists them, so write your template with that order in mind.

| You want | Do |
|----------|----|
| A template that already exists | Pick it in the list |
| A template with the same words as your message | Click **Make a template from my words**. RateUp sends it to Meta — *"Sent to Meta for approval"* — and picks it once made. Until Meta approves it, these customers get nothing |
| To reach only customers in an open chat | Pick **None: open chats only** |

<Callout type="warn">
  A template for **Confirm a cash order** needs quick-reply buttons named **Confirm order** and
  **Cancel**, and one for **Ask for a review** buttons named **Loved it**, **It was okay** and **Not
  happy** — taps on a button with other words do nothing. The card warns when a picked template's
  buttons don't match. **Make a template from my words** adds the right buttons for you.
</Callout>

A template made from your words keeps the words it was made with. When you edit the message later,
the card says *"This template still has your old words."* — click **Update the template** to send
the new words to Meta.

**Make a template from my words** needs a connected WhatsApp number, and Meta refuses a template
that starts or ends with a variable or runs past 1,024 characters once a link line is added. Your
words are kept either way, and the card says why the template wasn't made.

<Callout type="info">
  Meta charges for every template it delivers. Messages in an open chat are free.
</Callout>

### Turn Messages On or Off

Each card's switch saves straight away.

| Message | On by default |
|---------|---------------|
| **Cash order placed**, **Confirm a cash order**, **Bank transfer details** | Yes |
| **Payment received**, **Cash order confirmed** | Yes |
| **Packed** | No |
| **Shipped**, **Out for delivery**, **Delivered**, **Delivery failed** | Yes |
| **Cancelled**, **Refunded** | Yes |
| **Payment reminder**, **Cart reminder**, **Back in stock** | Yes |
| **Ask for a review** | Yes — two days after delivery |

**Confirm a cash order** and **Bank transfer details** follow **Cash order placed**'s switch until
you change them, and **Cash order confirmed** follows **Payment received**'s.

<Callout type="info">
  In the current build **Payment reminder**, **Cart reminder** and **Back in stock** go out whatever
  their switch says. To stop cart reminders, pick no times on
  [Checkout](/commerce/checkout-settings#remind-customers-who-did-not-pay).
</Callout>

<Callout type="warn">
  Keep **Confirm a cash order** on if you ask cash customers to confirm. With it off — or with no
  template for customers outside the 24-hour window — they get no **Confirm order** / **Cancel**
  message, and with **Ask the customer to confirm** on, those orders are cancelled when **Hours to
  confirm** runs out.
</Callout>

A storefront order whose number has not been confirmed on WhatsApp gets no messages until it is
paid; its timeline notes *"the number isn't confirmed yet"*. See
[Storefront](/commerce/storefront#how-shoppers-confirm-their-number).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"Could not load your shop settings"* | The shop could not be loaded | Reload the page |
| Fields are greyed out | You lack the permission for that tab | Ask an admin — see [Who Can Do What](/commerce#who-can-do-what) |
| A message never arrives outside 24 hours | The card shows **Open chats only**, or its template is awaiting Meta or rejected | Pick an approved template, or click **Make a template from my words** and wait for approval |
| *"Saved. The WhatsApp template wasn't made."* | The card's note gives the reason | Connect a WhatsApp number, or move a variable away from the start or end |
| Customers can't confirm a cash order from the template | The picked template's buttons aren't named **Confirm order** / **Cancel** | Rename its buttons, or use **Make a template from my words** |
| No **Legacy catalog** tab | Your account never used the older catalog tools | Nothing to do |
