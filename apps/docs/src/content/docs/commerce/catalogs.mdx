These are the **WhatsApp catalog tools** — *"From before Commerce had its own products. Manage items
straight in the Meta catalog and see orders sent from the catalog cart. New shops don't need
these."*

<Callout type="info">
  New shops add products under [Products](/commerce/products) and connect the Meta catalog under
  [Settings → Store](/commerce/settings#connect-your-meta-catalog); RateUp then publishes to the
  catalog for you. Use these tools only for a catalog you still manage in Meta directly.
</Callout>

Open them from **Commerce → Settings → Legacy catalog**. That tab appears only when your account has
products kept in the Meta catalog or orders from the catalog cart. It has **Catalog products**,
**Catalog orders**, and — with a catalog connected — **Catalog cart checkout** and **Catalog cart
payments**. The old **WhatsApp → Catalog** address also lands here.

## Pick the Store and Catalog

**Catalog products** (*"Manage products and collections synced to WhatsApp & Instagram."*) has two
switchers at the top:

| Switcher | Picks |
|----------|-------|
| **Select store** | The WhatsApp number whose catalog you are viewing |
| **Select catalog** | Which of your Meta catalogs to show. The one linked to the selected number is marked **Connected** |

The page has three tabs: **Products · N**, **Collections · N** and **Settings**.

---

## Connect a Catalog to Your WhatsApp Account

This links a catalog to the WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) behind a number, from the catalog's
**Settings** tab. For a Commerce shop, [Use this catalog](/commerce/settings#connect-your-meta-catalog)
does this for every account at once.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/commerce/catalog-settings.png" alt="A catalog's Settings tab: WhatsApp Business Account, Shopify checkout automation and Danger zone" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Choose the number in **Select store** and the catalog in **Select catalog**, then open **Settings**.
</Step>
<Step>
Leave **SMB (Coexistence Account)** off for a normal Cloud API number; turn it on for a number shared
with the WhatsApp Business app.
</Step>
<Step>
Choose the account under **Connected WhatsApp Business Account ID** and confirm **Connect**.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Mode | What connecting does |
|------|----------------------|
| **SMB (Coexistence Account)** off | *"Normal Account — will also update in Meta"* |
| **SMB (Coexistence Account)** on | *"DB only, no Meta update"* |

**Disconnect WhatsApp Business Account** undoes the link. *"The catalog in automation on the connected
account won't work"* afterwards.

## Manage Catalog Products

Each product shows its image, title, brand, price and an **Active** or **Out of stock** badge. **Add
Product** opens the product form; a product's menu deletes it.

<Callout type="warn">
  Deleting a product here *"removes the product from your Meta catalog and cannot be undone."*
</Callout>

Products live in the Meta catalog and are pulled in with **Sync with Meta** (*"Syncing with Meta…"*,
then *"Products synced"*). The **Collections** tab has its own **Sync collections**. Only product
catalogs can be managed; another type shows *"Unsupported catalog type"*.

---

## Send a Shopify Checkout Link Automatically

The catalog's **Settings** tab has **Shopify checkout automation** — *"Automatically send a Shopify
checkout link when a customer sends a cart."* Write the **Checkout message** with `{link}` where the
URL goes.

**Danger zone → Delete Catalog** is permanent: *"All products, orders, product sets, and automation
links tied to this catalog will be permanently deleted."*

## See Catalog Cart Orders

**Catalog orders** (also **WhatsApp catalog orders** on the [Orders](/commerce/orders) page) lists
*"Orders placed through the WhatsApp catalog cart checkout."*

| Stage | Shown as |
|-------|----------|
| Pending payment | **Pending** |
| Checkout sent | **Confirmed** |
| Pending delivery | **Packed** |
| Pending address | **Shipped** |
| Completed | **Delivered** |
| Cancelled | **Cancelled** |

The filter offers **All orders**, **Pending**, **To ship**, **Shipped** and **Delivered**, and each
order shows how it was paid (UPI, **Cash on delivery**, **WhatsApp Pay**, a card, or **Pending**).

## Set Up Catalog Cart Checkout and Payments

| Card | Covers |
|------|--------|
| **Catalog cart checkout** | *"What the catalog cart asks for and how it delivers"* — **Collection**, **Address Fields** and **Delivery Options** tabs |
| **Catalog cart payments** | *"Payment settings for the older checkout that runs on the WhatsApp catalog's own order message"* — Shopify checkout URL, WhatsApp Pay, UPI payment link, cash on delivery or bank transfer |

These settings apply only to the catalog cart checkout. Commerce orders use
[Getting Paid](/commerce/payments) and [Checkout](/commerce/checkout-settings).

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---------|--------------|-----|
| *"No catalog connected"* | The selected number has no linked catalog | Pick a catalog and connect it from **Settings** |
| No **Legacy catalog** tab | Your account never used these tools | Use [Products](/commerce/products) instead |
| *"Sync failed"* | The connection to Meta has expired | Reconnect the number under [Channels](/settings/channels) |
