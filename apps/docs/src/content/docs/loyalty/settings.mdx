Loyalty **Settings** (`/dashboard/loyalty/settings`) holds the decisions that apply to the whole
programme: *"the join link, approvals, integrations, expiry and the danger zone."* Names and
branding are in [Program](/loyalty/program) › Profile, the points name under Earning, and the
messages on [Messages](/loyalty/messages).

## Pause or Resume the Programme

The **Program** card says where the programme stands:

| State | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **On** | Points are earned on every purchase and messages send |
| **Paused** | *"Every balance is kept, earning is stopped, nothing sends."* |
| **Not activated yet** | Setup is not finished — **Continue setup** |

**Pause earning** asks *"Pause the program?"* — *"Earning stops and nothing sends. Every balance is
kept, and staff can still redeem rewards at the counter."* **Resume earning** turns it back on.

---

## Share the Join Link

The **Joining** card holds the ways customers sign up.

| Control | What it gives you |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Copy registration link** | The web join page |
| **Copy WhatsApp join link** | A link that opens WhatsApp with your join keyword ready to send |
| **Plain QR** | The join QR on its own |
| **QR poster** › **Download poster** | *"Your logo, colour and join code on one printable card."* — **Edit the card in Materials** |

The keyword and the welcome are the **Joining the program** reply — **Edit it in Messages**. The
WhatsApp link and QR need a connected number: *"Connect a WhatsApp number to get your join QR."*

## Decide Whether Corrections Need Approval

**Auto-approve staff adjustments** — *"On: a correction posts at once. Off: it waits under Activity ›
Pending for someone to approve."* See [Activity](/loyalty/activity).

## Choose the Sending Number and Channels

Under **Integrations**:

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **WhatsApp Business** | *"Connected. Pick which number the program sends from."* — pick the number. **Manage channels** opens [Settings → Channels](/settings/channels) |
| **Send WhatsApp notifications** | *"The master switch. Which messages send is decided on Messages."* |
| **Send as a plain message inside the 24-hour window** | When a customer has messaged you in the last 24 hours, the message goes as a normal chat message — free, and it works before Meta approves the template. *"Templates with buttons are always sent as templates."* |
| **Channels** | Shown only when more than one channel can send. Members hear from you on the channel they chose; *"If it cannot reach them, the next one on this list is tried."* Keep at least one on |
| **Email notifications** | *"Program notices by email."* |

<Callout type="info">
  The number you pick here is the one notifications and reminders send from. The keyword-menu
  journey picks its own **WhatsApp account** on [Messages](/loyalty/messages) › Journey.
</Callout>

## Earn Points on Online Orders

| Setting | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Shopify orders earn points** | *"Online orders follow the same earning rules as the counter."* |
| **WooCommerce orders earn points** | *"Earned when an order is marked Completed, and taken back if it is refunded or cancelled."* |

The store must be connected first in [Settings → Integrations](/settings/integrations).

## Award Points From a Till or POS

**POS and API access** — *"For a till or ordering system that awards points directly."* — uses your
organisation's API keys, the same ones listed under [Settings → Integrations](/settings/integrations).
Create a separate key for each system so you can rotate or revoke one without breaking the others.
See [External API](/external-api) for how to call it.

## Set Points to Expire

<Steps>
<Step>
Under **Points Expiration**, switch on **Enable Expiration**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick the **Expiration Period**: **3 months**, **6 months**, **12 months** or **24 months**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save Changes**.
</Step>
</Steps>

The card shows how many points are currently unspent, and what they are worth if every one is
redeemed.

<Callout type="warn">
  Switch on the **Points expiring** reminder on [Reminders](/loyalty/reminders) at the same time.
  Expiring points without a warning is the most damaging thing a loyalty programme can do to a
  customer who was saving. Give a period longer than your customers' usual gap between visits.
</Callout>

## The Danger Zone

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Reset Points** | *"Reset every balance to zero?"* — *"All points are removed from every member. This cannot be undone."* |
| **Delete Program** | *"This is permanent. Every member, balance, rule and reward is removed."* Type the programme's name to confirm. Only shown to people allowed to manage the programme |

<Callout type="warn">
  Neither can be undone. To stop the programme without losing anything, **Pause earning** instead —
  *"Pausing keeps every balance intact and simply stops earning."*
</Callout>
