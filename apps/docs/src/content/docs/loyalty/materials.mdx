**Materials** (`/dashboard/loyalty/materials`) holds *"Print-ready pieces for the counter and the
table: your join QR card and a rewards brochure."* It has two tabs, **Brochure** and **QR card**. The
logo, colour and photo come from [Program](/loyalty/program) › Profile — **Change in Profile** takes
you there.

## Make the Rewards Brochure

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Materials** › **Brochure**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a **Design** (see below), and a **Background** — *"Any colour. The text and the accents follow
it, so a dark card prints light type."*
</Step>
<Step>
Edit the **Title** and **Tagline**, and the **Earn line**. **Reset to my earn rate** puts back your
default earning rule, the way customers say it.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Rewards on the card**, tick the rewards to show, and change the line each one says on the
card if its name is not how you would put it. A tier-only reward is marked with its tier — for
example *"Only Gold members and above can redeem this"*.
</Step>
<Step>
Add any **Terms & Conditions**, and switch on **Print the join QR** to put the QR on the brochure.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save design**, then **Download** or **Print**.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Design | Use |
|--------|-----|
| **Menu card** | *"A slim brand band over a paper body. Handed to a customer at the table."* |
| **Poster** | *"The photo leads. For a wall or a counter easel, read from a distance."* |
| **Table tent** | *"Short and square, rewards in two columns, the code given room."* |
| **Typeset** | *"No colour band — the brand carried by type and rules alone."* |

Your hero photo is the background of the brochure header. Only points rewards can go on the card —
with none, the editor says *"No point rewards yet."* with **Add rewards in Rewards**.

## Print the Counter QR Card

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/loyalty/materials-qr-card.png" alt="Materials, QR card tab: headline, promise and under-the-code fields beside the card preview" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Open **Materials** › **QR card**.
</Step>
<Step>
Edit the **Headline**, the **Promise** (*"The coloured pill under the headline."*) and the text
**Under the code**.
</Step>
<Step>
Switch **Show hero photo** on, or leave it off for a band in your brand colour.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save design**, then **Download** or **Print**.
</Step>
</Steps>

Scanning the code opens WhatsApp with your join keyword ready to send — one tap and the customer is
a member. **Copy WhatsApp join link** copies the same link for your website, receipts or social
posts.

<Callout type="info">
  The join QR can only be made once a WhatsApp number is connected. Without one the tab shows
  **Connect WhatsApp to get your join QR**.
</Callout>
