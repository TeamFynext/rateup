**Set up your loyalty program** is a guided setup at `/dashboard/loyalty/onboarding`: *"Six short
steps, about ten minutes. Everything is saved as you go, and nothing reaches customers until you
activate."* It fills in points, tiers, rewards and WhatsApp messages suited to your kind of
business, and every one of them can be changed afterwards.

## Where You Land

An organisation with no loyalty programme is sent to the setup whichever loyalty page it opens.
Once you have saved a step, the programme exists as a **Draft**: you can leave and come back, and
the **Program** entry in the sidebar carries a **Draft** chip until you activate.

While the programme is a draft, only **Overview**, **Program** and **Rewards** open. Every other
loyalty page shows *"Finish setting up your program"* with a **Continue setup** button instead.
**Continue setup** on the Overview or the Program page resumes the setup at the first unfinished
step.

## Walk Through the Six Steps

<Steps>
<Step>
**Profile** — pick *"What kind of business is this?"*: **Cafe & Restaurant**, **Retail & Store**,
**Salon & Spa**, **Gym & Fitness** or **Something else**. Your choice seeds the earning rate,
rewards and messages in the later steps. Then fill in **Your business name**, **Your loyalty program
name**, **Currency**, **Time zone**, the **Primary contact**, and the branding: **Logo**, **Brand
colour** and **Hero image**.
</Step>
<Step>
**Earning** — set **What you call your points**, the rate for **Every purchase** (*"spend this, earn
that"*), and the **Typical spend in one visit**, which is what the giveback estimate is worked out
from.
</Step>
<Step>
**Tiers** — switch **Offer tiers** on or off. With tiers on, name the tier **Everyone starts as**,
then **Add a tier above** for each step up, with its **Spend to reach it**, **Points multiplier** and
**Perks**. **Spend counted within** sets whether tiers are reached on **Lifetime** spend or within a
number of months.
</Step>
<Step>
**Rewards** — review the suggested rewards, grouped by who can redeem them (**Rewards for everyone**
and one group per tier), and the **Points for joining and referring**. **Re-price from my earn rate**
resets their costs to about 3% back if you changed the rate.
</Step>
<Step>
**Messages** — under **When a customer texts you**, the keyword replies (**They text** / **You
reply**); under **What we send**, the notifications that need Meta's approval. **Write it from my
program** fills a reply in for you. The templates are submitted to Meta when you continue — you do
not have to wait for approval.
</Step>
<Step>
**Launch** — check the list, then click **Activate program**.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Step | Rough time | Where to change it later |
|------|-----------|--------------------------|
| **Profile** | 2 min | [Program](/loyalty/program) › Profile |
| **Earning** | 1 min | [Program](/loyalty/program) › Earning |
| **Tiers** | 1 min | [Program](/loyalty/program) › Tiers |
| **Rewards** | 3 min | [Rewards](/loyalty/rewards) |
| **Messages** | 2 min | [Messages](/loyalty/messages) |
| **Launch** | 1 min | [Settings](/loyalty/settings) — pause and resume |

<Callout type="info">
  Tiers take at most three in all: the starting tier and two above it. *"Three tiers is the most
  this programme can run."*
</Callout>

## Check the Launch List

The **Launch** step lists what is set up, under **Ready to go live**, and what is still **To do**.
Anything in **Worth knowing before you launch** does not block activation.

| Row | Done when |
|-----|-----------|
| **Venue & branding** | The profile is saved — a missing logo is fine, the poster uses your colour |
| **Earning rule** | *"Customers earn points on every purchase"* |
| **Tiers** | Tiers are saved above the starting tier, or switched off |
| **Rewards** | At least one reward exists |
| **WhatsApp messages** | Keyword replies and notifications are written |
| **WhatsApp channel** | *"Connected — the join QR opens a chat with your keyword ready to send"* |

Each row has a **Fix**, **Edit**, **Open** or **Connect** button that takes you back to it.

<Callout type="warn">
  Activating turns on point earning, keyword replies and notifications at once. Check the earning
  rate and reward costs before you click **Activate program** — changing them once customers hold
  points changes what those points are worth.
</Callout>

## After You Activate

*"You're live"* shows your counter QR poster, with **Print materials** and **Go to Overview**.
Scanning the QR opens WhatsApp with your join keyword ready to send. Without a connected WhatsApp
number you see *"Your programme is live. Connect WhatsApp to get the counter QR."* instead.

Points from a sign-up on the web join page are held until the customer's first real activity, so a
bot filling in the form gains nothing.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"Some keywords were already taken"* on Messages | Another automation or command already answers that word | Pick a different keyword and save again |
| *"No WhatsApp channel connected yet"* | No number connected | Set up the messages anyway; connect a number in [Settings → Channels](/settings/channels) — nothing sends until you do |
| **Move members down a tier?** when saving Tiers | You removed a tier that has members | Confirm to move them to the tier below, where they earn at its rate |
| A loyalty page shows *"Finish setting up your program"* | The programme is still a draft | **Continue setup** and activate |
