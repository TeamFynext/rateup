**Feedback** (`/dashboard/loyalty/feedback`) sends *"A short WhatsApp survey after points are
earned. Happy customers are asked for a Google review, and can be thanked with a reward."* The
sidebar entry shows **Draft** until the survey is submitted and **Awaiting Meta** while Meta reviews
it.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/loyalty/feedback.png" alt="A live feedback survey's status card above its Settings tab" />

---

## How It Works

| Step | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| A customer earns points | The survey is scheduled for the delay you set |
| They answer on WhatsApp | The answers land under **Results** |
| They rate at or above your **Count as happy** | They are sent your team's Google review link |
| They tap the link the first time | They get the review reward, if you set one |

## Set Up the Survey

<Steps>
<Step>
Go to **Meta → WhatsApp → Flows** and create a flow with the **Loyalty feedback** purpose. Choose a ready-made
survey for your kind of business, and publish it from the [flow editor](/whatsapp/flow-editor).
</Step>
<Step>
Back on **Feedback**, under **Pick a feedback flow**, choose the flow.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Where the review goes**, pick the **Team** whose Google review link happy customers are sent
to.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Use this flow**.
</Step>
<Step>
On the status card, click **Submit to Meta**. This publishes the survey and sends its message
template to Meta. *"Once published, the questions can no longer be edited — you would start over
with a new draft."*
</Step>
<Step>
When Meta approves the template, sending switches on.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Not set up** | No flow picked |
| **Draft — not submitted** | Picked, not yet sent to Meta — **Submit to Meta** |
| **Awaiting Meta** | *"Meta is reviewing the message template — usually hours, sometimes a day."* |
| **Live** | Sending |
| **Off** | **Send feedback requests** is switched off |
| **Rejected by Meta** | Detach, then select the flow again to resubmit |
| **Blocked by Meta** | *"This flow can no longer be sent. Detach it and pick another."* |

If the survey was published in the flow editor, the card asks you to **Create message template**
instead.

## Choose When to Ask and Who Counts as Happy

The **Settings** tab:

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Send** … **after the customer earns points** | **Straight away**, **30 minutes**, **1 hour**, **3 hours** or **Next day** |
| **Ask each customer at most every** … **days** | *"A regular is not asked again inside this window. 0 asks after every visit."* |
| **Count as happy** | **5 stars only**, **4 stars or more**, **3 stars or more** or **2 stars or more** — *"Only customers at or above this overall rating get the review link."* |
| **Google review** › **Team** | Whose review link is used |
| **Review reward** | A reward granted for tapping the link, or **No reward** |

Click **Save settings**.

<Callout type="warn">
  Keep **Count as happy** high. Asking an unhappy customer for a public Google review turns a
  private complaint into a one-star review. **2 stars or more** sends the link to people who just
  told you they were disappointed.
</Callout>

## Add a Google Review Link

Each team has its own link. If the chosen team has none, the page says *"Feedback is still
collected, but happy customers would have nowhere to go."*

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add link** (or **Edit Google review link**).
</Step>
<Step>
Paste the **Review link** — the *Write a review* link from your Google Business Profile.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Reward a Review

<Steps>
<Step>
Under **Review reward**, click **Create a review reward**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter the **Name** — *"Free coffee on your next visit"* — and the **Details**.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally pick the **WhatsApp message** that carries the code, or keep the **Default reward
message**.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Create reward**, then **Save settings**.
</Step>
</Steps>

A review reward *"costs no points and each customer can earn it once."* It is granted the first time
they tap the review link — RateUp cannot see whether they actually posted — and redeemed at the
counter by code like any other reward. It appears on [Rewards](/loyalty/rewards) with **Manage in
Feedback**.

## Read the Results

The **Results** tab, over **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 12 months**:

| Tile | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Asked** | Feedback requests delivered |
| **Answered** | How many finished the survey |
| **Completion rate** | Share of asks that were answered |
| **Happy** | How many rated you above the threshold |
| **Review link taps** | Happy customers who opened Google |
| **Rewards granted** | One per customer, on their first tap |

**By question** gives the average rating out of 5 for each question. The table lists every request
with its **Rating** (**Happy** / **Not happy**), **Status** (**Due**, **Sent**, **Answered**) and
whether they opened Google or were granted the reward. **Open every response** shows the full
answers.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"No Loyalty feedback flow yet"* | No flow with the Loyalty feedback purpose | Create one in **Meta → WhatsApp → Flows** |
| *"The selected feedback flow no longer exists"* | It was deleted in Meta → WhatsApp → Flows | **Detach**, then pick another |
| Nobody is being asked | **Send feedback requests** is off, or the template is still with Meta | Check the status card |
| Happy customers are not sent anywhere | The team has no Google review link | **Add link** |

**Detach** keeps the flow in Meta → WhatsApp → Flows and keeps your results; surveys already sent still
record their answers, but no review link is sent for them.
