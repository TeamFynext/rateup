**Overview** is the first entry under **Loyalty** (`/dashboard/loyalty/overview`, previously
**Dashboard**). It leads with what your programme gives back, then lists what needs doing, then the
numbers.

## Read the Status Strip

The line under the heading says where the programme stands.

| Shown | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Live since** a date | The programme is active |
| **Earning paused** | Paused in [Settings](/loyalty/settings) — balances are kept |
| **Draft — nothing has reached customers** | Setup is not finished |
| Members | The total number of members |
| **Giveback** a percentage **of revenue** | What the programme hands back, modelled at the best reward |
| Templates **awaiting Meta** | Messages still in review — click it to open [Messages](/loyalty/messages) › Templates |

While the programme is paused, a banner reads *"Earning is paused — balances are safe"*, with
**Resume in Settings**.

---

## Finish Setup From a Draft

A draft programme shows a card saying how many steps are left — *"Everything you have saved is kept.
Points start moving the moment you activate — nothing sends before that."* — with each step ticked
off and a **Start setup** or **Continue setup** button. See
[Set Up Your Programme](/loyalty/onboarding).

The numbers on a draft read *"Starts counting when you activate"*. Only the giveback estimate is
live, worked out from your typical spend.

---

## Check What Your Programme Gives Back

The giveback panel — **Your program gives back** (or **Your program would give back** on a draft) —
shows the share of revenue your rewards hand back. On a live programme the hint also gives what you
actually gave back in the period. Change the typical spend it is based on in
[Program](/loyalty/program) › Earning.

---

## Act on What to Do Next

**What to do next** lists nudges based on how the programme is set up and where members stand
today. It does not change with the period picker. Each one links to the fix.

| Suggestion | Link |
|------------|------|
| **Customers earn nothing right now** | **Open Program › Earning** |
| **There is nothing to redeem** | **Open Rewards** |
| A tier has too many members — *"lower the threshold"* | **Open Program › Tiers** |
| Messages *"still waiting on Meta"* | **Open Messages** |
| **Write your remaining messages** | **Open Messages** |
| **WhatsApp is not connected** | **Connect WhatsApp in Settings** |
| **Print the join QR for the counter** | **Open Settings** |

With nothing to flag it reads *"Nothing needs your attention right now."*

## The Numbers

Pick the period at the top right: **Last 30 days**, **Last 90 days** or **Last 12 months**. Each tile
compares with the period before. Labels use your own name for points — *"Beans issued"*, not
*"Points issued"*.

| Tile | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Members** | Everyone enrolled |
| Points **issued** | Points given out in the period |
| Points **redeemed** | Points spent on rewards, and how many rewards were handed over |
| **Redemption rate** | Redeemed as a share of issued — *"a healthy program sits near 40%"* |
| **Repeat visit rate** | Members buying twice or more in the period. *"Needs a week of trading to be useful"* |
| **Revenue on loyalty transactions** | Spend recorded against loyalty purchases |
| **Outstanding** points **liability** | Points unspent, with what they are worth if all redeemed |

## Charts and Recent Activity

| Chart | Shows |
|-------|-------|
| **Member growth** | Enrolment over time |
| **Members by tier** | How members are spread across your tiers |
| Points **issued and redeemed** | The two side by side |
| **Most redeemed rewards** | What people actually choose |
| **Recent activity** | The latest point movements, with **All activity** linking to [Activity](/loyalty/activity) |

Most programmes should look like a pyramid on **Members by tier**: many at the starting tier, fewer
at each step up. If nearly everyone sits in one tier, the threshold above it is too high.

<Callout type="warn">
  A large outstanding balance is a liability, not a success. Every unspent point is a promise you
  will eventually have to honour, and a customer who never redeems is one who stopped caring.
</Callout>
