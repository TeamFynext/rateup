---
title: "Notifications"
description: Loyalty notifications are now the Automatic tab on Loyalty › Messages, with scheduled ones timed on Reminders.
---

The messages the programme sends on its own — points earned, reward redeemed, tier upgrade — are on
**Messages** › **Automatic**. When the scheduled ones go out — birthdays, quiet customers, expiring
points — is set on **Reminders**. The master switch and the sending number are in **Settings**.

See [Messages](/loyalty/messages) and [Reminders](/loyalty/reminders).
