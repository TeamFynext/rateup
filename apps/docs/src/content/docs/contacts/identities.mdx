A contact is one person, and one person can be reached in several ways: a work and a personal
number, two emails, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, your website chat. RateUp keeps all of them on
the one contact. Each is an **identity**, and they are listed wherever you see the contact.

---

## What a Contact Can Hold

| Identity | Shown as | Notes |
|----------|----------|-------|
| **Phone** | `+919188346187` | Any number of them. One is the **main** phone |
| **Email** | `jane@example.com` | Any number of them. One is the **main** email |
| **WhatsApp** | The customer's WhatsApp name, their business-scoped user ID (BSUID) under it, and the business it belongs to | A customer who hides their number is known by a BSUID. Meta issues a different BSUID for each business, so a contact can hold one per [business](/settings/channels) |
| **Instagram** | `@username` and the Instagram account it came in on | One per Instagram account you connected — Instagram gives each account its own id for the same person |
| **Messenger** | The customer's name and the Facebook Page | One per Page, for the same reason |
| **Web visitor** | **Web visitor** | Someone who wrote through your website chat widget |
| **External ID** | The id, labelled **External ID** | Set from the contact form, an import or an integration |

A contact keeps **at least one** identity — the last one cannot be removed (*"A contact needs at
least one way to reach it"*).

### What "main" means

The **main** phone and email are the ones RateUp shows as the contact's phone and email: in the
contacts table, the contact's header, exports, automations, Zapier and CRM syncs, and the External
API's `phone` and `email` fields. The main phone is also the number WhatsApp messages go to. The
other phones and emails still find the contact — in search, when that number writes in, or when an
import or integration matches on it.

## Where Identities Show

| Where | What you can do |
|-------|-----------------|
| The Inbox ticket panel, **Identities** | See them all, add a phone or email, edit, make main, remove. Changes save immediately |
| A contact's details, **Identities** | The same block |
| **Edit Contact** | **Phone numbers** and **Emails** lists, saved with the rest of the form. WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger accounts are listed under **Accounts** |

## Edit Phones and Emails in the Contact Form

<Steps>
<Step>
### Open Edit Contact

From the contacts table, **⋮ → Edit**, or **Edit** in the contact's details.
</Step>

<Step>
### Change the lists

Under **Phone numbers** and **Emails**, each value has its own row:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| Type in a row | Changes that phone or email |
| ☆ **Make main** | Moves that row to the top, making it the main one |
| ★ (filled) | Marks the current main one — **Main number** / **Main email** |
| ✕ **Remove** | Takes that row off |
| **+ Add phone** / **+ Add email** | Adds an empty row |

The star and ✕ appear only when there is more than one row. At least one phone number is required
for a contact you added by hand.
</Step>

<Step>
### Save

Click **Save**. Every change in the lists is applied together with the rest of the form.
</Step>
</Steps>

Below the form, **Accounts** lists the contact's WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger accounts and its
merge history, with **Merge with…**. Changes made there save straight away, separately from **Save**.

<Callout type="info">
  The form only sends a phone, email or external ID you actually changed. If you make another
  number the main one under **Accounts** or in the Inbox while the form is open, pressing **Save**
  does not put the old number back.
</Callout>

## Edit Them From the Inbox or the Contact's Details

The **Identities** block saves each change the moment you make it.

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Add phone or email** | Type a phone (with country code) or an email and click **Add** |
| ✏️ **Edit** | Turns the row into a field — **Save** or ✕ to cancel (Enter / Esc work too) |
| ⭐ **Make main phone** / **Make main email** | Makes it the main one. *"Main phone changed"* confirms it |
| **Main** | Marks the main one, when there is more than one of the kind |
| ✕ **Remove** | Asks *"Remove +919000000001?"* — *"This contact will no longer be matched or reached on it."* |

Only phones and emails can be edited. WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger and web visitor identities come
from the platforms themselves, so they have no edit button — only ✕.

---

## When Another Contact Already Has It

A phone or email can belong to only one contact. Adding or editing one that another contact
already has is refused with *"Another contact already has this"* and the message *"+919000000001 belongs to a
different contact. If they are the same person, merge the two."* — click **Merge with that contact**
to open the [merge](/contacts/merging-contacts) for that pair. The pair is also added to **Possible
duplicates**.

Saving **Edit Contact** with such a value is refused too — *"Another contact already has this. Merge
them instead."* — and the dialog stays open. Adding a new contact whose phone or email another
contact already holds is refused as well.

## Finding a Contact by Any of Them

Search matches every identity, not only the main ones:

- The **Inbox** search box finds a ticket by any phone the contact has (with or without `+` and
  spaces), any email, or a WhatsApp or Instagram username.
- The contacts table's **Search by name, phone, or email...** matches every phone and email too.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---------|-------|-----|
| *"Another contact already has this"* | That phone or email belongs to a different contact | If it is the same person, click **Merge with that contact** |
| *"That doesn't look like a valid phone number or email"* | A phone without its country code, too short or too long, or a malformed email | Type the number with its country code — `447700900123`, not `07700900123` |
| *"This contact already has that"* | You edited a value into one this contact already holds | Remove the duplicate row instead |
| The ✕ is missing on the last identity | A contact needs at least one way to reach it | Add another phone or email first |
| No edit button on a WhatsApp or Instagram row | Platform ids cannot be edited by hand | Remove it if it is wrong; it is added back if the customer writes in again |
