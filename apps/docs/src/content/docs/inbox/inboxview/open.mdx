**Open** shows every **active** ticket: those already assigned and those still waiting for an
owner. Nothing closed appears here.

It is the default view when you enter the Inbox, and the one managers live in.

---

## What You See

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/view-open.png" alt="Assigned and unassigned tickets side by side" />

- Every ticket is still active
- Each row's status pill shows **New** (unassigned) or **Assigned**
- Unassigned rows with a message in the last 24 hours are highlighted: cream background, **amber
  left edge** and a filled **Unassigned** badge. Older unassigned rows are not highlighted — only
  their **New** pill gives them away
- The owner's avatar sits on the right. A `+2` only appears on older tickets that were assigned to several people

---

## Open vs Unassigned vs Assigned to Me

| View | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Open** | All active tickets, assigned or not |
| **Unassigned** | Active tickets with **no owner** and a message in the last 24 hours |
| **Assigned to me** | Active tickets assigned **to you** |

**Unassigned** and **Assigned to me** are both subsets of **Open**.

---

## Reading Ownership at a Glance

| Where | What it shows |
|-------|---------------|
| Conversation row | The owner's avatar — hover for the name |
| Chat header | Status pill: **Assigned** or **New** |
| Ticket Details panel | The owner's name and team — click it to reassign |

## Reassign a Ticket

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **panel icon** at the far right of the chat header to open **Ticket Details**.
</Step>
<Step>
Under **Assignment**, click the owner's name — or press **A**.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick another person, or a team under **Auto-assign in a team**. The ticket is reassigned instantly and a system note records it.
</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  A ticket has one owner. Picking a team hands it to **one** member of that team, turn by turn, so it
  lands in exactly one **Assigned to me** view.
  [How team assignment works →](/inbox/asigning-conversation#let-a-team-pick-whose-turn-it-is)
</Callout>

## Using Open as a Manager

- Sort by **oldest first** to surface tickets that have been waiting
- Use the **staff** and **team** filters to check one person's or one team's load
- Watch for **New** status pills — those tickets have nobody on them, highlighted or not
- Reassign when someone goes offline rather than letting their tickets stall

---

## Role-Based Behaviour

| Role | In the Open view |
|------|------------------|
| **Owner**, **Admin** | Sees every open ticket, on every channel |
| **Manager** | Sees open tickets on the channels their teams grant |
| **Staff**, **Agent** | The same channel scope, minus any assigned tickets their organisation hides from them |

Anyone who can see an open ticket can reply to it and reassign it, except a **Viewer**, who can only read.
