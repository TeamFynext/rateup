**Closed** holds tickets that have been **closed, resolved or marked unresolved**.

They are finished work: no longer active, but kept as a permanent record for reference and
reporting.

Tickets belonging to a contact you have flagged as spam are not here — they are in
[**Spam contacts**](/inbox/inboxview/spam-contacts), with the contact.

## Closed Tickets Are Read-Only

Opening a closed ticket replaces the composer with a banner:

> **This chat has been closed** by *RateUp Admin* at *16/12/25 11:01 PM*. Their 24-hour window is
> still open, so you can message them straight away.

Once the window has closed, the second sentence reads *"Start a new conversation with a
template."*

You cannot send a message or a private note on a closed ticket. The record stays exactly as it was
left. The banner's **New conversation** and **Assign to…** buttons, like **Reopen**, start a new ticket
rather than reviving this one.

You can still:

- Read the whole message history
- See the close result and who closed it
- Open the **Ticket Details** panel
- Star the ticket
- Export the conversation
- Delete it permanently, if you are an admin — from the **⋮** menu

## Reopening

Click **Reopen** in the chat header.

<Callout type="warn">
  Reopening does **not** revive the old ticket. It opens a **new ticket** for the same customer and
  carries over the people who were assigned when the original closed.
</Callout>

| | Original ticket | New ticket |
|---|-----------------|-----------|
| **Number** | Unchanged | A fresh number |
| **Status** | Stays Closed | Assigned |
| **Close time and result** | Frozen | Not set yet |
| **Where it lives** | Closed view + history bar | Open / Assigned to me |

Both remain visible in the customer's **previous conversations** bar, so the full story is never
lost.

---

## When a Customer Replies After Closure

You do not need to do anything. Their message creates a **new ticket**, which lands in **Unassigned**.
The closed ticket stays closed, so its resolution time and result stay accurate.

---

## When to Reopen

<Steps>
<Step>
**The ticket was closed by mistake** — reopen and carry on.
</Step>
<Step>
**The fix did not work** — reopen so the follow-up is tracked as active work rather than buried in
a closed thread.
</Step>
<Step>
**A customer asks about something unrelated** — do *not* reopen. Let their message open its own
ticket.
</Step>
</Steps>

---

## Role-Based Behaviour

| Role | In the Closed view |
|------|--------------------|
| **Owner**, **Admin** | Views and reopens any closed ticket, on every channel |
| **Manager** | Views and reopens closed tickets on the channels their teams grant |
| **Staff**, **Agent** | The same channel scope, minus any assigned tickets their organisation hides from them |

Anyone who can see a closed ticket can reopen it, except a **Viewer**.
