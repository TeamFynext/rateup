The **Ticket Details** panel is everything about a ticket and its customer that does not belong in
the message thread.

Open it with the **panel icon** at the far right of the chat header (**Toggle details panel**); close
it with the chevron in the panel's own header. On a wide screen it sits beside the conversation. On
a narrower one it opens over it.

## Panel Header

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Ticket #number** | The ticket's identity |
| **Copy link to ticket** (🔗) | Copies a link to this ticket to your clipboard; the icon briefly reads **Copied** |
| **Collapse Panel** (›) | Hides the panel to give the conversation more room |

## Assignment

Shows the owner and their team, or **Unassigned**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the owner (or **Unassigned**) — or press **A** anywhere in the ticket.
</Step>
<Step>
Search in **Search people or teams**, then click a person, **Assign to me**, **Unassign**, or a team
under **Auto-assign in a team** to hand the ticket to whichever member's turn it is.
</Step>
</Steps>

The change saves at once and replaces the previous owner. Without the **Assign conversations**
ability the owner is shown but cannot be clicked.

[Full assignment guide →](/inbox/asigning-conversation)

## Title & Description

The ticket's own words, directly under Assignment because they are what an agent reads first. A
ticket with no title shows **Untitled ticket**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the **pencil** (**Edit title and description**).
</Step>
<Step>
Write a short, specific **Ticket title**. It is what your team reads in the conversation list.
</Step>
<Step>
Add a **Description** for context the messages do not make obvious.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Save**, or **Cancel** to discard.
</Step>
</Steps>

| Good title | Weak title |
|-----------|------------|
| Refund not received for order 8821 | Question |
| Wrong size delivered — needs exchange | Customer issue |
| Bulk pricing request, 200 units | Follow up |

Hover a title in the conversation list to read its description without opening the ticket.

## Lead Stage

One row showing where this customer sits on your [pipeline](/pipelines) board. It shows the current
column in that column's colour, or **Not on the board**.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click the stage row to open the picker. It lists every column on the board, each marked as open,
won or lost.
</Step>
<Step>
Click a column. The customer moves there — a lead is opened for them if they had none.
</Step>
</Steps>

To take the customer off the board, open the picker and choose **Remove from the board** at the
bottom, then confirm with **Remove**.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Remove from the board** deletes the lead. Its notes, tasks, timeline and assignees go with it,
  and the confirmation counts each of them first. To record a deal that did not happen, mark it
  [lost](/pipelines/leads#closing-a-lead) instead.
</Callout>

This belongs to the **contact**, so it applies to all of their tickets and shows as a chip on each
of their rows. The section is absent for organisations without the **Pipeline** feature, and on a
board with no columns. Columns are configured in [Pipeline → Stages](/pipelines/stages).

## Conversation Tags

Tags on **this ticket only** — *Bug Report*, *Escalated*, *Refund*.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **+ Add**.
</Step>
<Step>
Type in **Search tags** and click a tag to add it. Click the **✕** on a tag chip to remove it.
</Step>
</Steps>

If no tags exist yet, the picker reads *"No conversation tags yet — create them in Inbox
Settings."* [Tags & labels →](/inbox/conversation-tags)

## Contact Labels

Colour-coded labels on the **customer** — VIP, wholesale, churn risk.

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **+ Add**.
</Step>
<Step>
Type in **Search labels** and click a label to add it. Click the **✕** on a label chip to remove it.
</Step>
</Steps>

With no labels yet, the picker reads *"No contact labels yet."* Labels are created under
[Contacts → Labels](/contacts/labels).

Labels show on every conversation row for that customer, and filter the Inbox from the sidebar's
**Contact Labels** section.

## Contact

The customer's avatar with a dot in their channel's colour, their name, phone number and **Customer
since** — the month and year the contact was created. A shield beside the name shows **Consent
given** (ticked) or **No consent** (crossed).

Click **View contact** to open the contact's details. Edits saved there — name, phone, email —
update the contact everywhere in RateUp, including this ticket and the conversation list.

<Callout type="warn">
  **Consent** controls whether this customer may receive template messages. A contact without
  consent shows a **DND** badge next to their name in the chat header.
</Callout>

## Identities

Every way this customer is known: their phone numbers and emails, their WhatsApp account
(name, business-scoped user ID (BSUID) and the business it belongs to), and their Instagram and
Messenger accounts. A customer who wrote to you on several channels — or two contacts you merged —
shows all of them here. [What identities are →](/contacts/identities)

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/ticket-panel-identities.png" alt="The Identities block of a contact known on WhatsApp and Instagram." />

Phone and email rows carry their own controls:

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Main** | Marks the main phone or email — the one shown on the contact everywhere else. Only shown when the contact has more than one of that kind |
| ⭐ **Make main phone** / **Make main email** | Makes this one the main one |
| ✏️ **Edit phone number** / **Edit email** | Edit the value in place; **Enter** saves, **Esc** cancels |
| ✕ **Remove** | Removes it after a confirmation: *"This contact will no longer be matched or reached on it."* |

WhatsApp, Instagram and Messenger rows can be removed but not edited — their ids come from Meta.
A contact always keeps at least one identity, so the ✕ disappears from the last one.

### Add a Phone or Email

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add phone or email**.
</Step>
<Step>
Type the number (with its country code) or the address, then click **Add**.
</Step>
</Steps>

If another contact already has it, you see **Another contact already has this** — they are probably
the same person, and **Merge with that contact** opens the merge.

### Possible Duplicates and Merging

When RateUp spots a strong sign that this customer is also another contact — the same phone or
email, or a webchat visitor who typed this contact's number — a banner appears above the list:
*"Possible duplicate of Jane Roe"*, with the reason underneath. Click **Review** to compare the two
and merge them.

**Merge with…** in the block's header does the same for a contact you pick yourself. Each merge
leaves a line under the list — *"Merged with Jane Roe by John Doe on 5 Nov 2026"*, or *"Linked
automatically from a WhatsApp link on …"* for a [Continue on WhatsApp](/automation/journey-builder#continue-on-whatsapp)
link. [How merging works →](/contacts/merging-contacts)

Adding, editing and removing need the **Add, edit and import contacts** permission; merging needs
**Merge duplicate contacts**, which Owners and Admins have by default.

## Ticket Info

Read-only facts about this ticket:

| Field | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Channel** | WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger or Webchat |
| **Account** | Which of your numbers or accounts the ticket came in on — marked *(no longer connected)* if you have since disconnected it |
| **Created** | When the ticket was opened |
| **Result** | The outcome it was closed with, if any |

Each ticket runs on one channel, so **Account** stays correct even if the customer has since written
to a different number of yours — that message opens its own ticket on that number.

## Spam, Block and Delete

Up to three buttons sit at the foot of the panel.

**Spam** — the same action as **Close → Mark contact as spam**. It flags the **contact**, closes
their open tickets and moves them into the **Spam contacts** tab. If the contact is already flagged, the
button reads **Not spam** and takes them back out. [More about spam →](/inbox/inboxview/spam-contacts)

**Block** — blocks the customer on WhatsApp after a confirmation: *"They will not be able to message
you, and you will not be able to message them."* It only appears inside the customer's 24-hour
window, because WhatsApp only accepts a block then, and not on a contact who is already blocked.

**Delete** — **admins only**. Permanently deletes this ticket and every message in it, after a
confirmation. The contact and their other tickets are untouched.
[More about deleting →](/inbox/conversation-lifecycle#deleting-a-conversation)

## Ticket Fields vs Contact Fields

Knowing which is which saves confusion:

| Belongs to the **ticket** | Belongs to the **contact** |
|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| Channel and account | Identities |
| Title and description | Name, phone, email |
| Assignment | Lead stage and lifecycle |
| Conversation tags | Contact labels |
| Status and result | Consent |
| Created and closed dates | Custom fields |
| Favourite star | Spam and blocked flags |

Change a contact field and it applies to every ticket that customer will ever have. Change a
ticket field and only this ticket is affected.
