**Calls** lists every WhatsApp voice call your team took or made, and shows how calling is going.
Open it from **Calls** in the Inbox section of the sidebar, below the views and filters
(`/dashboard/inbox/calls`). The page keeps the Inbox sidebar, so you can go straight back to a view.
It was previously **Analytics → Call History**, and before that sat under WhatsApp; old links to
`/dashboard/analytics/calls` and `/dashboard/whatsapp/calls` land here.

Calls only appear once calling is turned on for a number — see
[WhatsApp Voice Calls](/inbox/whatsapp-calls).

## See Who Is on a Call Right Now

Four tiles sit at the top. They read the phones as of this moment and ignore the date range below
them:

| Tile | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **On call** | Calls in progress |
| **Waiting** | Customers calling in whose call is still ringing |
| **Longest wait** | How long the longest-ringing of them has waited, counting up live. **Nobody waiting** when none are |
| **Missed** | Incoming calls nobody answered in the **last hour** |

The first three carry a pulsing green dot and the note **Real-time**. The tiles refresh every 15
seconds, and sooner when a call event arrives.

## Narrow the Page

| Control | What it does |
|---------|--------------|
| **Number** | One of your WhatsApp numbers, or **All numbers**. Only shown when you have more than one |
| **Date range** | **Today**, **Yesterday**, **Last 7 Days** (the default), **Last 30 Days** or **Custom Range** |

The **Number** select also narrows the live tiles. Both tabs below follow both controls.

## Read the Call Log

**Call log** is the first tab. Each row is one call, newest first:

| Part of the row | Shows |
|-----------------|-------|
| Time and date | When the call started. A green dot means it is still in progress |
| Agent | Who took or placed it — **No agent** if nobody did — with the outcome: **Answered**, **Missed**, **Declined**, **Failed** or **Ringing** |
| Customer | Their name and number, or just the number |
| Direction arrow | Incoming or outgoing. Red for an incoming call nobody answered |
| Duration | Talk time. A call still on the line counts up — ring time until it is answered, then talk time |
| Action button | **Call back** on an unanswered incoming call, **Open chat** on every other |

Unanswered incoming calls — missed, declined or failed — are tinted red so they stand out.

Filter the log with **Search name or number**, the direction select (**All directions**,
**Incoming**, **Outgoing**) and the outcome select (**All outcomes**, **Answered**, **Missed**,
**Declined**, **Failed**). Fifty calls load at a time; click **Load more** for the next fifty.

With nothing to show, it reads *"No calls — No calls match these filters. Try a longer date
range."*

### Call a customer back

<Steps>
<Step>
Find the missed call and click **Call back** at the end of its row.
</Step>
<Step>
The customer's chat opens in the Inbox.
</Step>
<Step>
Click the **phone** icon in the chat header to call them. WhatsApp's permission rules apply there —
see [Call a customer](/inbox/whatsapp-calls#call-a-customer).
</Step>
</Steps>

## Read the Call Analytics

The **Analytics** tab (`/dashboard/inbox/calls?tab=analytics`) is only shown to members with the
**See analytics** ability.

| Tile | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Calls** | Every call in the range, with *"N incoming · N outgoing"* |
| **Answer rate** | Answered incoming calls as a share of finished incoming calls. **No incoming calls** when there were none |
| **Missed** | Missed incoming calls, with *"N called back"* — how many of those customers your team then called within the range |
| **Avg call length** | Average length of answered calls, with *"Picked up in … on average"* — how long incoming calls rang before someone answered |

| Card | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Calls per day** | Answered against not answered (missed, declined or failed), per day |
| **Busiest hours** | Incoming calls by weekday and hour, in your time zone. Darker cells had more calls; hover one for its count |
| **Agents** | Answered and connected calls by who handled them: **Calls**, **Talk time** and **Avg length** |

Use **Busiest hours** to set your [calling hours](/inbox/whatsapp-calls#before-you-start) and to
plan who is at their desk when customers ring.

## Who Sees Which Calls

Calls follow the same rules as the Inbox:

| Member | Sees |
|--------|------|
| Org-wide roles | Every number's calls |
| Members whose teams reach only some numbers | Those numbers' calls |
| Members limited to their own chats | Only calls they answered or placed |

The **Call log** needs the **See conversations** ability.

<Callout type="warn">
  A high **Missed** count with a low *"called back"* figure means customers who tried to talk to you
  were never reached. Work the red rows in the call log every day — a missed call is a customer
  who wanted you badly enough to ring.
</Callout>
