Inbox Settings is where you configure how the Inbox behaves for everyone: when quiet tickets close,
how tickets are tagged, and what agents can insert with a quick reply.

Open it from **Inbox settings** at the foot of the Inbox section in the sidebar
(`/dashboard/inbox?tab=settings`). The page — *"Manage auto-close, conversation tags and quick
replies."* — has three tabs. **Auto-close** was previously called **Automation**.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/inbox-settings-tabs.png" alt="Inbox Settings with the Auto-close, Conversation tags and Quick replies tabs, open on Auto-close" />

| Tab | What it covers |
|-----|----------------|
| **Auto-close** | Auto-close rules for tickets that go quiet |
| **Conversation tags** | Tags that describe a single ticket — see [Conversation Tags](/inbox/conversation-tags) |
| **Quick replies** | Saved answers, with attachments, that agents insert in the composer |

Teams are managed under [Settings → Teams](/settings/teams), channels under
[Settings → Channels](/settings/channels), and contact labels under
[Contacts → Labels](/contacts/labels).

<Callout type="info">
  These settings apply to the whole organisation. Changing them affects every agent's Inbox
  immediately.
</Callout>

## Close Quiet Tickets Automatically

The **Auto-close** tab holds the auto-close rules: *"Close conversations that have gone quiet, so the
inbox only shows what still needs a reply."* There are two rules, each with its own switch:

| Rule | Applies to |
|------|-----------|
| **New and unassigned conversations** | Tickets nobody has picked up yet |
| **Assigned conversations** | Tickets an agent owns but has stopped replying to |

<Steps>
<Step>
Turn on the rule's switch. It starts at **24 hours** — *"Auto-close turned on"* confirms it.
</Step>
<Step>
Pick a period in the **Close after** select: 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 or 12 hours, 1, 2 or 3 days, or 1 week.
</Step>
<Step>
For anything else, choose **Custom…**, type a number of **hours** from 1 to 720, and click the tick.
</Step>
</Steps>

Each change saves straight away — *"Closes after … now"*. The rule's description then reads, for
example, *"Closed after 2 days without a new message."*

### Choosing sensible periods

| Rule | Typical | Why |
|------|---------|-----|
| **New and unassigned** | Shorter — 1–2 days | If nobody claimed it in two days, nobody will |
| **Assigned** | Longer — 3 days or more | An owner may legitimately be waiting on the customer |

<Callout type="warn">
  Set the assigned period too short and you will close tickets agents are still working on. Start
  generous and tighten it once you have seen the effect.
</Callout>

Auto-closed tickets behave exactly like manually closed ones: read-only, no assignees, and
reopenable. If a customer writes again, a new ticket is created.

## Quick Replies

Quick replies are saved answers agents insert instead of retyping the same thing all day. A quick
reply can carry **images, voice notes and videos** as well as text.

Agents reach them by clicking the **lightning** button in the composer, or by typing `/` in the
message box.

  src={`/docs/screenshots/inbox/quick-replies-list.png`}
  alt="Quick replies list"
  width={"70%"}
/>

### Add a quick reply

<Steps>
<Step>
Open the **Quick replies** tab and click **Add Quick Reply**.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **Key** — the short handle agents type after `/`. Keep it memorable: `refund-policy` beats
`qr-14`.
</Step>
<Step>
Enter a **Topic**, e.g. *Pricing*. Agents can search by topic as well as by key.
</Step>
<Step>
Write the **Message**. Use the format button for bold, italics and lists.
</Step>
<Step>
Optionally, under **Attachments**, click **Attach image, audio or video** and pick files.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Add quick reply**.
</Step>
</Steps>

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/inbox/add-quick-reply.png" alt="Add quick reply dialog with key, topic, message and the attachments button" />

A quick reply needs a message, an attachment, or both — an empty one gives *"Add a message or an
attachment"*. It is available in every conversation straight away.

### Attachment limits

| Type | Formats | Largest file |
|------|---------|--------------|
| **Image** | PNG, JPEG | 5 MB |
| **Audio** | AAC, AMR, MP3, MP4 audio, OGG | 10 MB |
| **Video** | MP4, 3GP | 10 MB |

Up to **5** attachments per quick reply. A file outside these limits is refused with a message
saying why, e.g. *"… is larger than 10 MB"*.

Files upload as soon as you pick them. If you close the dialog without saving, the uploads are
thrown away.

### What agents see

Quick replies with attachments show a **paperclip** in the composer's list. Picking one inserts the
text and adds the files to the message as attachments, where the agent can remove any they do not
want before sending. Removing an attachment from the message never removes it from the saved quick
reply.

Attachments are added only in the Inbox composer.

### Manage quick replies

From the list you can **edit** and **delete** replies. Changes apply instantly across the Inbox.

### Writing good quick replies

- Keep them short — agents will edit them before sending anyway
- Leave the greeting out, so agents can personalise the opening line
- Cover the questions you answer daily, not the rare ones
- Attach the price list or the how-to video once, instead of every agent hunting for the file
- Review them quarterly; outdated policies in a quick reply spread fast
- Remember customer messages cap at **1024 characters**
