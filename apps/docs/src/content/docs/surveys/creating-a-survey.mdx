A survey starts from **Surveys & Links → Surveys** (`/dashboard/surveys`), either blank or from one
of RateUp's templates. The page has two tabs: **Surveys**, your own, and **Templates**.

---

## Start Blank or From a Template

<Steps>
<Step>
To start blank, click **Create Survey** on the **Surveys** tab (or **Start Blank** on the
**Templates** tab). To start from a template, open the **Templates** tab, click a template, and
click **Try this Template** in the **Create Survey from Template** dialog.
</Step>
<Step>
In the **Create Survey** dialog, enter a **Title** (labelled **Template Selected** when you came
from a template), pick at least one language under **Select Languages**, and choose the teams it
belongs to under **Select teams** — **All Teams** or **Select Manually**. Pick a theme under
**Select Theme** if you like.
</Step>
<Step>
Click **Submit**. The editor opens on the **Questions** tab.
</Step>
</Steps>

Templates are worth browsing even if you end up starting blank — a template shows how a
well-structured survey is put together. If there are none, the tab shows *"No templates found"*.

---

## Adding Questions

The editor has three parts: the **question list** down the left, the **Page Editor** in the centre
with a **Preview** button in its header, and the **Add Element** pane you pick new questions from.

<ScreenShot src="/docs/screenshots/web-forms/editor.png" alt="The survey editor" />

<Steps>
<Step>
Click **Add Question**, then pick a [type](/surveys/question-types) from **Add Element**. The tiles
are grouped by category — type in **Search elements…** to jump straight to one.
</Step>
<Step>
Write the question in **Enter question here**.
</Step>
<Step>
Add a **description** if the question needs context — but if it needs a paragraph, rewrite the
question.
</Step>
<Step>
Configure the type-specific options.
</Step>
<Step>
Reorder questions from the sidebar by dragging.
</Step>
</Steps>

## Writing Questions People Answer

Completion rate is decided almost entirely by the questions, not the design.

| Do | Instead of |
|----|-----------|
| Ask one thing per question | Bundling two into one sentence |
| Use the customer's words | Internal jargon |
| Make optional questions optional | Requiring everything |
| Put the easy questions first | Opening with a long free-text box |
| Stop at five or six questions | Twenty "while we have you" extras |

<Callout type="warn">
  Every extra question costs completions. Before adding one, ask what decision its answer will
  change — if there is no answer to that, cut it.
</Callout>

---

## Previewing

Use **Preview** to take the survey as a customer would. Check it on a phone: most responses arrive on
one, and layouts that work on a laptop often do not.

The editor flags problems with an **Issue with your survey** warning — resolve those before
sharing.

---

## Status

A survey is **Active** or **Paused**. Paused surveys do not accept responses, which is the safe way to
stop collection without deleting anything.
